# Vision-Language & Multimodal Open-Weight Models That Fit 128 GB (state as of 2026-11-24)

Scope: image / video / document-OCR / GUI-agent / omni (audio) models whose weights are ≤ ~110 GB in an official (or widely used) precision. **Every size below was measured from the Hugging Face API** (`/api/models/<repo>?blobs=true`, sum of `siblings[].size`) on 2026-11-24. GB = 10^9 bytes. Excluded per assignment: GLM-5.1, GLM-4.6, gpt-oss-120b, Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507, DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, DeepSeek-R1-0528, Apriel-1.5-15B-Thinker, Kimi-K2-Instruct-0905, Llama-3.3-Nemotron-Super-49B-v1.5, Mistral-Small-3.2-24B-Instruct-2506.

## Key Question 1: Which multimodal models in this size class are current and strongest as of Nov 2026 (image, documents/OCR, video, GUI/computer-use, audio/omni), and which are superseded?

### Takeaway
The 128 GB class changed completely in 2026. The leaders are now **Qwen3.8-27B** (Aug 2026, dense, Apache-2.0, 55.6 GB BF16), **Thinking Machines' Inkling-2-Mini** (released 2026-11-20, 26.4B/3.4B-active MoE, Apache-2.0, 20.2 GB official), **Meta's Muse-Glimmer-30B** (Aug 2026, Apache-2.0), **Qwen3.6-35B-A3B**, **Gemma 4 31B / 26B-A4B** (now Apache-2.0), **NVIDIA Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL-120B-A12B NVFP4** (75 GB, 1M context), and, at the top of what fits, **Qwen3.5-122B-A10B (official GPTQ-Int4, 78.9 GB)** and **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next** via a 4-bit build (93.7–102 GB). Specialists: **PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 / GLM-OCR / Unlimited-OCR-2** (documents), **Fara1.5-27B** (web agent), **Nemotron-3-Nano-Omni, Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B, MiniCPM-o-4.5, Gemma 4 12B/E4B** (audio/omni). Superseded: Qwen3-VL, GLM-4.5V, Gemma 3, Llama 4 Scout, Kimi-VL, InternVL3.5 (no successor released), Phi-4-multimodal, olmOCR-2 and dots.ocr. GLM-5.3-Flash (the only GLM-5 vision model) is 321B and does not fit.

### Inferences
- **Best single pick for a 128 GB box:** Qwen3.8-27B (BF16 55.6 GB, leaving ~70 GB for KV cache) for maximum image/GUI/doc capability under Apache-2.0. **Inkling-2-Mini** (20 GB, 3.4B active) is the fastest top-tier option, competitive on MMMU-Pro and better on OmniDocBench and ScreenSpot-Pro in its own table. Both are newer than any model in the assignment's example list.
- **Highest-capacity options that still fit:** Qwen3.5-122B-A10B GPTQ-Int4 (official, 78.9 GB) and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next in 4-bit (NVIDIA FP4-Local 102.2 GB, or unsloth UD-IQ4_XS 93.7 GB). The latter beats Qwen3.8-27B on every shared row of Qwen's table, but carries a custom license and a brand-new architecture (see KQ4).
- **For DGX Spark specifically:** Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL NVFP4 (75 GB, 1M context, explicit GB10 recipe) is the "native" choice. On shared benchmarks it trails Qwen3.8-27B (OSWorld-Verified 51–52 vs 84.3; MathVision 78 vs 90; CharXiv 80.8 vs 83.7), though the harnesses differ.
- The assignment's example list is mostly superseded: Qwen3-VL → Qwen3.5/3.6/3.8; GLM-4.5V → GLM-4.6V; Gemma 3 → Gemma 4; Llama 4 Scout → Meta's Muse Glimmer; MiniCPM-V 4.5 → 4.7; Molmo 2 (Dec 2025) is still current for pointing/grounding research but weak overall.

## Key Question 2: For each model — exact repo id, total/active params, recommended precision and measured size, context length, input modalities, license, release date

### Takeaway
At least 20 strong multimodal models fit under 110 GB in official precision, most under Apache-2.0 or MIT. Watch the license flags: Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (qwen-community-1.0), Chandra 2 (modified OpenRAIL-M, commercial limits), Llama 4 (gated custom), Mistral Medium 3.5 (modified MIT with company exceptions), Tencent Hy, MiniMax (custom), and NVIDIA's OpenMDW-1.1 / Open Model Agreement.

### Cited Findings
**Measurement method.** All sizes come from `https://huggingface.co/api/models/<repo>?blobs=true`, summing `siblings[].size` over all files in the repo. "API params" is the API's `safetensors.total`, which **under-counts 4-bit packed (U8) weights**, so the card's parameter count is used where they differ. Mistral repos ship weights twice (HF format plus `consolidated*.safetensors`); I report one copy. "HF created" is repo creation time and can precede the public announcement. Context is from `config.json` (`max_position_embeddings`) unless the card states otherwise. Modalities are cross-checked against vLLM's supported-models table, where T = text, I = image, V = video, A = audio. — [vLLM supported models](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/docs/models/supported_models.md)

### Inferences
- Several "official" releases are already 4-bit: Inkling-2-Mini (ModelOpt), Mistral Small 4 NVFP4, Nemotron Super VL NVFP4, Bolt 24GB, and Qwen3.5-122B GPTQ-Int4. On a 128 GB box this means more models fit without community quants. NVFP4/ModelOpt formats target NVIDIA Blackwell (DGX Spark GB10 is named explicitly); Mac and Strix Halo users will need GGUF or MLX conversions instead.
- Molmo2 is stored in FP32 (~4 bytes/param), so its download is twice what a BF16 copy would need.
- For KV-cache headroom on a 128 GB machine, the comfortable tier is under ~80 GB (Qwen3.8-27B BF16, Qwen3.5-122B Int4, Nemotron Super VL NVFP4, Mistral Small 4 NVFP4). GLM-4.6V-FP8 at 110.0 GB fits only with a small context.

### Gaps
- The active-parameter count of GLM-4.6V was not confirmed from its card text (API total 107.71B; 128 experts, top-8 in config).
- Inkling-2-Mini appears to ship only the quantized checkpoint; whether a BF16 master exists was not verified.
- Context length for Qwen3-Omni could not be read reliably from config (thinker/talker split).
- Phi-4-multimodal-instruct, `rednote-hilab/dots.ocr-1.5` (HTTP 401; not public under that id), LiquidAI LFM3-Omni-2B, ServiceNow Apriel-Voice-27B-preview and Kwai Keye-VL were not measured.
- Full terms of qwen-community-1.0, OpenMDW-1.1, the NVIDIA Open Model Agreement, the Tencent Hy license and the MiniMax community license were not reviewed.

Vendor cards dominate the evidence, so cross-model comparisons rest on different harnesses, and some figures conflict sharply (Gemma 4 31B ScreenSpot-Pro is 75.9 in Meta's table vs 5.9 in Thinking Machines'). ### Inferences
- The best "stretch" options for a 128 GB box are **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next at ~4 bits** (93.7–102 GB; strongest vision numbers among fitting builds, but custom license, new architecture, and little KV headroom) and **Qwen3.5-122B-A10B GPTQ-Int4** (78.9 GB, Apache, mature tooling).
- GLM-4.6V is better run at Q4 (~65–71 GB) than at its 110 GB FP8 if long context matters.
- Step-3.7-Flash (Apache, image input) fits only at ~3–3.5-bit GGUF (94–105 GB + 4 GB mmproj). Command A+ would need a community ~3-bit quant (none measured).
- DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp and MiMo-V2.6-Flash would need sub-4-bit community quants (roughly a further 25–35% cut) to fit with headroom. No such builds were found, and both are new architectures with limited local tooling, so they should be treated as out of reach for now.

### Gaps
- I did not search exhaustively for community (unsloth / bartowski / mlx-community) quants of DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp, MiMo-V2.6-Flash, Command A+, Ming-flash-omni-2.0 or Ling-3.0-flash-VL.
- Quality loss of the 3-bit / IQ4 GGUF builds versus official precision is unmeasured for these vision models; NVIDIA's own Super VL table shows agentic tasks can degrade under 4-bit (WebVoyager −11 points).

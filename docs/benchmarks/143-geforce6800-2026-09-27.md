# .143 (1GHZ) — GeForce 6800 benchmark, 2026-09-27

**Box:** AMD Athlon 1000 MHz (K7, MMX/3DNow!, no SSE), 512 MB, VIA chipset
(686A southbridge, AGP 4x), NVIDIA GeForce 6800 128 MB on ForceWare 71.89, Windows XP SP3.
32-bit colour, fullscreen, vsync off where the engine allows it. Modes at the
driver's default refresh (60 Hz: XP gives fullscreen games 60 Hz unless told
otherwise), except where noted.

| test | 640x480 | 800x600 | 1024x768 | 1280x960 | 1600x1200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quake III 1.32, `timedemo` demo `four` | 74.8 | 74.4 | 74.1 | 73.5 | 73.9 |
| RTCW MP, `timedemo` of `tdbench` (see below) | 87.5 | 86.6 | 87.1 | 86.5 | 87.5 |
| UT 4.36 UTbench, OpenGLDrv (**vsync-capped at 60 Hz**) | 50.7 | 51.4 | 50.1 | 50.3 | 49.6 |
| UT 4.36 UTbench, D3DDrv | 35.9 | 35.9 | 37.0 | 37.1 | 37.3 |

UT OpenGL at 1024x768 with `refreshkeep 100` holding 100 Hz: **58.6 avg**,
37.8 min, 72.4 max (the 60 Hz runs all max out at exactly 60.0).

## What it means

- **Every title is CPU-bound.** Frame rate does not move from 640x480 to
  1600x1200, so the 6800 has headroom at every resolution; the 1 GHz Athlon sets
  the ceiling. A faster card cannot raise these numbers; a faster CPU would.
  Running at 1600x1200 costs nothing here.
- **UT 4.36's OpenGL renderer syncs to the refresh rate and has no setting to
  stop it** (it uses `WGL_EXT_swap_control`; `OpenGLDrv.dll` exposes no
  interval). At XP's 60 Hz default that caps it; at 100 Hz it is 17% faster.
  It still beats D3DDrv by ~35-58% on this card.
- **Quake II behaves like UT OpenGL** (measured the day before): 60.0 fps at
  60 Hz, 100.0 fps at 100 Hz with `refreshkeep` - the engine-default vsync is
  the whole story there.

## Method notes

- Scripts: `scripts/benchmarks/timedemo_sweep.py` (Q3, RTCW) and
  `scripts/benchmarks/utbench_sweep.py` (UT). Raw JSON in
  `scripts/benchmarks/results/` (git-ignored).
- **RTCW has no standard demo here**, and the GOG build removed `timerefresh`.
  `tdbench.dm_60` is a scripted spectator fly-through of `mp_beach` recorded on
  .143 (in `C:\Games\ReturnToCastleWolfenstein\main\demos`); comparable across
  resolutions and boxes, not with published RTCW scores. RTCW logs to
  `Main\rtcwconsole.log`, not `qconsole.log`.
- **UE1 ignores console commands on its command line**: `UnrealTournament.exe
  UTbench.dem?timedemo=1` is parsed as a server address ("Pending connect ...
  failed"). The script types `timedemo 1 1` / `demoplay utbench` into the
  console; the result lands in `System\fps.txt`.
- The existing `benchmark_runner.py` was not usable as-is on this box: it
  detected the removed Voodoo5 as the card, has no GeForce 6 profile, no RTCW,
  pre-library install paths, and its Q3 template never sets `timedemo 1`.

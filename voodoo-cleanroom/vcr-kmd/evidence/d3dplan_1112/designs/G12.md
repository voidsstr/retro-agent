## 0. Verdict

**The gap is real, fixable on this hardware, and wider than one bug.** The HAL's whole multitexture model is
the Voodoo2-era legacy combine, as follows:
- one op, done in the FBI, with "other = texture" and "local = diffuse" hard-wired;
- a TMU multiply or add for stage 1, applied *before* stage 0's diffuse term;
- `combineMode` never written.

The VSA-100 has an extended combine unit in all three of its combine stages (TMU1, TMU0 and the FBI). It computes `((A'+B')·C'+D') << shift` with selectable inputs and inverts. 3dfx's own V5 HAL implemented 20 texture ops on it. We advertise 7 and draw most of the rest as MODULATE.

**The design: a single owner for the combine, `KMD/include/vcr_combine.h`.** It is a Win32-free compiler:
- **Input:** the two stages' texture-stage state and which textures are usable.
- **Output:** the combine bits of `fbzColorPath`, the combine bits of both TMUs' `textureMode`, and the three `combineMode` words (FBI, TMU0 and TMU1).
- **A verdict:** EXACT, APPROX_SAT or LEGACY.

How it works:
- Every D3D op becomes a recipe for the extended unit.
- A placement step puts each colour and alpha channel of each stage on a unit that can see its inputs.
- Whatever cannot be placed exactly is drawn exactly as today (LEGACY) and reported, so G15 can make `ValidateTextureStageState` refuse it.

It ships in three default-off bits of `Diag\D3DFeat`:

| bit | name | what it adds | needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | `VCR_D3DF_CMB_FBI` | The HAL owns `combineMode`. Every single-stage op and modifier runs in the FBI. Two-stage setups run when their TMU half uses only legacy TMU combines (REPLACE, PASS, MULT, ADD), including product chains with the 2x/4x shift moved to the FBI, a texture-less "tint" stage 1, a folded untextured stage 0 and a dead stage 0. | G01 fixed for trustworthy runs |
| 7 | `VCR_D3DF_CMB_TMU` | The extended combine inside the TMUs: inverts, `X-1/2`, outshift and the TFACTOR constant (TMU `chromaKey`/`chromaRange` with `combineMode[30]`). Stage 0 runs in TMU1 and stage 1 in TMU0, in D3D's order. | bit 6 |
| 8 | `VCR_D3DF_CMB_ITER` | Iterated diffuse colour and alpha inside the TMUs. This fixes D3D's stage order for every setup in which stage 0 uses DIFFUSE. | bits 6+7, plus G58's packed `sARGB` armed |

**With bit 6 alone, 25 of the 26 combinations UT2003 logged on `.124` are drawn exactly** (§1.2).
- Eighteen were already right, and keep their registers.
- The seven that use MODULATE2X now draw right. These are G05's two cases: the lightmapped world (stage 1) and the vertex-lit meshes (stage 0).
- The 26th, BLENDCURRENTALPHA after MODULATE(TEXTURE, DIFFUSE), needs bit 8.

**It is not safe to land default-on.** It adds register traffic to every Direct3D draw on a VSA-100 (three `combineMode` words and `c0`), and the extended datapath's arithmetic is partly inferred. Two parts are exempt:
- The *shadow verdict* log (§3.7.6) is pure logging and may be default-on.
- With the bits off, the register traffic stays byte-for-byte what was proven on silicon.

## 2. What the VSA-100 offers, and the fact that makes this safe

### 2.1 The datapath

**The datapath.** With `combineMode[31]` (`USE_COMBINE_MODE`) set, every combine unit computes the following
(`INC:385-530`; GL5 `_grCCExtfbzColorPath`/`_grCCExtcombineMode` `gglide.c:4815-5183`; `grTexColorCombineExt` `gtex.c:1499-1975`; `hw.md` Q2):

```
out = [invert_output] clamp( ((A' + B') * C' + D') << outshift )             (clamp after the shift: inferred)
A' = zero_other ? 0 : invert_other(A)    invert_other: x | 0-x | 1-x | x-1/2
B' = sub_clocal ? invert_local(B) : 0    invert_local: 0-x (=legacy) | x | 1-x | x-1/2
C' = reverse_blend ? m : 1-m             m = mselect (+ mselect_7)
D' = invert_add_local ? 1-D : D          D = add_clocal: B raw | add_alocal: local alpha | both: FBI texture RGB / TMU iterated RGB
```

**Which register holds which control** (SPEC "combineMode Register" and "fbzColorPath Register"):

- **`fbzColorPath` (FBI) and `textureMode` (each TMU).** They keep the arithmetic controls: `zero_other`, `sub_clocal`, `mselect`, `reverse_blend`, `add_clocal`/`add_alocal` and `invert_output`, for colour and alpha.
- **`combineMode`.** It adds `invert_other`, `invert_local`, `invert_add_local` and `outshift` to every unit. In the FBI, the other/local/`mselect_7`, a_other and a_local selects come from `combineMode` **only when bit 31 is set**. Otherwise they come from `fbzColorPath[1:0]`, `[4]`, `[3:2]` and `[6:5]`.
- **The TMUs.** In a TMU, the other/local/`mselect_7` selects come **from `combineMode` always** (SPEC: "For the Texture Combine and Texture Alpha Combine units ... always come from the combineMode register"). A stale TMU `combineMode` from Glide therefore changes what *today's* D3D TMU combines compute.
- **Bit 30, `DISABLE_CHROMA_SUBSTITUTION`.** It turns a TMU's `chromaKey`/`chromaRange` into constant-colour sources (`CK_*` local, `CR_*` other and `mselect_7`), with the constant alpha in `chromaRange[31:24]`. Without bit 30, `chromaRange[30:29] = 3` enables texture chroma substitution (SPEC "chromaRange Register"; "Programming tips": "When the new texture and color combine unit functionality ... combineMode[30] must be set").
- **Bit 29, 2 pixels per clock.** Leaving it needs 12 `nopCMD` to the TMUs only (chip field 6) *before* the write that clears it. This is a hardware bug (SPEC "combineMode Register", "Important Note").
- **Write each unit separately.** The FBI `combineMode` is chip field FBI (address bit 10, `0x400`); TMU0 is `0x800` and TMU1 is `0x1000` (`GL5/fxcmd.h:584-589`; `distate.c:1966`, `:2343-2357`). A broadcast write would set all three to one value.

**Two avoidances:**
- In the FBI, the `cc_mselect` codes 2/3 (`a_other`/`a_local`) and the D source `add_alocal` read the *alpha unit's* selects. The engine never uses them, so colour and alpha stay independent. 3dfx's tables use `MATMU` and `mselect_7` the same way (`VH5/D6MT.C:598-744`).
- TMU1 has no upstream TMU, so OTHER is never routed in TMU1.

### 2.3 The fact that makes bit 6 safe

**Legacy is the extended mode with every new field at zero.** The extended select codes are a superset of the legacy ones, and the zero values of the new fields are the legacy arithmetic:

- The FBI's `cc_otherselect` 0/1/2/3 are `fbzColorPath` RGBSEL 0/1/2/3 (iterated, TMU, C1, LFB).
- `cc_localselect` 0/1 are LOCALSELECT 0/1 (iterated, C0).
- `cca_otherselect` 0/1/2 are ASEL; `cca_localselect` 0-3 are ALOCALSELECT.
- `invert_other` 0 = x, `invert_local` 0 = 0-x (legacy subtract), outshift 0 = 1x.

So for every setup the legacy path already draws right, the engine emits:
- **the legacy `fbzColorPath` and `textureMode` unchanged**;
- **`combineMode` words that "mirror" them**: `0xC0000000` plus the FBI selects copied over, with bit 30 also disabling TMU chroma substitution.

The native test (§5.2) pins this rule exactly. The existing 40/40 d3dprobe matrix with bit 6 armed is the silicon check of it.

## 3. The design

### 3.1 One owner, one header

**New file: `KMD/include/vcr_combine.h`.** It is integer-only and Win32-free, in the style of `vcr_3dseq.h`, `vcr_rtfmt.h` and `vcr_texlod.h`. The driver compiles it, and `tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_combine.c` compiles the *same* header. It contains:

- **Its own copies of the D3D enum values it needs:** `VCR_TOP_*` 1..24, `VCR_TA_*` with SELECTMASK 0xf, COMPLEMENT 0x10 and ALPHAREPLICATE 0x20, and the TSS indices. A Python test pins them to `d3dtypes.h`, because the header must build without the DDK.
- **The input:**
  ```c
  typedef struct vcr_cmb_stage { unsigned cop, carg1, carg2, aop, aarg1, aarg2, resultarg; int tex_ok; } vcr_cmb_stage;
  typedef struct vcr_cmb_in   { vcr_cmb_stage s[2]; unsigned tiers; /* VCR_CMB_T_FBI|_TMU|_ITER */ } vcr_cmb_in;
  ```
- **The output:**
  ```c
  typedef struct vcr_cmb_out {
      unsigned verdict;            /* VCR_CMB_EXACT, _APPROX_SAT, _LEGACY */
      unsigned why;                /* the first refusal: op / arg / complement / needs-tier-7 / needs-tier-8 / >2 stages */
      unsigned plan_c, plan_a;     /* the placement chosen for the colour and alpha channels (logged) */
      unsigned fbz_cmb;            /* the fbzColorPath combine bits [25:0] and bit 27; the caller ORs in PARMADJUST */
      unsigned tm0_cmb, tm1_cmb;   /* textureMode bits [29:12] for TMU0 and TMU1 */
      unsigned cm_fbi, cm_tmu0, cm_tmu1;
      int tmu0_stage, tmu1_stage;  /* which stage's texture each TMU samples, -1 = none */
      unsigned needs;              /* TMU constants (TFACTOR into chroma), iterated colour in TMUs */
  } vcr_cmb_out;
  unsigned vcr_cmb_compile(const vcr_cmb_in *in, vcr_cmb_out *out);
  ```
- **Its internals:** the recipe table (§3.3), per-unit source tables (§2.2), the placement step (§3.5) and two encoders (§3.6). All loops are bounded and every table index is range-checked: an op or argument value outside 1..24 / 0..15 is a LEGACY verdict, never an array read.

**`KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h` gains:**
- the chip field `V3D_FBI 0x400u`, and `V3D_CHROMARANGE 0x138`;
- the `CM_*` field macros of `INC:385-530`, renamed like the existing `CP_*`/`TM_*`;
- the remaining `textureMode` TC/TCA bits: `ZERO_OTHER`, `SUB_CLOCAL`, the `MSELECT` field, `ADD_ALOCAL` and `INVERT_OUTPUT` for both channels.

The existing offset test in `test_vcr_kmd_d3d.py` re-computes `0x138` from the GPL `h3regs.h`.

### 3.2 Inputs and normalisation (the D3D conventions kept)

Each rule below is applied per stage and per channel, in this order. Each one is pinned by a native test.

1. **A DISABLEd colour op ends the cascade** (D3D). Stage 0 DISABLE gives untextured gouraud (diffuse), as today.
2. **SELECTARG2 becomes SELECTARG1 of arg2.** This is today's rewrite (`:686-689`, `:721-724`).
3. **ALPHAOP DISABLE while COLOROP is enabled** is "undefined" in D3D. It passes CURRENT alpha: diffuse alpha at stage 0, as today (`:725-726`).
4. **A stage that references TEXTURE but has no usable texture** keeps today's convention: that channel passes CURRENT. This is how `color_path` treats `!tex`, and how today's stage-1 drop looks.
   - A texture is "usable" when its `view_of()` succeeds **and** its `TEXCOORDINDEX` names a set the FVF carries.
   - That second condition moves `prepare()`'s blunt "drop all texturing" (`:1342-1355`) into the compiler, so one missing set loses one stage, not both.
   - What D3D itself returns for an unbound texture is open question 3.
5. **`RESULTARG` other than CURRENT (DX8 TSS 28, if the runtime ever forwards it), SPECULAR, TEMP, PREMODULATE, BUMPENVMAP(LUMINANCE), DOTPRODUCT3, MULTIPLYADD (25) and LERP (26) give LEGACY,** each with its own `why`. G84 lists the hardware limits.
6. **Dead stage.** If stage 1's colour does not read CURRENT (no CURRENT argument, no BLENDCURRENTALPHA, no CURRENT|ALPHAREPLICATE), stage 0's *colour* is dead. The same holds for alpha. A dead channel is not computed.
7. **Fold.** Suppose stage 0 is untextured, or is treated as untextured by rule 4, and its channel's result is a single source: SELECTARG of DIFFUSE or TFACTOR, possibly complemented or alpha-replicated. Then stage 1's CURRENT for that channel is replaced by that source, and the setup becomes one stage.
   - This is how "stage 0 = SELECTARG1(DIFFUSE), stage 1 = MODULATE(TEXTURE, CURRENT)" renders. Today it draws diffuse only.
8. **More than 2 stages.** These cannot occur, because the walker drops TSS for stage 2 and above (`:1596-1599`). The compiler still answers `>2 stages` if one ever does, for G15.

### 3.3 Op recipes (the intermediate form)

X = arg1, Y = arg2. `aX` is X with ALPHAREPLICATE. `a_d`, `a_t`, `a_f` and `a_c` are the diffuse, this stage's texture, TFACTOR and CURRENT alphas. A recipe fills the slots A (with an `a_mode`), B (with a `b_mode`, gated by `sub_clocal`), C (with a `c_invert`), D (raw local, with a `d_invert`) and a shift. The formulas are D3D's. The shapes match 3dfx's tables (`VH5/D6MT.C:598-744` FBI, `:853-1039` TMU) and Glide's ext-combine argument model.

| D3DTOP | D3D value | A (a_mode) | B (b_mode) | C | D | shift | complement allowed on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SELECTARG1 | X | X (x) | - | 1 (`m=0`, not reversed) | - | 0 | X |
| MODULATE / 2X / 4X | X·Y·{1,2,4} | X (x) | - | Y | - | 0/1/2 | X (1-x on A), Y (`c_invert`) |
| ADD | X+Y | X (x) | Y (x) | 1 | - | 0 | X, Y (1-x on either) |
| ADDSIGNED / 2X | (X+Y-1/2)·{1,2} | X (x) | Y (x-1/2) | 1 | - | 0/1 | one of X, Y. A complemented Y is moved to A (1-x) and X to B (x-1/2); both complemented is refused |
| SUBTRACT | X-Y | X (x) | Y (0-x) | 1 | - | 0 | X only (`-(1-Y)` has no invert mode; SWARCH:753) |
| ADDSMOOTH | X+Y(1-X) | 0 (`zero_other`) | X (1-x) | Y | X | 0 | X (B becomes x, D inverted), Y (`c_invert`) |
| BLENDDIFFUSE/TEXTURE/FACTOR/CURRENTALPHA | X·a+Y(1-a) | X (x) | Y (0-x) | `a_d`/`a_t`/`a_f`/`a_c` | Y | 0 | X only (Y sits in both B and D) |
| BLENDTEXTUREALPHAPM | X+Y(1-a_t) | `a_t` (1-x) | - | Y | X | 0 | X (D inverted), Y (`c_invert`) |
| MODULATEALPHA_ADDCOLOR (colour only) | X+aX·Y | Y (x) | - | aX | X | 0 | X (C and D inverted), Y |
| MODULATECOLOR_ADDALPHA | X·Y+aX | Y (x) | - | X | aX | 0 | X, Y |
| MODULATEINVALPHA_ADDCOLOR | X+(1-aX)·Y | Y (x) | - | aX inverted | X | 0 | X, Y |
| MODULATEINVCOLOR_ADDALPHA | (1-X)·Y+aX | Y (x) | - | X inverted | aX | 0 | X, Y |
| PREMODULATE, BUMPENVMAP(L), DOTPRODUCT3, MULTIPLYADD, LERP | - | LEGACY | | | | | |

Alpha ops use the same rows, except the four colour-only MODULATE*_ADD* ops and DOTPRODUCT3. ALPHAREPLICATE is a no-op on an alpha argument.

**Complement rules** (in the slot, not per op):
- A: `x` becomes `1-x`.
- B: `x` and `1-x` swap; `0-x` and `x-1/2` cannot be complemented.
- C: toggles `c_invert`.
- D: toggles `d_invert`.

An argument that is placed in B *and* D (the BLEND*ALPHA family) takes both rules at once, so it can be complemented only where both allow it. These rules reproduce the vendor's `ARG1_COMP`/`ARG2_COMP` flags (`VH5/D6MT.C:504-512, 853-900`), and refusing the rest is what lets G15 answer honestly. The modifiers are G13. The engine *is* G13's implementation, because a modifier is part of routing an argument.

### 3.4 Routing

A recipe slot names a D3D source. Routing replaces it with that unit's select code from §2.2, with these limits:
- A source that is unavailable at the current tier (DIFFUSE inside a TMU below bit 8; TFACTOR inside a TMU below bit 7) fails the placement.
- D may only be the raw local select, so a recipe with D chooses B's source to be D's. Where the recipe leaves B empty (`b_mode` ZERO), the local select still names D's source.
- A conflict fails the placement. Example: a TMU recipe needing two different local sources.

### 3.5 Placement: where each channel of each stage runs

A **texture assignment** is chosen once:
- no texture;
- one texture, which is always on TMU0, whichever stage it belongs to;
- two textures: TMU1 gets stage 0's texture and TMU0 gets stage 1's, as today and as in 3dfx's `VH5/D6MT.C:4943-4947`.

Then **each channel** (colour, alpha) picks the first placement that routes. The TMU colour (TC) and alpha (TCA) units are separate, and so are the FBI CCU and ACU, so the two channels are independent. They share only the sampled textures and the constants, which are the same TFACTOR everywhere.

| order | placement | units | tier | exact when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **F**: FBI stage | TMU0 `REPLACE` (legacy bits) forwards its texel; the FBI runs the one live stage | 6 | always (every recipe routes in the FBI, §2.2) |
| 2 | **PROD**: product chain | TMU1 `REPLACE`, TMU0 `MULT` (both legacy), FBI `T × M << s`, where M is at most one of DIFFUSE/TFACTOR (or their alphas) and s is the sum of the stages' shifts | 6 | every op in the chain is MODULATE / 2X / 4X / SELECTARG of a plain source, and only the **last** stage carries a shift. A shift in stage 0 gives **APPROX_SAT**: wrong only where `2·t0·d` exceeds 1. Today the shift is lost everywhere |
| 3 | **SPLIT**: one texture reaches the result | TMU0 `REPLACE` its texel or `PASS` TMU1's (legacy), per channel; the FBI runs the last live stage | 6 | the channel depends on one of the two textures |
| 4 | **TINT**: stage 1 untextured | TMU0 runs stage 0 with legacy bits (`REPLACE`/`MULT`/`ADD`); the FBI runs stage 1 with CURRENT = TRGB/TA | 6 | stage 0 is TMU-expressible at the enabled tier |
| 5 | **T0F**: as TINT with the extended TMU0 | TMU0 runs stage 0 with inverts, shift and CR; the FBI runs stage 1 | 7 | |
| 6 | **T1T0**: D3D's order in the TMUs | TMU1 runs stage 0 (or no texture: constants or iterated only), TMU0 runs stage 1 with CURRENT = OTHER, the FBI passes `T`/`T.a` | 7 | stage 0 without DIFFUSE; with bit 8, any |
| - | otherwise | LEGACY: the old `color_path()`/`stage1_combine()` registers, unchanged, plus mirror `combineMode` words | - | - |

**Verdicts.** The setup's verdict is the worst of its two channels. An APPROX_SAT setup is drawn but reported; G15 decides whether Validate accepts it (open question 7).

**The order prefers the mechanisms proven first.** F and PROD come before TMU-extended placements. A setup bit 6 already proved keeps bit 6's registers when bit 7 is armed.

**Stage order.** After the fix, a setup is drawn in D3D's order whenever its verdict is EXACT:
- T1T0, T0F, TINT and SPLIT keep the order.
- PROD is order-free, because the product commutes and the shift is applied once, last.

Only LEGACY setups keep today's swapped order, and G15 refuses those.

### 3.6 Encoding, and the canonical register words

**Two encoders turn a routed recipe into words.**
- **FBI:** `fbzColorPath` CC/CCA bits plus `cm_fbi`.
- **TMU:** `textureMode` TC/TCA bits plus `cm_tmu`.

The encoders follow Glide's mapping (`gglide.c:4815-5183`, `gtex.c:1499-2100`):
- `a_mode` ZERO sets `zero_other`;
- any `b_mode` other than ZERO sets `sub_clocal`, with `invert_local` X=1, 1-X=2, X-1/2=3 and 0-X=0;
- `c_invert` clears `reverse_blend`;
- D: B sets `add_clocal`.

**Mirror and the constant bits.** Wherever a legacy code exists, the `fbzColorPath` select bits are also set to mirror the `combineMode` selects, so a write that somehow missed bit 31 degrades to the legacy picture. All three `combineMode` words always carry `USE_COMBINE_MODE | DISABLE_CHROMA_SUBSTITUTION` (`0xC0000000`), as 3dfx's HAL did in every unit (`VH5/D6MT.C:4412-4414, 4891-4893`). `combineMode[29]` (2 pixels per clock) is never set.

Worked words follow. They are pinned in the native test and computed from `INC` and `vcr_3dregs.h`. `tm` means the TMU's combine bits only; the format, filter and clamp bits come from `view_of()`.

### 3.7 Driver integration

#### 3.7.1 `vcrdd_d3d.c compute_regs()`

The function becomes `if (!pd->cmb_fbi) { the current body, untouched } else compute_regs_cmb(c, nsets)`. The new function:
- **Builds the input.** It builds `vcr_cmb_in` from `c->tss`, `c->tss1` and the two `view_of()` results. Texture usability includes the coordinate-set check: `nsets` comes from `prepare()`'s walk.
- **Fills the combine registers.** It calls `vcr_cmb_compile`, then sets `r->fbzColorPath = fbz_cmb | CP_PARMADJUST` (`CP_TEXTURE` when a TMU feeds the FBI), the TMU `textureMode`s as view bits OR'd with the `tm*_cmb` bits, and `r->cm_on = 1` with the three words.
- **Loads the constants.** `r->c0 = r->c1 = TFACTOR`: `c0` is the FBI's local constant (`C0_RGB`/`C0_A`), and 3dfx loads both (`VH5/D6DP2.C:7025-7026`). `r->tmuc = TFACTOR` is set only when `needs` has the TMU constant.
- **Records the TMU assignment.** It stores `c->tmu0_stage`/`c->tmu1_stage`, with `tex_w`/`tex_h` and `tex1_w`/`tex1_h` for whichever stage's texture each TMU holds, and the surfaces in `tex_surf0`/`tex_surf1` for `flush_written()`.
- **Falls back the old way.** On LEGACY it calls the unchanged `color_path()`/`stage1_combine()` and writes the mirror words, so it uses a single source of the legacy mapping.

#### 3.7.2 `prepare()`

TMU0 reads the set of `tss[tmu0_stage].TEXCOORDINDEX`; TMU1 reads stage 0's set. Today TMU0 reads stage 1's set exactly when TMU1 is in use (`:1339-1341`), which is wrong for FOLD and SPLIT. Without bit 6 the old mapping stays.

#### 3.7.3 `vcrdd_3d.c VcrDd3dState()`

When `r->cm_on` is set, it writes `combineMode` before every other 3D register, as 3dfx does (`VH5/D6DP2.C:6443-6452`):

1. If `!pd->cm_primed`: room for 12, then 12 × `w3(V3D_TMU0+V3D_TMU1+V3D_NOPCMD, 0)`, the SPEC's 2 pixel-per-clock exit; then `pd->cm_primed = 1`.
2. `w3(V3D_FBI + V3D_COMBINEMODE, cm_fbi)`, `w3(V3D_TMU0 + V3D_COMBINEMODE, cm_tmu0)`, `w3(V3D_TMU1 + V3D_COMBINEMODE, cm_tmu1)`. All three are written every flush; shadowing them is G53's job.
3. If `r->tmuc_on` and the value differs from `pd->tmuc` or `!pd->tmuc_valid`: `w3(V3D_TMU0+V3D_TMU1+V3D_CHROMAKEY, tf)` and `w3(... V3D_CHROMARANGE, tf)`, which is chip field 6, as `VH5/D6DP2.C:7019-7022`. This happens only *after* the `combineMode` words that carry bit 30, never before them.

FIFO room is accounted per group, as the existing code does. Without `cm_on` nothing here runs, and the write sequence is today's.

#### 3.7.4 `VcrDd3dForget(pd)`

This one hook clears `cm_primed`, `tmuc_valid` and, for G41, `fog_valid`. It is called:
- where a Glide client is granted exclusive ownership (`vcrdd_escape.c:128`);
- at its release (`:199`);
- in `DrvAssertMode(TRUE)` (`vcrdd.c:589`).

A new PDEV starts zeroed. After any Glide or OpenGL session, the first D3D flush therefore leaves 2 pixels per clock with the 12 NOPs, then writes all three `combineMode` words and the constants.

#### 3.7.5 Plumbing

- **The miniport** reads `x->d3dfeat = VcrDiagGet(L"D3DFeat", 0)` at boot, like `D3DBigTex`: the caps a title reads at its desktop must equal its fullscreen PDEV's. It appends `vcr_u32 d3dfeat` to `vcr_info`. An older miniport sends the shorter struct, which reads as 0.
- **The display driver** (`vcrdd_2d.c`, beside `tex_ext`) sets:
  - `pd->cmb_fbi = napalm && bit 6`;
  - `pd->cmb_tmu = cmb_fbi && bit 7`;
  - `pd->cmb_iter = cmb_tmu && bit 8 && pd->packed_argb`, where `packed_argb` is G58's.
  - A bit that is set but ineffective is logged: 513/25 "bit 8 ignored: no packed colour".
- **Banshee/Voodoo3** (the 86Box bed) ignore all three bits, like `D3DBigTex`.
- **Bit allocation.** `hal.md` §10 reserves `D3DFeat` bits 0-5 for Phase 2 and 6+ for Phase 3. If another item lands the `D3DFeat` plumbing first, this one reuses it.

#### 3.7.6 Logging: shadow verdicts, default-on and log-only

`log_combo()` already fires once per new combination, at most 64 per context (`:876-919`). It gains one event, 513/24 (the next free sub-code; 1-23 are taken), carrying:
- stage 1's arguments, which today's events omit;
- the compiler's verdict at each tier, computed into a **local** `vcr_cmb_out` even when every bit is off.

Example: `combine 21 legacy@6 legacy@7 exact@8 s1 16 2 1 a 3 2 1`. One LogLevel-2 run of a title then shows which combinations it uses and which bit fixes each one, before anything is armed. The registers are untouched, and a test pins that the shadow call cannot reach `c->regs`.

### 3.8 The old path, the fallback, and single pass

- **Bits off.** `compute_regs`, `VcrDd3dState`, the caps and `prepare` are today's code. The register traffic is byte-for-byte the proven sequence; the only addition is the 513/24 log events.
- **Bit 6 on, LEGACY verdict.** The draw uses today's `fbzColorPath`/`textureMode` and three mirror `combineMode` words (same arithmetic; `combineMode` now defined instead of Glide's leftover). It never draws worse than today.
- **Single pass only.** A DX7 driver has no hook to add passes. Splitting a draw would also fight the title's own framebuffer blend. What cannot be done in one pass is LEGACY plus an honest refusal (G15), and UE2 and Halo then take their own multipass paths (UE2's "No stages left for lightmap/detail", `games.md` §4.1).

### 3.9 Caps

`dwTextureOpCaps` stays `0x184f` unless `cmb_fbi` is set. With it set, the caps are `0x184f | VCR_CMB_TEXOPCAPS_PROVEN`, a constant in `vcr_combine.h` with one evidence comment per bit.

The constant grows only after an op's single-stage *and* stage-1 probe cells have passed on `.124` at 16 and 32 bpp. The groups, in order:
1. MODULATE2X/4X (`0x30`), the measured UE2 need;
2. ADDSIGNED/2X, SUBTRACT, ADDSMOOTH (`0x780`);
3. BLENDFACTORALPHA, BLENDTEXTUREALPHAPM, BLENDCURRENTALPHA (`0xE000`);
4. the four MODULATE*_ADD* ops (`0x1E0000`).

The end state is 3dfx's `0x001effff`. Widening comes **after G15**, because an advertised op invites combinations that are LEGACY below bit 8 and need an honest Validate. The runtime forwards unlisted ops anyway (`runtime.md` §8.1), so the probe can test every op before it is advertised. `wMaxTextureBlendStages` stays 2; a texture-less third FBI stage is open question 6.

## 4. Gating

- **Default off.** All three bits are absent = 0 and positive, so an older miniport never arms them. They are VSA-100 only and read at boot.
- **One bit per clean boot, in order: 6, then 7, then 8.** Bit 8 also needs G58 proven and armed first.
- **Each step is bisectable.** If any boot's checks fail, that bit is disarmed and the previous bit's state is the proven one.
- **Not default-on at landing.** The bits add new register traffic and inferred datapath semantics to every D3D draw.
- **Candidates for default-on later.** After bits 6 and 7 pass all of §6, they can become default-on in a follow-up commit, as `Accel2DPattern` did. Bit 8 follows G58.
- **Log-only part.** The shadow-verdict event (§3.7.6) may land default-on, because it writes no register.

### 5.1 d3dprobe (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`, D3D8)

**A table-driven cell framework.**
- **Layout.** Each test draws up to 64 cells: 24×24 quads in a grid of 32×32 cells inside the 256×256 readback window.
- **Textures.** 8×8 solid-colour **A4R4G4B4** textures in the managed pool. Every value is a multiple of 0x11, so it is exact on the default texture path; DEFAULT-pool textures cannot be locked (`onbox/session_log.md`).
- **State.** Point sampling, dither off, fog off. Each cell sets its own TSS, `TEXTUREFACTOR` and vertex colours.
- **Alpha.** Alpha cells are read through the blender: colour SELECTARG1(TFACTOR) with TFACTOR RGB white, `SRCBLEND=SRCALPHA`, `DESTBLEND=ZERO` over black, so the grey level is the alpha.
- **Reference.** A new Win32-free header, `KMD/include/vcr_texop_ref.h`, computes D3D's formulas in 8-bit integers, clamping after each stage, for one or two stages. **The native test uses the same header.**
- **Pass criterion.** Each cell's centre pixel must be within **14** per channel of the reference at 16 bpp (as `tex2mod`), or **6** at 32 bpp.
- **Diagnostics.** Each cell also prints `legacy`: the reference applied to what today's HAL draws (op mapped to MODULATE, stages swapped, stage 1 dropped, TFACTOR read as diffuse). A failure then reads "got = legacy" (engine not engaged or fell back) or "got = neither" (engine wrong).
- **Self-check.** At start-up the probe asserts `|want - legacy| >= 3 × tol` in some channel of every discriminating cell, so a cell that cannot discriminate is a probe bug, not a pass.
- **Negative control.** A new flag, `--expect legacy`, inverts the criterion: every discriminating cell must read its legacy value. `d3dprobe_run.py` passes the flag through.

### 5.2 Native regression test: `tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_combine.c` (new)

It compiles `vcr_combine.h` and `vcr_texop_ref.h`, plus a test-only **register-level model** of the three combine units, `tests/native/vcr_cmb_model.h`.

**The model** *decodes register words*: `fbzColorPath` + `cm_fbi`, and `textureMode` + `cm_tmu`, in legacy (`[31]=0`) and extended (`[31]=1`) form, using §2.1's datapath over 8-bit channels. A wrong shift position or select code in an encoder therefore fails. The model's inferred points are written down once, as the silicon cells settle them:
- clamp after the shift;
- `X-1/2` = 128;
- negatives clamp to 0;
- TMU1's OTHER input is 0.

**What it asserts:**

1. **Fixed AND old-buggy value**, for each defect, on the same inputs. "Legacy" is a transcription of today's `color_path`/`stage1_combine`, as `test_vcr_kmd_d3dseq.c`'s `old_target()` does.

   | case | old (legacy) | fixed (engine) |
   |---|---|---|
   | MODULATE2X | `t·d` | `min(1, 2·t·d)` |
   | UT2003 world: SEL1 + MODULATE2X | `t0·t1` | `2·t0·t1` |
   | stage-1 SELECTARG1(TEXTURE) | `t1·d` | `t1` |
   | BLENDTEXTUREALPHA(DIFFUSE, TEXTURE) | swapped | right |
   | MODULATE(TEXTURE, TFACTOR) | `t·d` | `t·f` |
   | fold | `d` | `t1·d` |
   | tint | `t0` | `t0·f` |
   | alpha SUBTRACT | `a1·a2` | `max(0, a1-a2)` |
   | (tier 8) MODULATE then ADD | `(t0+t1)·d` | `t0·d+t1` |

2. **Exhaustive single stage.** Every op × every argument pair over {DIFFUSE, TEXTURE, TFACTOR, CURRENT} × modifiers × 7 colour vectors. For an EXACT verdict, `model(regs) == ref` within ±2. Otherwise the registers equal the legacy registers, `why` is set, and the tier-specific EXACT counts are pinned, so a coverage regression is visible.
3. **Two stages.** Every op pair for the argument shapes in §5.1 and UT03, at each tier set {6}, {6,7}, {6,7,8}. UT03's 26 combinations are golden rows: their logged legacy words plus the engine words of §3.6. Combine 21 is LEGACY at {6} and {6,7}, and EXACT at {6,7,8}.
4. **Mirror.** For every setup today's HAL draws right, `fbz`/`tm` are bit-identical to legacy, `cm_tmu* == 0xC0000000`, `cm_fbi == 0xC0000000 | mirror(fbz)`, and the model output is bit-exact equal.
5. **Robustness.** 100k random inputs, including ops 0, 25-255 and `0xFFFFFFFF`, arguments ≥ 16, and `resultarg`: always a verdict, never an out-of-bounds read. Build it with `-fsanitize=address,undefined` when the host compiler has them.
6. **Tier 0.** With `tiers = 0`, the verdict is LEGACY, `cm` is "off" and the legacy registers are returned.

`tests/run_all.sh` globs `tests/native/test_*.c`, so no registration is needed.

   - **Negative control.** `--tests combine6,combine7,combine8 --expect legacy` must PASS, which proves every cell discriminates on silicon.
   - **Shadow verdicts.** At LogLevel 2, run the UT2003 flyby (staged), the UT2004 botmatch, the Halo timedemo and Red Faction's menu-to-first-level, and keep their 513/21-24 lines. That table becomes the measured per-title exposure (`hal.md` §11).
2. **Boot B: bit 6.**
   - **The safety gate:** the default matrix must be **unchanged**, cell for cell, at 16 and 32 bpp, fullscreen and windowed. The registers differ only by the mirror words, so any change stops the rollout.
   - Then `combine6` passes; `combine7`/`combine8` still read legacy.
   - **UT2003 flyby** at the mode it picks (640x480x16 today, `onbox/games/UT2003_flyby/UT2003.log`). Take frames at 30/60/90/120 s and compare them with boot A: lit and lightmapped surfaces should be about twice as bright and no longer "very dark". The same flyby frames on an NVIDIA fleet box are the reference for hue and brightness. Frame rate (`avgfps`) must stay within noise of boot A.
   - **UT2004 botmatch** frames at 65/90 s: the same comparison, against today's luma of 28.
   - **Glide/OpenGL coexistence.** Run Quake II on our ICD and h5 Glide (4-chip SLI timedemo) plus the OpenGL golden gate: unchanged. Then a d3dprobe `combine6` run after that clean Glide close must pass. This exercises `VcrDd3dForget` and the 12-NOP prologue after a real Glide session.
3. **Boot C: bits 6 and 7.** `combine7` passes, and the boot-B checks repeat. Then a TFACTOR coexistence step (§7): a d3dprobe run with TFACTOR alpha `0xFF`, then Quake II with two-texture Glide, must render unchanged.
4. **Boot D: bits 6, 7 and 8, after G58 is proven and armed.**
   - `combine8` passes.
   - UT2003/UT2004 terrain: combine 21's verdict line reads exact.
   - Halo timedemo frames at 60/90 s.
   - Max Payne's menu and first scene, attended, with the flight recorder.

**The proof is** (a) all cells of the tier passing at 640x480x16 and 1024x768x32, and (b) the UT2003 flyby world no longer half-bright at bit 6, against the NVIDIA reference frame.

## 7. Risks to proven paths

| path | risk | mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| default 16 bpp D3D path | none with the bits off: byte-for-byte unchanged (Python and native pins); only log events added | the bits are positive and default off |
| bits armed, setups that were already right | the inferred meaning of a zero field under `[31]=1` | the mirror rule makes these registers legacy plus zero fields; boot B's unchanged-matrix gate catches any difference; disarm |
| Glide/OpenGL after D3D | D3D leaves `combineMode[31:30]` set and, with bit 7, TFACTOR in the TMU chroma registers | Glide's open sends the 12 NOPs and rewrites all three `combineMode` words (`GL5/gsst.c:916-930`, `gtex.c:1043-1078`), and its 2 pixel-per-clock exit invalidates both TMUs' chroma, which its validator rewrites before the first triangle (`gtex.c:1019-1022`, `distate.c:1655-1666`). The TMU constants are loaded only when a bit-7 placement needs them. Boot C tests TFACTOR alpha `0xFF`, whose `[30:29]=3` would enable substitution in a TMU without bit 30. AmigaMerlin's Glide is assumed to do the same (inferred) |
| D3D after Glide | stale 2 pixels per clock, TMU selects, chroma substitution | improved over today: the 12-NOP prologue plus three explicit words after `VcrDd3dForget`. A *killed* client's command FIFO is G01, unchanged here |
| SLI state | none: writes go to chip 0's 3D block only; no `chipMask`/`sliCtrl` writes | if D3D SLI (G51) lands, these writes ride its broadcast like every other 3D register |
| flip path | none: no video or swap register is touched | the d3dprobe `present` and fullscreen flips stay in the matrix |
| CPU cost | one compile per state change (bounded table work, about 1 µs, inferred); 3 more MMIO writes per state flush; 12 NOPs once per Glide/assert epoch | measure with `d3dprobe perf --novsync` and the UT2003 `avgfps` at boot B; add a 16-entry per-context compile cache if the cost exceeds 1 % |
| title behaviour | widened caps steer titles onto new paths | caps grow only after G15 and per proven op group (§3.9) |
| kernel robustness | new code in a display driver | pure integer, bounded loops, range-checked tables, fuzzed in the native test under sanitizers |

## 8. Effort, dependencies, titles, priority

**Effort: L.** The work is:
- `vcr_combine.h`, about 800 lines (recipes, routing tables, placement, two encoders);
- `vcr_texop_ref.h`, about 150;
- the native model and test, about 900;
- driver integration, about 250 lines across `vcrdd_d3d.c`, `vcrdd_3d.c`/`.h`, `vcrdd.h`, `vcrdd_2d.c`, `vcrdd_escape.c` and `vcrdd.c`, plus the miniport and ioctl;
- the d3dprobe cell framework and about 30 cells, about 500;
- Python tests and documentation;
- four supervised boots.

Bit 8's slice is small, once G58 exists.

**Dependencies:**
- **G01:** a killed Glide client's command FIFO voids every on-box D3D result.
- **G58** for bit 8: the TMUs see diffuse only through packed `sARGB`, because `sRed..sAlpha` are FBI-only (SPEC register table: `sARGB` "FBI+TREX*", `sRed` "FBI").
- **G03** is soft: G12 takes over `combineMode` and the 2 pixel-per-clock exit, while clip1, chroma, stipple and `aaCtrl` stay with G03; until then, test from a clean boot.

Related items:
- **Subsumed:** G05 is this engine's bit 6, so implement it here rather than as a separate patch on the legacy path, so that `combineMode` has one owner. G13's modifiers live in the routing (§3.3). G14's FBI half is bit 6; its TMU half is bit 7's constant load.
- **Consumers:** G15 maps `verdict`/`why` to `D3DERR_UNSUPPORTEDCOLOROPERATION`/`ALPHAOPERATION`/`COLORARG`/`ALPHAARG`/`TOOMANYOPERATIONS`. G50 can set 2 pixels per clock only for placements using one TMU, and the engine reports that. G26 needs FBI-only placements so both TMUs stay free for single-texture trilinear. G41 shares `VcrDd3dForget`.

**Titles:**
- **Measured:** Unreal Tournament 2003 (combines 1/3/5/6/8/10/12 at bit 6, 21 at bit 8), and Unreal Tournament 2004 (the same D3DDrv, inferred).
- **Inferred** (the shadow verdicts of boot A turn these into measurements): Halo, Max Payne, Red Faction, Hidden and Dangerous Deluxe, Battlefield 1942 (LAN only; its single player is G72), and the DX6 Dark-engine and LithTech titles where they multitexture.

**Priority: P1.** It is a visible defect in staged titles, and the prerequisite for G15 and G50. Its bit-6 slice *is* G05's fix (the half-bright UE2 world in every scene, P0), so schedule bit 6 together with G05.

## 9. Open questions

1. **The extended datapath's arithmetic is partly inferred:** clamp after the shift, the exact `X-1/2` constant, negative intermediates, and TMU1's OTHER input. Each has a d3dprobe cell (saturating `mod2x`, `addsigned` around 0.5, reversed `subtract`). Where silicon disagrees, the model in `vcr_cmb_model.h` is corrected and the evidence recorded.
2. **Do the TMUs see iterated colour only through packed `sARGB`?** The spec table says so (inferred from the chip column). Bit 8's cells settle it after G58.
3. **What does D3D return for TEXTURE on a stage with no texture bound?** The HAL keeps today's "pass CURRENT". Calibrate by running the same d3dprobe cells on an NVIDIA fleet box, where the D3D8 HAL acts as a reference. If the answer differs, change the convention behind its own test.
4. **Does the XP D3D8/9 runtime forward DX8 TSS 26-28 (`COLORARG0`, `ALPHAARG0`, `RESULTARG`) to a DX7 DDI?** The compiler refuses `RESULTARG != CURRENT`; the shadow log records any arrival.
5. **Which combinations do Halo, Max Payne, Red Faction, H&D and BF1942 actually send?** Boot A's shadow verdicts answer this. Halo's soft sprites drawn as squares (G17), UE2's white sky and polygons (G18) and its blue grass (G44) may involve combinations; the log will say whether they do.
6. **Offer a texture-less third stage in the FBI** (`wMaxTextureBlendStages = 3`; placement T1T0 plus an FBI stage)? The hardware has it (inferred), but it changes UE1/UE2's paths. This would be a separate bit, later.
7. **Should Validate (G15) accept APPROX_SAT** (a product chain with a shift in stage 0, wrong only where an intermediate saturates)?
8. **`combineMode[30]` in the FBI:** 3dfx set it in all units. It should not affect the FBI's own chroma key (G09), which uses the FBI `chromaKey` only (inferred). G09's cells will show it.
9. **DX8 LERP and MULTIPLYADD** are not offered: the DX7 caps word cannot be relied on to carry them through the D3D8 runtime, and they fit only some argument sets (inferred). They stay LEGACY.

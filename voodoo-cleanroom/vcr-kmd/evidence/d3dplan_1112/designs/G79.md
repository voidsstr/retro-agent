# G79 design: frames that can be trusted under Direct3D exclusive mode, and a sweep verdict that reads them

- **Lane:** clean-room `vcr-kmd`, the Voodoo 5 6000 in `.124`. **Tooling only.** Phases A-C change no
  `vcrdd.dll`, `vcrmp.sys` or `vcrprobe.sys` byte. Phase D, a gather IOCTL in `vcrprobe.sys`, is conditional and
  needs no reboot.
- **Kind:** fixable. **Priority P1.** It is the measurement every in-game visual item rests on (G06, G17, G18, G44,
  G45, G67-G71, G73), and the 11-12 in-game frames themselves fall under its caveat (§1.2). **Effort L.**
- **Depends on:**
  - **G04.** Unattended probe reads under a D3D title wait for its E0/E1 result. Its `Diag\Live\D3DBeat` is the
    escape guard's signal.
  - **G01.** The silicon validation needs a clean engine: a stale Glide FIFO turns every D3D frame into clears.
- **Works with:**
  - hosted here: G06 (confetti metric), G44 (grass classifiers);
  - consumers: G73 (census), G39 (the stale-desktop detector checks its fix);
  - G74: if its d3dprobe framework lands first, `presentmark` registers there;
  - G78: the flip watch gives a vsync-on presented rate, and no capture may run inside a timed window;
  - G59: the flip rate measured at the scan-out.
- **Written:** 2026-11-12. Sources:
  - the reports `history.md` §3.7, §4.2, §8 (history-45) and `visual.md` V-10, V-11, V-13, §2 and §6;
  - `onbox.md` §4 and §7;
  - the code, the committed sweep evidence, and the sibling designs G04, G06, G44, G73, G74 and G78.

  Nothing was built, deployed or run on a box. The numbers in §1.3-§1.6 were computed for this design by scratch
  code over committed PNG and JSON files. That code is not committed; §2.2 gives the definitions to port.

## 0. Verdict

1. **The gap is real and fixable, and all of it is tooling.** Three separate defects share one symptom: a picture
   nobody can trust.
   - **The capture paths.** The default `fbshot` parks behind win32k's display lock. `--probe` is safe but slow
     under load. The agent's GDI `SCREENSHOT` may photograph the back buffer.
   - **No verdict reads a frame.** `lan_sweep.py`'s verdict never looks at the picture. `lan_check.py`'s only frame
     rule is `stddev < 4`.
   - **No sweep requires gameplay.** A menu passes exactly as gameplay would.
2. **Records that already exist show the back-buffer mechanism**, which the gap called "inferred". Only
   DirectDraw's surface swap at Flip remains general knowledge, and `presentmark` (§4) demonstrates it on silicon.
   - The GDI surface is the primary at `vcr_desktop_offset()`.
   - A D3D chain's second buffer comes from the DirectDraw heap:
     - 11-12: `primary at 3d00000`, then `ContextCreate 1: target 300000` (`ONBOX/ring_after_probes_decoded.txt`,
       seq 29401 and 29433).
     - 10-04: Max Payne's scan-out start read `004b0000`, while its GDI surface at 1280x960x32 sits at `03b50000`
       (`EV/gametune_1001/fbshot_probe/fbab_maxpayne.json`). At that moment a GDI capture read the other buffer.
3. **It delivers four things** (§2):
   - **(A) Capture** (`vcrctl`, user mode, escape-free):
     - `scanout [--watch]`: which buffer is on screen, whether GDI's surface is that buffer, and flips per second.
       Register reads only.
     - `fbshot --probe --lines N --burst K --gap MS --prio critical`: front-buffer thumbnails a second apart, with
       CPU-versus-wall telemetry that says whether a slow read was starved or bus-bound.
   - **(B) `KMD/tools/frame_verdict.py`**, the module G44 and G73 expect. It sorts every frame into `content`,
     `sparse`, `blank`, `stale` or `garbage`, and hosts the region metrics (G06 confetti, G44 grass) plus an edge
     check. On 257 committed `.124` frames it finds the two H&D noise frames the sweep passed and nine
     stale-desktop frames, and it marks no good frame as garbage or stale (§1.5).
   - **(C) Verdicts.** Multi-frame sample rules and title tiers: `FAIL` / `CHECK` / `PASS (menu only)` /
     `PASS (in-game)`. Three in-game recipes already proven on `.124` move into `lan_check`.
   - **(D) `d3dprobe presentmark`.** It proves the tooling on silicon against known frames.
4. **The most valuable first use is a recapture.** The 11-12 "solid white terrain", "striped band" and "white area"
   frames (UT2003, UT2004) are GDI frames from a 9.4 fps run in which DrawPrimitive took 116.7 of every 123 ms. About
   half the GDI frames of such a run are expected to be a back buffer caught mid-render **(inferred from the timing,
   §1.2)**. G18 and G06 need front-buffer frames of those scenes before they act.

## 1. What the record shows

### 1.1 The three capture paths today

| path | reads | under a D3D title | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vcrctl fbshot` (default) | the scan-out registers and memBase1, through escapes (`VCR_ESC_INFO`, HWCEXT mapping, `VCR_ESC_PCI`) | never finishes: every escape runs under win32k's display lock (Max Payne, 09-29 and 10-04: nothing in 25 s) | `KMD/tools/vcrctl.c:1171-1214`, `:159-161`; `EV/gametune_1001/fbshot_probe/README.md:1-31`; `FIND:332` |
| `vcrctl fbshot --probe` | the same frame through `vcrprobe.sys` (no GDI, no DC) | finishes, but 1.4 s on the idle desktop became ~120 s under Max Payne, and ~38 s at `start /high` | `fbab_desktop2.json` (1.4 s, 3191 IOCTLs), `fbab_maxpayne.json` (120.1 s) |
| agent `SCREENSHOT 0` | `BitBlt` of the screen DC, i.e. the GDI surface | works, but may be either buffer of the flip chain (§1.2) | `WT/agent/src/screen.c:98-156`; `WT/scripts/benchmarks/lan_sweep.py:175-181` |

**Why `--probe` crawls.** A full frame is three IOCTLs per line: the start register, then two LFB chunks of a
5120-byte line. That is 3191 calls for 1280x1024x32. Each call maps and unmaps its 4 KB uncached
(`KMD/probe/vcrprobe.c:149-156`). The process runs at normal priority. Under a game that holds the CPU above normal
priority (G04's H-D), the read gets the CPU in starvation-boost slices **(inferred)**. Whether the bus also slows
under a busy 3D engine is unmeasured; nothing records the read's own CPU time.

### 1.2 GDI reads the GDI surface, which under a flip chain is the back buffer half the time

- **Where the GDI surface is.** The miniport places the GDI surface at `desktop_offset` and points scan-out there at
  every mode set (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_hw.c:700-708`, `KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:706`). That offset is
  `fb_per_chip - roundup4k(stride*h)` (`KMD/common/vcr_modes.c:380-384`), unless `Diag\DesktopOffset` pins it
  (`vcrmp_hw.c:358`).
- **Where the chain's other buffer is.** It comes from the DirectDraw heap, which starts at 0. Its first block is
  lost: "offset 0 = no memory" (`KMD/include/vcr_ddheap.h`). So a single back buffer of the primary's size lands at
  `size` itself:
  - 11-12, 1024x768x32: `primary at 3d00000`, render target `300000` (ring seq 29401 and 29433);
  - 10-04, Max Payne 1280x960x32: scan-out start `004b0000` = 0x4B0000, the size of one 1280x960x32 buffer. The
    GDI surface was at `03b50000`, so it was the back buffer at that instant.
- **Why that breaks a capture.** Flip swaps the surfaces' memory, so the GDI surface alternates between front and
  back **(inferred, DirectDraw)**. A GDI `BitBlt` waits for the display lock, which the game drops between
  DrawPrimitives2 calls. So when it lands on the back buffer, it photographs a frame in progress **(inferred)**.
- **How often that happens.** UT2003's flyby spent 116.7 of 123 ms per frame in DrawPrimitive (`onbox.md` §4).
  About half its GDI frames land on the back buffer, and nearly all of those are mid-render **(inferred)**. That
  explains V-10: Halo all white and Shogo's black mesh, in a Glide-free boot. It may also explain V-11 (UT2003's
  thin band) and the 11-12 frames that are "broken" only at one sample: `UT2003_flyby_030s` and
  `UT2004_botmatch_065s`.

### 1.3 The verdicts never judge the picture

- **`lan_sweep.py:300-327`.** The verdict counts processes, dialogs, Dr. Watson, the board and the agent. The frame
  is saved and its `(w, h, mean, sd)` recorded (`:224`); nothing reads it. The 09-28 07:05 H&D frames are
  full-screen colour noise, with stats `(1280, 960, 111.3, 28.5)`, and the run verdict was **PASS**
  (`EV/silicon/d3d32_step16/d3d32_titles_sweep.json`).
- **`lan_check.py:1097-1098`.** The only frame rule is `stddev < 4`, "blank shot", and it applies only to engine
  screenshots. `Launcher` smoke runs (`:832-902`, `:1117-1126`) never judge their GDI frames.
- **`WT/scripts/fleet/lanparty.py:556-576`.** `.124` D3D titles get `SCREENSHOT 1`. Everything else gets the
  default fbshot, whose escapes are why `d3d124` titles are excluded. The verdict is "black" when every channel's
  extreme is under 8.
- **Correction to V-13's wording.** The sweep's PASS did not come from luma statistics. The sweep never reads the
  frame, and the statistics were all a reader had.

### 1.4 Every D3D title in a fullscreen chain sits on the desktop layer

- **What `fbshot` sees.** It reads `vidDesktopStartAddr` and follows flips line by line (`vcrctl.c:1322-1340`). The
  10-04 probe JSON shows `"layer":"desktop"` for Max Payne.
- **Glide and OpenGL are different.** Our ICD's titles scan out through the overlay, with the desktop off. A GDI
  frame of them is the GDI surface: the old desktop memory at the new mode. One example:
  `RES/sweep_armed3/20260929_041911/Unreal_Tournament_436_*`.

### 1.5 A frame classifier, measured on the committed frames

Prototype definitions (§2.2), run over 257 committed `.124` frames:
- all of `EV/d3dtune_1014`, `ONBOX/games` and `EV/lan_20260929_sweep`;
- `RES/night/d3d32_titles`, `RES/sweep_armed` and `RES/sweep_armed3`.

| class | frames | which |
|---|---|---|
| `garbage` | 2 | `RES/night/d3d32_titles/20260928_070504/Hidden_and_Dangerous_Deluxe_30s.png` and `_60s.png`: garbage tiles 0.682 / 0.686 (0.971 / 0.983 on an every-4th-line thumbnail). **Both were PASS.** |
| `stale` | 9 | AvP ×5 (`S00` 30/60 s; `RES/sweep_armed/20260928_232010` 30 s and `20260928_234620` 30/60 s); UT2004 30 s in `sweep_armed3`; `ONBOX/.../UT2004_botmatch_045s.png`; UT 436 30/60 s in `sweep_armed3` (a Glide title's GDI frame of the desktop surface, expected) |
| `blank` | 30 | Halo `S08F` 60 s (one colour 255,255,255), UT2003 `S08F` 60 s (dominant colour 0.995), R6 error frames, the 8 bpp DirectDraw frames black to GDI, loading screens |
| `sparse` | 28 | JK/MotS crawls, logo screens, Shogo `S08F` 60 s (dominant 0.865) |
| `content` | 188 | everything else, including today's in-game frames with real block noise |

**Margins:**
- **garbage tiles.** The highest `content` frame scores 0.048: `UT2003_flyby_060s`, which has real G06 block noise.
  The garbage frames score 0.68.
- **lag-1 amplitude.** Among `content` frames whose lag-1/lag-2 ratio is at least 3, it is 2.7 levels or less:
  Shogo's 16 bpp dither, Carmageddon 2. Every stale frame is above 14.3.
- **the guard was needed.** An early version without the amplitude guard called Shogo `S08F` 30 s `stale` on a
  thumbnail.
- **thumbnails agree.** Every-4th-line thumbnails give the same class as the full frame for 256 of 257 frames. The
  one exception goes from `sparse` to `blank`; both are inconclusive.

**Exact stale-desktop prediction.**
- **Method.** Take `ONBOX/state/desktop_start.png` (1280x1024x32, captured at 10:00), place it at `0x3b00000`, and
  reinterpret the bytes at the 1280x960x16 GDI offset `0x3da8000` as RGB565. The unused byte of each pixel is 0.
- **Score.** The prediction is compared on the pixels it predicts brighter than luma 40, with a per-channel
  tolerance of 8:

  | frame | matches |
  |---|---|
  | `UT2004_botmatch_045s.png` (10:17) | **70.3 %** |
  | `UT2004_botmatch_090s` | 0.0 % |
  | `UT2004_botmatch_065s` | 0.1 % |
  | `UT2004_statfps_080s` | 0.1 % |
  | 045 s, offset shifted by −4 bytes | 58.2 % |
  | 045 s, offset shifted by −5120 bytes | 39.5 % |
  | 045 s, offset shifted by −81920 bytes | 22.3 % |

- **What that proves.** G39's mechanism and the GDI-offset formula, to the byte. The unused byte is 0: with 0xFF the
  odd pixels match 1.4 %, with 0 they match 77 %.

### 1.6 The edge rule, measured

- **The rule.** The last column or last row is "unrendered" when its mean step to its neighbour exceeds
  `max(3 × the neighbour's own step, 12)`. It also triggers when that line is uniform (sd < 2) while its neighbour
  is not (sd > 8).
- **Before the fix.** In `S08` (b63ea6a5) the 60 s frames of Red Faction, Thief II, UT2003 and UT2004 trigger it:
  steps of 110, 25, 254 and 52 against 2, 0, 0 and 9.
- **After the fix.** In `S08F` (13256e46) none triggers. H&D, Halo and Shogo never trigger it.

## 2. Design

### 2.1 Phase A: capture (`KMD/tools/vcrctl.c`, `KMD/include/vcr_fbshot.h`; user mode, escape-free)

**A1. `vcrctl scanout [--watch MS] [--pid PID]`.** It uses vcrprobe alone, through the existing `fb_probe_open` /
`fb_reg`, and reads registers only (no memBase1). It prints one JSON object:

- **Layer and buffer.**
  - `layer`: desktop or overlay, from `vidProcCfg`.
  - `start`: the desktop or overlay start register.
  - `stride`, `w`, `h`, `fmt`.
  - `gdi_offset` = `Diag\DesktopOffset` if non-zero, else `vcr_desktop_offset(MemorySize, stride, h)`. That is the
    miniport's own function: link `common/vcr_modes.c` into `vcrctl` (Makefile line 85) rather than copy the
    formula.
  - `front_is_gdi` = `start == gdi_offset`. It is null for the overlay layer.
- **`--watch MS`** (default 1000, at most 5000).
  - **Sampling.** The start register is sampled every ~1 ms: `timeBeginPeriod(1)`, `Sleep(1)`, with the thread at
    `THREAD_PRIORITY_TIME_CRITICAL`; `timeEndPeriod` afterwards. Each sample is one 4-byte IOCTL.
  - **Reports:**
    - `samples`, `max_gap_us`;
    - `flips`, the start changes seen, and `flips_per_s`;
    - `buffers`: the distinct starts, at most 8;
    - `gdi_front_fraction`;
    - `lower_bound: true` when `max_gap_us` exceeds half the observed flip interval. An A→B→A inside one gap is
      invisible.

  This is the escape-free liveness signal that the Max Payne session lacked. A flipping title shows its present
  rate. A frozen one shows 0 flips at a fixed start. It works for D3D, DirectDraw and Glide (the overlay start) alike.
- **`--pid PID`** reports, before and after the watch, all read-only through Toolhelp32,
  `OpenProcess(QUERY_INFORMATION)` and `GetProcessTimes`:
  - the game's priority class;
  - its highest thread base priority;
  - its CPU share over the watch.

  Per-thread detail stays with G04's `threads` command.
- `GetForegroundWindow()`'s pid before and after: a capture that took the focus from the game is reported as
  `focus_lost`.

**A2. `fbshot --probe`: four optional flags.** With none of them the read is unchanged (A5).

- **`--lines N`** (1-8, default 1). It reads screen lines `0, N, 2N, ...` plus always line `h-1`, at full width, and
  writes a `w x rows` BMP. Full width keeps the horizontal structure the `stale` rule needs (§1.5).
  - Cost: N=4 is about 740 IOCTLs and 1.2 MB of uncached reads at 1280x960x32, about 0.35 s on an idle box (a
    quarter of the 1.4 s full read; an estimate to be measured).
  - It needs no kernel change: `IOCTL_VCRPROBE_MEM` already reads any line run.
- **`--burst K --gap MS`** (K ≤ 5, gap ≥ 250). K frames in one process: no process start per frame, and one EXECW.
  - Files are `<stem>_<k>.bmp`.
  - One JSON line per frame as it completes, so partial results survive a timeout, then a summary line.
- **`--prio normal|high|critical`** (default `normal`).
  - `critical` = `HIGH_PRIORITY_CLASS` + `THREAD_PRIORITY_TIME_CRITICAL` (15), **only around the read loop of each
    frame**. Normal is restored before the gap's `Sleep`. That beats any normal- or high-class game thread below
    15 and stays under the realtime input threads **(inferred)**. Not REALTIME: a 0.35 s uncached read at 24
    would stall mouse and keyboard.
  - **`--cap-ms`** (default 3000 when the priority is raised) bounds each frame. Past it the read stops, the rows
    left are black, and the frame is `ok:false, capped:true`. That bounds the time a game can be held off the CPU.
- **Telemetry per frame:**
  - `wall_ms`;
  - `cpu_ms`, from the `GetThreadTimes` delta;
  - `ioctl_us_avg` / `ioctl_us_max`;
  - `flip_rows` (up to 64), `start_first`, `start_last`.

  If `cpu_ms ≈ wall_ms` the read was bus-bound. If `cpu_ms ≪ wall_ms` it was starved. Today nothing tells the two
  apart (open question 1). `flip_rows` marks the seams a reviewer must not mistake for a rendering defect
  (`visual.md` §2: `mots_new.png`'s bands).

**A3. The SLI/AA recheck cadence with sparse lines.**
- **The bug a sparse read would inherit.** `vcr_fb_recheck_due(y, flipped)` is `flipped || y % 64 == 0`
  (`vcr_fbshot.h:455-457`). With N = 3 it fires only every 192 screen lines.
- **The fix.** A new `vcr_fb_recheck_cross(prev_y, y, flipped)` = `flipped || prev_y == -1 || y / 64 != prev_y / 64`.
  The loop calls it. For N = 1 it is the old rule, call for call. The AA gate stays as it is: a VSA-100 counts as
  multi-chip, the master's registers are read before line 0, at every flip followed and every 64 screen lines,
  and the read stops at an AA enable.

**A4. DC-free commands and the escape guard (G04's "G79: the escape guard and the no-DC rule").**
- **No DC.** `main()` takes `GetDC(NULL)` for every command except `fbshot --probe` (`vcrctl.c:2100-2104`). A pure
  table `vcr_ctl_needs_dc(cmd, probe)` decides instead. No DC for `scanout`, `fbshot --probe`, `probe-vga`,
  `probe-pci`, `probe-mem`, `pace-mark`, and G04's `threads` and `probe-regs` when they land.
- **The guard.** `esc()` (`:159-161`), the one place every escape passes, first calls `vcr_ctl_escape_guard()`, once
  per process.
  - Its signal is G04's `Diag\Live\D3DBeat`: refuse when the beat is under 3 s old with `ctxs > 0`, unless
    `--force` is given.
  - Until G04 lands the beat, the guard has no signal and passes. The harness discipline (A6) is then the
    protection.

**A5. The old path, byte for byte.** `vcrctl fbshot [path]` and `vcrctl fbshot [path] --probe` with no new flag
behave as today:
- the same registers, the same line loop, the same recheck calls (`recheck_cross` equals `recheck_due` at N = 1);
- the same BMP;
- the same JSON keys, plus the new telemetry keys appended.

No existing command changes behaviour, except that the DC-free commands no longer hold a screen DC they never used.

**A6. Harness discipline, pinned by a test.** While a title runs, `lan_sweep.py`, `lan_check.py` and `lanparty.py`
may call only the escape-free set: `scanout`, `fbshot --probe`, `probe-*`, `pace-mark`. `vcrctl log` and the other
escape commands run only after the title has closed. `lanparty.py`'s default fbshot for non-`d3d124` titles is a
listed, reasoned exception until it adopts `--probe` (C4).

### 2.2 Phase B: `KMD/tools/frame_verdict.py` (host, Pillow only; `lan_check` already needs Pillow)

Pure functions over an RGB image plus optional context. The prototype used numpy; the port uses Pillow's
`histogram()` per tile, so no new dependency is needed.

**Classes, tested in this order:**

| class | rule | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `blank` | one 15-bit colour (5 bits per channel) covers ≥ 98 % of the pixels, or luma sd < 3 | inconclusive: never a pass, never a fail alone (G73 §3.3) |
| `garbage` | ≥ 50 % of 16x16 tiles are confetti tiles: > 40 % of the tile's pixels are saturated (S > 0.35, V > 0.15) **and** those pixels' hue entropy over 12 bins is > 2.6 bits. Off for greyscale frames (an 8 bpp probe read has no CLUT) | the screen is colour noise |
| `stale` (exact) | given the pre-launch desktop and both modes: ≥ 50 % of the predicted pixels brighter than luma 40 match within ±8 per channel (§1.5 method) | the old desktop's bytes at the new mode (G39) |
| `stale` (heuristic) | horizontal mean \|lag-1\| / mean \|lag-2\| luma difference ≥ 3 **and** mean \|lag-1\| ≥ 8 levels | a 32 bpp desktop read at 16 bpp, when no desktop capture exists |
| `sparse` | dominant 15-bit colour ≥ 85 % | inconclusive: logos, crawls, dark loads |
| `content` | otherwise | informative |

**Region metrics.** They are recorded and never part of the class: other items' pass bars are written in them.
- `confetti_permille(img, region)`: G06's definition, verbatim (G06 §2.6).
- `grass_blue_permille` / `grass_green_permille`: G44 §1.3, verbatim.
- `edge_check(img)`: §1.6. Full-height frames only; a thumbnail checks the last column and, since it always
  includes `h-1`, the last row.
- `stale_score(frame, desktop, old_mode, new_mode, fb_per_chip, desktop_offset_override)`.

**Contact sheet.** `contact_sheet(frames, out)` writes one JPEG per title: every frame labelled with its source,
time, class and `flip_rows` ticks. It is the reviewer's artefact (G73 §6.0).

**Presentmark.** `presentmark_decode(img, geometry)` decodes §4's frames.

### 2.3 Phase C: sample and title verdicts; the harness

**C1. A sample** is the frames taken at one time point:
- `scanout --watch 1000 --pid <game>`;
- one GDI `SCREENSHOT 0`, kept for continuity;
- `fbshot --probe --lines 4 --burst 3 --gap 1000 --prio critical`.

Rules:
- **Overlay layer** (Glide or GL): GDI frames are dropped. They show the desktop surface (`lan_sweep.py:16-19`).
- **Each GDI frame gets a source tag:**
  - `gdi-front` when the watch saw no flip and `front_is_gdi`;
  - `gdi` (unknown buffer) otherwise.
- **The sample's best frame** is the first `content` frame in source order engine > probe > `gdi-front` > `gdi`.
  - **If one exists**, the sample is `content`, with `front_confirmed` set when that frame's source is engine,
    probe or `gdi-front`.
  - **If none exists**, every probe frame sharing one bad class makes that class persistent. With no probe frames,
    every GDI frame sharing it makes it persistent with `front_unconfirmed`. Anything else is `mixed`.
- **When the probe is unavailable** (no vcrprobe, a refusal, a timeout, the AA gate), the sample takes 3 GDI frames
  1 s apart instead, and records `capture: gdi-only`.

**C2. The title's picture and verdict**

| picture | rule | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `garbage` | ≥ 2 samples persistently `garbage` (or the only sample) | **FAIL**: no valid picture |
| `stale` | every sample persistently `stale` with the front confirmed | **FAIL**: never presented over the stale desktop |
| `ingame` | a `content` sample inside the recipe's in-game window, and the recipe's marker found after the run | **PASS (in-game, front buffer)**, or **PASS (in-game, GDI only)** when no frame was front-confirmed |
| `menu` | any other `content` sample | **PASS (menu only)** |
| `inconclusive` | blank/sparse/mixed only, or stale without front confirmation | **CHECK**: no informative frame (with counts) |

Two notes, each a CHECK reason:
- **"Stopped presenting at T s":** an earlier watch saw flips and a later one none, with identical probe frames.
- **"Edge unrendered":** `edge_check` triggered on a full-height frame.

A stale first sample followed by `content` is only noted ("stale VRAM at mode set, G39"). The old reasons
(dialogs, Dr. Watson, forced close, board, agent) are unchanged and still win. The prefixes stay `PASS` / `CHECK:` /
`FAIL:`; no tool compares the full string (checked: no consumer of `rec["verdict"]` besides the scripts
themselves).

**C3. "In-game" needs a recipe.** A recipe is the launch, the in-game window, the close, and a marker that confirms
it after the run. Three are already proven on `.124` (`ONBOX/README.md` runs 16, 17, 19). They move into `lan_check`
as `Launcher`-style D3D classes:

| key | launch (staged tree, as the 11-12 run) | in-game | close | marker |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. d3dprobe test: `presentmark`

Explicit mode, like `perf`; it registers in G74's registry if that lands first:

```
d3dprobe presentmark --full --res WxH --bpp 16|32 --seconds S --hold-ms H [--novsync]
```

**What it draws.** Device and back buffer as `render`: lockable, one back buffer, FLIP, vsync unless `--novsync`.
Each frame k:
1. `Clear` to the **hole colour**, magenta `0xFF00FF`, exact in 565.
2. `LockRect` 1x1 `READONLY` on the back buffer, then `UnlockRect`. That forces the runtime and the HAL to execute
   the clear.
3. `Sleep(hold_ms)`: the window in which the back buffer holds nothing but the hole colour.
4. A full-screen quad in `BG[k % 6]`: red, green, blue, yellow, cyan, orange. All are 565-exact and none is magenta
   or grey.
5. A black strip over rows [0.40H, 0.60H) with 16 cells at `x = 0.1W + i·0.05W`: cell i is white if bit i of k is
   set, (64,64,64) if not.
6. A one-pixel white border: rows 0 and H-1, columns 0 and W-1.
7. `Present`.

At the end it writes `C:\vcr\d3dprobe\presentmark.json`: presents, seconds, `present_fps`, hold, the palette and the
geometry.

**The checker, `KMD/tools/presentmark_check.py`.** It `LAUNCH`es the probe, so the agent stays free, waits 8 s,
then runs:
- `scanout --watch 2000`;
- one `fbshot --probe --lines 4 --burst 3 --gap 1000 --prio critical`;
- 10 GDI `SCREENSHOT 0`, ~1.5 s apart;

and finally reads the JSON.

**Pass criteria (reference values are the probe's own):**

| check | criterion | what a failure means |
|---|---|---|
| flip watch | \|`flips_per_s` − `present_fps`\| ≤ 5 % of `present_fps`, `lower_bound` false | the watch is no presented-rate meter |
| probe frames | **0 hole-colour pixels** (±12) in all 3; every seam-free band crossing the strip decodes a k; k does not decrease down an image or across the burst; each band's BG equals `BG[k % 6]`; the last column (and last row) white | the front buffer showed an unfinished frame: the HAL drew into the scanned-out buffer, or triangles vanished and only clears landed (the G01 stale-FIFO signature) |
| GDI frames | each frame decodes to a frame k or is ≥ 90 % hole colour, and **at least 1 of 10 is hole colour** | the mechanism of §1.2 demonstrated on silicon. Expected fraction ≈ 0.5 × hold / frame period (≈ 0.42 at 20 ms on 85 Hz); P(0 of 10) ≈ 0.4 % |
| classifier | hole GDI frames → `blank`; probe frames → `content` | the verdict module mis-sorts the two known shapes |

**Modes:** 640x480x16 and 1024x768x32, vsync on (the campaign's choice), hold 20 ms. `--novsync` is optional.

## 9. Open questions

1. **Why did `--probe` take 120 s under Max Payne?** CPU starvation or bus stalls: `cpu_ms` against `wall_ms`
   (A2) answers it on the first attended run. If uncached memBase1 reads stall behind a full PCI FIFO or a busy
   engine on the VSA-100 (unknown), priority cannot help and phase D or fewer lines must.
2. **Can the `stale` rule survive G39?** Once mode sets clear video memory, "never presented" becomes `blank` plus
   0 flips plus no `ContextCreate` in the ring (G73's ring evidence). Should C2 then make "0 flips at every sample
   on a title whose recipe expects a device" a FAIL?
3. **Should every recipe scene keep a per-title reference frame (G45 Q4)?** For example `.145`'s GeForce running the
   same recipe, compared by the region metrics rather than pixel diffs, so "looks wrong" can be told from "looks
   like that everywhere".
4. **Engine screenshots (UE2 `shot`, Halo's screenshot command).** They are the strongest evidence for texture and
   combine defects (G73 §2.2). Which titles have one usable unattended, and does UE2's `shot` read the front or the
   back buffer under our HAL?
5. **Is `THREAD_PRIORITY_TIME_CRITICAL` enough?** It is not if a title runs a thread at 15 or more (G04's H-D).
   `--pid` reports it. If one does, should the burst yield (cap) or accept round-robin?
6. **Should `lanparty.py` adopt `--probe` for every `.124` title?** For non-D3D titles it would then lose the 8 bpp
   CLUT colour of the default fbshot.
7. **Could a flip that latches at vsync show a buffer the register no longer names?** The watch reads the
   programmed start, not a latched one, so a frame pending at the moment of a read can be the buffer the monitor
   still shows for up to one refresh. Captures cannot see G55's render-after-flip tearing (G55 agrees).

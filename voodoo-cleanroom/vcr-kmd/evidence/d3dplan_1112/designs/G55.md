## 0. Verdict

- **Probably real, but not measured.** Three things point to it:
  - our HAL advertises `D3DDEVCAPS_CANRENDERAFTERFLIP` and never holds back a draw into the buffer that is leaving the screen;
  - 3dfx's own HAL gets this for free from in-order hardware: a vsync'd `SWAPBUFFER` blocks the command processor;
  - both of Microsoft's DDK sample D3D drivers handle it in the driver, and neither reports the cap.

  Two things count against it:
  - nobody has looked at an in-game D3D scene on this HAL with the eye (G73);
  - XP's D3D8 runtime could be waiting on its own, through a GetFlipStatus or Lock throttle that was not traced statically.

  So **measure first**.
- **What is built first:** a counter of "first write into a pending `flip_from`", classified by a witness, plus two `d3dprobe` modes:
  - `raster`: is `vidCurrentLine` a usable line counter on the VSA-100?
  - `raf`: the hazard scene, with a variant a person can judge on the CRT.
- **The fix, if E1 confirms the gap:**
  - **F1 (primary):** the S3 ViRGE pattern. At the first colour write of a DP2/Clear2 into `flip_from`, wait in place until the chip has latched the flip. This adds no register write. Pair it with an exact witness, `vidCurrentLine`, once E0 proves it.
  - **F2 (alternative, and G54's first step):** the vendor/Permedia pattern. Put a hardware vsync fence in the command stream right after the flip: `swapPending`, then `swapbufferCMD` = WAIT_ON_VSYNC | DONT_SWAP (0x201).
- **Caps do not change.** The fix makes `CANRENDERAFTERFLIP` true.
- **Priority P1.** Promote the fix to P0 if E1 shows hazards in game sessions. In a GPU-bound scene the hazard shows a partly drawn frame on most refreshes (§3).

It says nothing about frame N+1's writes before the latch, which is what `RPT/perf.md:491-502` (perf-10) is about.

## 2. Is it real? The evidence on both sides

**For**

1. **3dfx's HAL is in order by hardware.**
   - The desktop flip path (`VH5/DDFLIP.C:257-297`): `FXBUSYWAIT`, a direct `vidDesktopStartAddr` write, `INCSWAPCOUNT` (a direct `swapBufferPend` write, `:77`), then `swapbufferCMD = waitOnVsync` **queued in the command FIFO** (`:274-279`).
   - The 3D path (`:394-447`): `leftOverlayBuf` + `swapbufferCMD` in the FIFO.
   - SPEC §8.83.4 (L4060): *"If vertical retrace synchronization is enabled for double buffered applications, the graphics command processor blocks on a SWAPBUFFER command until the monitor vertical retrace signal is active."* So every draw queued after the flip waits for the vsync.
   - That is why its GetFlipStatus answers OK for 3D (`DDFLIP.C:701-718`; flip-in-progress is just `status[30:28] != 0`, `:1004-1016`).
   - That is why its `CANRENDERAFTERFLIP` (`RPT/vendor.md:1097`, DevCaps `0xae11`) is honest.
   - Its Lock even documents the side effect: *"A side effect is to flush previous swap buffer commands, which ends up causing a wait on vertical retrace"* (`VH5/DDSURF.C:1746-1751`).
2. **Microsoft's DDK samples guard it in the driver, and neither reports the cap.**
   - **S3 ViRGE (DX6 DP2 HAL):**
     - DrawPrimitives2 returns `updateFlipStatus()`, i.e. `DDERR_WASSTILLDRAWING`, when the render target is the surface being flipped from (`DDKS/s3virge/d3ddx6.c:153-166`).
     - Clear spins until the flip is done (`:2006-2009`).
     - Its status rule is the one `vcr_flip.h` refined: retrace sampling plus a one-refresh duration (`DDKS/s3virge/ddraw.c:687-742`).
     - DevCaps has no `CANRENDERAFTERFLIP` (`DDKS/s3virge/d3d.c:520-527`).
   - **Permedia 2:**
     - The flip is queued in the input FIFO as `SuspendUntilFrameBlank` (`DDKS/3dlabs/driver/dd.c:451-458`). This is the same hardware ordering 3dfx gets from SWAPBUFFER.
     - DevCaps has no cap either (`DDKS/3dlabs/driver/d3d.c:548-558`).
   - **(inferred):** the runtime does not wait for the flip before rendering, and the driver must make render-after-flip safe whatever it reports.
3. **Our own model names the pattern.** `vcr_flip.h:29-31`: an application that "does not poll between a flip and its next wait" is "the D3D pattern".

**Against, or unknown**

1. **No flicker has been reported, but nobody has looked.**
   - Only menus have been watched (G73), and menus are drawn fast.
   - With a fast frame the hazard is invisible: the draws finish inside the vertical back porch (§3).
2. **The runtime might wait on its own.**
   - `XPRT/d3d8.dll` imports `OsThunkDdGetFlipStatus` through one wrapper (`0x6d9b2da0`, installed in callback slot `0x4c` at `0x6d9b56b6`).
   - Its callers are indirect and were not traced.
   - Its Flip wrapper loops on `DDERR_WASSTILLDRAWING` (`0x8876021c`) while its wait flag is set (`0x6d9b26c8-0x6d9b26d9`).
   - A Lock-based throttle on the back buffer would also hide the hazard, because `Dd_Lock` refuses `flip_from`.
   - `d3dim.dll`/`d3dim700.dll` (the DX5-DX7 titles) carry a `SurfaceFlipNotify` entry (strings). What it does is unknown.
3. **Each runtime must be measured** (E1, per runtime class).

## 3. What the hazard would look like, and why no capture has shown it

**When the window opens.** With vsync on and double buffering, `Dd_Flip` returns just after the previous flip latched, because it refused until then. So flip N is written just after vsync V_k and latches at V_k+1. Frame N+1's Clear and draws start at once, into the buffer the CRT scans between V_k and V_k+1.

**What the eye would see, by frame cost:**

| frame | what the CRT shows |
|---|---|
| draws done inside the back porch: 25-47 lines, about 0.55-0.6 ms at the staged 85 Hz modes (`vcr_modes.c:31`, `:43`) | Nothing wrong. Frame N+1 is shown a refresh early. This covers most menus. |
| a few ms of drawing | The beam meets the frame in progress: the clear colour, or missing objects, in bands that change every frame |
| GPU-bound (UT2004 at 1280x960 on one chip, about 18 ms a frame, `RPT/perf.md` §4.1) | Most refreshes show a partly drawn frame |

**Why no capture has shown it.**
- A GDI capture or `fbshot` reads memory long after the refresh.
- `fbshot` follows the **written** `vidDesktopStartAddr` (the new buffer), not the one being scanned.
- Only the eye at the CRT, or the driver's own counters, can see it.

**The frame-rate side (model, inferred).**
- Today the hazard buys overlap. With 18 ms GPU frames at 85 Hz, frames reach the screen every 18 ms on average (55.6 fps, alternating 1 and 2 refreshes), but corrupted.
- Correct double buffering gives 42.5 fps, every second refresh.
- Triple buffering gets the overlap back legally: the render target is then never `flip_from` (G65 stages it where the engine offers it, e.g. UE2 `UseTripleBuffering`).
- Scenes that fit in one refresh lose nothing.

## 4. Investigation

### 4.1 Instrumentation (new pure-logic header `KMD/include/vcr_raf.h`, Win32-free, host-tested)

**Hook point.** One hook, `raf_check(c, what)`, called immediately before the first **colour** write of a call:
- in `prepare()`, just before `VcrDd3dTarget` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1361`);
- in `clear_rects()`, just before `VcrDd3dTarget` (`:1416`), only when `what & VCR3D_CLEAR_COLOR` (a Z-only clear never touches the flip chain).

**Why the guard can sit there:**
- `prepare` always reaches the target write at the first triangle of every DP2 call (`w.prepared` = 0 from the memset at `:1835`);
- it does so again after every SETRENDERTARGET (`:1742`);
- it never runs when the call is not drawable (`:1325`).

**Candidate test.** The cheap pre-test is `pd->raf && pd->flip.pending && c->target.rt_off == pd->flip_from`. It needs no I/O and is false on every other draw.

**Per candidate: one witness sample, then a classification.**

| counter | meaning |
|---|---|
| `raf_flips` | vsync'd flips whose `flip_from` was then targeted. Counted **once per flip**: the flip sequence number is remembered, so later prepares in the same window do not count again. |
| `raf_latched` | the sample found the flip already latched. Harmless. |
| `raf_hazard` | still pending: the write went (counters only), or would have gone (guard), into the scanned buffer |
| `raf_certain` | the subset of hazards that the line witness confirms (W3, §5.1; only with bit 0x20) |
| `raf_beam[3]` | the beam's third of the screen at the hazard (W3 only): where the corruption would show |
| `raf_after_us` max/sum | time from the flip write to the first candidate write |
| `raf_gfs`, `raf_locks` | GetFlipStatus calls, and `flip_from` Lock refusals, between a flip and its first candidate. These answer "does the runtime wait?" |
| `raf_waits`, `raf_wait_us` max/sum | F1's waits |
| `fence_written`, `fence_retired`, `fence_refused` + last reason, `fence_late`, `fence_untaken` | F2's fence (§5.2) |

**Logging.** The counters go out with the existing flip counters, as event 511 a=15 (draws) and a=16 (fence). They are logged at the same once-per-session points (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:262-313`):
- "exclusive end";
- "mode off";
- "DD local gone".

**When G46 lands first.** If G46's `Diag\D3DStats` lands first (`designs/G46.md:258` already lists a coarse "hazard" counter), the classification here replaces that counter, and the rest of `D3DFlipOrder` stays.

## 5. The fix

### 5.1 F1: wait in place before the first colour write into `flip_from` (primary; no register write)

**Where.** The hook of §4.1, with `D3DFlipOrder & VCR_RAF_GUARD`. It calls a new `VcrDdFlipGuard(pd, off, what)` in `vcrdd_ddraw.c`, where `flip_done` and the flip state live.

**The loop.**
- Sample `status` directly from the DLL's register window: `*(volatile ULONG *)pd->pjRegs`. This is the same bit 6 the vblank IOCTL reads (`VCR_STATUS_VRETRACE`, clear in retrace, `vcrmp_dd.c:90-93`); `Dd_GetBltStatus` already reads `status` this way (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:570`). Feed `vcr_flip_poll`, with `EngQueryPerformanceCounter` as the clock.
- A sample costs about 1 µs, not one IOCTL. That is fine enough to catch the 3-line pulse: 35 µs at 1280x960@85, 69 µs at 640x480@85.
- **Bounded by construction.** `vcr_flip_poll` completes at the flip's deadline at the latest: frame + 1/8, i.e. 47376 ticks = 13.2 ms at 85 Hz and 18.75 ms at 60 Hz (`vcr_flip.h:32-35`).
- **Never early.** The rule is never early (pinned by `T/native/test_vcr_kmd_flip.c`, its never-early sweeps) as long as the flip's sample is taken after the write, and it is (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:352`).

**Why wait in place, rather than return `DDERR_WASSTILLDRAWING` as the S3 sample's DP2 does:**
- XP re-targets inside the DP2 stream (§1 step 3), so the target at entry is not the one drawn;
- a DP2 cannot be resumed in the middle of the stream after such a return;
- the same sample spins in its Clear.
- The cost is holding win32k's display lock for at most one refresh. That is bounded, and the same as the sample's Clear.

**Witness, in order of preference:**

| witness | how | exactness | state |
|---|---|---|---|
| **W3 `vidCurrentLine`** (bit 0x20) | Dd_Flip keeps the post-write sample's `v.scanline` and `in_vblank` (both already in `vcr_dd_vblank`, `KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h:225-233`). The guard reads `pjRegs + 0x94` (& 0x7ff). A vsync has passed iff a retrace sample was seen, or L_now < L_flip, or elapsed ≥ one frame. A flip written inside the retrace counts as latching at the **next** one, which is never early. | exact | needs E0. It lifts `vcr_dd_vblank`'s "never for flip completion" rule deliberately. |
| W1 `vidCurrDesktopStartAddr` (E4 only) | the 0xfc select read | exact | experiment only here. Production use belongs to G59/G54, because each read is two register writes. |
| **W4: today's rule** | `vcr_flip_poll` only | never early, but **over-waits** when a latch happened unseen before the first draw. It then waits for the next pulse or the deadline: up to (deadline − elapsed). That happens only for frames whose flip was written at a random phase, i.e. frames that already missed a refresh, and can push them a refresh later. | the fallback if E0 fails; its cost is measured (`raf_wait_us`) and pinned by the host test (§8) |

**Cost.** The CPU waits until the latch. This is correct double buffering (§3).

**Caps.** Unchanged. The fix makes the advertised cap true.

**NOVSYNC.** A NOVSYNC flip is never pending (`vcr_flip.h:132-144`), so `d3dprobe perf --novsync` (151 fps) and any immediate-present title are untouched. The application asked to tear.

### 5.2 F2: a hardware vsync fence right after the flip (alternative; becomes G54's first step)

**Write.**
- In `Dd_Flip`, after a successful vsync'd `IOCTL_VCR_DDFLIP` with `D3DFlipOrder & VCR_RAF_FENCE`, a new `VcrDd3dVsyncFence(pd)` in `vcrdd_3d.c` does:
  - `VcrDdRoom(pd, 2)`;
  - 3D `swapPending` (0x24c) = 0;
  - 3D `swapbufferCMD` (0x128) = `SST_SWAP_EN_WAIT_ON_VSYNC | SST_SWAP_DONT_SWAP` = **0x201**;
  - `pd->g2d_busy = 1`, so every drain sees it.
- The two offsets go in `vcr_3dregs.h` and are computed from the GPL `h3regs.h` like the others.
- Spec protocol: `swapPending` first, *"available only through direct access"*, then the command (SPEC L2722, L3684, L4062).
- Bits 10:9 = 01: *"the outstanding swap count will be decremented, but no video swaps occur"* (L2720). So no overlay or desktop pointer moves, and a stale `leftOverlayBuf` from Glide is never pushed. Interval 0, as the spec recommends for vsync (L2718).
- **Post-condition.** Read `status` back. `(status >> 28) & 7` must now be ≥ 1, or the fence did not take (`fence_untaken`: masked by a stale chipMask, G03, or not supported). Then fall back to F1 or to the old path for this PDEV.

**Effect.**
- The command processor blocks until the retrace (L4060). Every DP2/Clear2 write after it (fastfill, triangles, texture flushes) waits in the PCI FIFO.
- The CPU runs until the 31-entry FIFO fills, then spins in `VcrDdRoom`. That spin is bounded by `SPIN_CAP`, about 2 s (`vcrdd_2d.c:71`), against a wait of at most one refresh.
- This is exactly the vendor's desktop path, minus its overlay swap.
- With G47's command ring armed: `swapPending` stays a direct write and `swapbufferCMD` becomes a packet-1 write in the ring. Draws then queue in the ring behind it and the CPU no longer stalls, which is the vendor's model.

**Exact completion (bit 0x08).** `flip_done`, and through it GetFlipStatus, Lock, Blt and the next Flip, answers from `status[30:28] == 0`. This is the vendor's `FXGETFLIPSTATUS`.

**Preconditions.** If any one fails: no fence, today's path, and a counted reason.

| precondition | why |
|---|---|
| VSA-100 | bits 9/10 are documented in the Napalm spec |
| a vsync'd flip | |
| `!pd->exclusive_pid`, `pd->g2d_ok` | |
| `cmdFifo0.baseSize` with `SST_CMDFIFOEN` clear | read as Reset3D's pre-check does (`vcr_3dseq.h:161-182`); G01 |
| `dramInit1[11]` clear | Triple buffer enable. Set, the swap does **not** block (SPEC L2724, L5917). Glide writes the bit at every open (`voodoo-cleanroom/build/retro3dfx-glide/glide3x/h5/minihwc/minihwc.c:5159-5167`); the vendor sets it for 3D flip chains of more than 2 surfaces (`VH5/DDSURF.C:372-392`). |
| `status[30:28] == 0` before the write | no stale swaps |
| `dacMode` bits 1-4 clear | DPMS: vsync is running (the vendor fakes retrace toggles under DPMS, `VH5/DDFLIP.C:1050-1067`) |

**Stuck fence.** If the count is still non-zero past deadline + one frame:
- count `fence_late`, log WARN once, disarm for this PDEV, and complete the flip by the rule;
- whatever the fence blocks drains at the next vsync;
- with no vsync at all, the next drain's `SPIN_CAP` gives up (`vcrdd_2d.c:83-89`; G02 makes that visible).

**The race, and how to close it.**
- The address is written directly; the fence releases when the retrace signal is **active** (a level).
- `leftDesktopBuf` and the overlay buffers are *"sampled at the end of vertical retrace"* (L3686, L3696); `vidDesktopStartAddr`'s own section does not say (L4680-4682).
- If the chip latches at vsync **start** (86Box's model, `T/native/test_vcr_kmd_flip.c:4-17`) and the write lands inside the pulse, the fence releases one frame early.
- **Close it:** if a status read just before the IOCTL shows the retrace, spin until it ends (≤ 70 µs) before writing.
- G54 removes the race by construction: `leftDesktopBuf` + `SST_SWAP_DESKTOP_EN` (`VH5/DDFLIP.C:404-415` uses that pair, for AA only).

**Costs.**
- Every drain between a flip and its vsync waits for the vsync: Lock (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:658`), TEXBLT and DrvAssertMode (`KMD/display/vcrdd.c:613`). This is the vendor's documented side effect, and it hits upload-heavy titles (Halo's Bink, G56).
- 2D/GDI work queued behind the fence waits up to a frame.
- DirectDraw 2D titles also receive fences.

**No offline bed.** `86B/voodoo_reg.c:98-127` ignores bits 9 and 10, and `86B/vid_voodoo_fifo.c:268-281` skips the vsync wait when its FIFO is full. The 86Box bed cannot validate F2; silicon only.

### 5.3 Rejected as the fix

- **Withdrawing the cap (F3).** It does not make the hazard go away by itself: both DDK samples guard in the driver without reporting the cap (§2, "For", item 2), and DX5-DX7 titles call Flip themselves. It is kept only as experiment E2.
- **Answering GetFlipStatus `DD_OK` for 3D, as the vendor does.** That is valid only with an in-order swap (F2/G54).
- **Returning `DDERR_WASSTILLDRAWING` from Clear2.** The DDK sample spins in Clear instead, and whether the runtime retries Clear2 is unknown.

## 6. Gating

**Switch.** `Diag\D3DFlipOrder` (DWORD).
- **Absent or 0 is today's driver byte for byte:** no extra read, IOCTL, write or wait, and the same caps.
- It is read at every `IOCTL_VCR_INFO`, as `DdHeapFloor`/`D3DStats` are.
- It reaches the DLL as an appended `vcr_info.d3d_flip_order` (`KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h:63-107`; appended after G46's `d3d_perf` if that lands first). It is read only when `info.size` covers it.
- **No flag-word bit:** `0x8000` is already claimed by five designs (`designs/G46.md:319`).

| bit | name | what it does | default-on? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0x01 | `VCR_RAF_STATS` | the counters of §4.1: reads only, plus one witness sample per candidate | after boot A shows `d3dprobe render --full` 40/40 and `perf` within 1 % (the flip counters' precedent) |
| 0x02 | `VCR_RAF_GUARD` | F1 | **Yes, once proven.** It is a pure correctness fix that changes no register write. Proof: E1 real, then one supervised boot with `raf` hazards 0, the visual check uniform, the full D3D title sweep with no hang and on-time no worse than §3's model. |
| 0x04 | `VCR_RAF_FENCE` | F2. Exclusive with 0x02: if both are set, the DLL logs it and keeps 0x01 only. | no; it is folded into G54 |
| 0x08 | `VCR_RAF_FENCEWIT` | F2's swap count decides flip completion (only with 0x04) | with G54 |
| 0x10 | `VCR_RAF_NOCAP` | E2 only: drop `CANRENDERAFTERFLIP` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:2068`) | never; never left armed |
| 0x20 | `VCR_RAF_LINEWIT` | W3 | after E0 passes on that box |
| 0x40 | `VCR_RAF_CURDESK` | E4: the 0xfc select read in the miniport. It is a register write. | no |

## 7. d3dprobe tests (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`, `d3dprobe_run.py`)

### `d3dprobe raster --full [--res WxH] [--bpp 16] [--ms 2000]` (E0)

**What it does.** It needs a fullscreen device. It calls `IDirect3DDevice8::GetRasterStatus` in a tight loop for `--ms` and records QPC, InVBlank and ScanLine.

**What it computes:**
- `vblank_entries` and `frame_hz`;
- `wraps`: a line decrease with no InVBlank sample between;
- `mono`: the share of consecutive active-display pairs that do not decrease;
- `max_line`, `distinct_lines`.

**RESULT:** `{"mode":"raster","res":…,"samples":…,"frame_hz":…,"vblank_entries":…,"wraps":…,"frames_expected":…,"max_line":…,"mono":…,"scanline_valid":…}`.

**Pass (`scanline_valid`), all four:**
- `vblank_entries + wraps` is within 2 % of elapsed x refresh;
- `mono` ≥ 0.99;
- 0.95 x height ≤ `max_line` ≤ vtotal (509 / 808 / 1011 at the three modes);
- `distinct_lines` > height / 2.

**Reference.**
- `.124` should pass at all three modes, for example 170 ± 3 frames in 2 s at 85 Hz.
- 86Box (0x7ff on every read) must report `false`. That is the control showing the test can fail.

### `d3dprobe raf --full [--bb 1|2] [--pre-us N] [--gap-us N] [--frames 300]` (E1, E3, and F1/F2 verification)

**Device.** Fullscreen with vsync (`PresentationInterval` default), `D3DSWAPEFFECT_FLIP`, `BackBufferCount` = `--bb`, `D3DCREATE_SOFTWARE_VERTEXPROCESSING`. Same set-up as `perf`, `d3dprobe.c:1419-1470`.

**Each frame, in order:**
1. `Present`.
2. Busy-wait `--pre-us` (the game logic before the first draw).
3. `Clear(TARGET|ZBUFFER, 0x202020)`, which reaches Clear2 at once.
4. Busy-wait `--gap-us`.
5. One full-screen light-grey quad (0xc0c0c0), flushed by the next Present.

**What d3dprobe reports.** fps, and the Present-to-Present histogram in refresh units (≤1.25 / ≤2.25 / ≤3.25 / more), using the refresh measured with GetRasterStatus. **`d3dprobe_run.py`** then reads the ring's tail (the existing `vcrlog.py` route) and joins the 511 a=15/16 lines of the session.

**Pass, fix armed (0x03, or 0x0D for F2):**
- `raf_hazard == 0` in every variant: `--bb 1` and `--bb 2`; `--pre-us` 0, 4000 and 12500; `--gap-us` 0 and 5000. For F2, also `fence_written` == vsync'd flips and `fence_late == fence_untaken == 0`.
- `raf_wait_us` max ≤ the flip deadline (47376 ticks = 13.24 ms at 85 Hz, `vcr_flip.h:32-35`).
- When the scene fits (640x480, `--gap-us` 0 or 5000): at least 95 % of flips one refresh apart.

**Reference values (the baseline, 0x01):**
- `--bb 1 --pre-us 0`: `raf_hazard` ≥ 0.9 x frames if the gap is real.
- `--bb 2`: `raf_flips == 0` with or without the fix. This control shows the target is never `flip_from` when triple buffered.

**Visual (E3, supervised; `--gap-us 5000` at 640x480x16 and 1280x960x16, 20 s each).** Ask the person one question: "is the screen one uniform light grey, or is there a dark band across the top?"
- Baseline with the hazard: a dark band of about 5/11.8 of the height.
- Fixed: uniform.
- Without a gap the dark phase ends inside the back porch, which is why menus look fine.

**Regression in every armed boot.**
- `d3dprobe render --full` (default list) 40/40;
- windowed `present` (no flips, so nothing armed fires);
- `perf --full --novsync` at about 151 fps (NOVSYNC is never pending).

## 8. Regression tests (land with the fix, asserting both the fixed and the old-buggy value)

### `T/native/test_vcr_kmd_raf.c` (new; `run_all.sh` picks up `test_*.c` by itself)

It includes the real `vcr_flip.h` and `vcr_raf.h` (TRUE-SOURCE). It reuses the CRT model of `T/native/test_vcr_kmd_flip.c`: `crt_make`/`flag_at`/`latch_after`, `:184-235`; the drive of `drv_flip`, `:350-366`; the D3D pattern, `:429-444`.

1. **`old_path_draws_into_the_scanned_buffer`.** Over 640x480@85, 800x600@85, 1280x960@85 and 1600x1200@75: a flip, then the first draw `pre` later (20 µs, 0.5 ms, 4 ms, 9 ms). Mode 0 (`vcr_raf_must_wait` = 0) draws before `latch_after(w)` in **every** steady-state frame. That is the old-buggy value, asserted.
2. **`guard_never_draws_before_the_latch`.** With `VCR_RAF_GUARD` and W4:
   - 0 draws before the latch, for every write line, sub-line offset and `pre` (the fixed value, 0);
   - the same with polls that never land in the pulse (the `avoid_pulse` path, deadline completions);
   - lateness bounded by the deadline.
3. **`the_line_witness_removes_the_overwait`.** In the missed-latch case (flip at a random phase, `pre` past the latch):
   - W4's over-wait exceeds 1 ms for some phase (the documented cost);
   - W3 (the model's line counter) is never early and at most one 2 µs poll late;
   - a flip written inside the pulse waits for the next one under W3 too.
4. **`counted_once_per_flip`.** Several prepares in one window give `raf_flips` += 1.
5. **`the_fence_word`.**
   - `vcr_raf_fence_cmd()` == 0x201: bit 0 and bit 9 set, bits 8:1 and 10 clear;
   - mode 0 or `VCR_RAF_GUARD` wants no fence (0 writes, the old path).
6. **`the_fence_refuses_unless_every_precondition_holds`.** A table: VSA-100, NOVSYNC, exclusive owner, `cmdFifo0` EN (bit 8 of baseSize), `dramInit1` bit 11, stale `status[30:28]`, each of the `dacMode` bits 1-4. Each failing precondition gives its own reason code, and only all-good arms the fence.
7. **`fence_completion_is_the_swap_count`.** Status 0 → done; 0x10000000 → pending; 0x70000000 → pending. A post-write count of 0 → `fence_untaken`.
8. **`a_fence_that_never_retires_is_dropped`.** A count stuck past deadline + one frame gives late = 1, the fence disarmed, and the flip done by the rule.
9. **`caps`.** `vcr_raf_devcaps(0, caps)` keeps 0x800 (unchanged); `VCR_RAF_NOCAP` drops it.

### `T/python/test_vcr_kmd_d3d.py` (additions)

- **`test_render_after_flip_guard_is_gated_and_precedes_the_first_target_write`:**
  - in `prepare()` and `clear_rects()`, the hook sits before `VcrDd3dTarget(`, inside `if (… & VCR_RAF_GUARD …)` / `pd->raf` (fixed);

## 10. Risks to proven paths

**Default path.** With the switch absent, nothing changes: no new read, write, IOCTL or wait, and the same caps. §8 pins this.

**F1.**
- **What it is.** Waiting only: the register stream is byte for byte the same, so it cannot mis-draw or wedge the chip.
- **What it can cost:**
  - a stall of up to a refresh inside DP2/Clear2 while win32k's display lock is held (bounded by the deadline);
  - with W4, an over-wait on frames that already missed (pinned and measured);
  - for GPU-bound double-buffered titles, the frame rate drops to the honest double-buffer rate (§3: 55.6 → 42.5 fps in the 18 ms model). That frame rate was never real: the frames were corrupted.
- **Where it does not apply.** A NOVSYNC flip, a windowed (Blt-present) title, a `--bb 2` chain and the DirectDraw-only titles are untouched.

**F2.**
- two writes per vsync'd flip;
- the command processor, 2D and GDI included, blocked for up to a refresh;
- every drain waits for the vsync;
- dependence on `dramInit1[11]` (Glide can leave it set: the fence is then refused and logged);
- a fence that never retires wedges the FIFO until `SPIN_CAP` (DPMS is pre-checked; G02 makes a give-up visible);
- the vsync-start race (§5.2);
- the DirectDraw 2D titles are affected.

**Glide/OpenGL coexistence.**
- No fence while `exclusive_pid` is set or `cmdFifo0` is on.
- The fence leaves only `fbiSwapHistory` and the internal retrace counter changed (SPEC L2716, L3697). Both are harmless: Glide's `grGet` swap history would include a few D3D fences.
- F1 touches no register.

**SLI.** D3D is chip 0 only, and so is the fence. A stale chipMask (G03) shows as `fence_untaken`, not as a silent hazard.

**The flip path.** F1 adds nothing to it. F2 adds the fence and, with 0x08, swaps the completion rule for the swap count. The deadline backstop of `vcr_flip.h` stays in both.

## 12. Open questions

1. Does XP's D3D8 runtime, or `d3dim`/`d3dim700` for the DX5-DX7 titles, wait for the flip before Clear2/DP2? It might do so through GetFlipStatus (`d3d8.dll` callback slot 0x4c, callers not traced) or a back-buffer Lock throttle. E1's `raf_gfs`/`raf_locks` answer it.
2. Does any runtime key a wait on `CANRENDERAFTERFLIP` (E2)?
3. Is `vidCurrentLine` monotonic through active display on the VSA-100, and what does it read in the blank (E0)?
4. On this silicon, does a `DONT_SWAP | WAIT_ON_VSYNC` swap block the command processor in double-buffer mode? Does it release at the start or the end of the retrace relative to `vidDesktopStartAddr`'s latch (boot C, the race)?
5. Is `dramInit1[11]` ever left set on `.124` after a Glide session? Read it before and after a 3-buffer Glide open.
6. Does the `vidCurrDesktopStartAddr` select (IO 0xfc bit 31) exist on this VSA-100 revision, and does writing 0 restore the overlay read that `fbshot` relies on (E4)?
7. Which staged titles present with one back buffer, i.e. carry the hazard? The per-title `raf_flips` answers it. G65 decides whether to stage triple buffering where the engine offers it.
8. Does the D3D8 runtime retry a DP2 that returns `DDERR_WASSTILLDRAWING` (the S3 pattern)? It is not needed for F1 as designed, and is recorded for G54.

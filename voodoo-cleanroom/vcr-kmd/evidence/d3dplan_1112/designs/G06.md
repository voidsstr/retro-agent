## 1. Verdict

**The gap is real. Its most likely cause is G07, with strong evidence but no silicon proof yet:
mipmap chains are split in video memory.**

- XP creates every mip level with its own `DdCreateSurface`. Our allocator gives each level its
  own block.
- The TMU reads level *k* at `top + chain_offset(k)`. It has a single base address, with no
  multibase in use.
- Wherever DirectDraw's heap did not put the levels back to back, a minified pixel reads another
  texture's bytes, or stale free memory, and decodes them in this texture's format. Random bits
  decoded as DXT1/DXT5/A8R8G8B8 give saturated, blocky, multi-coloured noise (inferred decode
  argument; it matches V-02's 4-byte-misaligned DXT noise of 2026-11-08).

This explains each observation in `onbox-04`:

| observation | explanation |
|---|---|
| close and magnified surfaces are clean (the near rock face, weapon, HUD, text, sky) | LOD 0 is the top level, which is always in its own block and always right |
| edge-on faces, distant terrain and rocks are noisy; in Halo, triangles of one rock mesh are clean or striped depending on their angle | LOD >= 1 is sampled there, and the LOD depends on distance and angle |
| "some patches correctly textured" | chains that happened to land back to back |
| d3dprobe passes 83/0 and 37/3 | a fresh heap allocates first-fit, ascending, back to back. `mip`/`mip2048` check levels 0-4 only, and every DXT and 8888 test uses `Levels = 1` |
| Halo shows it too | D3D9 reaches the same DX7 DDI path. The cause is not UE2-specific |

**What the evidence rules out:**

- **G01**: the captures were made with `cmdFifo0` off.
- **The FOURCC-mismatch fallback in `mipchain_ext`**: it cannot trigger with one level per call.
- **Wrong decode at LOD 0**: decoding is texel-exact at 1:1.

**The plan:**

1. One instrumentation build, which logs only.
2. A deterministic lab reproduction (`miptail`) plus a fragmented-heap one (`mipfrag`).
3. An in-game census of split chains.
4. The fix, behind `Diag\D3DMipChain`:
   - a **chain guard** that never lets the TMU read a level that is not where it looks;
   - a **whole-chain reservation** at the top level's `CreateSurface` (what 3dfx did).

## 2. Evidence (new in this design)

### 2.1 In code, every level is its own block

- **`mipchain_ext`** (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:367-460`) sizes and places only the surfaces of the
  current call:
  - it walks `p->lplpSList[0..dwSCnt)` (`:423-436`);
  - it allocates `vcr_tex_chain_offset(kind, w0, h0, levels)` bytes (`:437-441`);
  - it marks `s == top ? MIP_TOP : MIP_LEVEL` (`:457`).
- **`VcrDdD3dCreateMipChain`**, the default path, does the same (`:522-584`, `:562`, `:575`).
- **On XP `dwSCnt` is 1.** Three sources agree:
  - the DDK's `DD_CREATESURFACEDATA`: "usually 1 on Windows 2000" unless the driver answers
    `GUID_NTPrivateDriverCaps` with `DDHAL_PRIVATECAP_ATOMICSURFACECREATION` (`d_grafx.chm`);
  - our `DdGetDriverInfo` refuses that GUID (`runtime.md` §2, `fad16a23` → not available);
  - the repo's own measurement: "seven calls for a 64x64 D3D8 texture, DDSD_MIPMAPCOUNT 7 on
    each" (`WT/tests/python/test_vcr_kmd_d3d.py:106-114`).
- **The result:** every level becomes a one-level "chain". It gets its own 16-byte-aligned
  `HeapVidMemAllocAligned` block and is marked `MIP_TOP`.
- **The commit message is wrong.** e74510a ("the driver allocates the whole chain") and the
  README Finding (`KMD/README.md:628-631`, "a mipmap chain is one block") both describe a list
  that XP never sends.
- **The sampling side never checks placement.**
  - `tex_view_ext` (`:806-824`) and `tex_view` (`:754-786`) count the levels through the attach
    list (`next_mip`, `:617-626`).
  - They set `tLOD.LODMAX = lod + n - 1`.
  - They never check that level *k* lies at `top + chain_offset(k)`.

### 2.2 In the ring, no chain of more than one level was ever placed on `.124`

- Every `513/20` event in today's rings reads `1 levels`:
  - `ONBOX/ring_after_probe_ii_decoded.txt:102-167`;
  - `ONBOX/ring_end_decoded.txt:941-1006`.
- `grep -r 'mipmap chain' KMD/evidence | grep -v ' 1 levels'` finds nothing.
- The probe's own placements show the allocator working **first-fit and ascending** on a fresh
  heap: a 64x64 at `0xc0000`, then a 16x16 at `0xc2000 = 0xc0000 + 8192`
  (`ring_end_decoded.txt:986-989`).

### 2.3 On 11-08, UT2003's menu had other textures inside its own level-1 slots

The data is `S08/UT2003_combos_unaligned.txt`. It holds the `513/23` "tex: at" placements, all
of them `MIP_TOP` (`mip 7663000x`, so ours). The table below takes each top level's `at` and its
size, adds them to get where the TMU reads level 1, and lists the other live textures that sit
inside that slot.

| texture (line) | top at, size | `tLOD` | TMU's level-1 slot | other textures inside the slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| combine 8, 512x512 A8R8G8B8, TBIG (`:23`) | `0x3c21ac0`, 1 MB | `0x40000b08` (LODs 2-11) | `0x3d21ac0`-`0x3d61ac0` | combine 6, 512x512 DXT1 at `0x3d2c560` (`:18`); combine 5, 256x256 DXT1 at `0x3d4f000` (`:15`) |
| combine 12, 256x256 DXT5 (`:34`) | `0x3bb7000`, 64 KB | `0x800` (LODs 0-8) | `0x3bc7000`-`0x3bcb000` | combine 9, 128x128 DXT5 at `0x3bc8570` (`:26`) |
| combine 15, 128x128 32-bit (`:43`) | `0x3b6ba60`, 64 KB | `0x804` | `0x3b7ba60`-`0x3b7fa60` | combine 14, 128x64 32-bit at `0x3b7e510` (`:40`) |
| combine 16, 128x128 32-bit (`:46`) | `0x3b56510`, 64 KB | `0x804` | levels 0-6 fit exactly before `0x3b6ba60` | its 1x1 slot (`0x3b6ba60`) is combine 15's first texel |

**The counter-example is the useful row.** Combine 16's levels 0-6 sit back to back: fresh space
gives contiguous chains, which is exactly why the probe passes. A top level dropped into a hole
splits its chain at level 1.

**This is inference from addresses.** It assumes the listed textures were alive together, which
is likely because all of them are menu textures first drawn within seconds of each other. The
instrumentation in §3.1 measures it directly.

### 2.4 Even a fresh heap misplaces the tail of a 16 bpp chain

- Each per-level block starts 16-byte aligned (`al.dwStartAlignment = 16`, `:439-440`, `:560-561`).
- A 2x2 R5G6B5 level is 8 bytes. The 1x1 after it can therefore never be at `2x2 + 8`, where the
  TMU reads it.
- **Worked example: a 64x64 R5G6B5 chain.**

  | | offset of each level |
  |---|---|
  | TMU | `0, 8192, 10240, 10752, 10880, 10912, 10920` |
  | per-level blocks | `..., 10912, 10928` |

  The 1x1 level reads 8 bytes of padding.
- **8:1 chains (256x32) lose their last two levels** the same way: the 4x1 level is 8 bytes and
  the 2x1 is 4.
- **What is not affected:**
  - DXT1/DXT3/DXT5 levels are multiples of 16 in the TMU's units;
  - square 8888 chains are 16-aligned down to 2x2.
- **This is a deterministic lab reproduction** of the defect, with no fragmentation needed (§6.1,
  `miptail`).

### 2.5 How 3dfx did it on the same OS and silicon

**On NT5, 3dfx answered the GUID and used atomic creation.**
- The XP build uses DirectDraw's memory manager (`VH5/DRIVER.H:39-42`, `USE_NT5_DDMEMMGR` = 1
  for `_WIN32_WINNT >= 0x0500`).
- It answers `GUID_NTPrivateDriverCaps` with
  `ATOMICSURFACECREATION | NOTIFYPRIMARYCREATION` (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:1224-1248`).

**The whole chain is reserved at the top level's call.**
- The level count comes from `surfLCL->lpSurfMore->dwMipMapCount` (`VH5/D3TXTR.C:1635-1636`).
- The memory is `txtrCalcMemRequired` (`:2130-2145`, `:3855-3878`): natural level sizes, with only
  the total rounded to 16. This matches our `vcr_tex_chain_offset`.
- `initMipMapChain` (`:862-955`) walks the attach list and gives every level its
  `txtrComputeVAddr`.

**It also has a per-level fallback**, for when DirectDraw calls once per level ("a completely
bogus operation", `:1749-1785`). A level already stamped by its top level gets its `fpVidMem`
recomputed from the top level's descriptor.

**Its level layout rules match ours** (checked line by line):
- DXT1 is padded to 8x4, the other DXTn to 4x4, with a 4-texel minimum height (`:815-850`,
  `:3891-3955`).
- The base is rounded **up** to 16 (`:3763`, `:3840`).

### 2.6 What the frames measure (a "confetti" metric, proposed for G79's sweep verdict)

**Definition.** A pixel counts as confetti when all of these hold:
- its brightest channel is at least 16;
- its saturation (max - min) / max is at least 0.45;
- the pixel 2 to the right or 2 below is also saturated, but has a different hue sector, where a
  sector is the pair (argmax channel, argmin channel).

The metric is the count, in per mille of the pixels in a region.

**Baselines on today's frames**, computed for this design:

| frame, region | value |
|---|---|
| `UT2004_botmatch_090s`, terrain band (0,520)-(700,720) | 88.1 ‰ |
| same frame, the clean near rock (700,100)-(1280,900) | 3.1 ‰ |
| `UT2003_flyby_060s` / `_090s` / `_120s`, full frame | 95.6 / 52.6 / 75.2 ‰ |
| `Halo_timedemo_120s`, the striped rock (470,280)-(780,580) | 20.5 ‰ |
| `Halo_timedemo_120s`, a clean region (900,350)-(1280,600) | 1.0 ‰ |
| `UT2004_botmatch_025s` splash | 3.2 ‰ |

**Pass bar after the fix: 5 ‰ or less in the same regions.** The metric ignores brightness, so
G05's darkness does not mask it.

## 3. Investigation: confirm the cause, then rule out a second one

### 3.1 Instrumentation (log only; no register or allocation change; lands default-ON)

Each part follows the "Phase 0" rule in `hal.md` §10. Event numbers 513/24-27 are proposed;
coordinate them with the other designs.

**(a) Creation sequence: `513/24`, DEBUG.**
- Logged in `Dd_CreateSurface` for each `DDSCAPS_TEXTURE` surface, for the first 512 texture
  creations after boot only. That bounds the IOCTL cost of G52, and covers the d3dprobe run made
  first.
- Fields:
  - the surface pointer, its w x h and caps;
  - `lpSurfMore->dwMipMapCount`;
  - `lpDDSurfaceDesc` (pointer, `dwFlags`, `dwWidth`/`dwHeight`, `dwMipMapCount`);
  - whether `lpAttachList` is non-NULL, and the first attached surface's size;
  - `dwSCnt`, the pid;
  - the `fpVidMem` it got.
- What it settles:
  - whether levels arrive top-first and consecutively, with one shared descriptor;
  - whether `lpSurfMore->dwMipMapCount` counts down;
  - whether the attach list already exists at the top level's call.

  Those are the facts F2 (§4.2) relies on. It also shows the heap's placement policy.

**(b) Contiguity census at bind.** In `tex_view_ext` and `tex_view`, inside the `next_mip` walk
they already do:
- collect each level's `fpVidMem`, in-video-memory flag and format;
- call a new pure function, `vcr_tex_chain_contig(kind, w0, h0, addr[], n)`, which counts the
  leading levels that lie exactly at `addr[0] + vcr_tex_chain_offset(kind, w0, h0, k)`, in video
  memory and in the top level's format;
- when that count `c` is below `n`:
  - log `513/25`, WARN, **once per surface** (new `SF_SPLIT` bit, `0x8`, in the
    `dwReserved1` flag word, `:205-212`), at most 64 per context: "mip chain split: WxH fmt F:
    c of n levels where the TMU reads them; level c at X, TMU reads Y";
  - bump per-context counters: chains bound, split, split at level 1, split only in the last
    two levels, and a split count per kind (RGB16, ARGB32, DXT1, DXT35);
  - keep the first 8 split chains in the context.

**(c) Context summary: `513/26`, INFO.** Emitted in `ctx_free` (`:1210`) beside the existing
"context gone". It carries the counters, then re-logs the 8 kept chains.
- **Why at the end:** the 1024-entry ring wraps within seconds of a game starting (`onbox-11`),
  but its last 1024 entries always hold the "gone" summary. So the census survives at any
  `LogLevel`.

**(d) The ring.** `Diag\LogEntries` = 16384 (the miniport's maximum, `vcrmp_log.c:282-284`) for
the instrumentation boot. It is logging configuration and changes no rendering.

## 4. The fix

### 4.1 F1: the chain guard (`Diag\D3DMipChain` bit 0)

- **Where:** `tex_view_ext` and `tex_view`, using §3.1(b)'s walk.
- **What:** `n = min(n, c)` before LODMAX is computed, so `LODMAX = lod + c - 1` (with `c >= 1`).
  - It changes `tLOD` bits 13:8 (LODMAX), and only for a chain whose levels are not where the
    TMU walks.
  - `textureMode`, `texBaseAddr` and LODMIN are unchanged.
  - It is single-pass, with no caps change.
- **Effect:**
  - A split chain samples only its in-place prefix: distant surfaces alias, but never read
    foreign memory.
  - A level in system memory is caught as well, because its address does not match.
- **It is not byte-for-byte neutral on the proven path.** Every R5G6B5 full chain loses its 1x1
  level (§2.4), so `mip` and `mip2048` change LODMAX by one; their checked levels are unaffected.
  Hence the switch.

### 4.2 F2: whole-chain reservation (`Diag\D3DMipChain` bit 1)

This is the default and VSA-100 paths' version of 3dfx's approach. The logic is pure and
host-tested, in a new header `KMD/include/vcr_mipres.h` (static inline, like `vcr_rtfmt.h`). It
is used by `mipchain_ext` and `VcrDdD3dCreateMipChain`.

**1. Opening a reservation, at the top level's call.**
- **Conditions:** `dwSCnt == 1`, the caps hold `TEXTURE|MIPMAP` (and `COMPLEX`, if E0 confirms
  only the top level carries it), the chain has more than one level, and the top level is
  sampleable (`vcr_texlod_compute_ext` / `vcr_texlod_compute` succeed).
- **The level count:** `lpSurfMore->dwMipMapCount`, as 3dfx used it, else the descriptor's
  `dwMipMapCount` when `DDSD_MIPMAPCOUNT` is set.
- **Allocation:**
  - size `vcr_tex_chain_offset(kind, w0, h0, levels)`, rounded to 16, from the same
    `HeapVidMemAllocAligned` (16/16);
  - the top level goes at the start of the block and is marked `MIP_TOP`, which frees the
    block in `VcrDdD3dFreeMipChain`, as today.
- **The record:** an open `vcr_mipres` in the PDEV, keyed by `p->lpDD`, the pid, `w0`/`h0`/levels,
  the format key (FOURCC, or bit count plus R mask) and the descriptor pointer.

**2. Claiming a level, at each later call.**
- **Before** the sampleability check, and before `VcrDdD3dCreateTexSurface`: a DXT1 level
  narrower than 8 is claimed too, because its TMU-sized slot (16 bytes) holds D3D's 8.
- **Matching:** the level's size is `(w0>>k, h0>>k)` for an unclaimed *k* with the same format
  key; `lpSurfMore->dwMipMapCount == levels - k` where E0 shows that field counts down; and the
  same `lpDD` and pid.
- **On a match:**
  - `fpVidMem = base + vcr_tex_chain_offset(kind, w0, h0, k)`;
  - `lPitch` exactly as today (`w*2`, `w*4`, or the DXT linear size, `:454-455`);
  - caps `VIDEOMEMORY|LOCALVIDMEM`;
  - mark `MIP_LEVEL`, and return `DDHAL_DRIVER_HANDLED` / `DD_OK`.
- **The reservation closes** when the last level is claimed, at any call that does not match
  (logged), or when its top level is destroyed (`Dd_DestroySurface`, `KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c:663`).

**3. Linking the levels to their top level.**
- **If E0 shows the attach list already exists at the top level's call**, the top level's call
  stamps each attached level directly, as `initMipMapChain` does: `dwReserved1 = MIP_LEVEL`, and
  the target offset in `lpGbl->dwReserved1`. The level's own call then only restores
  `fpVidMem`, as 3dfx's per-level path does.
- **Otherwise** the keyed record above is used. dxg serializes one complex creation's driver
  calls (inferred; E0 confirms it).

**4. Fallbacks, which are never worse than today.**
- **No block big enough for the whole chain** (a fragmented heap): place the top level alone, as
  today, and log it. The guard clamps that chain.
- **A refused claim:** that level gets its own block, as today. The guard clamps at that level.
- **Every close and refusal** is logged as `513/27`, with its reason and the number of unclaimed
  levels.

**5. Interaction with the rest of the texture path.**

| path | effect |
|---|---|
| TEXBLT (`:1499-1549`) and Lock + CPU uploads | they write each level at its own `fpVidMem`, which now lies inside the block |
| `flush_written` | unchanged |
| freeing | the top level frees the whole block. A level's destroy frees nothing, in either order, as today |

### 4.3 F2-alt: atomic creation (`Diag\D3DMipChain` bit 2; only if E0 makes F2 unsafe)

**Use it only if** E0 shows levels arriving out of order or interleaved, or no reliable level
count at the top level's call.

**What it does:**
- Answer `GUID_NTPrivateDriverCaps` (`fad16a23-7b66-11d2-83d7-00c04f7ce58c`) in `DdGetDriverInfo`
  (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:683`) with `DD_NTPRIVATEDRIVERCAPS { sizeof, ATOMICSURFACECREATION }`. Do
  **not** answer `NOTIFYPRIMARYCREATION`.
- The existing list loops of `mipchain_ext` and `VcrDdD3dCreateMipChain` then receive the whole
  chain. That is the case they were written for.
- Harden those loops:
  - pick the *largest* surface as the top level, not `lplpSList[0]`;
  - reject a *k* of 12 or more, or a duplicate *k*, on the default path too (`:550-557` has no
    `k >= 12` check).

**Why it is not the first choice.** It is 3dfx's mechanism, but it changes how dxg calls
`CreateSurface` for *every* complex surface of every application: flip chains, and the
DirectDraw 2D titles. 3dfx handled flip chains itself; we return `NOTHANDLED`. ATOMIC combined
with `NOTHANDLED` lists is not vendor-proven. So a Boot with bit 2 must also pass:
- d3dprobe fullscreen 40/40 and windowed 42/42;
- one 2D DirectDraw title (Red Alert 2) to its menu;
- DOSBox `output=ddraw`.

### 4.4 The old path

With `Diag\D3DMipChain` = 0 (absent), allocation and register traffic are byte-for-byte today's.
The only additions are the §3.1 log events and counters.

**No caps change at any setting.** `TextureCaps` MIPMAP and the formats are as reported.

## 5. Gating

**Instrumentation (§3.1) lands default-ON.** It is log-only, with bounded volume.

**`Diag\D3DMipChain` is a new DWORD, read at boot in FindAdapter like `D3DBigTex`
(`KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:244`).** It reaches the display driver as positive `vcr_info.flags`:

| bit | value | `vcr_info.flags` | what it turns on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | `VCR_INFO_F_MIPGUARD` 0x8000 | the chain guard (F1) |
| 1 | 2 | `VCR_INFO_F_MIPRESERVE` 0x10000 | the whole-chain reservation (F2) |
| 2 | 4 | `VCR_INFO_F_MIPATOMIC` 0x20000 | atomic creation (F2-alt). Never together with bit 1 |

- The flag values are to be coordinated with parallel designs.
- `native/test_vcr_kmd_abi.c::every_vcr_info_flag_owns_one_bit` pins the map (today `seen == 0x7fff`).

**The rules for the switch:**
- It is armed on a VSA-100 and on Banshee/Voodoo3 alike, because the bug is on both paths.
- It changes once per clean boot.
- The proof order is bit 0 (Boot 1), then bits 0+1 (Boot 2).

**Not safe to land default-on now.** F1 changes LODMAX on the proven path (§4.1), and F2 changes
placement for every mipmapped texture.

**The plan to make it default-on:**
1. After Boot 2 and a D3D title sweep with value 3, flip the miniport default to 3 (absent = 3,
   0 = kill switch).
2. Correct the README Diag table, the README Finding at `:628-631`, and the test docstring.

## 8. Risk to proven paths

**The default 16 bpp path (no `D3DBigTex`).** With bit 1, `VcrDdD3dCreateMipChain` reserves too.
That is needed, because the same split exists there. The mitigations:
- the guard;
- per-level fallback on any mismatch;
- `mip`/`mip2048`/`render` must be unchanged at 16 bpp.

**Glide/OpenGL coexistence: none.** Only DirectDraw-heap textures change, and Glide owns the board
exclusively.

**SLI: none.** D3D draws on chip 0 only.

**The flip path.** F2 does not touch it. F2-alt does, which is why it is only the fallback and
needs its own checks.

**Memory.**
- A reservation is allocated whole. A chain that no longer fits as one block falls back to
  today's behaviour, never to `DDERR_OUTOFVIDEOMEMORY` that today would not happen.
- An abandoned reservation wastes at most a third of its top level until the texture dies, and
  it is logged.
- **G63** (a heap block at offset 0 reads as failure) applies to the single-pass allocator
  unchanged.

**Latent layout bugs.** Lower levels become *sampled* for the first time. A wrong rule for some
shape (aspect, TBIG, DXT tails) would then show. E3 and `mipaspect`/`mipbigdxt` gate that before
F2 goes on.

**Visual change.** Distant surfaces become mip-filtered, and without trilinear (G26) or the +0.5
LOD rounding (G27) mip bands show. That is correct, but different.

**Lifetime.** The free path keeps today's saved heap pointer (G43's open question about a mode
change while textures live). Nothing is added there.

**CPU cost.**
- The guard reuses a walk that already happens.
- The summary is one event per context.
- `513/24` is capped at 512 events.

**Stability: low risk.** Every write stays inside a block we allocated. A wrong placement can only
mis-sample; it cannot write out of bounds.

## 10. An adjacent defect found while designing this (file it as its own gap)

**A single 1x1 R5G6B5/1555/4444 texture is sampled 8 bytes before itself.**

**Why:**
- `vcr_texlod_compute` (`KMD/common/vcr_texlod.c:32`) and `_ext` (`:134-137`) compute
  `base = (offset - pre) & ~0xf`.
- For a 1x1 16-bit texture, `pre = 174760`, which leaves 8 modulo 16.
- So the TMU reads LOD 8 at `offset - 8`.

**3dfx hit the same thing.** It rounds the base up (`VH5/D3TXTR.C:3763`) and places 1x1 textures 8
bytes into their block ("1x1 textures need to be aligned 8 bytes", `:877-889`).

**No other shape is affected.** Every other top size leaves `pre` a multiple of 16: 2x2 gives
174752, and the aspect, 8888, DXT and TBIG cases all leave 0.

**Fix:** place such a texture at block + 8 in `mipchain_ext`.

**Test:** native, base + 174760 is `offset - 8` before the fix and equals the placement after it;
d3dprobe `tex1x1`, a 1x1 R5G6B5 next to a different colour.

It produces wrong flat colours, not confetti, so it is out of G06's scope.

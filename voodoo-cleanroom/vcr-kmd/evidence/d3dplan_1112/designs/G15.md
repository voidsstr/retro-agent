## 0. Verdict

- **The gap is real.**
  - `D3d_ValidateTextureStageState` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:1905-1910`) never reads the context. It answers `dwNumPasses = 1, ddrval = DD_OK` to every state.
  - Most of what `color_path` (`:676-745`) and `stage1_combine` (`:849-874`) only approximate is answered "OK", and so are sets with three stages.
- **It matters for fewer titles than the gap list says, and for different ones.** I scanned the renderer binaries of the staged D3D titles for calls to `ValidateDevice` (section 3):
  - **Callers:** Max Payne, Halo (through D3DX technique validation), Thief II (NewDark), Thief Gold (the DX6 Dark engine) and Battlefield 1942.
  - **Not callers:** UT2003, UT2004 and Red Faction. None of them calls `IDirect3DDevice8::ValidateDevice`.
  - **No ValidateDevice in their interface, or no call found:** Shogo (`d3d.ren` uses IDirect3DDevice2) and AvP.
  - So G15 does nothing for the dark Unreal Engine 2 world; G05 fixes that.
- **Turning the honest answer on is not risk-free.**
  - Thief Gold and NewDark validate at display start, and several of those calls are FATAL: a refusal means "your video hardware is not supported".
  - Every fatal set I could decode is drawn exactly by our HAL, except one: NewDark's mode 6 (a stage-1 BLENDTEXTUREALPHA). It is validated fatally at 0x606b30 when one render flag is set.
  - Hence the rollout below: the answer is computed and logged in every build first, and only then returned behind a switch that is off by default.
- **Plan:**
  - Part A: log-only and always on. This is safe to land default-on.
  - Part B: return the honest answer behind `Diag\D3DFeat` bit 0, default OFF.
  - Part B becomes the default only after Thief II, Thief Gold, Halo, Max Payne and BF1942 have each run armed with no regression.
- **Priority P1, effort M.** No register traffic changes and no caps change.

## 2. What the runtimes do with the answer

These were read from the XP SP3 binaries, not inferred.

### D3D8 (d3d8.dll 5.3.2600.5512)

- `IDirect3DDevice8::ValidateDevice` is vtable slot 64 in both device vtables (0x6d9a82f8, 0x6d9a88c8). It points to 0x6d9d91a0, which takes the API lock and calls the DDI object's slot +0x30.
- That slot is 0x6d9e0520 or 0x6d9e0f70. It does the following, in order:
  1. builds `{dwhContext, 0, 0, 0, 0}`;
  2. runs the per-stage texture update (0x6d9dab70: a loop over the stages and their dirty bits);
  3. flushes through the DDI vtable +0x2c, so **our walker has the current TSS and texture handles before we are asked**. The "update" and "flush" labels are inferred from the code's shape; the call order is read;
  4. calls the driver;
  5. copies `dwNumPasses` to the caller.
- A return other than DDHAL_DRIVER_HANDLED becomes `E_NOTIMPL`. A negative `ddrval` is returned to the application as-is.
- The kernel-thunk wrapper (0x6d9b20f0, installed at 0x6d9b571c only when the driver reports the callback) presets `ddrval = D3DERR_DEVICELOST` and `dwNumPasses = 0`, and turns `DDERR_SURFACELOST` into DEVICELOST.
- **There is no runtime-side check of ops or stage counts. The driver's answer is the answer.**

### D3D9 (d3d9.dll 5.3.2600.5512)

- The wrapper is at 0x4fdff350, and the DDI ValidateDevice is at 0x4fe769f0 and 0x4fe79560.
- The flow is the same as D3D8. The one addition: `D3DERR_DRIVERINTERNALERROR` from the driver becomes `D3DERR_INVALIDCALL`.

### DX6 and DX7 (d3dim.dll, d3dim700.dll)

- These get the callback through ddraw/gdi32's HAL tables (GdiEntry13).
- That they flush state before calling it is **inferred**. G75's DX7 probe is the lab test for it.

## 4. The vendor reference, and what not to copy

`VH5/D6MT.C:3186-4291` `ddiValidateTextureStageState` does the following.

**What it does:**

- **Stage count.** It counts stages until the first DISABLE colour op. A stage whose colour op samples TEXTURE with **no texture handle** ends the count (`:3222-3253`).
- **Nothing counted:** 1 pass, D3D_OK (`:3255-3262`).
- **Too many stages:** more than 2 on Napalm is `D3DERR_TOOMANYOPERATIONS` (`:3322-3328`).
- **Multitexture with trilinear:** TOOMANYOPERATIONS unless `LodDither` (`:3264-3305`).
- **Per stage**, against its op tables (`:3513-3787`):
  - an unsupported op (returned, quirkily, as `UNSUPPORTEDCOLORARG`);
  - a complement the table does not allow;
  - SPECULAR as an argument (`FAIL_SPECULAR_AS_ARG`, rev 31);
  - a missing or invalid texture handle, which gives `WRONGTEXTUREFORMAT`.
- **Palette conflict:** `CONFLICTINGTEXTUREPALETTE` (`:4198-4256`).
- **Passes:** failure leaves `dwNumPasses = 0` (`:3200`); success sets 1 (`:4284`).

**Take:**

- the stage-count rule, including the unbound-texture rule;
- 0 passes on failure, 1 on success;
- specular refused;
- TOOMANYOPERATIONS above 2 stages;
- WRONGTEXTUREFORMAT for a bound texture the TMU cannot sample.

**Do not take:**

- **Its op tables.** They describe 3dfx's extended-combine implementation, not our legacy one.
- Returning `UNSUPPORTEDCOLORARG` for an unsupported op.
- The disabled DCT/WHQL multipass hacks (`:3330-3510`).
- The trilinear rule. We never draw trilinear at all, and the caps already say so (section 5.5).
- The Unreal Tournament 3-stage remap (PRS 12099). That is the Voodoo3 branch (`:5326`). See G95.

## 5. Design

### 5.1 The rule

**Answer "1 pass, D3D_OK" exactly when `compute_regs` would draw the current state the way D3D defines it. Otherwise answer the D3DERR_* code that names the first stage, channel and reason that fails, with 0 passes.**

- The judgement is on texture-stage state only. Render states such as blend factors and Z modes are governed by the caps, which already tell the truth for blending: SrcBlendCaps 0x1f33 and DestBlendCaps 0x3f do not claim SRCCOLOR as a source or DESTALPHA.
- **Stage counting, as 3dfx did:**
  - the count stops at the first stage whose COLOROP is DISABLE;
  - it also stops at a stage whose colour op samples TEXTURE (a used argument is TEXTURE, or the op is BLENDTEXTUREALPHA or BLENDTEXTUREALPHAPM) while it has no texture handle.
- Our HAL draws such a stage as if the chain ended there:
  - with no stage-0 texture, `color_path` draws diffuse;
  - with no stage-1 texture, `two` = 0 and stage 1 is dropped.
- So the rule matches what we draw. It is also the vendor's shipped behaviour, which keeps an application that validates **before binding textures** (Thief Gold or NewDark at display start, if they do) on today's answer. Phase A logs the bound flags of every validated set, so this choice is checked against the real titles.

### 5.2 The exactly-drawn set today

Argument kinds: D = DIFFUSE, C = CURRENT (equal to D at stage 0), T = TEXTURE, F = TFACTOR.

- **Unsupported argument values:**
  - S (SPECULAR, drawn as diffuse);
  - TEMP (5);
  - any selector above 4.
- **Modifiers:**
  - on a colour argument, any modifier is unsupported;
  - on an alpha argument, ALPHAREPLICATE is a no-op and is allowed, and COMPLEMENT is unsupported.
- **Unused arguments are not checked:** arg2 of SELECTARG1, arg1 of SELECTARG2, and both of DISABLE.

**Stage 0 colour (FBI CCU):**

| op | exact when | else |
|---|---|---|
| DISABLE | always (no stage counted: 1 pass) | n/a |
| SELECTARG1/2 | the used argument is plain and in {D,C,T,F} | ARG |
| MODULATE, ADD | both plain, both in {D,C,T,F}, **at least one in {D,C}** (T*F, T*T, F*F are drawn as T*D) | ARG |
| BLENDDIFFUSEALPHA | arg2 in {D,C}, arg1 in {D,C,T,F} | ARG |
| BLENDTEXTUREALPHA | (arg1 = T and arg2 in {D,C}), or both in {D,C} | ARG |
| MODULATE2X/4X, ADDSIGNED(2X), SUBTRACT, ADDSMOOTH, BLENDFACTORALPHA, BLENDTEXTUREALPHAPM, BLENDCURRENTALPHA, PREMODULATE, the four MODULATE*_ADD*, BUMPENVMAP(LUMINANCE), DOTPRODUCT3, MULTIPLYADD, LERP, out of range | never | OP |

**Stage 0 alpha (FBI CCA):**

| op | exact when | else |
|---|---|---|
| DISABLE | always. D3D calls it undefined while colour is on; we draw diffuse alpha, as 3dfx accepted. NewDark mode 4 and Thief Gold modes 2-3 use it | n/a |
| SELECTARG1/2 | the used argument is in {D,C,T,F} (ALPHAREPLICATE allowed) | ARG |
| MODULATE, ADD | both in {D,C,T,F}, at least one in {D,C} | ARG |
| everything else (including BLEND*ALPHA as alpha ops) | never | OP |

**Stage 1**, when two stages are counted:

- Each channel is first classed as PASS, REPLACE, MULT, ADD or not drawable.
- Then the order is checked against stage 0, because the FBI applies stage 0's op after the TMU.

| stage-1 class | colour form | alpha form | stage 0 must be (colour / alpha) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | SELECTARG1(C), SELECTARG2(_, C) | the same, or DISABLE | anything exact |
| REPLACE | SELECT of T | SELECT of T | SELECT(T) / SELECT(T) |
| MULT | MODULATE {T, C} | MODULATE {T, C} | SELECT(T) or MODULATE(T, D or C) for each channel |
| ADD | ADD {T, C} | ADD {T, C} | SELECT(T) / SELECT(T) |
| anything else | SELECT/MODULATE/ADD with other args | likewise | ARG |
| | any other op (MODULATE2X, BLENDTEXTUREALPHA, BLENDCURRENTALPHA, ...) | | OP |

A stage-1 class whose stage-0 condition fails is an ORDER failure, reported as OP.

**One cross-channel rule.** Stage 0's colour BLENDTEXTUREALPHA takes its factor from the TMU *alpha* output (`CC_M_ATMU`). With two stages, that output is stage 1's alpha result, not tex0's alpha. So a counted stage 1 after a stage-0 BLENDTEXTUREALPHA must be PASS in both channels; otherwise the answer is an ORDER failure, reported as UNSUPPORTEDCOLOROPERATION. BLENDDIFFUSEALPHA reads iterated alpha and is not affected. The soundness test (7.1) is what proves that no other cross-channel case exists.

**Stage 2:** if COLOROP is not DISABLE and stages 0 and 1 are both counted, the answer is TOOMANYOPERATIONS. The stage-2 texture rule applies the same way, using the recorded stage-2 handle.

**Texture format:** a counted stage that samples TEXTURE and has a handle whose surface `view_of()` refuses is WRONGTEXTUREFORMAT. Our HAL would draw it untextured.

**Error codes.** These are Win32-free constants in the new header; the HAL statically asserts they equal `d3d.h`'s values.

| reason | D3DERR_* code |
|---|---|
| more than 2 stages | `TOOMANYOPERATIONS` 0x8876081d |
| colour op not drawable, or colour order | `UNSUPPORTEDCOLOROPERATION` 0x88760819 |
| colour argument or modifier | `UNSUPPORTEDCOLORARG` 0x8876081a |
| alpha op not drawable, or alpha order | `UNSUPPORTEDALPHAOPERATION` 0x8876081b |
| alpha argument or modifier | `UNSUPPORTEDALPHAARG` 0x8876081c |
| unsamplable bound texture | `WRONGTEXTUREFORMAT` 0x88760818 |

**Check order:** stage count, then stage 0 colour, stage 0 alpha, stage 1 colour, stage 1 alpha, the two order checks, then texture formats.

### 5.3 Code

All of this is CPU-side. No register write changes.

**New `KMD/include/vcr_tssval.h`** (Win32-free, so the host test and d3dprobe compile it):

```c
typedef struct vcr_tv_stage {
    unsigned cop, carg1, carg2, aop, aarg1, aarg2;
    int bound;      /* a texture handle is set */
    int ok;         /* ... and the TMU can sample it */
} vcr_tv_stage;
typedef struct vcr_tv_in  { vcr_tv_stage s[3]; } vcr_tv_in;   /* s[2]: cop, carg1/2, bound */
typedef struct vcr_tv_out { unsigned long hr; unsigned passes, why, stage; } vcr_tv_out;
#define VCR_TV_F_NONE 0u   /* G05/G12/G13/G14 add bits: the combine features the armed HAL draws */
static vcr_tv_out vcr_tssval(const vcr_tv_in *in, unsigned feat);
/* what the HAL returns: the 2026-09 answer (DD_OK, 1 pass) unless honest */
static vcr_tv_out vcr_tssval_answer(const vcr_tv_in *in, unsigned feat, int honest);
```

**`vcrdd_d3d.c`:**

- `D3d_ValidateTextureStageState` does the following:
  - `honest = (pd->d3dfeat & VCR_D3DFEAT_VALIDATE)`;
  - with a context it fills `vcr_tv_in` from `tss`, `tss1`, the new stage-2 record, the handles, and `view_of()`;
  - it calls `vcr_tssval`, logs the result (below), and returns `vcr_tssval_answer(...)`;
  - with a bad context it returns `D3DNTHAL_CONTEXT_BAD` only when honest, and the old answer otherwise.
- The function stays integer-only (no FPU bracket) and touches no register.
- **Walker (`:1591-1611`):** for stage 2, store `COLOROP`, `COLORARG1/2` and `TEXTUREMAP` into `c->tss2[4]` **without setting `c->dirty`**. `compute_regs` never reads them, so drawing is unchanged.
  - `ctx_defaults`: stage-2 COLOROP = DISABLE.
  - Count stage-2+ records and TSS indices above 24 (COLORARG0, ALPHAARG0, RESULTARG) received per context, for the open questions.
- **Logging (Part A, every build), all at INFO:**
  - **Event 513 `vset`:** each **distinct** validated set, the first 32 per context. Two ring messages, because a message holds 95 characters:
    - the first: stage 0, stage 1 and stage 2 op/args;
    - the second: the bound/ok flags, the honest verdict (hr, why, stage), and whether it was returned.
  - **Event 513 `vsum`, at `ctx_free`:** validate calls, distinct sets, sets the honest rule refuses, refusals actually returned, the first refused set's packed ops, and the stage-2/high-TSS counters. That line survives a wrapped ring (G76).
  - Also put the drawn verdict into `log_combo`'s what-22 line (`:909`): append `v <why>`, from the same function on the drawn state. Every existing LogLevel-2 game run then also measures how often each title DRAWS an approximated set, validated or not. If G12's default-on "shadow verdict" log (its section 3.7.6) has already landed, that line is this field: log the verdict once, not twice.
  - **Event numbers are the integrator's call.** `vset` and `vsum` are working names. The sibling designs G01, G03, G05, G10, G11 and G16 each claim 513 what 24 and/or 25 (1-23 are taken). G13's optional "validate counter" (its what 26) is this item's `vsum`, so G13 need not add it. Document whatever numbers are given in `KMD/include/vcr_events.h`'s 513 text.
- **Diag plumbing, following the existing pattern:**
  - miniport `x->d3dfeat = VcrDiagGet(L"D3DFeat", 0)`, read at boot;
  - **append** `vcr_u32 d3dfeat;` after `mon_src` in `vcr_info` (`KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h`), the "appended - older tools read a shorter struct" convention, with `size` checked by the reader;
  - `VcrDd2dInit` sets `pd->d3dfeat` (0 from an older miniport);
  - event 513 what 16-style "armed" line when non-zero.
  - Bit 0 is chip-independent. The legacy combine is the same on Banshee, Voodoo3 and VSA-100, so it can be tried on the 86Box Voodoo3 bed.
  - **One word for the whole switch, not a flag bit per feature.** The sibling designs disagree here: G08 and G16 each propose `VCR_INFO_F_* 0x8000` for their own bit, while G14 points out that a dozen planned D3DFeat bits would exhaust `vcr_info.flags`. The appended word ends both the collision and the shortage. Bit 0 (this item) and hal.md's numbering stay as planned: G08 bit 1, G16 bit 4, G12 bits 6-8, G13 bit 7 provisional. Whichever item lands first adds the word, and the rest reuse it.

### 5.4 How the later combine items extend it

- G05, G12, G13 and G14 change what is drawn exactly. Each widens what the validator accepts, but only under its own armed feature and **in the same commit**. Examples:
  - G05 armed: MODULATE2X/4X at stage 0 and stage 1;
  - G12: stage order, stage-1 BLENDTEXTUREALPHA, and a stage 1 without a stage-0 texture;
  - G13: the modifiers;
  - G14: TFACTOR as a TMU argument.
- In G12's design, G05 is the bit-6 slice of G12's compiler, and G13's and G14's rules ride on it too. In that case the `feat` mask is simply the armed G12 tiers, and the widening is the compiler's EXACT verdict (next paragraph). Separate `VCR_TV_F_*` bits are needed only for an item that lands without the compiler.
- The HAL passes the feature mask it actually armed, so the answer follows what is drawn.
- The cross-check test (7.1) fails if a widened table claims a set the translation still draws wrong. Its curated list fails if a set a title needs has become exact and is still refused.
- **G08**, when it lands, maps the legacy DX5 states into stage 0. The validator must see the mapped values: G08 writes them into `c->tss`, or passes them in `vcr_tv_in`.

**With G12's combine compiler.** G12's design makes `KMD/include/vcr_combine.h` the single owner of the combine. It gives every set a verdict: EXACT, APPROX_SAT or LEGACY. LEGACY sets are drawn with today's `color_path`/`stage1_combine` registers. The validator then answers:

- **OK** when an **armed** G12 tier places the set EXACT;
- **OK** when the set is LEGACY and this item's 5.2 rules accept it. LEGACY means today's registers, and 5.2 is exactly the list of sets those registers draw right;
- **refused** otherwise, with G12's `why` mapped to the codes of 5.2;
- **APPROX_SAT is refused.** This answers G12's open question 7. D3D clamps after every stage, so a shift moved past a later multiply is wrong exactly where a game uses the overbright range. A validating title has a fallback for that; one that does not validate still gets the drawing.

**G12's tier 0 (bits 6-8 off) answers LEGACY for every set**, the right MODULATE(T,D) included. So that verdict must **never** be mapped to refusals directly: doing so would refuse everything, and a fatal NewDark or Thief Gold site would end the game. The 5.2 classification is what makes a LEGACY set answerable. It lives in `vcr_tssval.h` as long as no G12 tier is armed. If G12's compiler grows a legacy-exact verdict of its own, the 5.2 named cases (7.1 item 2) stay as its test. G13's modifier rules arrive through G12's verdict (G13 section 4.3); G15 keeps the plumbing.

### 5.5 Deliberately not judged in this item

- **Filters.** MIPFILTER LINEAR is drawn as nearest-mip. TextureFilterCaps already omits MIPFLINEAR. 3dfx's "multitexture + trilinear" rule describes an implementation we do not have. Refusing it could fail NewDark's fatal sites, whose filter state we cannot see. G26 adds its rule when it adds trilinear. Max Payne already "reduces trilinear to bilinear" on its own when told to.
- **MIRROR/BORDER addressing, and PROJECTED texture coordinates.** No D3DERR code covers them, the caps already deny them, and G33, G86 and G92 own them.
- **DX8-only RESULTARG and ARG0.** They are counted (5.3), not judged, until we know a DX7 DDI ever receives them.
- **Caps are unchanged.** TextureOpCaps lists only ops we draw exactly in at least one form; validation covers the rest.
- **The HAL never multipasses.** An application that receives a refusal splits the work itself.

## 6. Gating and rollout

**Part A: default-on, safe to land.**

- What it is: the stage-2 record (no dirty flag, never drawn), the verdict computation, the 513 `vset`/`vsum` events, and the `log_combo` verdict field.
- What it leaves unchanged:
  - the returned answer stays (DD_OK, 1);
  - `compute_regs` and every register write are untouched;
  - there is no hardware access in the callback.
- Logging is bounded by the 32-set de-duplication per context, plus one summary line.
- Pinned by Python source tests: the answer is unchanged, the stage-2 store does not set `dirty`, and the callback has no `wr()`/`VcrDd3d*` call.

**Part B: `Diag\D3DFeat` bit 0, default OFF.**

- What it does: returns the verdict.
- **It is not safe to land default-on.**
  - It changes code paths inside games: NewDark's and Thief Gold's fatal display-start validation, Max Payne's material passes, and Halo's technique choice.
  - Max Payne is the title with a freeze on record (G04).
- **Steps, one Diag change per clean boot:**
  1. Run Part A's log-only pass on every caller title (section 8, step 2).
  2. Arm bit 0 in one supervised clean boot, and run the same titles.
  3. Make bit 0 the default only when all of these hold:
     - d3dprobe `validate` passes armed at 16 and 32 bpp;
     - Thief II and Thief Gold reach a mission with no "not supported" dialog;
     - Halo's timedemo frames show no missing effect;
     - Max Payne has had a supervised armed run;
     - BF1942 gets in-game.
- **The NewDark contingency.** If Phase A shows NewDark validating mode 6 at 0x606b30 with textures bound (a refusal would be fatal), the options are, in order of preference:
  - (a) G12 makes stage-1 BLENDTEXTUREALPHA and the stage order exact, so the honest answer is OK;
  - (b) until then, keep bit 0 off by default. A box that arms it runs NewDark with `d3d_disp_forcevalid 1` in `cam_ext.cfg`. The launcher's per-box `newdark_display_swap` (`scripts/fleet/stage-fleetres.py`) already checks vcrmp's D3D32 and D3DBigTex values; it would also write the override, and only where vcrmp runs with D3DFeat bit 0 armed. Per the staged-game rule, the fix goes in the library and is redeployed.
  - Thief Gold has no such override. A fatal refusal there can be fixed only in the HAL (G12), or by keeping bit 0 off.
- **Never** weaken the rule for one title. A loosened answer lies to every other title that has a fallback.

## 7. Tests

### 7.1 Native test: `tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_tssval.c`

It compiles `KMD/include/vcr_tssval.h`, the extracted legacy translation (item 5), a D3D reference and a register-level model. It needs no Win32 and is picked up by `run_all.sh`'s `test_*.c` glob.

The reference and the model are the ones G12's design names:

- the reference: `KMD/include/vcr_texop_ref.h`, D3D's op formulas in 8-bit integers, clamped after each stage;
- the model: `tests/native/vcr_cmb_model.h`.

G13 already noted that the model is shared. Whichever of G12, G13, G15 or G77 lands first creates them, and the others extend them. G15 needs only the legacy units of the model: the FBI CCU/CCA and the TMU TC/TCA.

1. **`old_answer_and_new_answer`**, the fix and the bug both asserted:
   - `vcr_tssval_answer(stage-0 MODULATE2X(T,D), 0, honest=0)` equals `{0 /*DD_OK*/, 1}`. That is the 2026-09 answer, still returned with the bit off.
   - `honest=1` gives `{0x88760819, 0}`.
   - The same pair is asserted for UT2003's logged stage-1 MODULATE2X (`evidence/d3dtune_1014/sweep_1108/UT2003_combos_unaligned.txt`, combine 1/5/6) and for a 3-stage set (`{0, 1}` versus `{0x8876081d, 0}`).
2. **`named_cases`**, about 45 sets with exact expected (hr, passes, why):
   - every row of 5.2;
   - NewDark modes 0-7 and Thief Gold modes 0-4 (verdicts as in section 3);
   - d3dprobe's `tex2add` (stage 0 MODULATE(T,D) + stage 1 ADD(T,C)). It is **refused**: it passes in d3dprobe only because the diffuse is white;
   - `tex2mod` is OK;
   - MODULATE(T,TFACTOR) is ARG;
   - ALPHAREPLICATE: refused on a colour argument, allowed on an alpha argument;
   - SPECULAR, COMPLEMENT;
   - stage 0 DISABLE with a MODULATE2X stage 1 is OK;
   - the unbound-texture rule: a stage-0 MODULATE2X with no handle gives 1 pass, the vendor rule, documented as such.
3. **`soundness`**, the core property: **a verdict of OK implies the hardware draws what D3D defines.**
   - The test carries a software model of the legacy combine units as our register fields program them:
     - FBI `fbzColorPath`: rgb/alpha select, local = iterated, zero_other, sub_clocal, mselect, reverse, add_clocal/alocal, `CP_TEXTURE` gating;
     - TMU `textureMode` TC/TCA, bits 12-29 (`KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h`);
     - 8-bit arithmetic;
     - undefined inputs (an unused TMU's "other", texture data with `CP_TEXTURE` clear) become random garbage, so any dependence on them is caught.
   - Coverage:
     - every stage-0 colour and alpha op 1-26, crossed with argument values {0-5} x {0, COMPLEMENT, ALPHAREPLICATE};
     - every stage-1 op against a set of stage-0 forms;
     - 2^20 random two- and three-stage sets (fixed seed) with bound/ok flags;
     - 64 random input vectors each: texels, diffuse, specular, TFACTOR, in [16,239].
   - Assertion: verdict OK implies every vector is within ±3 of the `vcr_texop_ref.h` result for every channel.
   - Refused-but-exact cases are only counted, except the curated list in item 4.
4. **`completeness_curated`**: sets the titles use that are exact must be accepted:
   - NewDark 0-4 and 7, Thief Gold 0-3;
   - d3dprobe's `tex`, `modulate`, `tex2mod`;
   - UT2003 combine 7, SELECTARG1(TFACTOR).
   - G05 and G12 add their feature-gated entries here.
5. **`translation_unchanged`**. The model needs `color_path`, `stage1_combine` and the `tex`/`two` choice of `compute_regs` in a host-compilable form.
   - That is G77's work. If G77 has not landed, this item does the minimal version: a **pure move** of those functions into `KMD/include/vcr_cmb_legacy.h`, taking a `tss` array and VCR_ constants (statically asserted equal to the D3D ones).
   - `vcr_combine.h` is G12's compiler, so the legacy mapping gets its own name. G12's LEGACY branch can include it, which gives G12 its "single source of the legacy mapping".
   - This test holds a verbatim copy of the 2026-11-08 functions (shimmed). It asserts equal register output over the whole domain, colour and alpha halves separately, about 60k cases.
   - So `feat = 0` stays write-for-write the proven translation, both now and after every later item.

### 7.2 Python source tests in `tests/python/test_vcr_kmd_d3d.py`

- `D3DFeat` is a default-off switch: `VcrDiagGet(L"D3DFeat", 0)`, the appended `vcr_info.d3dfeat`, and `pd->d3dfeat` read only from it (the `test_d3d32_is_a_default_off_switch` pattern).
- The callback returns `vcr_tssval_answer(..., honest)` with honest = `d3dfeat & VCR_D3DFEAT_VALIDATE`. It has no `wr(`, `VcrDd3d`, `EngSaveFloatingPointState` or `VcrDd2dSync`.
- The walker stores stage-2 COLOROP, args and TEXTUREMAP without `c->dirty = 1`. `ctx_defaults` sets stage-2 COLOROP = DISABLE.

### 7.3 d3dprobe test: `validate`

Explicit at first (`--tests validate`), and in the default list once bit 0 is the default. It goes in `KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`. It needs a device, so it lives in `render` mode: `d3dprobe caps` creates no device and cannot call `ValidateDevice`. Its stage-0 MODULATE2X cell is the "ValidateDevice of MODULATE2X" question runtime-27 asked.

**What it draws:**

- About 41 cells:
  - 25 single-stage: every row of 5.2, Max Payne's ADDSIGNED and complement, SPECULAR;
  - 15 two-stage: NewDark modes 4-7, tex2mod, tex2add with grey diffuse, UT2003's stage-1 MODULATE2X and BLENDCURRENTALPHA, an untextured stage 0 with a textured stage 1, a TFACTOR tint stage 1, the alpha order cases;
  - 1 three-stage.
- Two 64x64 **A4R4G4B4** solid textures (offered on every path, alpha exact in 4 bits): T0 = (a 66, r 88, g 44, b cc) and T1 = (bb, 33, aa, 55). Diffuse = 0x99c08040, TFACTOR = 0x7020a0e0.
- For each cell:
  1. set the TSS and textures;
  2. call `ValidateDevice(&n)` and record hr and n;
  3. draw a full quad (`quad_t2`) twice: blend off, then SRCALPHA/ZERO over black, to read alpha as colour;
  4. lock the back buffer as today.
- The expected answer comes from the same `vcr_tssval.h` compiled into the probe.
  - `honest` and `feat` are read from `HKLM\...\Services\vcrmp\Diag\D3DFeat` and recorded in the RESULT, to be cross-checked against the ring's "armed" event.
  - The expected pixels come from `vcr_texop_ref.h`.
  - Expectations apply only when `GetAdapterIdentifier` names `vcrdd.dll`. On another driver (for example the vendor's on `.143`) the cell answers are reported for reference.

**Pass criterion:**

- **Answers:**
  - armed: every cell's (hr, n) equals `vcr_tssval`'s;
  - not armed: every cell is (D3D_OK, 1). That pins the old answer on silicon.
- **Pixels:**
  - armed: every **accepted** cell's centre pixel, blended and unblended, must be within ±10 per channel at 16 bpp (±5 at 32 bpp) of the reference;
  - refused cells are reported as "approx: got/ref", with no verdict. In an unarmed run they show the gap on silicon (MODULATE2X drawn as MODULATE, and so on).
- **The three-stage cell.** If the runtime never forwards stage 2, the walker's stage-2 counter (in `vsum`) stays 0 and the cell answers OK. That is recorded as a runtime fact (open question 1), not as a HAL failure.
- **RESULT:** `"validate":{"armed","feat","cells","answer_ok","answer_bad","render_ok","render_bad","approx"}`.

## 9. Risks

- **Proven paths.** None changes:
  - the default 16 bpp target sequence, `compute_regs` and every register write are untouched;
  - the extracted translation is pinned equal to the 2026-11-08 code (7.1 item 5);
  - the callback has no hardware access, so there is no Glide/OpenGL coexistence, SLI or flip interaction;
  - with bit 0 off, the answer is byte-for-byte the old one.
- **Behaviour (bit 0 on):**
  - NewDark and Thief Gold can refuse to start their display (section 3 and the contingency);
  - Max Payne takes multipass paths. It is the G04 freeze title, and multipass also costs triangles on a HAL already at about 9 fps in UT2003 (G46);
  - Halo may lose an effect whose every technique is refused;
  - BF1942's reaction is unknown.
- **Measurement.**
  - The ring wraps (G76); the `vsum` summary at context destroy mitigates this.
  - DX7-interface titles that record state blocks have their TSS applied at record time (G40), so their validated state can be wrong. D3D8/9 never send STATESET (runtime report), so they are unaffected.
  - `c->pd` is read in the callback (feature word, `view_of`): the same stale-PDEV exposure as DP2 (G43). Nothing new.
- **Logging cost:** at most 64 events plus 1 per context.

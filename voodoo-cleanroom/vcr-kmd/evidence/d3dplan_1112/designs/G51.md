## 0. Verdict

1. **The gap is real and fixable, and it is the largest fill-rate item in the stack.** Our Glide measured
   330.2 Mpix/s on one chip and 1124.6 Mpix/s on four at 1024x768 (`EV/glidelab/vcrkmd-paced-2.jsonl:1,4`).
   D3D uses one chip: every 3D write goes to chip 0's register window with snooping off
   (`KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c:24-27`, `KMD/include/vcr_3dseq.h:97-99`), and SLI is reached only through Glide's
   `SLI_AA_REQUEST` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_escape.c:221-243`).
2. **It buys nothing today, and must not be started before the transport is fixed.** The first D3D game
   numbers on our HAL are CPU/transport-bound, not fill-bound. UT2003 flyby at 640x480x16 ran at 9.41 fps,
   with **116.7 ms of a 123 ms frame inside DrawPrimitive**. UT2004 at 1280x960 shows "4 cur / 6 avg FPS"
   (`EV/d3dplan_1112/onbox/session_log.md`, 10:23 and 10:40 entries). SLI multiplies fill only. Every chip
   still sets up every triangle, so setup-bound and CPU-bound frames gain nothing. The order is: measurement
   (G46, G78), transport (G47 CMDFIFO, G48, G49), 2PPC (G50), hardware swap (G54), then this. That is the
   plan's P9: last, and supervised.
3. **The shape is the proven Glide cfg-5 shape, nothing else.** This means 4 chips, SLI on, AA off, analog,
   and Glide's per-mode band height, programmed by the kernel's existing `vcr_sli_set_ex(…, 0)`. That exact
   sequence ran Quake II in 4-chip SLI on `.124` from 640x480 to 1600x1200 at 16 and 32 bpp
   (`EV/glide_q2_4chip_cfg5_vcrkmd_build1.csv`). Its config space equals AmigaMerlin's
   (`KMD/README.md`, "FOUR CHIPS ON OUR KERNEL DRIVER"). No AA bit can be set on this path, by construction.
4. **Our memory layout forces one design choice: Direct3D renders the fullscreen flip chain into private,
   packed, tiled SLI buffers and shows them through the video overlay.** Two facts drive it:
   - Our desktop and DirectDraw heap are linear (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_hw.c:607-611`).
   - DirectDraw rotates the chain's memory on every flip, so the GDI desktop block (`fpPrimary`) becomes a
     render target after the first flip.

   Every other surface stays linear. memBase1 snooping copies it into every chip.
   - Locks and blits of chain surfaces **resolve** through the SLI tiled aperture. The aperture moves to the
     upper half of memBase1 with Napalm's `lfbMemoryTileCompare` (SPEC L5756-5782), so the linear desktop and
     heap keep their addresses.
   - A session that locks often drops back to one chip.
   - This is the vendor's design (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:2382-2449`, `VH5/DDMEMMGR.C:111-160` with
     `ENABLE_NAPALM_SLI_EXTRA_LINEAR_HEAP = 1`, `VH5/DRIVER.H:53`), adapted to a driver whose desktop is linear.
5. **The fixes the gap text lists are settled by vendor code, not new invention:**
   - **SLI off for every non-chain target.** `sliCtrl = 0` before rendering to it (`VH5/D6DP2.C:6027-6081`),
     which makes render-to-texture correct.
   - **Full-target clears with SLI off over the packed rows.** This runs on all four chips in parallel
     (`VH5/D7DP2.C:883-940`) and sidesteps the FASTFILL single-chip-speed bug (SPEC L2705-2709). No 2D blits
     are needed.
   - **Room checks over every chip** (`VH5/CFIFO.C:266-391`).
   - **SLI swaps through `leftOverlayBuf` + `swapbufferCMD`** (`VH5/DDFLIP.C:328-446`).
6. **One hardware fact is contradicted in the record, and it decides the transport (experiment E1, §6).**
   SPEC says *"memory writes are not snooped for memBaseAddr0"* (L5426). The vendor relies on the opposite:
   - It re-inits the CMDFIFO on the master because *"slaves are snooping membase0"* (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:3161`).
   - It writes `leftOverlayBuf`, `swapbufferCMD` and `swapBufferPend` straight to chip 0 in SLI
     (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:2705-2712`, `VH5/DDFLIP.C:77`).
   - So does our kernel's comment (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_sli.c:1146-1148`).

   If memBase0 writes are snooped, today's PIO path broadcasts by itself and needs only all-chip room
   checks. If not, SLI waits for the CMDFIFO (G47), which is snooped through memBase1 in any case.
7. **Gating.** It lands **default-OFF** behind `Diag\D3DSli`:
   - bit 0: the single-chip "SLI-shaped" stage (private tiled chain, overlay scanout, resolve);
   - bit 1: four chips;
   - bit 2: fast full-target clears.

   There is a flushed `Diag\D3DSliLive` marker that disarms the switch on the next boot after a freeze.
   **It is not safe to land default-on.** It needs a supervised silicon campaign (§7) and measured gains
   (§2). **Priority P2** (performance with a measurable gain, once the transport lets games be fill-bound).
   **Effort XL.**
8. **Interim, no driver change:** stage the fill-heavy 32 bpp titles at 1024x768 until this lands (the
   operator's choice, perf §4.1).

### 1.2 3dfx's D3D on the 4-chip board (`VH5`, `VMP`)

- **When.** `Compute_SLIAA_Config` runs at creation of an exclusive, fullscreen primary flip chain or Z
  (`VH5/DDSURF.C:413-462`). Its rules (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:3572-3901`):
  - default config 5 on 4 chips (4-way SLI, AA off, analog);
  - no SLI for single-buffered chains (`:3815-3818`);
  - no SLI with scanline doubling (`SST_HALF_MODE`, `:3680-3685`).
- **Promotion order.** `Enter_3DApplication` (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:2382-2449`):
  1. video processor off;
  2. `Promote_DeviceToSLIAA` (`:2952-3191`): CMDFIFO off, the miniport IOCTL, CMDFIFO re-init (*"slaves are
     snooping membase0"*), then *"reload dac so slaves will snoop it"*;
  3. `Promote_PrimaryToOverlay` (`:2522-2727`): desktop off, overlay on, in SLI only the point or 4x4
     overlay filter, `leftOverlayBuf` + `swapbufferCMD`;
  4. the D3D context is dirtied.

  `Exit_3DApplication` undoes it (`:2464-2500`).
- **Band height.** 16 lines below 768, 32 from 768 up, registry override 2..128 (`:3015-3025`). The packed
  height is `dwMaxHeight` (`:3029-3034`).
- **Per render target** (`VH5/D6DP2.C:6027-6081`):
  - a tiled (chain) target: for each chip, `chipMask = BIT(i)`, then `sliCtrl = base | i << log2band`, then
    `chipMask` restored;
  - **any other target: `sliCtrl = 0`**, so render-to-texture is rendered whole by every chip.
- **Full-screen clear in SLI** (`VH5/D7DP2.C:883-940`): `sliCtrl = 0`, clip bottom = `dwMaxHeight` (the
  packed rows), `fastfillCMD`, then per-chip `sliCtrl` again. Each chip fills its own packed buffer in
  parallel. Partial clears use plain fastfill under SLI.
- **Flip:**
  - swaps are queued in the CMDFIFO as `leftOverlayBuf` + `swapbufferCMD`;
  - they are throttled by status[30:28] to `WaitOnVsync + 1`, capped at 6
    (`MAXPENDINGBUFFERS_TOAVOIDHANG`, `VH5/DDFLIP.C:328-446`);
  - the pending count is read from the **master only** (`:77-78`).
- **Transport:**
  - the CMDFIFO in memBase1 (snooped);
  - room = the minimum over chips of each chip's `cmdFifo0.readPtrL` distance (`VH5/CFIFO.C:266-391`);
  - per-chip register mappings come from the miniport (`GetSLIAAInfo`, `VH5/DDFXNT.C:2870-2895`).
- **Memory:**
  - back buffer and Z sit at precomputed SLI places, with LFB pointers from `SLI_HwPtrToLfbPtr`
    (`VH5/DDMEMMGR.C:111-160`, `VH5/DDFXNT95.H:318-421`);
  - textures go to an "SLI extra linear heap", made possible by the tile-compare split
    (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:3109-3140`).
- **Miniport:**
  - `EnableSLIAA` refuses a request for fewer chips than the board has (*"Asking a 4-chip board for 2 chips
    ... wedged the machine hard"*);
  - it copies `lfbMemoryConfig`, `vidDesktopStartAddr`, `vidProcCfg`, `vidOverlayDudxOffsetSrcWidth` and
    `vidDesktopOverlayStride` from master to slaves (`VMP/SLIAA.C:3481-3570`).
- **Per-chip LOD bias** (0/0.25/0.5/0.75) applies only to multitexture *with* LOD-dither trilinear
  (`VH5/D6MT.C:4300-4348`, `VH5/D6DP2.C:6476-6500`). We have no trilinear (G26), so it is not needed now.

### 1.3 Our Glide on our kernel (proven on `.124`)

- **Band height.** 32 lines from 768 up, else 16, then clamped to divide `height / chips`
  (`GL5/glide3/src/gsst.c:1684-1689, 2210-2236`). On the 6000 that gives:

  | mode | band (lines) |
  |---|---|
  | 640x480 | 8 |
  | 800x600 | 2 |
  | 1024x768 | 32 |
  | 1280x960 | 16 |
  | 1600x1200 | 4 |

  All of these ran Quake II in 4-chip SLI (`EV/glide_q2_4chip_cfg5_vcrkmd_build1.csv`). The packed height
  is then exactly `height / 4`.
- **Buffers.** Glide renders into tiled buffers shown by the overlay, with the desktop off
  (`GL5/minihwc/minihwc.c:4980-5060`). It programs the master's video registers **before** its SLI request.
  The kernel then copies them to the slaves (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_sli.c:586-619`).
- **LFB stride.** Lines are 8 KB apart (`HWC_RAW_LFB_STRIDE`, `minihwc.c:1046-1047`).
  `lfbMemoryConfig` = tile begin at its first colour buffer, then TileCompare = 0 (`minihwc.c:5104-5116`).
- **Per-chip state** goes through its CMDFIFO with `chipMask` per chip (`gsst.c:4068-4126`).
- **Proof.** SLI band read-back was correct per chip (`EV/sli_shots/`, glidelab `bands`).

### 1.4 Our kernel today

- `vcr_sli_set_ex(io, &req, 0)` is the dos_mode.c port that ran cfg 5 (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_sli.c:1106-1168`).
  It sets snooping, swap sync, `cfgSliLfbCtrl` with CPU/dispatch/read enables, the video mux, and per-chip
  `sliCtrl`.
- **`VcrSliOff` runs at every mode set and every display reset** (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_hw.c:674`,
  `KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:693`).
- Slaves are placed and mapped at boot (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_multi.c:148-217`).
- **It does not copy `lfbMemoryConfig` to the slaves** (the copy lists are at `vcrmp_sli.c:422-427` and
  `586-619`).
- The DirectDraw process view is capped at one chip's memory (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_dd.c:36`).

## 3. The design

### 3.1 Stages

| stage | switch | what it adds | proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| S0 | none (lab) | experiments E1-E4 (§6) | the transport choice (E1); title behaviour and fill-boundness |
| S1 | `D3DSli` bit 0 | private **tiled** chain buffers + Z, **overlay** scanout, `swapbufferCMD` flips, resolve-on-Lock through the upper-half tiled aperture, promotion/demotion lifecycle, **on one chip** (n = 1, no packing) | everything except multi-chip, with the existing d3dprobe readback (which Locks, so it exercises resolve) |
| S2 | `D3DSli` bits 0+1 | the kernel cfg-5 enable, per-target `sliCtrl`, all-chip room/idle/swap checks, texture replication, packed buffers (n = 4) | 4-chip correctness (`slibands` etc.) and the fill gain |
| S2+ | bit 2 | full-target clears with SLI off over the packed rows (the vendor trick) | clear speed; without it, clears are plain FASTFILL under SLI (correct, single-chip speed) |
| S3 | `D3DSliBand` (log2 override 1..6) | band tuning | load balance |

### 3.2 Transport

All D3D register traffic must reach every chip, and every chip's FIFO must have room.

- **T-A: PIO, if E1 shows memBase0 writes are snooped.**
  - The existing `w3()` writes to chip 0's window go to all four chips.
  - `VcrDdRoom` and `VcrDd2dSync` become all-chip:
    - read the status of every live chip through the slave register mappings (new `IOCTL_VCR_SLI_REGS`
      returns the miniport's system-space VAs, `x->chip[c].regs`);
    - room = min(`status[4:0]`);
    - idle = every chip not busy with a full FIFO;
    - bounded by the same `SPIN_CAP`.
  - Room is credit-based (read all four once, spend the minimum), as G48 designs for one chip.
    Otherwise 4 status reads per vertex would multiply today's main cost.
  - The wait states snooping needs (§1.1) slow every PIO write (inferred: by ~1 PCI clock each).
- **T-B: the CMDFIFO (G47).** This is the preferred and only safe route if E1 shows no snooping.
  - One cmdFifo0 in memBase1 (snooped). Its base/size are programmed on every chip: explicitly per chip, or
    by snooped init writes if E1 shows those.
  - Room = min over chips of each chip's readPtr distance (the `VH5/CFIFO.C:266-391` pattern).
  - Per-chip registers go in packet 1 behind `chipMask`.
- **Either way:**
  - The texture-port write-back of `VcrDd3dTexFlush` (memBase0 + 0x600000, `KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c:258-268`)
    and its 2D NOPs are memBase0 PIO. They broadcast only under T-A.
  - Under T-B they go through the FIFO (packet 5 / packet 2), or the write-back is skipped while live. The
    `texBaseAddr` invert/rewrite flush is enough for silicon (`vcrdd_3d.c:240-252` comment).

### 3.3 Memory layout (Approach R: private SLI buffers keyed by chain block)

**Why private buffers.** DirectDraw owns the chain's memory blocks:
- `B0` = the GDI desktop at `fpPrimary`, linear, `lDisplayPitch`;
- `B1..Bk` = back buffers that dxg allocated from our linear heap.

dxg rotates them between surface objects at every flip. The driver is not even told when the primary is
created on NT unless it implements `GUID_NTPrivateDriverCaps` (DDK `DdCreateSurface` remarks; our HAL does
not, `KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c:633-643`). The vendor could make every block an SLI buffer only because its
desktop is tiled whenever DirectX is active (`ddPrimaryInTile`). Ours is linear. So:

- At promotion the HAL allocates, from DirectDraw's own heap (`HeapVidMemAllocAligned` on `pd->pvmList`, as
  `mipchain_ext` already does, `KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:367-471`), **one private tiled buffer `P(B)` per
  chain block, plus `P(Z)`**:
  - packed height `Hp` (§3.4);
  - `tiles = ceil(width x Bpp / 128)`;
  - colour buffers on even pages, aux on odd (Glide's rule, `GL5/minihwc/minihwc.c:2479-2505`);
  - the same address on every chip, since all allocations are replicated.
- Rendering to a chain surface whose `fpVidMem == B` goes to `P(B)`. Flip to `B` shows `P(B)`. DirectDraw's
  blocks keep their linear memory and are touched only by resolves.
- **The tiled aperture lives in the upper half of memBase1** (memBase1 decodes 2 x memory,
  `KMD/include/vcr_regs.h:207-215`):

  | register | value |
  |---|---|
  | TileCompare | `fb_per_chip >> 12` with bit 15 set: CPU addresses below 32 MB stay **linear**, so GDI, the heap, textures and `pvRamBase` are unchanged |
  | TileCtrl | translation base `T`, 8 KB aperture stride (Glide's, `SST_RAW_LFB_ADDR_STRIDE_8K`), tiles-in-X = `tiles` |

  - Following the vendor's `SLI_HwPtrToLfbPtr` (`VH5/DDFXNT95.H:375-421`), a buffer at hw `P` appears at
    LFB `T + ((P - T) / (tiles x 4096)) x (32 x 8192 x n)`.
  - `T = P0 - m x tiles x 4096` is chosen as the smallest m that puts every chain buffer's aperture range
    inside `[fb_per_chip, 2 x fb_per_chip)`.
  - Z goes last and is never CPU-accessed, so it is exempt.
  - Example, 1280x960x32 with n = 4 (`k = 6.4`): with `P0` = 15 MB, m = 21 and `T` = 11.72 MB. The chain's
    aperture spans 32.7-56.7 MB.
  - No feasible m means no promotion (reason `APERTURE`).
- Pure function `vcr_d3dsli_layout()` (new `KMD/include/vcr_d3dsli.h`, Win32-free, host-tested):
  - inputs: region base, width, height, Bpp, Z Bpp, number of chain blocks, n, band;
  - outputs: per-buffer hw/tiles/tile rows/bytes/aperture LFB, plus TileCtrl and TileCompare words.

### 3.4 Band height and the `sliCtrl` words

- **Band rule.** `vcr_d3dsli_band_log2(h, n)` is **Glide's rule**: `min(h >= 768 ? 5 : 4, v2(h / n))`, where
  v2 is the 2-adic valuation. Refuse if the result is < 1 (dithering, SPEC L6244) or the masks reach 256
  (SPEC L3426). Reason: every value it yields at our modes ran on silicon in Glide cfg 5. A D3D capture is
  then register-comparable with a Glide golden at the same mode (§7). The vendor's 16/32 rule is an S3
  candidate.
- **Packed rows.** `Hp = ceil(h / (n x band)) x band`, which equals h / n under this rule:

  | mode | band | Hp | tile rows |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 640x480 | 8 | 120 | 4 |
  | 800x600 | 2 | 150 | 5 |
  | 1024x768 | 32 | 192 | 6 |
  | 1280x960 | 16 | 240 | 8 |
  | 1600x1200 | 4 | 300 | 10 |

- **`sliCtrl` per chip** = the kernel's `vcr_sli_slictrl(4, 1 << b, 0, 0, c)` (`vcrmp_sli.c:1082-1104`):

  | band | chip 0 | chip 1 | chip 2 | chip 3 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 16 | 0x060F0030 | 0x060F1030 | 0x060F2030 | 0x060F3030 |
  | 32 | 0x061F0060 | 0x061F2060 | 0x061F4060 | 0x061F6060 |

- **One source.** The band the HAL sends in its request (`dwsli_nlines = 1 << b`) is the band it uses in
  every `sliCtrl` it writes. The kernel programs `cfgSliLfbCtrl` and `cfgVideoCtrl1` from the same request,
  so the 3D, LFB and video bands always agree. A test pins this.

### 3.5 Promotion (`KMD/display/vcrdd_sli.c`, new; called from `D3d_ContextCreate`)

**Eligibility.** All of these must hold (`vcr_d3dsli_eligible()`, each failure a named reason, logged once):
- `D3DSli` bit 0, a VSA-100;
- for bit 1: `glide_chips == 4` (no SLI if `Diag\Sli = 0`) and **the request covers every chip** (the
  vendor's V56K-CHIPCOHERENCE finding);
- DirectDraw exclusive (`pd->dd_exclusive`);
- the context's target lies in a primary flip chain of at least 2 buffers;
- target and Z at the screen size;
- 16 or 32 bpp;
- no `SST_HALF_MODE` (doublescan) in the mode;
- `exclusive_pid == 0` (no Glide owner);
- the engine alive (`g2d_ok`; G02);
- no stale CMDFIFO on **any** chip (G01, extended to slaves);
- band OK; layout feasible; the heap allocation succeeds.

**Chain.** The chain blocks come from walking the target's attach lists, the same walk `name_surface` uses
(`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:1921-1949`). `B0` must equal the desktop offset.

**Order.** It follows Glide's proven order (§1.3). Each step is a flight-recorder event and a phase:
1. `VcrDd2dSync` (all chips).
2. Allocate the region. Compute the layout. Save the old `vidProcCfg` and `vidDesktopOverlayStride`.
3. **Master video registers** (`vcr_d3dsli_overlay_regs()`, values taken from Glide's cfg-5 golden for the
   mode):
   - `vidProcCfg`: desktop off, cursor off, overlay on, overlay tiled, `RGB565D` at 16 bpp or `RGB32U` at
     32 bpp; filter 4x4 at 16 bpp, point at 32 bpp, as `GL5/minihwc/minihwc.c:4977-5028`;
   - `vidOverlayStartCoords = 0`;
   - `vidOverlayEndScreenCoord = ((h-1) << 12) | (w-1)`;
   - `vidOverlayDudx`/`Dvdy` = 0;
   - `vidOverlayDudxOffsetSrcWidth = (w x Bpp) << 19`;
   - `vidDesktopOverlayStride = (tiles << 16) | tiles`;
   - `vidMaxRGBDelta = 0x100810`;
   - `leftOverlayBuf = P(B_displayed)` plus an immediate `swapbufferCMD 0`.

   The master's video timing is **not** touched, so this is no mode switch for the CRT.
4. **S2 only:** `IOCTL_VCR_D3DSLI` enable (§3.10). The kernel:
   - runs `vcr_sli_set_ex()` with the cfg-5 request: `{chips = glide_chips, sliEn 1, aaEn 0, sampleHigh 0,
     analog 1, nlines = 1 << b, swap 1, TotalMemory = fb_per_chip, TileMark = TileCmpMark = T, Bpp}`. It is
     the same function and flags 0, so the same writes as Glide's cfg 5. It copies the overlay registers to
     the slaves (`copy_video`).
   - then, **per chip explicitly**: saves both `lfbMemoryConfig` halves, writes TileCtrl/TileCompare, and
     copies the master's CLUT (the vendor's *"reload dac so slaves will snoop it"*, done without relying on
     snoop).
   - sets the flushed `Diag\D3DSliLive = 1`.
5. **S1 only:** the master's TileCtrl/TileCompare are written by the same IOCTL with n = 1.
6. Clear every `P` (§3.7, SLI off over the packed rows), then write the per-chip `sliCtrl` (§3.6).
7. `pd->sli.live = 1`; `pd->sli.gen++` (texture replication generation); `c->dirty = 1`.

### 3.6 Rendering (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c`, `vcrdd_3d.c`, `KMD/include/vcr_3dseq.h`)

**Target.**
- `target_of()` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1047-1090`): when `pd->sli.live` and `c->rt`'s `fpVidMem` is a chain block,
  the target becomes `P(B)` with `tiled = 1`, `rt_tiles`, `z = P(Z)`, `sli_target = 1`.
- A chain target with a Z that is not the chain's Z demotes (reason `FOREIGN_Z`).
- Any other target is linear as today, with `sli_target = 0`.

**Target sequence.** `vcr_3d_target_seq()` gains a tiled form:
- `colBufferStride = 0x8000 | tiles`, `auxBufferStride = 0x8000 | ztiles`;
- the clip stays the logical w x h, since the hardware packs after the clip test (inferred from the
  vendor's clip use, `VH5/D7DP2.C:883-940`: the logical bottom under SLI, the packed rows only with SLI off).

New `vcr_d3dsli_target_prefix()`, emitted by `VcrDd3dTarget` before the buffer writes, **only while live**:
- SLI target (S2):

  ```
  chipMask=1  sliCtrl=s0  chipMask=2  sliCtrl=s1
  chipMask=4  sliCtrl=s2  chipMask=8  sliCtrl=s3
  chipMask=0xFFFFFFFF
  ```

  These are 9 writes (`VH5/D6DP2.C:6045-6070`).
- non-chain target: `sliCtrl = 0` (1 write; `VH5/D6DP2.C:6074-6080`). Every chip renders the whole
  surface, so render-to-texture, offscreen targets and their Z stay replicated and complete.
- not live: no prefix. **The proven sequence is unchanged, write for write** (pinned, §5.1).

**G03 order.** G03's baseline (`chipMask = ALL`, `sliCtrl = 0`, ...) runs first in `prepare()` and
`clear_rects()` (G03 §2.2), and the prefix comes after it in `VcrDd3dTarget`. So the last `sliCtrl` each
chip sees before a triangle is the right one. This is the resolution G03 §2.8 asks of G51; a native test
asserts the concatenation.

**Textures.** Each texture carries the SLI generation it was last made coherent in.
- At bind (`prepare()`, next to `flush_written`, `vcrdd_d3d.c:1322-1335`), a texture whose generation is
  older than `pd->sli.gen` is **replicated**:
  1. read every dword of its chain through `pvRamBase`; the lower half is linear and the master answers;
  2. write it back, which the memBase1 snoop copies into every chip;
  3. the `texBaseAddr` flush.
- That covers textures created before promotion, and memory left by a Glide session.
- Writes during the session are replicated by the snoop and stamped current by `VcrDdD3dTexWritten`
  (Lock/Unlock, TEXBLT).
- Render-to-texture under `sliCtrl = 0` is written by every chip. Its TMU flush is G37's, broadcast.

**Y origin.** `fbzMode` bit 17 (Y origin) is never set by the HAL (D3D is top-left). Pinned, so the SPEC
L6241 compare-mask flip never becomes necessary.

### 3.7 Clears

`clear_rects()` → `VcrDd3dClear` (`vcrdd_3d.c:136-179`):
- **SLI target, rect = the whole target, bit 2 set:** the new `vcr_d3dsli_clear_seq()`:

  ```
  sliCtrl=0
  clipLeftRight = (0 << 16) | w
  clipBottomTop = (0 << 16) | Hp
  fastfillCMD
  <the S2 prefix>
  ```

  Every chip fills rows `[0, Hp)` of its own buffer at once. Colour and aux together if both are cleared.
  The HAL re-sends the real clip and state afterwards, as it already does (`c->dirty`, `w->prepared = 0`).
- **SLI target, partial rect, or bit 2 clear:** today's fastfill under SLI. This is correct; it is slow only
  by the SPEC L2706 bug.
- **Non-chain target:** today's path (`sliCtrl` is already 0).
- Stencil is out of scope (G35).

### 3.8 Flips (`KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c` `Dd_Flip` :315, with G54)

While live, `Dd_Flip` maps the target block to `P(B)`. It queues `leftOverlayBuf = P(B)` and
`swapbufferCMD = 1` (wait for vsync; 0 for `DDFLIP_NOVSYNC`) through the transport, plus the
`swapBufferPend` increment, under G54's throttle (≤ `WaitOnVsync + 1` pending, cap 6).

- **Completion** = swap-pending (status[30:28]) is 0 **on every live chip**. This is stricter than the
  vendor's master-only read.
- The SLI swap algorithm (sli_syncin/out, swap master = chip 0) is already set by the kernel
  (`vcrmp_sli.c:911-918`), so the four chips swap at the same retrace.
- `GetFlipStatus` uses the same rule.
- `flip_from` (G55) becomes the old `P`.
- Not live: today's `vidDesktopStartAddr` flip, unchanged.

### 3.9 Lock, Blt, GetDC on chain surfaces

**Lock** (`Dd_Lock` :647) of a chain surface while live:
1. Sync all chips.
2. **Resolve** `P(B)` → `B`: read through the aperture (`sli_lfb_rd_en` returns the whole frame) and write
   the linear block. The display driver needs a kernel mapping of the aperture span: new
   `IOCTL_VCR_MAP_APERTURE`, mapped at promotion and unmapped at demotion.
3. Return NOTHANDLED, so the runtime hands out `B`'s linear pointer as today.

**Unlock** (`Dd_Unlock` :676) without `DDLOCK_READONLY` marks `P(B)` stale. It is uploaded (`B` → aperture,
`sli_lfb_cpu_wr_en` distributes lines) before the next render or flip of `B`.

**Demotion policy.** `vcr_d3dsli_lock_policy()`: more than 8 resolves within 2 s demotes the session, once,
with a log line naming it. A lock-every-frame DX5/6 title therefore drops to one chip after about 8 frames.
d3dprobe (one Lock per test) stays in SLI.

**Blt** (`Dd_Blt` :468):

| case | handling |
|---|---|
| `DDBLT_COLORFILL` of a whole chain surface | an SLI clear on `P(B)` |
| any other blit touching a chain block | demote first (v1). This includes D3D8 `SWAPEFFECT_COPY` present |
| a blit between linear surfaces, T-A | the 2D engine (broadcast) |
| a blit between linear surfaces, T-B, before G47 routes 2D through the FIFO | NOTHANDLED (HEL, via Lock; replicated by snoop) |

**GetDC** goes through Lock.

### 3.10 Demotion and the kernel (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_multi.c`, `vcrmp_sli.c`, `KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h`)

**`VcrDdSliDemote(pd, why, resolve_front)`.**
- Called from:
  - `DrvAssertMode(FALSE)`, before `RESET_DEVICE` (`KMD/display/vcrdd.c:570-618`);
  - `DrvDisableDirectDraw`;
  - `Dd_SetExclusiveMode(0)` (:582);
  - `Dd_FlipToGDISurface` (:594);
  - `Dd_DestroySurface` of a chain block (:663);
  - `ContextDestroy`/`ContextDestroyAll` of its last context;
  - `DestroyDDLocal` of the owner;
  - `HWCSETEXCLUSIVE` (`vcrdd_escape.c:126`);
  - an engine give-up (G02);
  - the lock policy.
- Steps:
  1. sync all chips, bounded;
  2. if the app lives on, resolve the displayed buffer to its block;
  3. `IOCTL_VCR_D3DSLI` disable;
  4. master: overlay off, desktop on at the displayed block (or the GDI surface), cursor restored;
  5. free the region.
- After demotion, a context renders linear (today's path) until it is destroyed. A context is never
  re-promoted.

**`IOCTL_VCR_D3DSLI`** (new):
- Enable: §3.5 step 4. Refused if:
  - an AA field is non-zero (never sent);
  - `dwChips != glide_chips`;
  - the session's owner is Glide;
  - the kernel's `vcr_sli_policy` refuses.
- Disable:
  1. per chip, restore the saved `lfbMemoryConfig` (TileCtrl, then TileCompare; selector left on TileCtrl);
  2. `VcrSliOff(x, "D3D demote")`;
  3. `Diag\D3DSliLive = 0`.
- `VcrSliOff`, when the owner was D3D, does the same `lfbMemoryConfig` restore first. Mode sets and display
  resets then clean a session the display driver never demoted.
  - **This protects Glide.** Glide writes its own `lfbMemoryConfig` on the master before its SLI request.
    A slave left with our TileCompare bit 15 would answer Glide's SLI LFB reads and writes at the wrong
    aperture (inferred; pinned by a mock test).
- `x->sli_owner` (`GLIDE` | `D3D`) is logged with every SLI event.

**`IOCTL_VCR_SLI_REGS`** (new): `{nchips, regs[4]}` system VAs. **`IOCTL_VCR_MAP_APERTURE`** (new): maps and
unmaps the aperture span.

**Boot guard.** A boot that finds `Diag\D3DSliLive = 1` sets `Diag\D3DSli = 0` and records
`Diag\D3DSliAutoOff` = the dead boot. It is modelled on the AA auto-disarm (`KMD/include/vcr_aaguard.h`,
`VcrSliAABootGuard` `vcrmp_multi.c:442-465`).

**State record.** `Diag\D3DSliState` (REG_BINARY, flushed at promotion) holds the layout, band, TileCtrl and
TileCompare. `vcrphases.py` decodes it, and `fbshot --probe` reads it to find the aperture. The probe path
must never write `lfbMemoryConfig`'s selector.

### 3.11 Caps, passes, the old path

- **Caps:** no D3D or DirectDraw cap changes. The overlay is used internally and is not offered as
  `DDCAPS_OVERLAY` (G61 unaffected). Free video memory drops by the private region while promoted.
- **Passes:** single pass. Each chip rasterises its own bands of the same command stream.
- **Old path:** with `D3DSli` absent, no new code runs. No IOCTL is issued, no allocation is made, and the
  register traffic is byte-identical (§5.1). Windowed D3D, DirectDraw-only titles and non-exclusive devices
  are never promoted.

### 3.12 Deliberately not done

- AA, of any kind (user choice, and the wedge history).
- 2-chip SLI on the 6000.
- SLI for windowed devices.
- 2D-engine SLI blits (`DdSli2DScn2Scn`-style).
- Per-chip LOD bias, until G26 adds trilinear.
- Re-promotion after demotion.
- Making the GDI desktop tiled (the vendor's `ddPrimaryInTile`).

## 4. Gating

**`HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\vcrmp\Diag\D3DSli`** (DWORD, read at boot, absent = 0):
- bit 0: S1 (single chip);
- bit 1: S2 (four chips; needs bit 0);
- bit 2: fast full-target clears.

It is reported in `vcr_info.flags`, and as event 513 when armed.

**`Diag\D3DSliBand`** (DWORD, 0 = Glide's rule, else log2 1..6) is for S3 only.

**Arming rules:**
- One Diag change per clean boot: arm bit 0 first, then 0+1, then 0+1+2.
- Every run is supervised (P9).
- Activation check, then `safe-reboot.py`.
- AA stays off (`Diag\SliAA` untouched; the D3D request cannot carry AA).

**Not safe default-on.** It changes the scanout layer, the memory layout and the transport fan-out for
every fullscreen D3D title. Default-on requires all of:
- S2 shows 0 bad rows in every d3dprobe SLI test at all six modes and both depths;
- the register capture equals the Glide golden;
- no freeze in the supervised game runs (a person at the box);
- a measured gain in at least one staged title (§7);
- the user's decision.

**Kill switches:** `D3DSli = 0`; `Diag\Sli = 0` (no multi-chip at all); and the boot auto-disarm.

## 6. Investigation before code (S0)

### E1: are memBase0 writes snooped? Decides T-A vs T-B

- **Setup:** one supervised clean boot with `Diag\AllowPoke = 1` only. Then
  `vcrctl sliaa 4 1 0 0 1 16 16 0 --i-am-at-the-box` (the kernel's cfg 5, no Glide; the desktop may look
  striped while it is on).
- **Test:**
  1. Read `cmdFifo1.baseAddrL` (CMD block, memBase0 + 0x80000) on chips 0-3 with `vcrctl reg`. FIFO 1
     is not used by our Glide, the vendor HAL or us, so writing its base while it is disabled has no
     effect (inferred; read its `baseSize` enable bit first and stop if it is set).
  2. Write a distinctive page number to the **master's** copy only.
  3. Read chips 1-3 again.
  4. Repeat with a readable 2D register.
  5. Restore every chip, then `vcrctl sliaa off`.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Slaves follow the master → memBase0 writes are snooped (the vendor's practice; SPEC L5426 is stale) →
    T-A is possible.
  - They do not → T-B only (wait for G47).
- The 3D block cannot be checked this way. A read of 3D `sliCtrl`/`aaCtrl` does not return the written
  value: `sli_golden` read `0x280`/`0x1e0` (640/480, the mode) on every chip under Glide's live cfg 5
  (`KMD/golden/sli_vcrkmd-sli_cfg5_192.168.1.124.json`). The pixel statistics counters (`fbiPixelsIn`
  0x14c) might serve but are unproven as readable. The 3D block's verdict is S2's first `slibands`: under
  T-A, chips 1-3 drawing nothing means 3D writes are not snooped.

### E2: where does the time go per title? (G78/G46 instrumentation, single chip, after G47-G50)

- **Measure:** fill time against CPU+HAL time per frame for UT2003/2004, Halo, Thief II and H&D at 1280x960.
- **Outcomes:** fill > 50 % of the frame → SLI pays; CPU-bound → skip this item for that title.

### E3: how do the titles use their chain? (G76 counters, single chip)

- **Measure:** Locks, Blts, GetDCs on chain surfaces per frame; `SWAPEFFECT_COPY`; render-target textures;
  textures created before the device.
- **Outcomes:** these predict demotions (§3.9) and replication cost (§3.6). A title that locks every frame
  will run single-chip under SLI. Report it rather than chase it.

### E4: the tile-compare split on our linear desktop (single chip, part of the S1 boot)

- **Run:** after promotion, the d3dprobe readback tests resolve through the upper-half aperture (S1,
  n = 1). After demotion, gdilab passes on the desktop.
- **Outcomes:** a resolve mismatch means the aperture translation base is not TileCtrl as the vendor's maths
  implies (§3.3). Fall back to Glide's arrangement (compare = TileCtrl) with the region placed so that no
  linear surface lies above it, or refuse.

## 8. Risks to proven paths

- **Default 16 bpp path:** none. With the switch absent, no code runs and the writes are byte-identical
  (§5.1). The new IOCTLs are inert unless called.
- **Box stability (the SLI/AA history):**
  - Every wedge on record was an **AA** configuration or an AA LFB read (`KMD/README.md` SliAA row,
    `vcrmp_multi.c:255-262`). The non-AA cfg-5 shape has run clean under Glide at every mode.
  - New exposure:
    1. overlay promotion from DirectDraw;
    2. the tile-compare split, shipped by 3dfx but never run by us;
    3. non-AA SLI LFB reads through it, proven in cfg 5 with Glide's arrangement;
    4. all-chip room checks. A missed one overflows a slave's FIFO (inferred: lost writes or a hang). G02
       must turn that into a visible give-up.
  - Mitigations: supervision, phases, and the auto-disarm.
- **Glide/OpenGL coexistence:**
  - The SLI state is shared in the miniport. D3D refuses promotion while a Glide owner exists, demotes at
    `HWCSETEXCLUSIVE`, and the owner is logged.
  - Glide's cfg-5 path is not changed: same function, flags 0.
  - The real hazard is a slave's `lfbMemoryConfig` left by D3D. It is closed by the per-chip restore in
    every exit path (pinned).
  - After a Glide session, any chip's stale `cmdFifo0` blocks promotion (G01 extended).
- **SLI state:** the D3D session ends through `VcrSliOff`, the same teardown every mode set already runs.
- **Flip path:** the desktop flip is untouched when not promoted. The overlay flip is new (G54's swap queue
  plus the overlay target).
- **Monitor:** no new mode switch. Promotion changes display layers, not timing. The kernel's SLI enable
  briefly cycles each slave's video processor (`copy_video`), as every Glide SLI open does.
- **Memory:** the private chain costs 0.6-7.8 MB of heap per mode (§2). An allocation failure keeps the
  title single-chip (reason logged), never broken.
- **Hardware cursor:** hidden while promoted. A title that shows the Windows cursor in a fullscreen D3D menu
  loses it (open question 6).

## 10. Open questions

1. **E1:**
   - Does a write to the master's memBase0 reach the slaves (CMD, 2D and 3D blocks)? SPEC L5426 says no.
     The vendor and our kernel's comment assume yes.
   - Are init-register writes snooped as `cfgInitEnable` bit 30 says?
2. Is the tile-compare translation relative to TileCtrl, as the vendor's `SLI_HwPtrToLfbPtr` implies? Do SLI
   LFB reads work above a TileCompare at 32 MB (E4)?
3. Are the target titles fill-bound once G47-G50 land, or bound by software T&L on the Athlon XP 2400+ (E2)?
4. How often do they lock or blit chain surfaces, and do any use `SWAPEFFECT_COPY` (E3)?
5. Gamma across bands in 4-way analog SLI: the kernel powers the slave RAMDACs down (`vcrmp_sli.c`, D:1453),
   while the vendor reloads the DAC "so slaves will snoop it". A per-chip CLUT copy is in the design;
   confirm visually that a game's gamma ramp is uniform.
6. Does the hardware cursor appear only in chip 0's bands under SLI (inferred)? The design hides it while
   promoted; which staged D3D title shows the Windows cursor in fullscreen?
7. Overlay filter at 32 bpp in SLI (point) and at 16 bpp (4x4): copy Glide's choice. Does 4x4 un-dithering
   look right on D3D's 16 bpp output?
8. Band tuning: Glide's rule gives 2-line bands at 800x600. Does the vendor's 16/32 rule balance better for
   D3D scenes (S3, `perf fill`)?
9. Does the D3D runtime ever render into the block a queued swap is still showing (G55) under a 2-deep chain?
10. Is a resolve through the aperture (~5 MB at 1280x960x32) fast enough (inferred 0.1-0.2 s) that 8 in 2 s
    is the right demotion threshold?

## 11. Corrections to the record

- **"Use 2D blits for clears in SLI"** (gap text, SPEC L2709): 3dfx's own D3D does full-screen clears with
  the 3D fastfill **with SLI switched off over the packed rows** (`VH5/D7DP2.C:883-940`). Every chip fills
  its own share at once. No 2D engine is needed.
- **"Render-target textures need SLI off (inferred)"** (hw Q12): confirmed by the vendor, which writes
  `sliCtrl = 0` for every non-tiled target (`VH5/D6DP2.C:6074-6080`).
- **Band height:** the gap and vendor text say 16/32 lines. On this board our proven Glide cfg 5 uses
  8/2/32/16/4 lines at 640x480/800x600/1024x768/1280x960/1600x1200 (`GL5/glide3/src/gsst.c:2210-2236`).
  The design follows the proven values.
- **memBase0 snooping:** the record contradicts itself (SPEC L5426 against `VH5/DDFXNT.C:3161` and
  `KMD/miniport/vcrmp_sli.c:1146-1148`). Treat it as unknown until E1, and write E1's answer into the README
  and `retro-3dfx/FINDINGS.md`.
- **"1.33 Gpix/s on 4 chips at 2 px/clk"** (gap text) is a peak model. The measured ceiling is 1124.6 Mpix/s
  (`EV/glidelab/vcrkmd-paced-2.jsonl:4`).

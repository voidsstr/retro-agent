# G48: a status read before every vertex. Spend the PCI FIFO's free count as credit

## 0. Verdict

- **The gap is real, as stated.** `vertex()` calls `VcrDdRoom` before every vertex, which costs one uncached
  `status` read plus one more per spin (`KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c:188`, `KMD/display/vcrdd_2d.c:92-108`). Each
  triangle therefore costs at least **3 status reads**, plus **27/36/45 uncached writes** for 0/1/2 texture stages
  (`vcrdd_3d.c:183-239`). The count holds for every primitive type, because `walk()` turns everything into `tri()`
  (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:1612-1689`).
- **How much it costs is not known.** Every bus-cost number on record is inferred. One measurement puts an upper
  bound on it: the UT2003 flyby on `.124` (§1.3).
  - It spends **116.7 ms of a 123 ms frame** inside DrawPrimitive.
  - That frame carries about **18.5 K triangles** that reach the HAL.
  - So the cost is **at most about 6.3 µs per triangle**, and that figure also includes the runtime's software
    vertex processing.
- **The fix the gap proposes needs one correction, and the design below makes it.** The gap says to spend the
  credit "as the text path's `VCR_MONO.credit` already does". That would spend the **whole** free count.
  - The Napalm spec's `pciInit0` has a **PCI FIFO low-water mark** (§2.1). On `.124` it is set to 10
    (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_hw.c:328-341`).
  - When fewer entries than that are empty, the chip **retries** PCI writes. A CPU stuck on a retried uncached
    write cannot be bounded by `SPIN_CAP`. The project's bounded-wait rule exists to prevent exactly that kind of
    wait.
  - The design therefore keeps the low-water mark in reserve, as 3dfx's own Voodoo Graphics Glide did (`swFifoLWM`,
    §2.4).
  - With a reserve, taking credit one vertex at a time gains nothing for 12- and 15-entry vertices. So inside the
    vertex the credit is spent **one write at a time**.
- **Expected effect** (from a model; §4.8):
  - status reads per triangle go from 3 to 1.29 / 1.71 / 2.14 for 0/1/2 stages;
  - a single-textured triangle saves 0.6-1.9 µs of bus time;
  - the UT2003 flyby should rise from about 8 fps to about 9-11 fps.
- **This is not the fix for G46.** G47 (CMDFIFO), G49 (strips and fans), G58 (packed colour) and G50 (2PPC) are each
  larger. G48 is the cheapest step on the triangle path. Its A/B run also gives the **first measurement of the
  status-read cost**, which decides how urgent G47 is. The improved direct path also stays useful after G47, as the
  fallback transport.
- **Gating.** The change is default-OFF behind `Diag\D3DFastSetup` bit 1 (0x2), which is perf.md P3's switch with
  its bit order. The value is read at boot. With the switch off, the register traffic, reads included, is byte for
  byte what it is today. It must not go default-on before it has been proven on silicon.
- **Priority P2, effort M.**

## 1. What happens today

### 1.1 The per-vertex room check

`vertex()` (`vcrdd_3d.c:183-223`) does the following for each vertex:

1. `VcrDdRoom(pd, 9 + 3·tex0 + 3·tex1)` (`:188`). `VcrDdRoom` reads `status` until `status[4:0] >= n`, up to
   `SPIN_CAP` = 2,000,000 reads, then gives up (`vcrdd_2d.c:92-108`). The reserved `n` includes the
   `sBeginTriCMD`/`sDrawTriCMD` write that `VcrDd3dTriangle` makes after the vertex (`:225-239`).
2. 8 writes: `sVx`, `sVy`, four float colours, `sVz` and `sOowfbi` (`:190-209`).
3. 3 more writes per texture stage (`:210-221`). TMU1's `sOow1` repeats the same `oow` (`:218`).

| triangle | entries per vertex | uncached writes | status reads (minimum) |
|---|---|---|---|
| untextured | 9 | 27 | 3 |
| one stage | 12 | 36 | 3 |
| two stages | 15 | 45 | 3 |

The other 3D emitters reserve room the same way, one status read per call:

- `VcrDd3dTarget`: room for `max(n,7)` (`:64`).
- `VcrDd3dState`: 12, then 8 per fog-table chunk, then 4, 5 and 4 (`:76`, `:93`, `:99`, `:109`, `:120`).
- `VcrDd3dClear`: 3, then 3 per rectangle, then 2 (`:156`, `:167`, `:173`).
- `VcrDd3dTexFlush`: 7 (`:256`).

Every call site reserves at least as many entries as it then writes. I checked each path through `VcrDd3dState`
(fog reload, compressed, two stages). The credit design depends on that.

### 1.2 Why a read is expensive (inferred, unmeasured)

- **A read must wait for all earlier writes.** The register window is mapped uncached
  (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:728-738`). Under PCI ordering a read cannot pass the posted writes ahead of it, so each
  read waits until every earlier write has crossed both bridges: the host bridge, then the 6000's HiNT PCI-PCI
  bridge to chip 0. Only then does the round trip itself start.
- **The FIFO is short.** It is 32 entries deep, and `status` reports 31 when it is empty (`vcrdd_2d.c:68`; measured
  as "FIFO free when empty 31" in `KMD/evidence/silicon/d3d32_step16/d3d32_boot_vcrlog.txt:106`). So a read buys
  room for only about 2.5 vertices at most.
- **When the chip keeps up, the read is pure overhead.** That is the case with the small triangles of a modern
  scene, where the chip finishes a 40-pixel triangle long before the CPU has written the next 36 registers. The
  read nearly always finds the FIFO empty (31 free) and changes nothing.

### 1.3 The one measurement that bounds the cost

The on-box agent ran the UT2003 flyby on 2026-11-12 (`ONBOX/games/UT2003_flyby/`). It ran at 640x480x16@120, with
software vertex processing and D16 (`ONBOX/session_log.md`, 10:23 entry).

- **Engine numbers.** The `avgfps` log gives "2.067308 / 9.411991 / 1.#INF00 fps", Score 9.419. Recomputed from
  `stats-2026-11-12-10-28-15.csv.gz` (1998 frames):

  | column | value |
  |---|---|
  | `Frame Frame` | 123.0 ms mean, 98.3 ms median |
  | `Hardware DrawPrimitive` | **116.7 ms per frame**, 288 calls per frame |
  | `Hardware NumPrimitives` | 29.4 K per frame |
  | `Hardware NumSVPVertices` | 33.8 K per frame |

- **Ring line.** "context 1 gone: 59589 DrawPrimitives2, 37033284 triangles, 3985 clears"
  (`ONBOX/games/UT2003_flyby/ring_decoded.txt:1015`). That works out to **about 18.5 K HAL triangles per frame**,
  about 30 DP2 calls per frame and about 620 triangles per DP2 call. The 2 clears per frame match the engine's
  `Hardware Clear` count.
- **What it shows.** DrawPrimitive takes 116.7 ms / 18.5 K ≈ **6.3 µs per HAL triangle**. That figure includes the
  D3D8 runtime's software vertex processing and the HAL's CPU work. The frame is CPU-bound in DrawPrimitive: 116.7
  of 123 ms.
- **What it fits.** The model (36-45 writes at `t_w` plus 3 reads at `t_r`) fits that figure with `t_w` ≈ 45-150 ns
  and `t_r` ≈ 0.5-1.5 µs. It cannot tell the two terms apart. §6 measures them.

## 2. Corrections to the gap text, and the hardware facts the design rests on

### 2.1 What the gap's evidence gets wrong or leaves out

- **Wrong citation.** "`KMD/display/vcrdd.h:199-203 (VCR_MONO.credit)`" does not exist: `vcrdd.h` is 199 lines long.
  `VCR_MONO` is declared at `vcrdd.h:120-128`, and its credit logic is `mono_room` at `vcrdd_2d.c:249-284`.
- **The "vendor comment" is ours.** `VH5/CFIFO.C:12-15` ("Each spin is an uncached MMIO read (~1us)") is not 3dfx
  text. This project added it in retro-3dfx commit `bebc83e` (2026-08-13, "V56K-WEDGE-WATCHDOG"; checked with
  `git blame`), so "~1 µs" is our own estimate.
  - `vcrdd_2d.c:71`'s "~a second" for 2 M reads would mean about 0.5 µs, which is also an estimate.
  - **Nothing on record measures `t_r` or `t_w`.**
- **The cited precedent has never run on silicon.** The text path's credit (`Diag\Accel2DText`) is default OFF. It
  was proven only on the 86Box bed, where it was also slower than software (`KMD/README.md:115`;
  `WT/docs/v56k-benchmark-plan.md:760-771`).
- **The gap leaves out the PCI FIFO low-water mark** (SPEC, `pciInit0` section). The spec says:
  - "Bits 6:2 determine the PCI fifo Low water mark. This value should never be 0 (no overflow checking is done)
    and should be set greater than 2 for any fast device operations".
  - "PCI FIFO Empty Entries Low Water Mark. Valid values are 0-31."
  - "Bits 17:13 allow the retry interval to be increased, while bits 12 and 11 allow retries to be disabled."

  3dfx's miniport calls the field the "PCI FIFO empty retries low water mark"
  (`/home/voidsstr/development/retro-3dfx/3dfx Driver Code/H5/W2K/Src/Video/Miniport/H5/H3.C:2346-2358`). Our miniport
  sets it to 10 on every VSA-100, the vendor's multi-chip value (`Diag\PciLowThresh`, `vcrmp_hw.c:328-341`). So:
  - once fewer than 10 entries are empty, the chip **retries** the next write instead of accepting it;
  - the write is not lost, but the CPU sits on it until the chip drains;
  - if the chip has hung, that wait never ends, and no software bound applies.
- **Today's code already reaches that zone.** `VcrDdRoom(n)` guarantees only `free >= n`, not `free >= n + LWM`, so
  when the chip is the bottleneck (large triangles) it can already write into the retry zone. A credit that spent
  the whole free count would write into it more often. This design never does (§4.1), which makes the credit path
  *safer* than today's path in that case. The native test pins both behaviours (§6.1).

### 2.2 The PCI FIFO also carries linear-frame-buffer (LFB) writes

The SPEC's `pciInit0` text says: "Bits 28:27 control the adjustable timeout for PCI writes travelling into the frame
buffer. After the indicated number of clocks, writes in the PCI fifo will get flushed out to the frame buffer".

So CPU writes to video memory, through `pvRamBase` or a user-mode DirectDraw mapping, take FIFO entries as well. Any
credit becomes stale after such writes. Two cases follow:

- **Inside a DP2 call, our own LFB writes are always preceded by `VcrDd2dSync`.** TEXBLT does this
  (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1513`, `:1532`), as does the one-rule comment at `vcrdd_2d.c:13-16`. Voiding the credit there covers
  them (§4.3).
- **Another thread's LFB writes while our DP2 thread is preempted** are not covered. Today's per-vertex window, from
  the read to the 12 writes after it, has the same exposure; the credit window is just wider. §4.4 and §8 cover how
  this is measured and bounded.

### 2.3 What else can and cannot write chip 0's FIFO during a DP2 call

DrawPrimitives2 and Clear2 run under the device lock, which serialises them with GDI, DirectDraw and the 2D engine.

- **The pointer.** `DrvMovePointer` is not asynchronous: no `GCAPS_ASYNCMOVE` is set (`KMD/display/vcrdd.c:337-349`).
  It moves the cursor through an IOCTL to I/O registers, which do not go through the FIFO (`vcrdd_pointer.c:65-81`).
- **Glide.** Glide draws only while it holds exclusive mode, and then `drawable` is 0
  (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1859-1860`, Clear2 `:1887-1888`).
- **What remains:** our own 3D emitters, TEXBLT's sync-then-memcpy, and other threads' LFB writes.

### 2.4 Precedents

- **3dfx Voodoo Graphics Glide 2.x is the closest precedent.** It uses exactly this credit with a reserve.
  `GR_CHECK_FOR_ROOM` subtracts from `fifoFree` and refills only when it goes negative
  (`GL/glide2x/sst1/glide/src/fxglide.h:1012-1019`). `_grReCacheFifo` re-reads the status and subtracts
  `swFifoLWM + n` (`GL/glide2x/sst1/glide/src/diglide.c:127-144`).
  - The h3 and h5 copies of that code are dead. The h5 copy is under `#if 0`
    (`GL/glide3x/h5/glide3/src/diglide.c:206-217`), because Glide 3 on these chips always uses the CMDFIFO.
- **3dfx's own Direct3D HAL is not a safety precedent.** It ships with the CMDFIFO (`-DCMDFIFO`, `VH5/SOURCES:52`).
  Its direct-write build variant does **no room check at all**: `CMDFIFO_CHECKROOM` is empty and `SETPD` is a plain
  store (`VH5/FIFOMGR.H:420-432`), so it leaned on the chip's retries. It is a bring-up path.

## 3. What the design does not change

- **Register writes.** Their order, their values and their count all stay as they are. In credit mode the chip sees
  the same stream of writes; only the `status` reads around it change.
- **Caps, single or multi pass, and formats.** None of these change, because this is a transport change only.
- **The write diet belongs to other gaps.** Cutting writes per triangle is G58's job (packed `sARGB`: 3 fewer per
  vertex; W1 sharing: 1 fewer per two-stage vertex) and G49's (strip and fan continuation: 1 vertex per triangle
  instead of 3).
  - This design creates the seam they plug into: a "vertex → (register, value) list" builder with interchangeable
    emitters (§4.2).
  - One write cut has no owner and is worth noting for G58's designer: dropping `SM_Z`/`sVz` when depth is off, and
    `SM_WFBI`/`sOowfbi` when neither fog nor a W-buffer uses it. The SPEC's `sSetupMode` text says an unset
    parameter "is not passed down to the triangle, and the previous value will be used". Each of those cuts needs its
    own pixel proof.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The rule: a pure header, `KMD/include/vcr_fifocredit.h` (new, Win32-free)

```c
typedef struct vcr_credit {
    vcr_u32 credit;     /* entries writable now with no status read */
    vcr_u32 held;       /* the reserve the last refill kept (the verify check needs it) */
    vcr_u32 reserve;    /* empty entries never spent: pciInit0[6:2] (10 on .124), 0 with bit 6 */
    vcr_u32 full;       /* status[4:0] of an empty FIFO, learned at init (31) */
    vcr_u32 reads, waits, entries, misses;      /* counters - CPU side only, no traffic */
} vcr_credit;

/* n entries out of the credit, no read; 0 = not enough left */
static __inline int vcr_cr_charge(vcr_credit *c, vcr_u32 n);
/* a status read saw `free` empty entries: 1 = n entries may be written now and the
 * rest is credit. The reserve is cut to full - n so a take of n <= full stays
 * reachable (a bad Diag\PciLowThresh can never turn into an endless spin);
 * n = 0 just re-bases the credit (the verify read). */
static __inline int vcr_cr_refill(vcr_credit *c, vcr_u32 free, vcr_u32 n);
/* the invariant: with no other writer the FIFO only drains, so empty entries
 * can never be fewer than the credit we still hold plus the reserve we kept */
static __inline int vcr_cr_honest(const vcr_credit *c, vcr_u32 free) { return free >= c->credit + c->held; }
static __inline void vcr_cr_drop(vcr_credit *c) { c->credit = 0; }
```

**The refill rule:**

```
r    = min(reserve, full - n)
room = free > r ? free - r : 0
take n if room >= n; credit = room - n; held = r
```

- **The safety argument.** Count the writes since the last read; their maximum is `free - r`.
  - **Assumption:** nobody else writes the FIFO.
  - **Result:** the empty count never falls below `r`. With `r` equal to the programmed low-water mark, none of our
    own writes is ever retried.
  - **Margin:** under the "fewer than the mark" reading of the spec there is one entry to spare.
  - **What remains bounded:** the only wait left is the refill's read loop, bounded by `SPIN_CAP` as today.
- **Reuse by G47.** The header knows nothing about `status[4:0]`: the caller passes `free`. G47's command FIFO can
  therefore reuse it for its cached room-to-read-pointer (vendor `VH5/CFIFO.C:456-465`), which is the same idea with a
  1 MB ring.

### 4.2 Where the credit is spent

**`vcrdd_2d.c`**: the room loop gets a counting form, and the 3D emitters get their own entry point.

```c
static BOOL room_loop(VCR_PDEV *pd, ULONG n, vcr_credit *k);   /* VcrDdRoom's loop, unchanged; k counts */

BOOL VcrDdRoom(VCR_PDEV *pd, ULONG n)          /* 2D, text, Reset3D: any other writer voids the credit */
{
    vcr_cr_drop(&pd->cr);
    return room_loop(pd, n, NULL);
}

BOOL VcrDd3dRoom(VCR_PDEV *pd, ULONG n)        /* the 3D emitters only */
{
    if (!(pd->d3d_fast & VCR_FS_CREDIT))
        return room_loop(pd, n, &pd->cr);      /* the proven path, read for read */
    if (!pd->g2d_ok) { vcr_cr_drop(&pd->cr); return FALSE; }   /* a give-up stops everything, as today */
    if (!(pd->d3d_fast & VCR_FS_VERIFY) && vcr_cr_charge(&pd->cr, n))
        return TRUE;                           /* no read */
    for (i = 0; i < SPIN_CAP; i++) {
        s = rd(pd, 0);  pd->cr.reads++;
        if (i == 0 && (pd->d3d_fast & VCR_FS_VERIFY) && !vcr_cr_honest(&pd->cr, s & ST_FIFO_FREE))
            credit_miss(pd, s);                /* counted; the first 4 logged */
        if (vcr_cr_refill(&pd->cr, s & ST_FIFO_FREE, n)) { pd->cr.waits += i; return TRUE; }
    }
    vcr_cr_drop(&pd->cr);
    give_up(pd, 1, s);                         /* the same give-up, reason and log line as today */
    return FALSE;
}
```

**`vcrdd_3d.c`, the seam.** `vertex()` is split into a builder and an emitter:

- **The builder.** `vertex_seq(d, v, cmd, w)` builds the `vcr_regw` list of one vertex plus its command, 9, 12 or 15
  entries. It uses the **same expressions in the same order** as today's `vertex()` (`:190-221`), with the command
  appended: `sBeginTriCMD` for the first vertex and `sDrawTriCMD` for the other two.
- **The emitter.** `emit(pd, w, n)` writes the list:

```c
static BOOL emit(VCR_PDEV *pd, const vcr_regw *w, ULONG n)
{
    ULONG i;
    if (!(pd->d3d_fast & VCR_FS_CREDIT)) {     /* proven: one room check, then the n writes */
        if (!VcrDd3dRoom(pd, n))
            return FALSE;
        for (i = 0; i < n; i++)
            w3(pd, w[i].off, w[i].val);
        return TRUE;
    }
    if ((pd->d3d_fast & VCR_FS_VERIFY) && !VcrDd3dRoom(pd, 0))   /* verify: one checked read per vertex */
        return FALSE;
    for (i = 0; i < n; i++) {                  /* credit: charged per WRITE - a read mid-vertex is harmless */
        if (!vcr_cr_charge(&pd->cr, 1) && !VcrDd3dRoom(pd, 1))
            return FALSE;
        w3(pd, w[i].off, w[i].val);
    }
    return TRUE;
}
```

**`VcrDd3dTriangle`** emits the three lists in turn, then sets `g2d_busy` as today.

**Why per write.** With a reserve of 10, one read buys 21 entries. Spent one vertex at a time, that is one 12- or
15-entry vertex per read, which is exactly today's count. Spent one write at a time, it is 36/21 = 1.71 reads per
single-textured triangle.

- **It is safe:** splitting a vertex across a status read changes nothing for the chip. The FIFO entries are
  independent register writes, and the fog-table loop at `:92-95` already reads part-way through a sequence.
- **It is cheap:** the per-write check is one decrement and one predictable branch, about 1 ns against a write of
  45 ns or more.

**The other emitters** (`VcrDd3dTarget`, `VcrDd3dState`, `VcrDd3dClear` and `VcrDd3dTexFlush`) swap `VcrDdRoom(` for
`VcrDd3dRoom(` with the same `n`. In credit mode those become one take per call site, which is safe because every site
over-reserves (§1.1). After that change no `VcrDdRoom(` call is left in `vcrdd_3d.c`.

**What the off path costs.** With the switch off, the bus traffic is unchanged: the same reads before the same `n`
writes, in the same order, with the same values. The CPU-side difference is the vertex values passing through a
15-entry stack array, plus the counters: about 10-20 ns per vertex against 1.5-5 µs of bus time (inferred).

**Why not keep the old `vertex()` for the off path.** One function with two emitters is chosen deliberately over a
second copy of `vertex()`:

- G58, G49, G25, G32, G23 and G92 all change what a vertex sends;
- two copies would have to be kept in step by hand;
- the builder is also the seam G47's packet 3 and G49's strip continuation need.

### 4.3 Where the credit is voided

| point | why |
|---|---|
| first statement of `VcrDdRoom` | every 2D, text and Reset3D writer puts entries in the FIFO the credit does not know about |
| first statement of `VcrDd2dSync`, before its early return | every CPU access to video memory is preceded by it, and TEXBLT's `memcpy` into the LFB takes FIFO entries (§2.2) |
| `give_up` | the engine is gone |
| entry and every exit of `D3d_DrawPrimitives2` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1821-1871`) and `D3d_Clear2` (`:1873-1903`) | the credit never outlives the call that earned it, because between calls anything may write; each DP2 call pays one refill read, about 30 per frame in UT2003, which is negligible |
| PDEV creation | the PDEV is `EngAllocMem(FL_ZERO_MEMORY…)` (`vcrdd.c:397`), so the credit starts at 0 |

### 4.4 The verify and reserve-0 bits

**`VCR_FS_VERIFY` (bit 5, 0x20) is a correctness probe.** With bit 1 it still reads `status` at every take of the
other emitters and at the start of every vertex, and before acting on the read it checks the invariant
`free >= credit + held`.

- **Cost:** about what today's path costs, so it gives no speed. It **acts on the fresh read**, so it is as safe as
  today.
- **What a miss means:** either one of our call sites wrote more than it charged (an accounting bug), or another
  writer was there: another thread's LFB writes while DP2 was preempted.
- **What it measures:** the frequency of the residual risk, in d3dprobe and in a real game.

**`VCR_FS_NORESERVE` (bit 6, 0x40) is an experiment, supervised only.** With bit 1 the reserve is 0, so the whole
free count is spent, as `mono_room` does.

- It can write into the retry zone when the chip is the bottleneck, as today's path already can.
- It exists to measure the upper bound of the gain: 36/31 = 1.16 reads per single-textured triangle.
- Adopt it only on evidence (§7).

### 4.5 Counters and the ring

`pd->cr`'s counters (`reads`, `waits`, `entries`, `misses`) count only the 3D emitters' traffic, in both modes, so
boot A of the A/B gets its baseline from the same build. They are zeroed at `ContextCreate`.

`ctx_free` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1210-1221`) logs one more event, using the next free `VCR_EV_DD_D3D` code (24 at the time of
writing; codes 1-23 are in use):

```
context %u FIFO: %u status reads (%u while waiting) for %u entries, %u credit misses (D3DFastSetup %x, reserve %u)
```

- **Why at context end:** the event is written last, so it survives the ring's wrap during a game (G76), just as
  "context 1 gone" did in the UT2003 ring.
- **What the host derives:**
  - reads per triangle: `reads / tris`, taking `tris` from the existing line;
  - waits per triangle;
  - entries per triangle, which should come to 27/36/45 plus state traffic.
- **Other contexts:** two contexts alive at once (rare) share the counters, so their numbers are mixed. The log says
  so.

### 4.6 The switch, plumbed like D3DBigTex

1. **Miniport.** `KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c` `VcrFindAdapter` reads `x->d3dfastsetup = VcrDiagGet(L"D3DFastSetup", 0);`
   **at boot** (`vcrmp.h` gets the field).
2. **The `vcr_info` word.** `vcr_info` gains `vcr_u32 d3d_fastsetup` **appended** at its end, after `mon_src`, or
   after `d3dfeat` if G05, G12 or G15's word lands first.
   - The word is appended because the sibling designs found `vcr_info.flags` too small for multi-bit switches (G15.md,
     G23.md).
   - `fill_info` copies the value into it.
3. **Display driver.** `KMD/display/vcrdd_2d.c` `VcrDd2dInit` (`:432-502`):
   - **`memset(&info, 0, sizeof info)` before the IOCTL.** Today the struct is not zeroed (`:436-443`); after this
     change an older miniport's shorter struct leaves the field 0.
   - Once the register window is mapped and `g2d_ok` is set, it sets
     `pd->d3d_fast = pd->g2d_ok ? info.d3d_fastsetup & VCR_FS_IMPLEMENTED : 0`. Bits a build does not implement are
     ignored, so a later G49/G58 bit armed on this build does nothing.
   - It also sets `pd->cr.full = pd->g2d_fifo_full` and
     `pd->cr.reserve = (fast & VCR_FS_NORESERVE) ? 0 : (rd(pd, VCR_R_PCIINIT0) >> VCR_PI0_LOWTHRESH_SHIFT) & 0x1f`.
     Here `VCR_R_PCIINIT0` is offset 0x04 of chip 0's window (`KMD/include/vcr_regs.h:40-41`).
   - It logs the arming as the next `DD_D3D` code (25 at the time of writing):
     `armed (Diag\D3DFastSetup %x): FIFO credit ON, reserve %u (pciInit0 low-water %u), verify %s`.
4. **Bit allocation in `D3DFastSetup`.** Bits 0 and 2-4 keep perf.md P3's order so the sibling items fit without
   renumbering. The macros live in `vcr_fifocredit.h`.

   | bit | value | name | owner |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 0 | 0x01 | packed colour | reserved for G58 |
   | **1** | **0x02** | **FIFO credit (`VCR_FS_CREDIT`)** | **this item** |
   | 2 | 0x04 | strip/fan continuation | reserved for G49 |
   | 3 | 0x08 | autostrip | reserved for G49 |
   | 4 | 0x10 | W1 sharing | reserved for G58 |
   | 5 | 0x20 | verify (`VCR_FS_VERIFY`) | this item |
   | 6 | 0x40 | no reserve (`VCR_FS_NORESERVE`) | this item, experimental |

   Bits 5 and 6 do nothing without bit 1.
5. **Which chips.** The rule is chip-generic: Banshee, Voodoo3 and VSA-100 all have `status[4:0]` and `pciInit0`'s
   mark. So the switch is not gated to `napalm`, which lets the 86Box Voodoo3 bed exercise the code first. It is
   proven on `.124` only.

### 4.7 What happens on the old path

When the switch is absent, every 3D room check is `room_loop` with counters. That is `VcrDdRoom`'s exact loop, so
there are the same reads, the same give-up and the same `n`. Every vertex is one such check, then its 9, 12 or 15
writes in today's order.

### 4.8 Expected numbers (model; `t_w` = 45-150 ns, `t_r` = 0.5-1.5 µs, both inferred)

Reads per triangle assume the chip keeps up, so each refill read sees 31 free.

| | 0 stages | 1 stage | 2 stages |
|---|---|---|---|
| entries per triangle | 27 | 36 | 45 |
| reads per triangle today | 3.00 | 3.00 | 3.00 |
| reads per triangle, bit 1 (reserve 10 → 21 per read) | 1.29 | 1.71 | 2.14 |
| reads per triangle, bits 1 + 6 (reserve 0 → 31 per read) | 0.87 | 1.16 | 1.45 |
| bus time today | 2.7-8.6 µs | 3.1-9.9 µs | 3.5-11.3 µs |
| saved with bit 1 (= reads saved × `t_r`) | 0.86-2.6 µs | 0.64-1.9 µs | 0.43-1.3 µs |

**Applied to the UT2003 flyby** (18.5 K HAL triangles per frame; a mix of one- and two-stage draws):

- DrawPrimitive time drops by **about 12-36 ms per frame** from 116.7 ms;
- the frame goes from 123 ms to about 87-111 ms, which is **about 9-11.5 fps against 8.1 mean**;
- at the likeliest `t_r` (0.8-1.2 µs) the gain is about +18-30%.

This leaves the chip and CPU serialised (perf-01), so the rest is G47's.

**With G58 later** (a 9-entry single-textured vertex), bit 1 gives 27/21 = 1.29 reads per triangle. G49's strip
continuation then cuts both the entries and the reads to about a third.

### 4.9 Relation to the other gaps

| gap | relation |
|---|---|
| G47 | When armed, G47 replaces this path. It reuses the header for its cached room. The direct path, and with it this credit, stays as the **fallback** when `cmdFifo0` is not ours: G01's stale Glide FIFO, or G47 off. |
| G58, G49 | They feed this item's builder seam. Their bits sit in the same switch. |
| G51 (SLI) | If Direct3D ever runs in SLI, every chip that receives the writes has its own PCI FIFO. Room must then be the **minimum `free` over those chips**. | G46 | G46 is the measured symptom. G48 is one of its parts. |
| G76, G78 | This item brings its own minimal counters and the `perf --mode setup` scene. If G76's `D3DStats` lands first, the FIFO line moves into it. |

## 5. d3dprobe checks (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`, `d3dprobe_run.py`)

### 5.1 `perf --mode setup`: the speed check

The existing `perf` scene is fill-bound and cannot see the triangle path (perf.md §3.2). The new scene is:

```
d3dprobe perf --mode setup [--tex 0,1,2] [--tris N=16384] [--tri-px P=2] [--repeat R=3]
              [--frames F=30] --full --novsync --res 640x480 --bpp 16
```

- **What it draws.** One fullscreen device. For each listed stage count, R times:
  - 5 warm-up frames, then F frames;
  - each frame is a target `Clear`, then N triangles of P×P pixels (right triangles on a 4-pixel grid across the
    target), drawn as `TRIANGLELIST` through `DrawPrimitiveUP` in 1024-triangle calls, then `Present`.
  - State: Z off, culling none, dither off, blend off, point sampling.
  - Stages: 0 = diffuse only; 1 = a 64x64 R5G6B5 checker with `MODULATE`; 2 = `tex2mod`'s two-stage setup
    (`d3dprobe.c:1058-1097`), which passes today.
  - Calibration: F empty frames (`Clear` + `Present` only) are timed in the same device.
  - Everything runs on one device, so one run costs **two mode switches** for the whole matrix, which respects the
    CRT pacing rule.
- **What it reports.** Each RESULT entry carries:
  - `us_per_tri` = (frame_ms − empty_ms) × 1000 / N, per repeat, plus the median;
  - `tris_s`;
  - the refresh the runtime picked.
- **The GPU-bound variant.** `--tri-px 48 --tex 1` gives triangles big enough for the chip to be the bottleneck. It
  shows that the credit path costs nothing there, and it makes the refill loop *wait*, which exercises `waits`.
- **Pass criteria on silicon (A/B, §7):**
  1. In the bit-1 boot, the median `us_per_tri` at `--tex 1` is **≤ 0.90×** boot A's. Each stage count is no worse
     than boot A's (≤ 1.00×).
  2. The ring's FIFO line for the run gives reads per triangle within **±0.05** of the reference: boot A 3.00; bit 1
     1.29 / 1.71 / 2.14 for 0/1/2 stages. Waits per triangle stay < 0.05, which shows the chip kept up.
  3. The `--tri-px 48` frame time is within **±3%** of boot A's, with no 513 a=9 give-up.
- **What the A/B also gives.** Within one stage count the writes and CPU work are identical in both arms, so
  `t_r = Δus_per_tri / Δreads_per_tri`. That yields three independent estimates of the status-read cost.

### 5.2 `render --tests fifogrid`: correctness under long credit runs

The existing tests draw 2-4 triangles a frame, so a refill never falls at an arbitrary point mid-vertex. This test
makes it fall everywhere.

- **What it draws.** One frame in the 256x256 readback window:
  - 32x32 cells of 8x8 pixels, each cell a quad of 2 triangles, coloured from an 8-colour palette by
    `(row·31 + col) mod 8`;
  - row `r` uses kind `r mod 3`: untextured, one stage (a 32x32 texture whose texel `(c, r)` holds the cell's
    colour, sampled at the texel centre on all four vertices), or two stages (tex2mod's setup with stage 1 grey);
  - each row is one `DrawPrimitiveUP` of 64 triangles;
  - after row 15 there is a mid-frame `Clear` of row 15's first 8 cells. It goes through `Clear2`, which voids and
    then restarts the credit.
- **Its size.** 32 draws, 2048 triangles, and about 75 K FIFO entries. At 21 entries per read, a refill falls at
  every offset of every vertex kind.
- **Pass criterion.** All 1024 cell centres match their analytic colour within tolerance 24. Row 15's first 8 cells
  are the clear colour. The two-stage rows are expected at tex2mod's product, with tolerance 14 as tex2mod uses.
  - It runs at 640x480x16 and at 1024x768x32 (D3D32 armed).
  - The **pixels are identical with the switch off and on**, because the credit changes no write.
  - In the bit-5 boot, the ring line must read **0 credit misses**.

## 6. Regression tests

### 6.1 `WT/tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_fifocredit.c` (new; true source: compiles `include/vcr_fifocredit.h`)

**The model FIFO.**

- 31 entries, a low-water mark of 10, and a chip that drains `d` entries per CPU write (`d = ∞` is instant).
- A read returns the empty count. Counted events:
  - `zone_writes`: a write that arrives with empty < mark, which the real chip would retry;
  - `overflow`;
  - `reads`.
- Two transports drive it:
  - **old:** today's `VcrDdRoom(n)` then `n` writes per vertex;
  - **new:** the header's per-write charge and refill.
- The workload is 1000 triangles at 27, 36 or 45 entries.

**The cases.** Each asserts the old value and the fixed one.

1. **`old_room_reads_three_per_triangle`:** instant drain, all three stage counts → old reads **3000**. This pins
   today's cost and the gap's claim.
2. **`credit_reads_per_triangle_with_the_reserve`:** instant drain → new reads **1286 / 1715 / 2143**
   (= ⌈27000/21⌉, ⌈36000/21⌉, ⌈45000/21⌉), with `zone_writes` 0 and `overflow` 0.
3. **`credit_reads_without_reserve`:** reserve 0 → **871 / 1162 / 1452** (⌈…/31⌉).
4. **`a_slow_chip_never_meets_a_retried_write_on_the_credit_path`:** `d` = 1/4 →
   - new: `zone_writes` **0**, `overflow` 0, `waits` > 0;
   - old: `zone_writes` **> 0**. This pins today's exposure as the old-buggy value, so the test documents why the
     reserve exists.
5. **`a_stalled_chip_gives_up_bounded`:** the chip stops draining after k writes →
   - the take fails after exactly `spin_cap` reads (a small cap in the test);
   - the writes issued are ≤ (`free` at the last good read − reserve);
   - no write lands with empty < mark.
6. **`drop_forces_a_read`:** after `vcr_cr_drop`, the next take reads.
7. **`the_reserve_is_cut_so_every_take_is_reachable`:** reserve 31 → `vcr_cr_refill(free=31, n=15)` succeeds with
   room 15, and `n=1` with room 1. The old-buggy shape would be an endless spin.
8. **`verify_finds_a_foreign_writer`:** k entries injected behind the credit's back → `vcr_cr_honest` = 0. With no
   injection there are 0 misses over 1000 triangles at every `d`.
9. **`refill_with_zero_rebases_the_credit`:** the verify read.

## 8. Risk to proven paths

**Default 16 bpp path, switch off.** The traffic is identical, read for read and write for write, and the tests in
§6.2 pin it. The CPU cost is a 15-entry stack array per vertex and the counter increments, under 1% of a vertex's bus
time (inferred). No new wait is added.

**Switch on, the same writes.** Only reads are removed. The exposures:

1. **An accounting error.** A call site writes more than it charged. The chip then retries the excess, which stalls
   the CPU but corrupts nothing. The native model (§6.1) and verify mode (§4.4) catch it.
2. **A foreign LFB writer.** Another thread writes the LFB while DP2 is preempted, which makes the credit optimistic
   by at most what that thread wrote.
   - This is the same kind of exposure as today's read-then-12-writes window, but wider: up to 21 writes after a
     read instead of up to 15.
   - The consequence is PCI retries until the chip drains. That is harmless unless the chip hangs at that moment,
     which is today's residual risk too.
   - Verify mode measures how often it happens. Halo's Bink movies, with `-novideo` removed, are the likeliest
     source.
3. **A hung chip.** Our own writes never enter the retry zone, so a hung chip is found by the next refill's
   `SPIN_CAP` give-up (about 1-2 s, as today), never by a CPU frozen on a retried write. That is strictly no worse
   than today, whose `VcrDdRoom(n)` can write into the zone when the chip is the bottleneck (§6.1, case 4).

**Glide and OpenGL coexistence.** No change.

- The credit exists only inside DP2 and Clear2.
- `drawable` is 0 while Glide holds the chip.
- Glide's own transport is its CMDFIFO.
- G01's stale-FIFO state behaves identically with the switch off and on: triangles vanish in both.

**SLI state.** No change: Direct3D draws on chip 0 with SLI off. For a future SLI Direct3D, see §4.9 (G51).

**Flip path.** No change: `Dd_Flip`'s `VcrDd2dSync` (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:334`) voids the credit, and the flip itself
writes I/O registers through the miniport.

**2D, GDI and text.** `VcrDdRoom` gains one store (the drop); its reads and give-up are unchanged.

**Effort: M.**

- Header and native model test: about 1 day.
- Builder and emitter refactor, the `VcrDd3dRoom` swap, the invalidation points, counters and Diag plumbing (miniport,
  `vcr_info`, `VcrDd2dInit`): about 1 day.

**Priority: P2.** It is a performance change with a measurable gain that the A/B decides. Its tooling half, the
`perf --mode setup` scene and the FIFO line, could land first at P1 as measurement for G46 and G47.

## 10. Open questions

1. **What are `t_r` and `t_w` on `.124`**, through the host bridge and the HiNT bridge? The A/B in §5.1 gives `t_r`.
   If it is below about 0.3 µs, G48 is not worth graduating.
2. **What exactly are the low-water mark's semantics?** Is it "empty < LWM" or "empty ≤ LWM"? Does the host bridge or
   the HiNT bridge ever give up on a retried posted write? The reserve keeps one entry of margin under the first
   reading.
3. **Does the chip really keep up with tiny triangles?** If the setup unit is slower than the bus time of 36 writes,
   the refill reads find a part-full FIFO, waits appear, and the benefit shrinks. The counters show it.
4. **How often do other threads' LFB writes make the credit optimistic in real games?** Verify mode answers this:
   Halo with movies, and texture-streaming UE2.
5. **Is reserve 0 (bit 6) worth re-entering the retry zone when the chip is the bottleneck?** Only boot D answers it.
6. **Does the off path's array copy show in CPU time at all?** Expected below 1%.
7. **Should the text path's `VCR_MONO` credit (`vcrdd_2d.c:263-284`) adopt the same reserve** before
   `Accel2DText` is ever tried on silicon? That is GDI, not Direct3D, and its own item.
8. **After G47, how often will the direct path still run?** That happens when someone else's `cmdFifo0` is on, or
   G47 is off. The answer decides whether bit 1 is worth graduating at all once G47 exists.

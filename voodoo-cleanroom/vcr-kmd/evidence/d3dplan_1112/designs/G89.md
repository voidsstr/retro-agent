## 1. Verdict

**The gap is real, but its limit is four times wider than it says, and its cap half is a plain caps bug.**

1. **The setup unit does not iterate in 14.18.** 14.18 is the format of the *integer* MMIO registers
   `startS/startT/dSdX/...` (SPEC L2244-2245, L2568; `H3REGS.H:302-303`). vcr-kmd never writes those. It writes the
   triangle-setup unit's IEEE float registers `sS/W0`, `sT/W0`, `sS/Wtmu1`, `sT/Wtmu1` (SPEC §8.69-8.73, L3789-3797;
   `KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c:213-220`). SPEC itself only says that the chip "automatically converts both the start and
   fstart registers into an internal fixed point notation used for rendering" (L2564).
2. **3dfx's own simulator states that internal notation: 48-bit, signed 16.32** for S/W, T/W and 1/W
   (`CSIM/H3SIM.H:40-57`: `SST_ST64_SIZE 48`, `SST_ST64_INTBITS 16`, `SST_ST64_FRACBITS 32`, the same for W).
   - The setup unit converts each vertex float to 32 fraction bits and **masks it to 48 bits**
     (`CSIM/SETUP.C:550-561` `SET_VERTEX64_P ... & FX_MASK64(size)`, used for TMU0 at `:660-664`, TMU1 at `:698-705`;
     the conversion is `_convertFloat2Fix64`, `:413-436`).
   - The per-span iterators are masked to 48 bits again (`CSIM/GO.C:239-244`).
   - The TREX bit model takes the same 16.32 values: `TRX/TREXFUNC.H:402, 410` ("16.32 signed"). The diag masks its
     per-pixel S to `SST_ST64_SIZE` before the model sees it (`H5/DIAGS/TREX/SSTTEX.C:1868-1883`).
3. **So the real limit is |S/W| < 32768.** At our scaling (`VcrDd3dTexScale`, wide side = 256, `vcrdd_3d.c:273-285`)
   that is **|u| x rhw < 128** on the wide axis. The narrow axis gets 128 x aspect. The gap's "+/-32 repeats" is the
   14.18 reading, too small by 4x. 1/W is 16.32 as well, so **rhw above 2 is not a problem**: the "2.30" in SPEC is
   again the integer register.
4. **Beyond the limit the value WRAPS (two's complement, mod 65536). It does not saturate.**
   - The texel then moves by 65536/rhw in S, i.e. (65536/rhw) mod 256 texels.
   - **That is invisible whenever 256/rhw is an integer** (rhw = 1, 2, 4, 1/2, ...). WRAP addressing truncates the
     integer part (SPEC §8.76 L3865) and 65536/rhw is then a whole number of periods.
   - **So every 2D, HUD or menu quad at rhw = 1 is immune at any u.**
   - A perspective triangle with u x rhw >= 128 shows **bands of shifted, but correctly coloured, texture**. The bands
     follow lines of equal depth, because the shift changes with rhw.
5. **The mip level does not depend on the integer offset.** The TREX LOD unit computes the true perspective derivative
   `(d(S/W)/dx - S*d(1/W)/dx) * W` (`CSIM/LOD.C:58-73`; bit model `TRX/TREXFUNC.C:1012-1060` `lod_calc_sq_term`
   multiplies S by dW and subtracts it). An integer shift of u therefore leaves the LOD unchanged, apart from rounding
   in a 12-bit-mantissa product.
   - **86Box (and MAME) leave that term out.** 86Box takes the LOD from the S/W gradients per triangle
     (`86B/voodoo_render.c:1561-1576`) plus log2 W per pixel (`:402`). It also never wraps at 48 bits
     (`86B/voodoo_setup.c:204`: int64 x 2^32, no mask).
   - **So the 86Box bed can show that a rebase is invisible. It can never show that one is needed.**
6. **Precision starts to matter before the overflow.** The TMU normalises S/W to a 20-bit signed mantissa before it
   multiplies by W (`TRX/TREXFUNC.C:772-789` `it_s_mant_20s`, `:847`).
   - The texel error is therefore of the order of |S| x 2^-19. It can be up to about 3x that, because W's own
     mantissa is 19 bits including the leading 1 (`:745-768`).
   - That is 1/8 texel (up to ~3/8) at |u| = 256 on a texture up to 256 wide. On a 2048-wide TBIG texture it is x8,
     because one S unit is 8 texels there. Inferred from the bit model; E3b measures it.
7. **The cap.** `dwMaxTextureRepeat` is never set, so it is reported as 0 (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:2150-2173`), and
   `D3DPTEXTURECAPS_TEXREPEATNOTSCALEDBYSIZE` is absent (`:2034-2035`).
   - 3dfx reported **256 with that flag**, which is exactly the 16.32 range at the 256 scale (full range of u = 2 x 128).
   - That holds for W2K (`VH5/D3INIT.C:2824-2826, 3027-3029, 3204-3210`, with `TEXTURE_REPEAT_NOT_SCALED` defined
     by `VH5/DDFXNT95.H:70`).
   - It holds for the Win9x retail build too: `H5/Win9x/DX/BUILD/STBPERF.INC:85` defines it, *"Per Gary Tarolli"*.
     The retail listing compiles it: `H5/Win9x/DX/D3D/RETAIL/D3INIT.COD:6699` `mov edi, 256` and `:6786`
     `mov DWORD PTR [edx+36], edi`. Offset 36 of `D3DHAL_D3DEXTENDEDCAPS` is `dwMaxTextureRepeat`, next to
     `[edx+40], 8`, the aspect ratio.
   - **hw-28 is right and hal-26's 32768 is the unused `#else` branch.**
8. **Which titles could exceed it.** None is known to. The census decides.
   - UE2 (UT2003/UT2004): unlikely. Its rhw stays at or below 1 (inferred).
   - Plausible: Halo (detail maps on near surfaces, small near plane), Battlefield 1942 LAN (terrain detail),
     Max Payne and Red Faction (inferred, unmeasured).
   - The 11-12 "confetti" in UT2004/UT2003/Halo is multi-coloured. That points at foreign memory (G06/G07), not at
     a coordinate wrap, which keeps the texture's own colours.

**The plan:**

- **Step 0, census (default-on, log only).** Per-context counters of |S/W|, |S| and rhw, plus one line at context end.
- **Step 1, measure on silicon.** d3dprobe `texrepeat`, `texrepeat_span`, `texrepeat_lod` and `texrepeat_prec`
  confirm the 16.32 range, the wrap mode, LOD invariance and precision.
- **F0, the cap.** Report 256 + `TEXREPEATNOTSCALEDBYSIZE`. It changes the report only and is safe default-on once
  Step 1 has confirmed the number.
- **F1, the rebase.** Per triangle, an integer offset subtracted from WRAP axes that are at risk. It sits behind
  `Diag\D3DFeat` bit `STREBASE`, default OFF, and is **built only if the census finds at-risk WRAP triangles in a
  staged title**.
- **F2, W normalisation: rejected.** W is 16.32.

**Priority P2.** The cap is caps hygiene, and the investigation is cheap and rides the planned instrumentation boot.
It becomes P1 if the census finds an at-risk title.

## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 What our HAL sends

- **Vertices are pre-transformed only** (`fvf_layout`, `vcrdd_d3d.c:1258-1259`). rhw comes from the application or
  from the runtime's software vertex pipeline, as `1/w_clip`.
- **`vertex()` writes floats to the setup unit** (`vcrdd_3d.c:210-221`):
  - TMU0: `sOow0 = rhw`, `sSow0 = u * s_scale * rhw`, `sTow0 = v * t_scale * rhw`;
  - TMU1 (two stages): the same from `tex1_off`.
  - In two-stage mode TMU0 samples stage 1's set and TMU1 samples stage 0's (`prepare`, `vcrdd_d3d.c:1339-1350`).
- **`s_scale`/`t_scale` are 256 on the wide side and 256/aspect on the narrow side**, for every size, TBIG 2048
  included (`vcrdd_3d.c:273-285`). On a 2048-wide texture one S unit is therefore 8 texels.
- **Addressing.** Every view sets `TM_PERSPECTIVE | TM_CLAMPW`, and `TM_CLAMPS/T` only for `D3DTADDRESS_CLAMP`
  (`vcrdd_d3d.c:787-795`, `:815-833`). MIRROR and BORDER fall to WRAP (G33, G86).
- **Nothing rebases, clamps or checks coordinates** anywhere in `vcrdd_3d.c` or `vcrdd_d3d.c`. MesaFX does not either
  (`build/retro3dfx-gl/src/mesa/drivers/glide/fxddtex.c:690-740`, same 256 scaling). Neither of 3dfx's HALs does:
  a case-insensitive search of `VH5` and `H5/Win9x/DX` for coordinate rebasing found nothing. Both multiply the
  coordinate by `scaleS`/`scaleT` (= `MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE_U` = 256 on the wide side, `VH5/D3TXTR.C:1846-1908`) and by
  rhw, with `DCT_FIX` never defined (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:958-984`; `VH5/SOURCES:41-84` and the whole H5 tree define no
  `DCT_FIX`).

### 2.2 What the chip does with it

| stage | format | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| float -> start value at vertex A | 16.32, masked to 48 bits (wraps) | `CSIM/SETUP.C:550-561, 660-705`; `_convertFloat2Fix64` `:413-436` |
| gradients dS/dX, dS/dY | 16.32, 48 bits | `CSIM/SETUP.C:584-592` (`SETUP64_P`) |
| per-pixel S/W, T/W, 1/W | 48-bit iterators, masked each span | `CSIM/GO.C:239-244`; `TRX/TREXFUNC.H:402, 410` |
| perspective divide | S/W -> 20-bit signed mantissa x 19-bit W mantissa | `TRX/TREXFUNC.C:772-789, 838-849` |
| texel coordinate | 12/13 integer bits plus 8 (or, TBIG, 11) fraction bits; wrap by truncation, clamp flag | `TRX/TREXFUNC.C:896-919`; SPEC §8.76 L3865 |
| LOD | `log2(max abs(dST/dx - ST*dW/dx)) + log2(W) - bias`, 12-bit mantissas in the product | `TRX/TREXFUNC.C:1012-1060, 1116-1230, 1430-1452`; `CSIM/LOD.C:58-73` |
| integer registers (unused by us) | `startS` 14.18, sign-extended into the same 48 bits | `CSIM/CSIMIO.C:1094-1130`; SPEC L2244-2245, L2568 |

**Caveat.** CSIM is 3dfx's validation model, and `TREXFUNC.C` calls itself bit accurate, but neither is the silicon.
Step 1's `texrepeat` settles the range on `.124` in one probe run. It tells the 16.32 model from the 14.18 one by
where the first wrap appears and by how many wraps a cell shows (section 3.2).

### 2.3 The numbers

At `s_scale` 256 (wide axis), the largest |u| that stays inside the iterator:

| rhw | 16.32 (CSIM, expected) | 14.18 (the gap's reading) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.01 | 12,800 | 3,200 |
| 0.1 | 1,280 | 320 |
| 0.5 | 256 | 64 |
| 1 | 128 | 32 |
| 2 | 64 | 16 |
| 10 | 12.8 | 3.2 |

The TMU precision is independent of rhw: the texel error is of the order of |u| x 256 x 2^-19 texels (up to ~3x with
W's mantissa), which is 1/8 texel at |u| = 256. **x8 on a 2048-wide texture.**

**When a wrap shows.** One wrap moves S by 65536/q S units. That is invisible when 256/q is a whole number (16.32).
Under the 14.18 reading it would be 16384/q, invisible when 64/q is whole. **Every q = 64/m is therefore silent under
both models.**

### 2.4 Who could reach it (inferred, unmeasured; the census replaces this)

| title | why it might | why it might not |
|---|---|---|
| Halo (D3D8, software VP) | detail maps scale base UVs by 16-64 through texture transforms; world units ~3 m with a small near plane give rhw > 1 on near surfaces | detail stages may be multipass or off on the fixed-function path |
| Battlefield 1942 (LAN only on `.124`, G72) | terrain detail textures from world coordinates, metres, small near plane | the detail coordinates may be patch-relative |
| Max Payne, Red Faction | close cameras, large tiled floors and Geo-Mod surfaces | unknown |
| UT2003/UT2004 (UE2) | terrain layers from world positions | rhw is at most 1 (near plane >= 1 unit, inferred), and terrain is at least ~50 units away, so u x rhw stays far below 128 |
| Dark, Sith, LithTech, Red Storm DX5/DX6 titles | - | per-polygon UVs near the origin (inferred) |

The 11-12 frames do not show the signature.
- The UT2004 terrain zoom (`onbox/games/UT2004_botmatch/UT2004_botmatch_090s_zoom_terrain.png`) and Halo's rock
  triangles (`onbox/games/Halo_timedemo/Halo_timedemo_120s.png`) carry saturated red, green and blue blocks on grey
  and green textures. That is foreign memory, G06/G07.
- A coordinate wrap keeps the texture's own colours and shifts them in depth bands. **G89 may co-exist there, unseen.**
  The census in G06's instrumentation boot says so directly.

## 3. The investigation

### 3.1 Step 0: the census (log only, default-on)

**Data, per context** (`vcr_d3dctx` gains `vcr3d_stcensus st`; all fields `ULONG`, so the integer-only HAL file can
own and log it):

```c
typedef struct vcr3d_stcensus {      /* KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.h - written by vcrdd_3d.c (x87), logged by the HAL */
    ULONG tris;                      /* textured triangles scanned */
    ULONG sw_max, s_max, q_max;      /* largest |S/W| or |T/W|, |S| (= |u|*scale), rhw: IEEE bits
                                      * (positive floats order as unsigned integers) */
    ULONG over_8k, over_16k, over_32k;  /* triangles with a TMU axis at or above 8192 / 16384 / 32768 */
    ULONG over_clamp;                /* ... >= 32768 on a CLAMP axis: a rebase cannot help */
    ULONG over_flat;                 /* ... >= 32768 with three equal rhw and 256/rhw whole: the wrap is invisible */
    ULONG s_risk;                    /* triangles with |S| >= the TMU precision threshold (section 4.2) */
    ULONG q_over1, q_over2;          /* triangles with rhw > 1, >= 2 */
    ULONG rebased, unfixable, skipped;   /* F1 only (0 while it is off) */
    ULONG first_logged;
} vcr3d_stcensus;
```

**Where it is computed.** In `VcrDd3dTriangle` (`vcrdd_3d.c:225-239`), before the first vertex is written:
- a new static `st_scan(d, a, b, c, &off)` reads the three vertices' rhw and their coordinates for each active TMU;
- it forms exactly the products `vertex()` will write;
- it updates the counters once per triangle.

It runs inside the existing `EngSaveFloatingPointState` bracket. It costs about 30 x87 operations per triangle,
against the 27-45 uncached register writes the same triangle already costs (G48). It writes no register, so the
register traffic is unchanged byte for byte.

**Plumbing.**
- `vcr3d_draw` gains `vcr3d_stcensus *st` and `ULONG st_mask` (section 4.2).
- `D3d_DrawPrimitives2` sets `w.d.st = &c->st;` right after `VcrDd3dDrawInit` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1862`).
- No float enters `vcrdd_d3d.c`; `test_floats_live_in_one_object_behind_the_fpu_bracket` keeps passing.

**Log** (event `VCR_EV_DD_D3D`; the "what" numbers are provisional and allocated at integration against G02 §3.7):

- **513/`STCENSUS`, INFO, in `ctx_free`** (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1203-1215`), only when `st.tris > 0`:
  `"context %u texcoords: %u tris, max |S/W| %08x |S| %08x rhw %08x; >=8192 %u >=16384 %u >=32768 %u (clamp %u,
  invisible %u), |S| risk %u, rhw>1 %u rhw>=2 %u; rebased %u unfixable %u skipped %u"`.
- **513/`STFIRST`, INFO, once per context:** the first triangle at or above 16384. It names the TMU, the axis, its
  three coordinates and rhw as IEEE bits, CLAMP or WRAP, and the bound texture handle, so the offending surface can be
  found.
- `tools/vcrlog.py` gains a decoder that prints the bit fields as floats (optional).

The line sits next to 513/2 at the end of a session, so it survives the ring's wrap if the ring is fetched as soon as
the program exits (`onbox/README.md`, "per-run ring.txt keeps only that session's last 1024 entries"). G76's counter
framework can absorb these fields when it lands.

### 3.2 Step 1: the d3dprobe tests (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`)

**General rules.**
- All four are explicit (`--tests ...`), never in the default list. Each fits the probe's 256x256 readback window.
- Expected values are analytic: the ideal D3D WRAP result.
- A hardware oracle predicts what the 16.32 model (H16) and the 14.18 model (H14) would show instead, so each cell is
  classified rather than just failed.
  - The oracle is `vcr_ref_st_hw()` in G74's `KMD/include/vcr_d3dref.h`, created by this item if it lands first.
  - It takes a true S/W, rhw and a modulus (65536 or 16384), and returns the wrapped texel.
  - It is native-tested (section 6.2), and no driver source includes it (G74's rule).
- **Shared helper:** `quad_tq(x0, y0, x1, y1, u0, u1, v0, v1, q_top, q_bot)` draws a two-triangle strip with
  per-edge rhw.

**Textures** (R5G6B5, MANAGED, point min/mag, MIPFILTER NONE unless stated, WRAP):
- `BAND_U` 256x256, texel (x, y) = `PAL[x >> 4]`;
- `BAND_V`, the same in y;
- `BAND1` 256x256, `PAL[x & 15]` (1-texel bands);
- `PAL` is 16 colours, exact after 565 expansion, every pair at least 48 apart in some channel, none equal to the
  clear colour.

#### `texrepeat` (E1): the range, the failure mode, both axes, both TMUs, and rhw > 2

**Cell shape.** A cell is a 128x8 px quad with constant rhw q and u from k to k+1 across it.
- **Samples:** 16 per cell, at (x0 + 8i + 4, y0 + 4), each one band centre.
- **Each sample is classified** as `ideal`, `wrap16`, `wrap14`, `sat` (constant colour) or `other`.
- **The cell's `model`** is the one all of its samples agree with.
- **Pass (D3D):** `ideal`.
- **Without F1:** a cell whose oracle says it wraps is `xfail G89` (G74's outcome model), and the note gives the shift.

**Cells** (rows of 8 px, two columns; one frame for TMU0, one for TMU1):

A cell spans u in [k, k+1], so its |S/W| runs from 256·q·k to 256·q·(k+1). **One wrap up** (the value passes
+limit) reads band **(i - 5) mod 16** at q = 0.75. That is the same under both models: -65536/192 = -341.33 and
-16384/192 = -85.33 repeats are both a -1/3 repeat, i.e. -5.33 bands. **Two wraps up** read (i + 5) mod 16, and
**one wrap down** (k negative) reads (i + 5) mod 16. So at q = 0.75 the models are told apart by **where** the wrap
starts (k = 43 against k = 171), and at k = 171 by **how many** wraps (one against two). The probe takes every
sample's prediction from the oracle, so the table is a summary.

**Group F guard.** The F reference cell (k = 0) must read `ideal` first. If it does not, the group is `na` ("stage 1
does not pass stage 0 through on this build": G12).

**Result fields.** `"limit_sw"` is the smallest max |S/W| of a cell that is not `ideal`, leaving out cells where the
oracle says the wrap is invisible. `"mode"` is `wrap16`, `wrap14`, `sat` or `none`. `"w_path"` is `ok` when every D
cell in range is `ideal`.

**The D group is also the check of the "2.30" W reading.** Under it, every D cell would be garbage even at k = 0.
That would be a major finding for every title with a near plane under 0.5. It is expected `ideal`.

#### `texrepeat_span` (E2): many repeats in one triangle, centred and not

**Texture and sampling.** The texture is 256x256: red where frac(u) < 0.5, blue above. Cells are 256x8 px. N repeats
across the cell at P px per repeat. The samples sit at the quarter points, never on an edge.

| q | u span | P | max \|S/W\| | without F1 | with F1 (`STREBASE`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.75 | [-32, 32] | 4 | 6,144 | ideal | ideal (not at risk, unchanged) |
| 0.75 | [150, 214] | 4 | 41,088 (crosses at u = 170.67) | right part `wrap16` (`xfail G89`) | ideal (rebased about 182: \|u'\| <= 32) |
| 0.75 | [1000, 1064] | 4 | 204,288 | `wrap16` throughout | ideal |
| 1.0 | [-64, 64] | 2 | 16,384 | ideal | ideal |
| 3.0 | [-16, 16] | 8 | 12,288 | ideal | ideal |
| 3.0 | [-64, 64] | 2 | 49,152 | wraps | **still wraps**: the span is beyond the cap at rhw 3, so `unfixable` is counted (`xfail G89-span`) |

**The last row is deliberate.** It is the one case F1 cannot fix: a triangle whose own span exceeds the range. D3D
puts that on the application (`MaxTextureRepeat`), and the census counts it.

#### `texrepeat_lod` (E3): the LOD does not move with an integer offset

**Setup.**
- A 256x256 chain with 9 levels, each one solid colour (G06's palette: L0 red, L1 green, L2 blue, L3 yellow,
  L4 magenta, L5 cyan, ...). MIPFILTER POINT.
- Two perspective quads side by side, x in [4, 124) and [132, 252), y in [8, 248).
- rhw is 1.0 at the bottom edge and 0.125 at the top. u runs from k to k + 0.5 across, v from 0 to 16 bottom to top,
  so the rows walk through several levels. Which rows show which level does not matter; only left against right
  does.
- **Frame 1:** left k = 0, right k = 24 (max |S/W| = 24.5 x 256 = 6,272, inside both models).
- **Frame 2:** right k = 100 (25,728, inside H16 only): the cancellation check.

**Read.** The level index of each quad's centre column at 30 rows.

**Pass (D3D):** level(left) == level(right) on every row.

**Tolerance on `.124`.** At most 2 rows may differ, and only by exactly one level: the 12-bit product rounds near a
level boundary.

**Expected:** pass (TREX LOD model, §2.2).

**What a difference would mean.** The LOD depends on the absolute coordinate.
- Record it as a note for G26/G27, and add |S| to F1's risk test for mipmapped stages.
- A difference everywhere matches 86Box/MAME's model. On silicon that would contradict CSIM.
- The comparison is left against right, so a G07 split chain that garbles a level garbles it identically on both
  sides. It does not confound the test.

#### `texrepeat_prec` (E3b): TMU precision at large |u| without overflow

**Setup.**
- `BAND1`, magnified: 128x8 px cells, u from k to k + 16/256, so 8 px per texel.
- **Samples:** each of the 16 texels at its centre (x0 + 8j + 4) and both edges (x0 + 8j + 1, x0 + 8j + 6).
- **Cells:** k = 0 (q = 1); k = 32, 128, 256, 512, 1000 and 3000, with q = 64/k so that |S/W| stays at 16,384, mid-range.
- **Optional TBIG cells:** `BAND1` at 2048x256, needing D3DBigTex bit 0, `SKIPPED` otherwise. Their predicted error
  is x8.

**Predicted error** (TRX 20-bit S/W mantissa): k/2048 texels, i.e. 0.016, 0.06, 0.125, 0.25, 0.49, 1.46. W's 19-bit
mantissa can add up to about 2x more.
- Edge samples (0.19 texel from an edge) start to miss somewhere between k = 130 and 400.
- Centres start to miss between k = 350 and 1000.

**Pass (D3D):** exact.

**On our HAL without F1:** a `note` with the first k at which edges and centres miss. Precision is characterised
here, not failed.

**With F1:** exact. The |S| threshold rebases from k = 256.

## 4. The fix

### 4.1 F0: report the range (default-on once E1 has measured it)

**Code, `KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c`:**
- `prim_caps` (`:2034-2035`): add `D3DPTEXTURECAPS_TEXREPEATNOTSCALEDBYSIZE`. It reaches both `dpcTriCaps` and
  `dpcLineCaps` (`:2071-2072`), as in 3dfx's `VH5/D3INIT.C:2824-2826, 3027-3029`.
- The extended caps (`:2150-2173`): `x.dwMaxTextureRepeat = VCR_ST_MAX_REPEAT;` (256).

**Why the flag is the true one.**
- D3D defines the value as "the full range of the integer bits of the post-normalized texture indices", with
  `TEXREPEATNOTSCALEDBYSIZE` meaning the range does not depend on the texture's size.
- Our scale is the constant 256 for every size, so the range in u is the same for a 16-texel and a 2048-texel texture.
- **Without the flag an application would divide 256 by the texture size and conclude a 256 texture cannot repeat
  at all.** So the value and the flag go together, or neither.

**If E1 measures `wrap14`:** `VCR_ST_MAX_REPEAT` = 64. Either way the number is derived from the measured limit by
`vcr_st_max_repeat(limit) = 2 * limit / 256`, so the header carries one constant.

**What changes.** Two caps, visible to every D3D application, and no register traffic. Halo only dumps the caps:
`halo.exe` holds the string in its caps table, and its `config.txt` has no rule on it. **No staged title is known to
branch on the value.**

### 4.2 F1: rebase the at-risk WRAP axes, per triangle (default OFF, built only if E4 asks for it)

**The decision is a pure, host-tested header, `KMD/include/vcr_strebase.h`** (Win32-free).
- An integer section first: the constants and the mask bits, usable from `vcrdd_d3d.c`.
- The float section is guarded by `#ifndef VCR_ST_NO_FLOAT`, which `vcrdd_d3d.c` defines, so no float reaches the
  `-mgeneral-regs-only` object.

```c
#define VCR_ST_MAX_REPEAT   256u        /* dwMaxTextureRepeat: 2 x 32768 / 256 (CSIM/H3SIM.H:47-49) */
#define VCR_ST_AX_S0  0x01u             /* TMU0 S may move by whole periods */
#define VCR_ST_AX_T0  0x02u
#define VCR_ST_AX_S1  0x04u             /* TMU1 */
#define VCR_ST_AX_T1  0x08u
#define VCR_ST_MIR_SHIFT 4              /* bits 4-7: the same axis mirrors (G33): period 2 */
#ifndef VCR_ST_NO_FLOAT
#define VCR_ST_SW_LIMIT 32768.0f        /* |S/W|: the 48-bit 16.32 iterator */
#define VCR_ST_SW_RISK  16384.0f        /* rebase from half of it: room for edge overshoot */
#define VCR_ST_S_RISK   65536.0f        /* |S| at which the 20-bit S/W mantissa leaves 1/8 texel
                                         * on a texture up to 256 (scaled down x size/256 above) */
/* the integer k (a multiple of period) to subtract from c[0..2]: returns VCR_ST_KEEP (k = 0, nothing at risk -
 * the triangle is sent as before), VCR_ST_REBASED, VCR_ST_UNFIXABLE (rebased, still >= the limit) or
 * VCR_ST_SKIPPED (period 0, a non-finite value, rhw <= 0, |k| >= 2^23) */
static __inline unsigned vcr_st_rebase(const float c[3], const float q[3], float sigma, unsigned period,
                                       float s_risk, float *k);
#endif
```

**The algorithm, per axis:**
1. **Skip** for period 0 (a CLAMP axis), a non-finite coordinate, or any q <= 0.
2. **At-risk test.** m_sw = max |c_i * sigma * q_i| (the very products `vertex()` writes) and m_s = max |c_i * sigma|.
   If m_sw < `VCR_ST_SW_RISK` and m_s < `s_risk`, then k = 0 and the triangle is unchanged.
3. **Weighted minimax.** The best real offset minimises max_i q_i |c_i - k|. For each pair with c_i >= c_j, compute
   M_ij = (c_i - c_j) q_i q_j / (q_i + q_j). The largest M_ij binds, and k* = (c_i q_i + c_j q_j) / (q_i + q_j).
   - By 1D Helly, the intervals [c_l - M*/q_l, c_l + M*/q_l] then all contain k*, so k* is optimal.
   - When the q are equal it is the midpoint.
   - In a perspective floor it sits by the near vertex, where S/W is largest. A plain midpoint would put the near
     vertex half a span away, at the largest q.
4. **Round.** k = period x round(k*/period), half away from zero, through an integer cast: the file already converts
   floats that way (`vcrdd_3d.c:153`). No libm.
5. **Flag.** If max |(c_i - k) sigma q_i| is still >= `VCR_ST_SW_LIMIT`, the result is `UNFIXABLE`. It is still sent,
   and counted.

**Where it runs, `KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c`:**
- `st_scan()` (section 3.1) also computes the offsets for the axes in `d->st_mask`. It returns a pointer to a filled
  `vcr3d_stoff {float s0, t0, s1, t1;}`, or **NULL when no axis moved**.
- `vertex(pd, d, v, po)` keeps **today's statements, text and all, on the NULL branch**, and adds a second branch:

  ```c
  if (!po) {                                              /* the proven path, unchanged */
      wf(pd, V3D_SSOW0, uv[0] * d->s_scale * oow);
      wf(pd, V3D_STOW0, uv[1] * d->t_scale * oow);
  } else {                                                /* G89 F1: whole periods removed */
      wf(pd, V3D_SSOW0, (uv[0] - po->s0) * d->s_scale * oow);
      wf(pd, V3D_STOW0, (uv[1] - po->t0) * d->t_scale * oow);
  }
  ```

  It is the same for TMU1 with `s1`/`t1`.
- Keeping the old text matters. G45 proposes a source test that pins `uv[0] * d->s_scale * oow` in `vertex()`
  (`G45.md:307`). G81's optional texel nudge (`G81.md:293`) composes as `(... * d->s_scale + d->s_nudge) * oow` on
  both branches.
- `VcrDd3dTexScale`/`VcrDd3dTexScale1` (FPU) also store the precision threshold per TMU:
  `d->s_risk = VCR_ST_S_RISK * 256 / max(w, h)`, i.e. 8192 for a 2048 texture.

**Which axes, `KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c` `prepare()`** (integer only, after `compute_regs`):
- `w->d.st_mask` is built from what the TMU is about to do, not from D3D's state names.
- An axis is rebasable when its textureMode CLAMP bit (`TM_CLAMPS`/`TM_CLAMPT`, `KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h:147-148`)
  is clear in `regs.textureMode` (TMU0) or `regs.textureMode1` (TMU1), for a TMU in use.
- A mirror bit (G33's tLOD bits 28-29, when it lands) adds the axis's bit at `VCR_ST_MIR_SHIFT`, which makes the
  period 2.
- Only when `pd->st_rebase`; otherwise the mask is 0 and `st_scan` only counts.

**What it guarantees:**
- **Exact for WRAP.** Subtracting a whole period from u moves S by k x sigma, a multiple of the axis's period in
  S units at every LOD. That holds for bilinear taps too, because all of them truncate.
- **The float subtraction loses nothing.** Wherever F1 acts, |k| is large against the span, so c and k lie within a
  factor of 2 and c - k is exact (Sterbenz). Where k is 0 there is nothing to subtract. The products that follow are
  smaller, so they round less, not more.
- **The LOD is unchanged** (§2.2), apart from making the 12-bit cancellation smaller. E3 confirms it.
- **Adjacent triangles may get different k with no seam:** WRAP is periodic, and the rounding difference is
  2^-24 of a smaller number.

**Interactions:**
- **G49 (hardware strips).** A triangle that F1 rebases is always sent as an independent triangle, never continued.
  G49's own guard already breaks any continuation above |S/W| 256 (the vendor's "continuation WRAP bug"), which is far
  below F1's 16384, so in practice a run never carries a rebased vertex. G49's guard tests the values actually
  written, i.e. after a rebase.
- **G33 (MIRROR).** The period is 2, from the mask.
- **G86 (cylindrical WRAPn, if ever done on the host as `VH5/D3TRI.H:110-140` does).** It runs before the rebase.
- **G92 (projected coordinates, if ever).** That stage's mask bit stays clear.
- **G81 (a texel-centre offset, if ever).** It is added after scaling, so it is untouched.
- **G16 (lines and points as quads).** They pass through `VcrDd3dTriangle`, so they are covered.

**Cost when armed.** The at-risk test adds about 12 x87 operations per axis. The minimax runs only for an at-risk
axis, at about 40 operations. Both are negligible against the PCI writes.

### 4.3 Considered and rejected

- **F2, per-triangle W normalisation** (scale TMU 1/W, S/W and T/W by 1/max q). It is invariant for the perspective
  divide and the LOD, and would help where rhw > 1. **Rejected:** W is 16.32 (CSIM), and rebasing already shrinks
  |S/W| at any rhw. It is revived only if E1's D group fails.
- **Splitting triangles whose span exceeds the range.** That is perspective-correct re-interpolation of every
  attribute, on the CPU, for a case D3D's contract assigns to the application. It is reconsidered only if E4 counts a
  large `unfixable` in a title.
- **One offset per DP2 batch or primitive.** A terrain sector or strip can span more than the range. It saves nothing
  measurable, and it couples triangles that are independent today.
- **Always rebase every triangle.** It would change the register values of every textured triangle (a larger A/B
  surface) for no gain, because the LOD is already offset-invariant on this TMU.
- **Reporting 32768.** That is 3dfx's `#else` value, for a device that scales by the texture size (no flag). An
  application reads it in texels: 128 repeats of a 256 texture, but only 16 of a 2048 one. That is not what our
  constant 256 scale does, and 3dfx's shipped builds used 256 with the flag.
- **Leaving 0.** A 0 tells an application nothing. 3dfx's own number for this chip is 256.

  - E2: rows 1-5 `ideal`, row 6 `xfail` with `unfixable` counted.
  - E3b: exact.
  - The default gate and the 11-12 texture lists (`d3dprobe_textures_a/b` sets) give identical results.
  - **The title scene that E4 flagged,** at the same mode and the same time stamps as boot A: before and after frames
    side by side, `rebased` > 0, and no new artefact.
- **Graduation:** after boot B passes, `STREBASE` becomes default-on (a per-feature kill bit, per the plan's
  convention).

**What 86Box can and cannot add.** Its setup unit keeps 32.32 in int64 with no 48-bit mask (`86B/voodoo_setup.c:204`),
so `texrepeat` passes there with or without F1. Use it only for:
- an A/B that F1 is visually neutral (identical frames);
- crash safety before boot B.

## 8. Risk to proven paths

- **Default 16 bpp path.**
  - Census: CPU-only reads and counters, no register traffic change.
  - F0: two caps.
  - F1 off: byte-identical.
  - F1 on: byte-identical below the thresholds, which covers everything in the default gate.
- **Glide/OpenGL coexistence, SLI state, the flip path, the miniport:** untouched. The work is CPU-side vertex
  arithmetic inside the existing FPU bracket of `VcrDd3dTriangle`.
- **The FPU rule.** All float work stays in `vcrdd_3d.c`, and `vcrdd_d3d.c` only computes an integer mask. The
  existing `test_floats_live_in_one_object_behind_the_fpu_bracket` holds.
- **Wrong-axis rebase.** Rebasing a CLAMP axis would shift a clamped texture. The mask is derived from the TMU's own
  clamp bits and pinned by both tests.
- **Caps.** An application that branches on `MaxTextureRepeat` > 0 takes a different path. None is known. The boot A'
  menu pass covers it.
- **Cost.** The census adds about 30 x87 operations per textured triangle, unmeasurable beside 27-45 uncached
  register writes (G48). F1 adds about 40 more only on at-risk axes.
- **Stability:** no new hardware access, no new wait, no new allocation. One log line per context.

## 11. Open questions

1. Does the VSA-100 silicon iterate the setup unit's S/W in 48-bit 16.32, as CSIM and the TREX bit model state, and
   does it wrap rather than saturate? E1 settles both, and its D group settles 1/W at rhw > 2.
2. Is the silicon's LOD offset-invariant, as `TREXFUNC.C`'s `lod_calc_sq_term` says? E3. The answer also matters for
   G26/G27, because it says whether 86Box's LOD model can stand in for `.124` in their tests (it cannot, if E3
   passes).
3. What rhw and |u| do the staged titles send? This is Halo's near plane, BF1942's terrain detail coordinates, and
   Max Payne's close cameras. E4.
4. Is the TMU precision loss at large |u| (E3b) visible in any title? `s_risk` in E4, then a frame check.
5. The vendor's "continuation WRAP bug" (no autostrip above S/W 257, `VH5/D6TRI2I.C:986-995`) suggests a narrower path
   in the strip continuation than in independent triangles. Its mechanism is undocumented. G49 owns it. If G49's E4
   shows it, F1's at-risk threshold does not change, because F1 rebased triangles are never continued.
6. The D3DFeat bit number for `STREBASE`, and the 513 "what" codes for `STCENSUS`/`STFIRST`/`STARMED`: integrator's
   allocation (G02 §3.7 collision table).
7. Should F0 wait for E1, or land on 3dfx's precedent? This design says wait. One probe run is cheap, and an
   unmeasured cap is what this gap exists to remove.
8. **Doc corrections for the integrator** (this design may not edit them):
   - `reports/hw.md` §1/§4.5 "about +/-32 repeats": +/-128 at rhw 1 (16.32 setup-unit iterators); 14.18 is the integer
     registers only.
   - `reports/hal.md` §3 "3dfx reported 32768": 256 + `TEXREPEATNOTSCALEDBYSIZE` in both the W2K and the Win9x
     retail builds.
   - The quad sizing in G06, G07, G26 and G49 ("below 8192") stays valid. It is conservative by 4x.

## 0. Verdict

**The half rate has not reproduced on any build after 2026-09-26. The gap as written is stale.**
Recommendation: reclassify G59 as *"not reproduced since 2026-09-27; the cause of the 09-26
measurement is not established"*, give it **P3**, and close it after one short confirmation run
(E1, §4.2). That run needs no driver change, no Diag change and no reboot.

The record that the gap and both reports rest on (games §5.5 item 4; history §3.4 and §8 item 11)
stops at **2026-09-27 00:43**. That is when `d0e6af7` wrote the README and FINDINGS paragraph
"the 16 bpp half rate is NOT the completion rule". **Three later silicon measurements exist, and no
document mentions them:**

1. **`SIL/vcrkmd-v5-7.jsonl`**, 09-27 21:48, build install8 (the evidence for `66a75cc`). It is the
   same battery as v5-2 in the same order: the 16 bpp flip is the first fullscreen run and the only
   depth change, 60 frames, on the same Sony CPD-G200 at the same 800x600@85.
   - 16 bpp: **87.4 flips/s; first-to-last 86.1; slow_frames 0; longest frame 14.61 ms; first
     frame 0.78 ms.**
   - 32 bpp: 87.3 / 86.1.
2. **`SIL/vcrkmd-256-1.jsonl`**, 09-27 23:10, the 256 MB VBIOS (`dbec148`), HP P1120 monitor,
   800x600@100.
   - 16 bpp: **102.6; first-to-last 101.3; slow 0.**
   - 32 bpp: 101.8 / 100.6.
3. **`ONBOX/ring_after_probes_decoded.txt`**, 11-12, the deployed `95678704` build. The D3D8 perf
   run at **640x480x16@85 with vsync** made 85.1 fps (`ONBOX/d3dprobe_perf_640x480x16_vsync.txt`)
   and logged:
   - **511/12 `flips 300: retrace 297, deadline 2, superseded 0, pending 1 (mode off)`**
   - 511/13 `flip reads 262965: longest 1228 us; longest wait 6728 us; deadline 13235 us`

   These are the **first silicon flip counters on record, and they are healthy.** The
   1024x768x32@85 run made 84.4 fps with retrace 284, deadline 15.

The two half-rate runs, **v5-2 (42.1)** and **v5-6 (46.9)**:
- were made on **09-26 builds**;
- used a ddlab with **no per-frame timing**;
- ran a flip completion that is **code-identical to today's**: `d0e6af7` is a refactor, pinned to
  the tick by the host test.

The rule bounds a DDLOCK_WAIT spinner at no less than about 1/1.125 of the refresh, whatever the
poll cadence and whatever the sense of the retrace flag (§2). Those runs therefore lost 0.50-0.65 s
*outside* the rule. The data cannot say whether that was one stall or every frame.

**What this design adds.** All of it is cheap:

1. **The record correction** (§5.1).
2. **A 12-minute confirmation (E1, §4.2)** on the deployed build. It uses the existing ddlab plus two
   small tool additions, ddlab `vbduty` and d3dprobe `vsync`. There is no driver change.
3. **Logging-only counters**, event 511 a=15 and a=16 (§5.3):
   - NOVSYNC presents;
   - a histogram of the time between presents;
   - the time callers waited.

   With these, any later session, a game included, records by itself whether it was limited by
   the driver, limited by the game, or had no vsync. The recorder must be large enough to keep
   them. The existing `Diag\LogEntries` does that (§4.4).
4. **A vendor finding the flip rule depends on (§3):**
   - Every 3dfx display driver reads `status[6]` as the **raw VSYNC level** and XORs it with the
     sync polarity in Misc Output bit 7. That covers the H5 drivers for W2K, NT4 and Win9x, and the
     Mac "Avenger" driver.
   - Our miniport reads "clear = in retrace" at every mode, as Glide's `grSstVRetrace` and 86Box do.
   - **The desktop mode 1280x1024@85, and 800x600@85, 1024x768@85 and 1280x960@85, all have
     positive vsync.** If the vendor is right, our `in_vblank` is inverted at those modes.

   That does **not** change a flip *rate* (§3.3; a host test pins it), so it is not G59's cause.
   It does three other things:
   - it inverts GetScanLine, GetVerticalBlankStatus and D3D8 GetRasterStatus;
   - it moves the returns of WaitForVerticalBlank BLOCKBEGIN and BLOCKEND by one sync pulse;
   - it lets a flip written *inside* the pulse complete one frame early.

   E1 settles the question read-only, with no mode switch, because the desktop mode has positive
   vsync. The fix (F-P) waits on that result and would land default-off.

The contingent fixes apply only if E1 reproduces a low rate. §5.4 lists each one with its
trigger. The robust one is the vendor's own completion test: the chip's swap-pending count
(`swapBufferPend`, plus `swapbufferCMD` waiting for vsync). That mechanism belongs to G54.

## 2. Why 42-47 flips/s cannot come from the completion rule

### 2.1 ddlab's loop is a spinner

Each frame (`KMD/tools/ddlab.c:1093-1121`) runs these steps:
1. `Lock(back, DDLOCK_WAIT)`
2. write two rows
3. `Unlock`
4. `Flip(DDFLIP_WAIT)`
5. `Lock(front)`
6. read the two rows back
7. `Unlock`

The back buffer is the surface the previous flip is taking off the screen. `Dd_Lock`
(`KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c:647-659`) answers `DDERR_WASSTILLDRAWING` for it until `flip_done`, and
the runtime retries. That is a spin of vblank IOCTLs. When `Flip` comes, it finds the previous flip
already done.

### 2.2 The rule's bound

`vcr_flip_poll` (`KMD/include/vcr_flip.h:156-183`) completes a flip in one of two ways:
- at the first poll that reads "in retrace", after a poll that read "active" after the write;
- otherwise, at the deadline: one nominal frame + 1/8 = **47,376 ticks = 13.235 ms at 85 Hz**
  (`vcr_flip_deadline`, `:116-128`; 511/13 shows `deadline 13235 us` on silicon).

A spinner's period is therefore at most *deadline + P + δ*:
- P is the poll cadence, 13.4 µs mean on 11-12.
- δ is the time from completion to the next start-address write: Lock returns, two rows, Unlock,
  then the Flip call. That is well under 1 ms (inferred).

That gives **at least ≈ 75 flips/s at 85 Hz**, the README's "~77 floor". It holds for either sense
of `status[6]` (§3.3): an inverted flag still needs one poll inside the pulse, or it falls back to
the deadline. **To halve the rate, a rule has to need a second retrace for every flip.** The header
has no such path. The new host test pins the mutation that would (§8) as the 09-26 signature.

### 2.3 What 42.1 and 46.9 imply

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| 60 frames at 42.1 flips/s | 1.425 s |
| 60 frames at 46.9 flips/s | 1.279 s |
| the rule's floor for 60 frames | ≈ 0.78 s |
| time lost outside the rule, per run | **0.50-0.65 s** |

That loss is either one stall of 0.5-0.65 s or about 8-11 ms on every frame. The 09-26 ddlab
cannot tell which: it took no per-frame time stamps. Those arrived in `d0e6af7` for exactly this
question (`ddlab.c:13-19`).

The v5-2 run has a second anomaly. Its `vblank_hz` was **87.6** at 16 bpp, against 85.0 in every
other run. That means one of its 30 BLOCKBEGIN waits returned about one frame early. The BLOCKBEGIN
loops end early only if a vblank IOCTL *fails* (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:166-174`: `&& vblank(pd, &v)` ends
the loop and the wait returns DD_OK), or if the flag glitches. That run's timing was disturbed in
more than one way.

### 2.4 Hypotheses for 09-26, and what E1 shows for each

| # | hypothesis | E1 signature |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | **a one-time stall at session start**. Candidates: the first Lock maps all video memory into the process (`Dd_MapMemory`, `IOCTL_VIDEO_SHARE_VIDEO_MEMORY`, 32 MB a chip then); GDI repaints at the new depth after the 32→16 change while it holds the devlock; the CRT settles after the mode set | `flips_s` low but first-to-last ≈ R; one long `first_frame_ms` or `max_frame_ms`; slow_frames ≈ 1 |

## 3. A vendor finding: `status[6]` is the VSYNC level, and its sense follows the sync polarity

### 3.1 The evidence (vendor source, guidance only; filenames found case-insensitively)

The vendor drivers XOR the bit with the sync polarity:
- **`VH5/DDFLIP.C:1031-1069` `FXINVERTICALBLANK`**:
  `if ((status & SST_VRETRACE) ^ (_FF(ddMiscFlags) & DDMF_VSYNC_POLARITY_MASK)) return TRUE;`
  If `dacMode` holds the DPMS or force-sync bits, the function returns a faked toggle instead,
  because "the VSYNC status does not change when in DPMS". That comment says the bit is the sync
  *output*.
- **`VH5/DDFXNT.C:654-656`**: `ddMiscFlags |= (inp(pjIoBase + 0xCC) & 0x80) >> (7 - DDMF_VSYNC_POLARITY_BIT)`.
  That reads Misc Output (port 0x3CC); bit 7 set means negative vsync, and it becomes flag bit 6
  (`VH5/DRIVER.H:815-818`).
- The same XOR appears in:
  - `VH5/DDGLOBAL.H:584-586` (`WAIT_ON_VSYNC`);
  - `VH5/PALETTE.C:598-681`;
  - the NT4 tree, `H5/WinNT/Src/Video/Displays/H5/DDFLIP.C:804`;
  - the Win9x DirectDraw drivers: `H5/Win9x/DX/DD16/QMODES.C:821`, `SETMODE.C:1188`,
    `H5/Win9x/DX/DD32/DDFXS32.C:1006`, `DDFLIP.C:1069`.
- **`H5/MacOS8/GDX/SRC/Avenger_Interrupt_Handling.c:162-178`**: "Negative sync, trigger on falling
  edge" and "Positive sync, trigger on rising edge" (intrCtrl `SST_INTR_VSYNC_FALLING/RISING_ENABLE`,
  `GraphicsPrivData.h:43-50`). The same file's wait loop at `:226-243` uses
  `(status & SST_VRETRACE) ^ syncPolarity`.

Code that ignores polarity:
- Glide's `grSstVRetrace`: `return ((status & SST_VRETRACE) == 0)`, at `H5/GLIDE3/SRC/GSST.C:3292-3303`.
- The HAL test utility, `H5/HAL/UTIL.C:235-253`.
- The vendor miniport's DDC code waits on both edges, so it does not depend on polarity
  (`H5/W2K/Src/Video/Miniport/H5/DDC.C:144-150`).
- 86Box sets status bit 6 = `!(cgastat & 8)`, the VGA retrace, which has no polarity
  (`~/retro-vm/86box/vid_voodoo_banshee.c:1147-1148`).

Ours: `KMD/miniport/vcrmp_dd.c:89-93` sets
`v->in_vblank = (VcrRd(x, 0, VCR_R_STATUS) & VCR_STATUS_VRETRACE) ? 0 : 1` at every mode. The 09-26
sense fix was found on 86Box (`KMD/README.md`: "status[6] is CLEAR during vertical retrace ... on
silicon too. Found on the 86Box bed"). The README sentence names no mode for the silicon part.

### 3.2 Our modes' polarity

`KMD/common/vcr_modes.c:283-287` computes `misc = 0x0f | HNEG<<6 | VNEG<<7` from the timing flags.

| mode | vsync | line (µs) | vtotal | pulse (µs) | duty if the flag is "low in the pulse" (our model) | our duty if the vendor's model holds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 640x480@85 (R6, AvP, RF as measured, SS2, Thief Gold, d3dprobe perf) | **−** | 23.11 | 509 | 69.3 | 0.59 % | 0.59 % (same) |
| 800x600@85 (every ddlab run) | + | 18.63 | 631 | 55.9 | 0.48 % | **99.52 %** |
| 1024x768@85 | + | 14.56 | 808 | 43.7 | 0.37 % | **99.63 %** |
| 1280x960@85 (JK, MotS, Shogo, UE2, Halo, Thief II) | + | 11.64 | 1011 | 34.9 | 0.30 % | **99.70 %** |
| 1280x1024@85 (the desktop) | + | 10.97 | 1072 | 32.9 | 0.28 % | **99.72 %** |

Negative-vsync modes in the table: every 640x480 mode, 1024x768@60/70, 320x240, 512x384, 720x480
and 1600x1024. Every other mode has positive vsync.

### 3.3 What an inverted sense does, and what it does not do

- **The flip rate does not change.** Read inverted:
  - "seen active" is set only by a poll inside the pulse;
  - "done" comes at the first poll after the pulse.

  The correct sense also needs a poll inside the pulse. Either way the cost is one poll in a
  33-70 µs window, or else the deadline. The §8 host test pins this.
- **A rare early completion.** Take a write that lands *inside* the pulse, after this vsync's latch:
  - inverted, it is sampled as "active", so it completes at the end of the pulse, **one frame
    early**;
  - with the correct sense, it is sampled "in retrace" and waits a frame, so it is never early.

  The chance is about pulse/frame: 0.3-0.5 % of flips when the write phase is random (the D3D
  pattern), and about 0 for a spinner, which writes just after a pulse. When it happens, the
  application renders into, or flips away from, a buffer that stays on screen for one more frame:
  a tear or a dropped frame.
- **WaitForVerticalBlank:**
  - BLOCKBEGIN returns at the *end* of the pulse, one pulse late;
  - BLOCKEND returns at its *start*;
  - `DDWAITVB_I_TESTVB` answers "in VB" for the whole visible frame.
- **GetScanLine, and D3D8 GetRasterStatus through it**, answer `DDERR_VERTICALBLANKINPROGRESS` for
  about 99.5 % of the frame.
- **Today's evidence cannot tell the two models apart.**
  - Every ddlab run on silicon was at a positive-vsync mode, and each is consistent with both
    senses.
  - The `scanline 0` of v5-7 and 256-1 is sampled straight after a BLOCKBEGIN, which is inside the
    blank under either model.

### 3.4 How the vendor completes a flip

In `VH5/DDFLIP.C:218-279` the vendor driver:
1. waits for the previous flip. `FXGETFLIPSTATUS` is the swaps-pending count in status bits 30:28
   (`:1005-1016`; `SST_SWAPBUFPENDING`, `VH5/H3DEFS.H:106-107`);
2. calls `FXBUSYWAIT`;
3. writes `vidDesktopStartAddr`;
4. calls `INCSWAPCOUNT()`, which writes `swapBufferPend` = 0 (`:76-78`);
5. queues `swapbufferCMD` = `waitOnVsync`.

The chip lowers the count when that swap runs at vsync, so **the hardware decides when the flip is
complete**. There is no pulse to catch, no polarity and no deadline. That mechanism is G54's
hardware swap queue, which must cap pending swaps at 6. It is fix F2c below.

### 4.4 E2: real titles (part of G73's in-game sweep, after G65)

   - 511/4 (`DirectDraw exclusive 1`, the session start);

**Titles:**

- Red Faction, 640x480x16 as measured, with `Vsync=0` staged (G65/G66).

**Classify each session:**

| class | signature | what it means |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | vsync'd; the "≈1 frame" bucket dominant; average ≈ R | the game runs at the refresh |
| (b) | vsync'd; the 1.2-1.5, ≈2 or ≥2.5 buckets dominant with waits ≈ 0 | the game's own frame time. That is a CPU or HAL throughput question (G46-G53), not the flip path |
| (c) | the ≈2 bucket dominant with waits ≈ flips and mean wait ≥ 0.5 R | **the driver holds each flip for a second refresh**: G59 is real, go to E3 |
| (d) | `novsync` ≈ presents | the title presents without vsync: a G65 config item (the user wants vsync on) |

### 5.3 Counters (logging only; `KMD/include/vcr_flip.h`, `KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c`)

New fields in `vcr_flip_state`, zeroed by `vcr_flip_stats_reset`:

```c
vcr_u32   novsync;      /* DDFLIP_NOVSYNC presents: never pending, not in `flips` */
vcr_u32   iv[6];        /* present-to-present, in nominal frames (1/16 steps):
                         * <0.5, <1.0625, <1.1875, <1.5, <2.5, >=2.5 */
vcr_u32   have_last;
vcr_ticks last;         /* the previous present since the reset */
vcr_u32   waits;        /* waits that ended: a caller was told "still drawing", then done */
vcr_ticks wait_sum;     /* their total */
```

```c
/* every present, NOVSYNC too; frame = the NOMINAL frame in ticks (qpf / pd->freq) */
static inline void vcr_flip_note_present(vcr_flip_state *s, int novsync, vcr_ticks now,
                                         vcr_ticks frame)
{
    if (novsync)
        s->novsync++;
    if (s->have_last && frame > 0 && now >= s->last) {
        vcr_ticks d = (now - s->last) * 16;
        s->iv[d < frame * 8 ? 0 : d < frame * 17 ? 1 : d < frame * 19 ? 2 :
              d < frame * 24 ? 3 : d < frame * 40 ? 4 : 5]++;
    }
    s->last = now;
    s->have_last = 1;
}
```

**`vcr_flip_poll`.** Only the bookkeeping where a wait ends changes:

```c
if (s->waiting) {
    vcr_ticks w = now - s->wait_t0;
    s->waits++;
    s->wait_sum += w;
    if (w > s->max_wait)
        s->max_wait = w;
}
```

Every return value stays as it is.

**`vcr_flip_note_owner`.** Every present now names the session (it was vsync'd flips only).
Today a session made only of NOVSYNC presents has owner 0, so *any* process's DestroyDDLocal can
log and reset it in mid-session. That breaks the "another process's teardown does not cut a live
session" invariant, which the vsync'd case already keeps.

**`vcr_flip_stats_any`** adds `|| s->novsync`. Today a NOVSYNC session is "any" only through its
`polls`, because `flip_sample` counts one poll per flip.

**Glue in `Dd_Flip`** (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:315-361`), after `vcr_flip_begin`:

```c
vcr_flip_note_present(&pd->flip, novsync, now, f / (pd->freq > 1 ? pd->freq : 60));
```

**`flip_stats_log`** (`:277-292`) adds two INFO lines, each within the 96-character message
limit (`VCR_LOG_MSG_LEN`):
- 511 a=15, with b=novsync, c=waits, d=wait µs:
  `presents: novsync %u, waits %u (%u us)`
- 511 a=16, with b=iv[1], c=iv[2], d=iv[4]:
  `frames apart: <.5 %u, ~1 %u, 1.06-1.19 %u, 1.19-1.5 %u, ~2 %u, >2.5 %u`

Codes 15 and 16 are unused under 511 (`grep VCR_EV_DD_DDRAW, 1[5-9]`: none). Also update the 511
text in `KMD/include/vcr_events.h:73`.

With these fields, the class (a)-(e) of §4.4 is read straight off a session's last lines. The
09-26 runs had no way to make that distinction.

## 7. d3dprobe test: `d3dprobe vsync` (test key `vsync_rate`)

```
d3dprobe vsync [--res WxH] [--bpp 16|32] [--frames N] [--hz N] [--work-us N]
```

**The device:**
- fullscreen (`vsync` implies `--full`);
- `D3DSWAPEFFECT_FLIP` with one back buffer;
- **`FullScreen_PresentationInterval = D3DPRESENT_INTERVAL_ONE`**;
- `FullScreen_RefreshRateInHz = --hz`, default 85, so the reference rate is known;
- software vertex processing, as `perf`.

**The raster window**, before the loop: 0.5 s of polling `IDirect3DDevice8::GetRasterStatus`. It
goes through `Dd_GetScanLine` and needs `D3DCAPS_READ_SCANLINE`, which comes from our
`DDCAPS_READSCANLINE`. It yields:
- `raster_hz`, from the **median** period between InVBlank rising edges, so a missed pulse cannot
  bias it;
- `invb_duty`.

**The loop**, `--frames` (600) times:
1. a target-only `Clear` that alternates two colours;
2. one 64x64 checker-textured quad (2 triangles, MODULATE, as `perf`);
3. `Present`;
4. a QPC stamp after each `Present`;
5. `--work-us` of busy work after `Present`, if asked.

**RESULT fields:** `present_hz`, `present_hz_first_last`, `raster_hz`,
`ratio` (= first_last / raster), `first_frame_ms`, `max_frame_ms`, `min_frame_ms`,
`slow_frames` (>1.5 R), `two_frame` (1.5-2.5 R), `fast_frames` (<0.5 R), `invb_duty`, `sense`,
`work_us`.

**Pass**, with `--work-us 0`:
- **0.98 ≤ ratio ≤ 1.02.** References: the 11-12 d3dprobe perf made 85.1 fps at 85 Hz
  (ratio 1.00); v5-7 ddlab made 86.1 first-to-last at `vblank_hz` 85.0 (1.013).
- `slow_frames + two_frame` ≤ 3 of 600.
- `fast_frames` ≤ 1. The first interval may legitimately be short.
- `raster_hz` within 1 % of the nominal rate. Otherwise the result is "inconclusive", not a pass.
- At 16 bpp, and at 32 bpp where `D3D32` is armed (otherwise "skipped"): |ratio16 − ratio32| ≤ 0.02.

Run it at **640x480x16@85** (negative vsync) and **1280x960x16@85** (positive vsync, 35 µs
pulse).

**Reported, not gated** until F-P is decided: `sense` = `"pulse"` (duty < 2 %), `"blank"`
(2-10 %), `"inverted"` (> 90 %), otherwise `"unknown"`.

**Informational:** `--work-us 12353` (1.05 R) should give about 0.889 R under the nominal rule
and ≥ 0.94 R with `FlipDeadline` = 1. That is the PLAN step 16 A/B, run on the D3D path.

## 10. Risk to proven paths

- **E1:** read-only, apart from 16 paced CRT mode switches, which is a normal battery's load.
  d3dprobe needs a clean 3D engine (G01) to draw its quad, but the timing does not depend on it.
- **§5.3 counters:**
  - The flip path gains a few integer operations per present, and each session gains two INFO
    lines.
  - There is no register access.
  - Every completion answer is pinned identical (§8 test 5).
  - NOVSYNC sessions now name their owner. That attribution change is intended.
  - Unaffected: Glide and OpenGL (they never call `Dd_Flip`), SLI state, and the default 16 bpp
    path's behaviour.
- **`LogEntries` = 16384:** 2 MB of non-paged pool allocated in DriverEntry on a 256 MB box. A ring

- **F-P, if pursued,** would change `in_vblank` at every positive-vsync mode, the desktop included:
  - GetScanLine;
  - WaitForVerticalBlank;
  - GetFlipStatus, Flip, Lock and Blt completion.

  That reaches every DirectDraw and D3D title at those modes, so it stays default-off until its
  A/B. Glide reads `status` itself and is not touched.
- **F2c** touches the 3D swap counter that Glide also uses, and must handle SLI. It is G54's.

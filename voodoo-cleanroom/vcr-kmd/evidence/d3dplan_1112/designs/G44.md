## 0. Verdict

**Both defects are real, but G44 is not specific to UT2003, it is not a texture-format swap, and the
checkerboard is not UE2's default texture.** Host analysis of the staged game data settles four
things.

1. **The grass is the same object in both games.**
   - Both maps draw one deco layer: `TowerStatic.Rock.TowerDecoGrassA`, a 28-triangle mesh.
   - Its material is `TowerTerrain.General.TowerGrassFinalBlend`: AlphaBlend, AlphaTest,
     AlphaRef 57, TwoSided.
   - The material's texture, `General.TowerGrass`, is a **512x512 DXT3 with 10 levels**
     (`Format` 7, `bAlphaTexture`, `NormalLOD` 1).
   - `TowerTerrain.utx` is **byte-identical** in UT2003 and UT2004 (md5 `7ac4cc2c…`).

   So "a red/blue swap in one texture format" cannot make the two games differ: they upload the
   same DXT3 data.
2. **The blue pixels are not TowerGrass texels under any colour combine.**
   - **The texture's colour.** Decoded, every TowerGrass texel that passes the alpha test is
     yellow-green: mean RGB (81-90, 102-108, 34-48) on levels 0-6. **None of the roughly 89,000
     such texels has blue above red or green**, and blue's 99th percentile is 57-123.
   - **The frames' colour.** UT2003's grass pixels average **(49, 79, 149)**.
   - **No combine reaches that from this texture.**
     - MODULATE by any colour of 1.0 or less can never raise blue above the texel's own blue,
       whose 99th percentile is 57-123, against 149 observed. The HAL draws MODULATE2X/4X as 1x
       (G05).
     - ADD cannot lower red from about 87 to 49.
   - **The shape is not the texture's either.** The rendered quads are about 70-90 % covered by
     regular diagonal stripes that rotate with each quad, a texture-space pattern. TowerGrass
     covers only 25-34 % of any level, as arching blades.

   So the TMU samples memory that does not hold TowerGrass at the LOD in use.
3. **The "UT2004 is right" control was captured at a different LOD.**
   - UT2003 ran at 640x480 (G19's auto-configuration), with the flyby camera passing over the
     grass. Its near quads are about 60-120 px wide for a 256-texel level, so the TMU samples
     **level 1-2**.
   - UT2004's only grass frame (`UT2004_statfps_080s`) is a bot standing inside the clump at
     1280x960. That grass is magnified, so the TMU samples **level 0**.
   - Level 0 is the only level that is always its own block (G06, G07).

   The two games' DM-Antalus share the same `SceneManager` "flyby". The decisive control is
   therefore UT2004 on the **same flyby path at 640x480**, which no one has run (§2.1).
4. **The checkerboard is not `Engine.DefaultTexture`.**
   - That texture is a 128x128 P8 of grey-green bubbles, average (131, 140, 122).
   - The wedge in `UT2003_flyby_120s.png` is a regular light/dark grey tiling with a period of
     about 12 px: means (110, 109, 114) and (73, 72, 70).
   - Two candidates remain: a tiny mip level read from the wrong place and tiled across a
     heavily minified triangle (the G07 family), or real content (the map imports catwalk
     meshes). A crisply aliased checker at a grazing distance favours the first (inferred).

**Leading hypothesis (H1):** G44 is G06's "confetti" and G07's split mip chains, seen through
UT2003's 640x480. The grass's DXT3 chain is placed one `CreateSurface` per level, and the TMU reads
level k at `top + offset(k)`, where another texture's bytes sit. DXT3-decoded foreign data gives
this blue stripe pattern (inferred).

**Two alternatives survive the colour argument:**
- **H6:** the wrong surface is bound, for example through the handle table (G42).
- **H7:** a contiguous DXT3 chain is mis-laid out or mis-decoded at LOD 1 or more. No test has ever
  sampled a DXT3 chain below level 0.

Every hypothesis in which the TMU samples TowerGrass's own texels is excluded: a combine mapping
(G05/G12-G14), texture coordinates, fog, or a cache flush. G37 is excluded as well, because the
defect persists unchanged across frames 30 s apart.

**The plan:**
1. Four game-side runs with no driver change, which separate H1/H7 (LOD-dependent) from H6
   (LOD-independent) (§2.1).
2. A log-only extension of `log_combo` that names the grass draw, its bound texture, its fog,
   TFACTOR, texture coordinates and one vertex. It rides in G06's instrumentation boot (§2.2).
3. A d3dprobe replay of the grass: a DXT3 chain, alpha-tested and blended, on a fresh and on a
   fragmented heap (§2.3).
4. The A/B in G06/G07's `Diag\D3DMipChain` boots (§2.4).

**No G44-specific HAL fix is expected.** The fix is G07's whole-chain placement. G44 closes when
UT2003's flyby at **640x480** shows yellow-green grass with that fix armed. It must **not** be
closed on frames taken at 1280x960 after G19's fix, which only moves the near grass to level 0 and
hides the bug.

Priority **P1**; effort **M**.

**What the blue looks like:**
- **Colour.** The pixels average (49, 79, 149). The most frequent are (41,73,140), (41,69,140) and
  (33,65,132): 565-dithered, at a nearly constant hue.
- **Shape.** Zoomed (`UT2003_flyby_090s`, 4x), each crossed quad of the mesh is mostly filled with
  parallel diagonal stripes about 4 px apart, and the stripe direction rotates with the quad. That
  is a texture-space pattern, not screen-space dither. **No grass blades are visible.**
- **Persistence.** The same blue appears at 60, 90 and 120 s.

**The checkerboard.** In `UT2003_flyby_120s`, box (285,115)-(365,145) holds a wedge of regular
light/dark grey cells: light (110,109,114) and dark (73,72,70), with luma along row 125 repeating
about every 12 px. The white wedge beside it is G18, not this gap.

### 1.4 Why the colour cannot be TowerGrass's

| combine (any arguments) | can it give (49, 79, 149) from §1.2's texels? |
|---|---|
| MODULATE (the HAL also draws 2X/4X this way, G05) | **no.** It needs B_out > R_out with B_tex < R_tex, so diffuse B/R > 2 and B_out <= B_tex, which is at most 123 (p99) against 149 observed |
| ADD (texture + diffuse) | **no.** R_out >= R_tex, at least about 49 (p1) and typically 87, against 49 observed |
| SELECTARG(DIFFUSE / TFACTOR / fog) | the colour is possible, **but then the alpha is still the texture's**, and the coverage would be blades at 25-34 %, not stripes at 70-90 % |
| any texture-coordinate error on this texture | **no.** It samples other TowerGrass texels, and every one is yellow-green |
| a stale TMU cache (G37) | **no.** A small cache's stale lines are evicted within a frame, while the defect is identical at 60/90/120 s |

**What remains:** the TMU reads bytes that are not TowerGrass's, at the LOD in use.

### 1.5 The LOD at which the grass is drawn

| capture | mode | grass quad on screen | texel:pixel on the 256 level | level sampled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UT2003 flyby (60/90/120 s) | 640x480 (G19: auto-configuration, every launch) | about 60-120 px | about 2-4 | **1-2** |
| UT2004 `statfps_080s` | 1280x960 | about 300-500 px (a bot inside the clump) | under 1 | **0** |
| UT2003 flyby at 1280x960 (never run) | - | about 120-240 px | about 1-2 | 0-1 |

These estimates assume each blade quad maps the whole texture (inferred). UE2's
`DefaultTexMipBias` -0.5 is ignored by the HAL (G27), which pushes sampling toward the smaller
levels.

**Why level 0 is safe and the others are not.**
- Level 0 is always the top level's own block.
- Levels 1 and below are separate `CreateSurface` blocks on XP, which a fragmented heap scatters
  (G07 §1, G06 §2.1-2.3).
- `mipchain_ext` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:367`) places only the level of the current call.
- `tex_view_ext` (`:806-830`) sets LODMAX over every attached level without checking where each
  one lies (`:820`).

## 2. Investigation

All runs are on `.124`, supervised, after a clean boot with no Glide or OpenGL session since (G01:
a stale `cmdFifo0` hides every D3D triangle). Check `vcrctl reg` shows `cmdFifo0.baseSize` 0
**before** the first D3D program, and never run vcrctl while a game runs.
- Mode switches: one at each game start and exit, at least 5 s apart. 640x480@120 and 1280x960
  are both modes this box already ran on 11-12.
- UE2 ignores WM_CLOSE, so close it with the console `exit` (`gamerun.py --console-exit`).
- Activation check and `safe-reboot.py` before each boot.

4. Read the 513/7 refusals and 513/9 "table full" events from the same ring.

## 4. Gating

- **The instrumentation (§2.2) lands default-ON, with no switch.**
  - It is log-only: no register write, allocation or control-flow change on the drawing path. That
    is the hal.md "Phase 0" rule and G06 §5's call for its own census.
  - Its volume is bounded: at most 128 combines × 5-6 events per context (513/21-23, 28, and
    one or two 513/29), each costing one IOCTL (G52). On a settled title this is a few hundred
    events per session.
  - It needs the 86Box bed run plus the host tests before the box, because it runs in kernel
    mode: a bad read is a bugcheck. It does not need a silicon proof boot of its own, and it
    rides in G06's Boot 0.
- **The d3dprobe tests** are explicit-only and change nothing in the default `render` set (40/40).
- **The fix is G07's**, default-OFF behind `Diag\D3DMipChain` until proven in one supervised clean
  boot per bit (G06/G07 §5). G44 adds no Diag switch of its own.
- **E0** changes nothing on the box beyond run-local ini copies under `C:\vcr\onbox\`.

## Appendix A: the host analysis script (reproducible)

Run as `python3 -I g44_ue2_grass.py /mnt/retro-share/Files/Games-Library` (read-only). The
implementer commits it with its output under `KMD/evidence/g44_ue2grass/`. The frame metrics of
§1.3 are the §5.3 classifiers applied to the committed PNGs.

```python
import struct, sys, hashlib, statistics

class R:
    def __init__(s, b, o=0): s.b, s.o = b, o
    def u8(s): v = s.b[s.o]; s.o += 1; return v
    def i32(s): v = struct.unpack_from('<i', s.b, s.o)[0]; s.o += 4; return v
    def u32(s): v = struct.unpack_from('<I', s.b, s.o)[0]; s.o += 4; return v
    def u16(s): v = struct.unpack_from('<H', s.b, s.o)[0]; s.o += 2; return v
    def raw(s, n): v = s.b[s.o:s.o + n]; s.o += n; return v
    def ci(s):                                   # FCompactIndex
        b0 = s.u8(); v = b0 & 0x3f
        if b0 & 0x40:
            sh = 6
            while True:
                b = s.u8(); v |= (b & 0x7f) << sh; sh += 7
                if not b & 0x80: break
        return -v if b0 & 0x80 else v
    def fstr(s):
        n = s.ci()
        if n < 0: return s.raw(-2 * n).decode('utf-16le').rstrip('\0')
        return s.raw(n).decode('latin-1').rstrip('\0')

class Pkg:
    def __init__(s, path):
        s.b = open(path, 'rb').read(); r = R(s.b)
        assert r.u32() == 0x9E2A83C1
        s.ver, s.lic, _ = r.u16(), r.u16(), r.u32()
        nc, no, ec, eo, ic, io = [r.u32() for _ in range(6)]
        r.o = no; s.names = []
        for _ in range(nc): s.names.append(r.fstr()); r.u32()
        r.o = io; s.imp = []
        for _ in range(ic):
            r.ci(); cn = r.ci(); pk = r.i32(); on = r.ci()
            s.imp.append((s.names[cn], pk, s.names[on]))
        r.o = eo; s.exp = []
        for _ in range(ec):
            c = r.ci(); r.ci(); pk = r.i32(); on = r.ci(); fl = r.u32(); sz = r.ci()
            s.exp.append((c, pk, s.names[on], fl, sz, r.ci() if sz > 0 else 0))
    def path(s, ref):                            # 0 none, <0 import, >0 export
        if ref == 0: return 'None'
        parts = []
        while ref:
            if ref < 0: _, ref, n = s.imp[-ref - 1]
            else: _, ref, n = s.exp[ref - 1][:3]
            parts.append(n)
        return '.'.join(reversed(parts))
    def cls(s, i):
        c = s.exp[i][0]
        return 'Class' if c == 0 else s.imp[-c - 1][2] if c < 0 else s.exp[c - 1][2]
    def find(s, full):
        return next(i for i in range(len(s.exp)) if s.path(i + 1) == full)
    def props(s, i, b=None, o=None):             # UE2 tagged properties -> (list, end offset)
        if b is None:
            c, pk, n, fl, sz, off = s.exp[i]; b, o = s.b, off
            r = R(b, o)
            if fl & 0x02000000:                   # RF_HasStack
                node = r.ci(); r.ci(); r.raw(12)
                if node: r.ci()
            o = r.o
        r = R(b, o); out = []
        while True:
            nm = s.names[r.ci()]
            if nm == 'None': return out, r.o
            info = r.u8(); t = info & 15; sc = (info >> 4) & 7
            if t == 10: r.ci()                      # struct name
            size = [1, 2, 4, 12, 16][sc] if sc < 5 else [r.u8, r.u16, r.u32][sc - 5]()
            idx = 0
            if info & 0x80 and t != 3:
                x = r.u8()
                idx = x if x < 0x80 else ((x & 0x7f) << 8 | r.u8()) if x < 0xc0 else \
                      ((x & 0x3f) << 24 | r.u8() << 16 | r.u8() << 8 | r.u8())
            d = r.raw(size) if t != 3 else bytes([info >> 7])
            out.append((nm, t, idx, d))
    def val(s, p):
        nm, t, idx, d = p
        if t == 1: return d[0]
        if t == 2: return struct.unpack('<i', d)[0]
        if t == 3: return bool(d[0])
        if t == 4: return round(struct.unpack('<f', d)[0], 4)
        if t == 5: return s.path(R(d).ci())
        return d.hex()
    def struct_array(s, d):                      # array of structs: count, then tagged structs
        r = R(d); n = r.ci(); o = r.o; out = []
        for _ in range(n):
            ps, o = s.props(None, d, o); out.append({p[0]: s.val(p) for p in ps})
        return out
    def mips(s, i):                              # UTexture native data after the properties
        _, end = s.props(i); r = R(s.b, end); out = []
        for _ in range(r.ci()):
            r.i32(); d = r.raw(r.ci()); w = r.i32(); h = r.i32(); r.raw(2); out.append((w, h, d))
        return out

def c565(c): r, g, b = c >> 11, (c >> 5) & 63, c & 31; return (r << 3 | r >> 2, g << 2 | g >> 4, b << 3 | b >> 2)
def dxt3(d, w, h):                               # -> list of (r, g, b, a)
    px = [None] * (w * h)
    for by in range(max(1, h // 4)):
        for bx in range(max(1, w // 4)):
            o = (by * max(1, w // 4) + bx) * 16
            a = struct.unpack_from('<Q', d, o)[0]; c0, c1, ix = struct.unpack_from('<HHI', d, o + 8)
            p0, p1 = c565(c0), c565(c1)
            pal = [p0, p1, tuple((2 * x + y) // 3 for x, y in zip(p0, p1)), tuple((x + 2 * y) // 3 for x, y in zip(p0, p1))]
            for k in range(16):
                x, y = bx * 4 + (k & 3), by * 4 + (k >> 2)
                if x < w and y < h: px[y * w + x] = pal[ix >> 2 * k & 3] + (((a >> 4 * k) & 15) * 17,)
    return px

lib = sys.argv[1]
md5 = lambda p: hashlib.md5(open(p, 'rb').read()).hexdigest()
for g in ('UT2003', 'UT2004'):

    print(g, 'DM-Antalus', md5(f'{lib}/{g}/Maps/DM-Antalus.ut2'), 'deco layers:', m.struct_array(deco[3]))
    sm = Pkg(f'{lib}/{g}/StaticMeshes/TowerStatic.usx')

    print(g, 'TowerStatic', md5(f'{lib}/{g}/StaticMeshes/TowerStatic.usx'), 'TowerDecoGrassA:', sm.struct_array(mats[3]))
    print(g, 'TowerTerrain', md5(f'{lib}/{g}/Textures/TowerTerrain.utx'))
t = Pkg(f'{lib}/UT2003/Textures/TowerTerrain.utx')
for name in ('General.TowerGrassFinalBlend', 'General.TowerGrass'):
    print(name, {p[0]: t.val(p) for p in t.props(t.find(name))[0]})
for k, (w, h, d) in enumerate(t.mips(t.find('General.TowerGrass'))):
    if w < 8: continue

          % (k, w, h, len(d), len(op) / (w * h), [round(statistics.mean(q[c] for q in op)) for c in range(3)],
             sorted(q[2] for q in op)[int(len(op) * .99)], sum(q[2] > min(q[0], q[1]) for q in op)))
```

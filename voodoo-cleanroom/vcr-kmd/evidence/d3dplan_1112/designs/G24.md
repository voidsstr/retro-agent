## 1. Verdict

**The gap is real and fixable. It has two parts of very different weight, and four details of the gap text need correcting.**

- **Part A, flat shading, is a pure correctness fix at zero register cost.** The VSA-100 has no flat mode, so the fix sends D3D's "first vertex" colour for all three vertices of a flat triangle. The register writes are exactly today's, in the same order and the same count; only the colour values of two vertices change. A triangle whose three vertices carry one colour is what d3dprobe `flat` already draws on silicon (40/40). **Recommendation: land it default ON**, with a kill switch, and prove it with new d3dprobe tests in the first supervised boot of the build.
- **Part B, FILLMODE wireframe/point, is new register traffic.** It draws triangles as G16's line and point quads, so it **depends on G16**. **Recommendation: land it default OFF** behind its own `Diag\D3DFeat` bit. It is debug-mode territory: no staged title is known to need it in play.

**Corrections to the gap text.**

1. **Jedi Knight and MotS are not users of flat shading or fill modes.** Their execute-buffer render-state block writes `SHADEMODE = GOURAUD` and `FILLMODE = SOLID` as constants, and no other SHADEMODE write exists in either exe (Appendix A: `JK.EXE` 0x42b916/0x42b961, `JKM.EXE` 0x42ead6/0x42eb21). The likely users are elsewhere (section 11): the old Dark engine (Thief Gold, System Shock 2) has a shade setter that sends `D3DSHADE_FLAT`; Rainbow Six selects FLAT only for a software device; BF1942 has one FLAT site but is blocked by SafeDisc; Halo and Unreal Engine 2 carry wireframe debug paths.
2. **The "flat flags" in an INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST record's `wFlags` are not shading flags.** `D3DTRIFLAG_STARTFLAT(len)`, `ODD` and `EVEN` (the low 5 bits) are strip/fan **culling hints**: "START_FLAT contains a count from 0 to 29 that allows the whole strip or fan to be culled in one hit" (`DDK8/d3dtypes.h:1749-1755`). A driver that culls every triangle in hardware ignores them correctly; 3dfx's NT HAL and MSP2 never read them, and only 3dfx's Win9x DX5 path used them to reuse strip vertices (`W9X/D3TRI.C:709-767`). The bits of `wFlags` that matter are `D3DTRIFLAG_EDGEENABLE1..3` (0x100/0x200/0x400), and only in WIREFRAME fill (Part B). Flat shading takes its colour from the record's first index, `wV1`.
3. **D3D's "first vertex" is not the first vertex we send to the setup unit.** For odd strip triangles the walker sends `(s+i+1, s+i, s+i+2)` to keep the winding (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:1628`), and D3D's first vertex is the second of those. For every fan triangle the walker sends the hub first (`:1640`), and D3D's first vertex is `v[i+1]`. A fix that took "vertex a" would be wrong on exactly the primitives games use most (section 3.1).
4. **Flat shading is mandatory, not an extra.** The W2K DDK states: "It is assumed, in general, that if a device supports a given command (such as D3DOP_TRIANGLE) at all, it supports the D3DSHADE_FLAT mode" (DDKDOC, page `D3DPRIMCAPS`, `dwShadeCaps`). D3D8 and D3D9 have no flat cap bits at all: `D3DPSHADECAPS_*` there is only COLORGOURAUDRGB, SPECULARGOURAUDRGB, ALPHAGOURAUDBLEND and FOGGOURAUD (`DDK8/d3d8caps.h:246-249`). That is why the live D3D8 ShadeCaps read `00004008` while we report `0x500a` (`caps_124.txt`; `reports/runtime.md` section 3). Every D3D8/9 title may send `D3DSHADE_FLAT` without checking any cap.

**Hardware.** The colour always iterates from three vertices: startR/G/B/A are FBI registers (SPEC L2239-2243); "Napalm supports Gouraud shading by providing RGBA iterators ... the host provides starting RGBA" (SPEC L551); the setup unit computes the gradients from the three vertices it was sent (sSetupMode, SPEC L3718-3734). Flat is therefore a host trick (`reports/hw.md` section 4.11). 3dfx replicated the first vertex's colour on the host (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:764-800`), and noted "HW cannot support FLAT_SHADE and Auto Strip/Fan" (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:767`, `VH5/D6STRIPI.C:803`).

**Priority P2. Effort M** (Part A S, Part B M on top of G16).

## 3. The rules

### 3.1 Which vertex colours a flat triangle

DDKDOC lists each DP2 triangle form's triangles in D3D order, and the first vertex of that order is the flat-shading vertex (DDKDOC `D3DSHADEMODE`: "The color and specular component of the first vertex in the triangle are used to determine the color and specular component of the face"; `D3DRENDERSTATE_SHADEMODE`: "derived from the triangle's first vertex").

| DP2 form (opcode) | D3D order of triangle i (DDKDOC page) | D3D's first vertex | our setup order (walker) | slot of the first vertex |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TRIANGLELIST (18) | `(s+3i, s+3i+1, s+3i+2)` (`D3DNTHAL_DP2TRIANGLELIST`) | `s+3i` | same | a |
| INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST (3) | `(wV1, wV2, wV3)` (`D3DNTHAL_DP2INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST`) | `wV1` | same | a |
| INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2 (26) | `s + (wV1, wV2, wV3)` (`D3DNTHAL_DP2INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2`) | `s+wV1` | same | a |
| TRIANGLESTRIP (19) | even `(s+i, s+i+1, s+i+2)`, odd `(s+i, s+i+2, s+i+1)`: "(v[0], v[1], v[2]), (v[1], v[3], v[2]), (v[2], v[3], v[4])" (`D3DNTHAL_DP2TRIANGLESTRIP`) | `s+i` | even same; odd `(s+i+1, s+i, s+i+2)` | even a, **odd b** |
| INDEXEDTRIANGLESTRIP (20) | the same over `idx[]`: "(v[1], v[3], v[6]), (v[3], v[4], v[6]), (v[6], v[4], v[5])" for idx {1,3,6,4,5} | `s+idx[i]` | even same; odd `(idx[i+1], idx[i], idx[i+2])` | even a, **odd b** |
| TRIANGLEFAN (21) | `(s+i+1, s+i+2, s)` (`D3DNTHAL_DP2TRIANGLEFAN`) | `s+i+1` | `(s, s+i+1, s+i+2)` | **b** |
| INDEXEDTRIANGLEFAN (22) | `(idx[i+1], idx[i+2], idx[0])` (`D3DNTHAL_DP2INDEXEDTRIANGLEFAN`) | `s+idx[i+1]` | `(idx[0], idx[i+1], idx[i+2])` | **b** |
| TRIANGLEFAN_IMM (23) | `(V[i+1], V[i+2], V[0])` (`D3DNTHAL_DP2TRIANGLEFAN_IMM`) | `V[i+1]` | `(V[0], V[i+1], V[i+2])` | **b** |

**The references agree for the stored primitives.**
- 3dfx NT: lists use `pA` (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:764-800`; revision note "Referencing pB when flat shading is wrong, should be pA", `:66`). Strips use `pF`, which is `pA` on even and `pB` on odd triangles (`VH5/D6STRIP.C:145-159`; "restore use of pF in dp2StripAll when shadeMode=FLAT (fixes Test 7)", `VH5/D6STRIPI.C:65`). Fans use `pB` = `v[i+1]` (`VH5/D6FANI.C:720-735`; "use color from vertexB in dp2FanAll when shadeMode=FLAT (fixes Test 6)", `:59`). These revisions are fixes for failed shading tests ("PRS 11863 - DCT D3D Shading Flat/Gouroud test fails", `VH5/D6FANI.C:57-59`; "Flat shading quality test 1 in 3DWB 2k", `VH5/D6TRI2I.C:66-67`), so this is the tested behaviour.
- MSP2 replicates its first argument's colour (`d3dtri.c:237-258`). Its solid TRIANGLEFAN_IMM call passes `(V[i+1], V[i+2], V[0])` (`d3ddx6.c:1223`), i.e. the fan rule.
- **One disagreement, in wireframe only:** both references colour a TRIANGLEFAN_IMM's wireframe edges from `V[0]` (`VH5/D6DP2.C:2583-2620`; `MSP2/d3ddx6.c:1194-1218`), while the DDK's own triangle list makes `V[i+1]` the first vertex. This design uses `V[i+1]` everywhere; d3dprobe `flatclip` and `wireclip` record what the runtime's clipped output looks like (open question 2).

### 3.2 What flat shading flattens

| component | flat mode | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| diffuse RGB | the first vertex's | section 3.1 |
| diffuse **alpha** | the first vertex's | DDKDOC `D3DPRIMCAPS`: ALPHAFLATBLEND, "the alpha color component for a primitive is provided as part of the color for the first vertex of the primitive"; 3dfx NT replicates the whole ARGB (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:787-789`). **3dfx's Win9x DX5 path kept per-vertex alpha** (`W9X/D3TRI.C:303-304`: `bColor = (aColor & 0x00FFFFFF) \| (pB->color & 0xFF000000)`); we follow the NT path and the DDK, and `flatalpha` checks it against the reference driver |
| specular RGB | the first vertex's | 3dfx `aSColor = pA[FVFO_SPECULAR]; bSColor = cSColor = aSColor` (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:781-782`). Matters only once G25 draws specular |
| specular **alpha** (the D3D vertex fog factor) | **per vertex**, interpolated | 3dfx computes the fog from `pA`, `pB` and `pC` whatever the shade mode (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:880-896`); the DX8/9 SDK's `D3DSHADEMODE` page says "The specular alpha is interpolated" (inferred: from the SDK, not a file here) |
| position, Z, W, texture coordinates | per vertex | obvious |

### 3.3 Fill modes

- **WIREFRAME.** Each non-culled triangle draws its edges.
  - INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST draws only the edges its `wFlags` enable: EDGEENABLE1 = `wV1-wV2`, 2 = `wV2-wV3`, 3 = `wV3-wV1` (DDKDOC `D3DNTHAL_DP2INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST`; `VH5/D6DP2.C:1925-1955`; `MSP2/d3ddx6.c:795-806`). Only execute-buffer titles produce this opcode: the DX6+ API's indexed lists come as INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2 (the comment at `MSP2/d3ddx6.c:758-762`).
  - TRIANGLEFAN_IMM (the runtime's clipper output) draws only the outline edges its `dwEdgeFlags` enable: bit 0 = `V[0]-V[1]`, bit k = `V[k]-V[k+1]` for k = 1..n, bit n+1 = `V[n+1]-V[0]` (DDKDOC `D3DNTHAL_DP2TRIANGLEFAN_IMM`; both references implement exactly this loop). The fan diagonals are never drawn.
  - Every other form draws all three edges of every triangle; a shared edge is drawn twice, as in both references.
- **POINT.** Each non-culled triangle draws its three vertices (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C` `D3DFILL_POINT` case; `MSP2/d3ddx6.c:789-794`).
- **Culling** happens on the CPU before any edge or point is drawn, with 3dfx's and Microsoft's `CULL_TRI`: area `<= 0` (counter-clockwise on a y-down screen, and zero area) is culled by `D3DCULL_CCW`, area `> 0` by `D3DCULL_CW` (`VH5/D6DP2.C:869-874`; `MSP2/d3dhw.h:297`). The setup unit cannot do it: G16 clears the cull bits for its quads, whose winding follows the line.
- **Flat colour of edges and points:** the triangle's first vertex (`VH5/D6LINE.C:456-463`, `VH5/D6POINT.C:142-150`; `MSP2/d3dline.c:197-202`). MSP2's point path uses each point's own colour (`MSP2/d3dpoint.c:136-175`): a note in `pointfill`, not a criterion.
- D3D defaults: FILLMODE = SOLID, SHADEMODE = GOURAUD (DDKDOC; `VH5/D3RSTATE.C:3695`).

## 4. Design

### 4.0 Step 0: counters (default ON, log-only, no register write)

This is the measurement hal.md's Phase 0 asks for ("SHADEMODE flat, FILLMODE not solid", `reports/hal.md` section 10). If G76's counter block exists when this lands, use it.

- New `vcr_d3dctx` fields: `flat_tris` (flat triangles **drawn**, not state sets: Rainbow Six sets FLAT at init before it knows the device, Appendix A), `flat_ops` (an 8-bit mask of the triangle forms that drew flat: bit 0 TRIANGLELIST, 1 TRIANGLESTRIP, 2 TRIANGLEFAN, 3 INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST, 4 INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2, 5 INDEXEDTRIANGLESTRIP, 6 INDEXEDTRIANGLEFAN, 7 TRIANGLEFAN_IMM), `flat_off_tris` (triangles that would have been flat with the kill switch at 0), `wire_tris`, `point_tris` (triangles that arrived under a non-solid FILLMODE, drawn as edges/points or, unarmed, solid), and `shade_logged`.
- `prepare()` computes the request and the switch separately (`w->flat_req = vcr_flat_on(shademode, diff_off, 1)`, `w->flat = w->flat_req && pd->d3d_flat`), so `flat_off_tris` counts with the switch off.
- **Event 513 what 27 (provisional), INFO, at `ctx_free` only when any counter is non-zero:** "context %u: %u flat triangles (forms %x), %u would-be-flat, %u wireframe, %u point-fill (%s)", the last field "drawn" or "solid: Diag\\D3DFeat FILL off".
- **Event 513 what 28 (provisional), INFO, once per context** at the first flat or non-solid triangle, with the DP2 ordinal: this dates the scene.
- **Event 513 what 29 (provisional), INFO, once per PDEV** from `VcrDd2dInit`: the switch notes ("flat shading off (Diag\\D3DFlat = 0)", "armed: Direct3D fill modes (Diag\\D3DFeat FILL)").
- Cost: an increment per flat or non-solid triangle and two log calls per context lifetime. The 513 sub-codes 24-26 are already contended (G05, G16); the plan's owner allocates the final numbers.

### 4.1 Part A: flat shading

**(a) The triangle decode, out of the DDI file.** A new section of `KMD/include/vcr_dp2prim.h` (G16's header for line and point records; whichever item lands first creates the file). It is integer-only and Win32-free, so the `-mgeneral-regs-only` HAL can include it and a native test can execute it.

```c
/* DP2 triangle opcodes (DDK d3dnthal.h; pinned against the DDK in vcrdd_d3d.c) */
#define VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST   3
#define VCR_DP2_TRIANGLELIST         18
#define VCR_DP2_TRIANGLESTRIP        19
#define VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLESTRIP 20
#define VCR_DP2_TRIANGLEFAN          21
#define VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLEFAN   22
#define VCR_DP2_TRIANGLEFAN_IMM      23
#define VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2 26

/* triangle i of a DP2 triangle command: a b c in the order the walker has always
 * fed the setup unit (winding kept: the hardware culls on it), pv = D3D's FIRST
 * vertex - the flat-shading colour (DDKDOC D3DNTHAL_DP2* triangle sequences):
 * lists 3i / wV1, strips i, fans i+1. rec: the record after the DP2 header and
 * after the start-vertex word for the forms that carry one (indexed forms only);
 * s: that start vertex (0 for INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST, whose indices are absolute).
 * TRIANGLEFAN_IMM numbers its inline vertices 0..n+1. kind: the bit in flat_ops. */
typedef struct vcr_tri { unsigned a, b, c, pv, kind; } vcr_tri;

static __inline unsigned vcr_rw(const unsigned char *p) { return p[0] | ((unsigned)p[1] << 8); }

static __inline void vcr_dp2_tri(unsigned op, const unsigned char *rec, unsigned s, unsigned i,
                                 vcr_tri *t)
{
    unsigned x, y;
    switch (op) {
    case VCR_DP2_TRIANGLELIST:
        t->a = s + 3 * i; t->b = t->a + 1; t->c = t->a + 2; t->pv = t->a; t->kind = 0; break;
    case VCR_DP2_TRIANGLESTRIP:               /* odd: (i+1, i, i+2), D3D's first is i */
        t->a = s + i + (i & 1); t->b = s + i + !(i & 1); t->c = s + i + 2; t->pv = s + i;
        t->kind = 1; break;
    case VCR_DP2_TRIANGLEFAN:                 /* (s, i+1, i+2), D3D's first is i+1 */
        t->a = s; t->b = s + i + 1; t->c = s + i + 2; t->pv = t->b; t->kind = 2; break;
    case VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST:         /* {wV1, wV2, wV3, wFlags}, absolute */
        t->a = vcr_rw(rec + 8 * i); t->b = vcr_rw(rec + 8 * i + 2); t->c = vcr_rw(rec + 8 * i + 4);
        t->pv = t->a; t->kind = 3; break;
    case VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2:        /* {wV1, wV2, wV3}, relative to s */
        t->a = s + vcr_rw(rec + 6 * i); t->b = s + vcr_rw(rec + 6 * i + 2);
        t->c = s + vcr_rw(rec + 6 * i + 4); t->pv = t->a; t->kind = 4; break;
    case VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLESTRIP:        /* n+2 indices relative to s */
        x = s + vcr_rw(rec + 2 * i); y = s + vcr_rw(rec + 2 * i + 2);
        t->a = (i & 1) ? y : x; t->b = (i & 1) ? x : y; t->c = s + vcr_rw(rec + 2 * i + 4);
        t->pv = x; t->kind = 5; break;
    case VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLEFAN:
        t->a = s + vcr_rw(rec); t->b = s + vcr_rw(rec + 2 * i + 2); t->c = s + vcr_rw(rec + 2 * i + 4);
        t->pv = t->b; t->kind = 6; break;
    default:                                  /* VCR_DP2_TRIANGLEFAN_IMM */
        t->a = 0; t->b = i + 1; t->c = i + 2; t->pv = t->b; t->kind = 7; break;
    }
}

/* the decisions, testable on the host */
static __inline int vcr_flat_on(unsigned long shademode, unsigned long diff_off, int enabled)
{ return enabled && diff_off && shademode == 1 /* D3DSHADE_FLAT; GOURAUD 2, PHONG 3 = gouraud */; }
```

The `a b c` expressions are today's walker expressions, moved and not changed. The native test pins them against a verbatim copy of the walker loops (section 8), so the proven gouraud stream cannot move: the refactor *adds* coverage of the strip/fan winding that history.md section 6.1 lists as untested.

**(b) The register writer** (`KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c`, the FPU object). One extra pointer, no extra write:

```c
/* v: the vertex - position, Z, 1/W, the fog factor (specular alpha) and the
 * texture coordinates. cv: the vertex whose DIFFUSE colour, alpha included, is
 * sent - v itself, or under D3DSHADE_FLAT the triangle's first vertex. The fog
 * factor stays v's: D3D interpolates the specular alpha in flat mode too. */
static BOOL vertex(VCR_PDEV *pd, const vcr3d_draw *d, const UCHAR *v, const UCHAR *cv)
{
    ...
    ULONG argb = d->diff_off ? *(const ULONG *)(cv + d->diff_off) : 0xffffffffu;
    ... (everything else unchanged: the spec_off read at :205 stays on v)
}

/* f: the flat-shading colour vertex, NULL = gouraud (each vertex its own) */
BOOL VcrDd3dTriangle(VCR_PDEV *pd, const vcr3d_draw *d, const UCHAR *a, const UCHAR *b,
                     const UCHAR *c, const UCHAR *f)
{
    if (!vertex(pd, d, a, f ? f : a)) return FALSE;
    w3(pd, V3D_SBEGINTRICMD, 0);
    if (!vertex(pd, d, b, f ? f : b)) return FALSE;
    w3(pd, V3D_SDRAWTRICMD, 0);
    if (!vertex(pd, d, c, f ? f : c)) return FALSE;
    w3(pd, V3D_SDRAWTRICMD, 0);
    ...
}
```

With `f == NULL` every read is today's address, so every write is today's value. If G16 has landed first, the colour-source argument goes into its `vertex_at(pd, d, v, cv, x, y)` instead (G16 section 4.3 reserves exactly this), and G16's source test of `vertex()`'s body is updated in the same commit.

**(c) The walker** (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c`):
- `dp2walk` gains `int flat;`. `prepare()` sets it right after `compute_regs(c)`, beside `w->d.fog_vertex` (`:1358`):
  `w->flat = vcr_flat_on(c->rs[D3DRENDERSTATE_SHADEMODE], w->d.diff_off, c->pd->d3d_flat);`
  `set_rs` already marks every changed state dirty (`:1402-1405`), so a SHADEMODE change mid-stream reaches `prepare()` before the next triangle. (That re-sends the full state, as any state change does today; G53 removes that cost generally.)
- `tri(w, const vcr_tri *t)` keeps its bounds check and passes `w->flat ? vb + t->pv * stride : NULL`. `imm_tri()` takes the same `vcr_tri` with indices into the inline block. `pv` is always one of `a, b, c`, so it is inside the checked range by construction.
- Each of the eight triangle cases keeps its own `NEED(...)` and pointer advance (the Python test wants at least 20 `NEED(` in `walk()`), and its loop body becomes `vcr_dp2_tri(op, rec, s, i, &t); tri(w, &t);`.
- `ctx_defaults` sets `rs[D3DRENDERSTATE_SHADEMODE] = D3DSHADE_GOURAUD` and `rs[D3DRENDERSTATE_FILLMODE] = D3DFILL_SOLID` (D3D's documented defaults; no register depends on them).
- A compile-time pin in `vcrdd_d3d.c`: `typedef char vcr_dp2_ops_match[(VCR_DP2_TRIANGLELIST == D3DNTDP2OP_TRIANGLELIST && ... ) ? 1 : -1];` for all eight opcodes.
- `float` still never appears in `vcrdd_d3d.c`: the choice is a pointer.

**(d) The kill switch.** `Diag\D3DFlat` (DWORD): **absent = 1**, flat shading honoured; **0** = SHADEMODE ignored, every triangle gouraud, i.e. today's behaviour. It follows the `Accel2DLine`/`Accel2DPattern` pattern:
- `KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c` `hwinfo()` (`:453-466`): `(VcrDiagGet(L"D3DFlat", 1) ? VCR_INFO_F_D3DFLAT : 0)`, read at every `IOCTL_VCR_INFO`.
- `KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h`: `VCR_INFO_F_D3DFLAT` on the next free flag bit (0x10000 if G16's `VCR_INFO_F_D3DLINES` = 0x8000 has landed; flags 0x1..0x4000 are taken, `:109-143`). A **positive** flag: an older miniport never sets it, so a new `vcrdd.dll` alone cannot turn the feature on.
- `KMD/display/vcrdd_2d.c` `VcrDd2dInit` (`:432-473`): `pd->d3d_flat = 0` with the other resets before the IOCTL, then set from the flag; when it is 0 with a new miniport, log 513 what 29 "flat shading off (Diag\\D3DFlat = 0)".
- `KMD/display/vcrdd.h`: `ULONG d3d_flat;`.
- It changes no caps, so reading it per mode change (not latched at boot) is safe: the next PDEV, i.e. the next mode change such as a game's own, picks it up without a reboot.
- It is chip-independent (Banshee, Voodoo3 and VSA-100 share the setup unit and `vertex()`), so the 86Box Voodoo3 bed can prove it first.

**(e) What does not change:** caps (COLORFLATRGB and ALPHAFLATBLEND simply become true), the state registers (`fbzColorPath`, `fbzMode`, `alphaMode`, `fogMode`, `setupMode`, the TMUs), the room reservations, the order and the number of writes per triangle, Clear2, flips, textures, the fog path.

**(f) Rules for neighbouring items.**
- **G49 (strip/fan setup mode):** flat triangles must stay independent triangles, because each needs its own constant colour while the setup unit reuses latched vertices ("HW cannot support FLAT_SHADE and Auto Strip/Fan", `VH5/D6TRI2I.C:767`). G49's emitter must test `w->flat`. (The sSetupMode route of leaving RGB/alpha out of the setup and writing startR..startA per triangle, SPEC L3719, would allow strips under flat at 4 writes per triangle; it is unverified on silicon and not proposed here.)
- **G25 (specular):** the specular RGB comes from `cv`, the specular alpha (fog) from `v`.
- **G58 (packed sARGB):** unaffected; only where `argb` is read changes.
- **G57 (guard band):** if `flatclip` shows that the runtime's clipped output loses the flat colour, reporting a guard band stops most of that clipping (section 12, question 2).

### 4.2 Part B: FILLMODE wireframe and point (needs G16)

**Prerequisite.** G16's `VcrDd3dLine`, `VcrDd3dPoint`, `quad`, `quads_begin` and `quads_end`. This item adds a colour-source argument to them: `VcrDd3dLine(pd, d, a, b, f)` and `VcrDd3dPoint(pd, d, v, f)`, with `f` = NULL for own colours. G16's own line opcodes then pass `w->flat ? a : NULL`: D3D's flat vertex of line i is its first (2i in a list, i in a strip), and that is G16's `a` (G16 section 4.3 leaves this to G24).

**Decisions in `vcr_dp2prim.h`** (integer, host-tested):

```c
#define VCR_FILL_SOLID 0
#define VCR_FILL_WIRE  1
#define VCR_FILL_POINT 2
static __inline unsigned vcr_fill_mode(unsigned long fillmode, int armed)
{ return !armed ? VCR_FILL_SOLID : fillmode == 2 ? VCR_FILL_WIRE : fillmode == 1 ? VCR_FILL_POINT
                                                                                 : VCR_FILL_SOLID; }
/* 3dfx's / Microsoft's CULL_TRI (VH5/D6DP2.C:869-874): front = area > 0 (clockwise on a
 * y-down screen); area <= 0, zero included, is counter-clockwise. D3DCULL_NONE 1, CW 2, CCW 3 */
static __inline int vcr_cull_drops(int front, unsigned long cull)
{ return cull == 2 ? front : cull == 3 ? !front : 0; }
/* the edges to draw for triangle i in WIREFRAME, in SETUP order: bit 0 a-b, 1 b-c, 2 c-a */
static __inline unsigned vcr_dp2_tri_edges(unsigned op, const unsigned char *rec, unsigned i,
                                           unsigned n, unsigned long imm_flags)
{
    unsigned e = 0;
    if (op == VCR_DP2_INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST)          /* EDGEENABLE1..3; the low 5 bits are */
        return (vcr_rw(rec + 8 * i + 6) >> 8) & 7;  /* STARTFLAT/ODD/EVEN cull hints */
    if (op != VCR_DP2_TRIANGLEFAN_IMM)
        return 7;
    if (i == 0 && (imm_flags & 1)) e |= 1;                                   /* V[0]-V[1]     */
    if (i + 1 < 32 && ((imm_flags >> (i + 1)) & 1)) e |= 2;                  /* V[i+1]-V[i+2] */
    if (i + 1 == n && n + 1 < 32 && ((imm_flags >> (n + 1)) & 1)) e |= 4;   /* V[n+1]-V[0]   */
    return e;
}
```

(For TRIANGLEFAN_IMM the setup order is `(V[0], V[i+1], V[i+2])`, so `a-b`, `b-c` and `c-a` are exactly the DDK's three outline candidates. A flag bit past 31 reads as 0, as the references' shifting loops do; the runtime's clipper emits small fans, inferred.)

**The FPU side** (`vcrdd_3d.c`):
- `int VcrDd3dTriFront(const UCHAR *a, const UCHAR *b, const UCHAR *c)`: 1 when `(b.x - a.x)(c.y - a.y) > (c.x - a.x)(b.y - a.y)`, on D3D's x/y (the bias does not change the sign).
- `BOOL VcrDd3dTriWire(pd, d, a, b, c, f, edges)`: `VcrDd3dLine` for each enabled edge.
- `BOOL VcrDd3dTriPoints(pd, d, a, b, c, f)`: three `VcrDd3dPoint`.

**The walker.**
- `dp2walk` gains `unsigned fill;`, set in `prepare()`: `vcr_fill_mode(c->rs[D3DRENDERSTATE_FILLMODE], c->pd->d3d_fill && c->pd->d3d_lines)`.
- `tri()`:
  - `fill == SOLID`: today's `VcrDd3dTriangle` call, unchanged.
  - otherwise: `if (vcr_cull_drops(VcrDd3dTriFront(a, b, c), c->rs[D3DRENDERSTATE_CULLMODE])) return;`, then G16's `quads_begin(w)`, then `VcrDd3dTriWire` with the edges the case computed (`vcr_dp2_tri_edges`, read only when `fill == WIRE`; the IMM case keeps `rd(p)` of `dwEdgeFlags` before skipping it) or `VcrDd3dTriPoints`.
- Every triangle case ends with G16's `quads_end(w)`: it writes nothing unless a quad was drawn, so the solid path costs nothing.

**The switch.** `Diag\D3DFeat` bit **FILL** (provisionally bit 9, 0x200: bits 1, 4 and 6-8 are claimed by G08, G16, G05, G12 and G13; the plan's owner fixes the number). It is a positive flag `VCR_INFO_F_D3DFILL` read at every mode change (it changes no caps, like G16's bit 4), `pd->d3d_fill`, and 513 what 29 "armed: Direct3D fill modes". It is **effective only together with G16's `d3d_lines`**; armed without it, it is logged and ignored.

## 5. Caps

**No change, in either switch state.**
- With Part A in, `D3DPSHADECAPS_COLORFLATRGB` and `ALPHAFLATBLEND` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:2032-2033`) become true. 3dfx's matrix says Napalm does both (`D3DXLS` "ColorFlatRGB", "AlphaFlatBlend": Yes).
- With `D3DFlat = 0` the caps still advertise flat. That is today's state, and the DDK treats flat as a baseline every device has (section 1, correction 4); dropping the bits would change nothing for D3D8/9 and would need a boot-latched switch.
- Part B has no cap: wireframe and point fill are not capped in DX7 or D3D8 (inferred from `DDK8/d3dcaps.h` and `d3d8caps.h`, which have no fill-mode bits). Line caps are G16's.

## 10. Risk to proven paths

- **Default 16 bpp path.** Contexts that never set FLAT: identical register stream (the same addresses read, the same values written). Contexts that set FLAT: colour values only. The decode refactor is pinned by the native test against today's loops and by the default set plus `cull` on silicon. Counters: integer increments and two log calls per context.
- **32 bpp, D3D32, BigTex:** untouched (the change is above the target and texture code).
- **Glide/OpenGL coexistence:** no state register is touched, so nothing new is inherited from or left for Glide (G01/G03 still apply as today).
- **SLI:** none (D3D draws on chip 0, G51). **Flip path:** none.
- **Behavioural risk:** a title that sets FLAT by mistake and was only ever looked at on a driver that ignored it. No such title is known, every vendor driver honoured FLAT, and the kill switch reverts it without a reboot.
- **Part B (armed only):** G16's risks (quads at screen edges, cull-bit writes) plus a CPU cull test in the FPU bracket, only for contexts that set a non-solid FILLMODE.
- **Interaction:** G49 must keep flat triangles independent (4.1 f); G16's `vertex()` source test changes in whichever commit lands second.

- Part A: S. About 90 lines of header, 40 in the walker, 10 in `vcrdd_3d.c`, 15 of switch plumbing; native test about 250 lines, Python about 80; d3dprobe about 250 (six tests); one bed session and one `.124` boot.
- Part B: M on top of G16. About 80 lines HAL, 150 probe, 120 tests; one more boot.

**Dependencies.**
- Part A: none blocking. G01 is the precondition for any valid silicon D3D run; G76 is soft (Step 0 is its shade/fill slice).
- Part B: **G16** (hard).
- Related: G49 (strip mode must respect flat), G25 (specular colour source), G57 (guard band, if `flatclip` fails), G75 (a DX5 probe is the only lab path for INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST edge flags), G74 (probe infrastructure).

**Games** (static scan, Appendix A; unmeasured until the Step-0 counters run):
- Part A: **Thief Gold and System Shock 2** (the Dark engine's lgd3d has a shade setter that sends `D3DSHADE_FLAT` for a false argument; who calls it with false is not settled statically); **Rainbow Six** only on a software device (on `.124`'s HAL it switches to GOURAUD at init); **BF1942** (one FLAT site; blocked by SafeDisc 2.80, G72); any D3D8/9 title that sends FLAT (UE2 and Red Faction send a constant GOURAUD; Halo also has three register-valued sites).
- **Not** Jedi Knight or MotS: constant GOURAUD/SOLID.
- Part B: Halo's and Unreal Engine 2's wireframe debug modes (inferred).

**Priority: P2.** Correctness and caps hygiene: no staged title is shown to draw wrong in common scenes because of this. Part A is nearly free and should ship with the next build. It becomes P1 if the Phase-0 pass shows Thief Gold or System Shock 2 drawing flat triangles in normal play. Part B alone would be P3.

## 12. Open questions

1. Which staged titles draw flat triangles in play, through which DP2 forms, and how many? (Step-0 counters; Thief Gold and SS2 first.)
2. Does XP's runtime keep the flat colour when it clips a flat triangle into a TRIANGLEFAN_IMM? (`flatclip`.) If not, no vertex choice in the driver can recover it, and reporting a guard band (G57) is the remedy, since the runtime then stops clipping most screen-edge triangles.
3. Which DP2 opcodes does the D3D8 runtime use for DrawPrimitiveUP and DrawIndexedPrimitiveUP fans and strips on a DX7 driver? (The 513/27 form mask during `flatshade` and `flatshade_idx`.)
4. Flat point fill: the triangle's first vertex (3dfx) or each point's own colour (MSP2)? (The 86Box reference driver decides; `pointfill` records it as a note.)
5. TRIANGLEFAN_IMM wireframe colour: `V[i+1]` (the DDK's triangle order, used here) or `V[0]` (both references' wireframe paths)? (`wireclip` with FLAT, a note.)
6. Zero-area triangles in wireframe: culled under CCW as `CULL_TRI` does; what does the reference rasterizer do? (inferred unknown; a note only.)
7. Numbering: the `VCR_INFO_F_D3DFLAT` bit, the `D3DFeat` FILL bit, and the 513 sub-codes (27-29 here; G05 and G16 already contend for 24). This is a plan-level allocation.
8. Does UE2's `rmode 1` reach `D3DRS_FILLMODE` (UT2004 `D3DDrv.dll` writes FILLMODE from a cached state at 0x1001a5b3)? (Boot B counters.)
9. Did any DX5 title depend on 3dfx's Win9x quirk of flat RGB with gouraud alpha (`W9X/D3TRI.C:303-304`)? (`flatalpha` on the reference driver says what XP-era hardware did; no staged title is known to.)

## Appendix A: static scan of the staged binaries (heuristic)

Method: `i686-w64-mingw32-objdump -d` of each binary, then a search for (1) execute-buffer stores `movl $0x9,(reg)` / `movl $0x8,(reg)` followed by the value store, and (2) COM calls `push value; push 9|8; push this; call *OFF(reg)` with OFF the SetRenderState slot of IDirect3DDevice2 (0x5c), 3 (0x58), 7 (0x50), 8 (0xc8) or 9 (0xe4). A wrapper or a table-driven state write can hide from both, so **a miss is not evidence of absence**; the Step-0 counters are the measurement.

## 0. Verdict

1. **The gap is real.** Shogo has never been shown to render correctly on vcr-kmd in a way anyone can trust.
   Of the eight `.124` frames on file:
   - one shows the complete menu (S08, 60 s);
   - two show the backdrop before any text (30 s);
   - one shows a black wavy mesh with the cloth only at the borders (S08F, 60 s);
   - four show the menu text on black, or plain black, after an OpenGL session.

   `PLAN:107` nevertheless lists Shogo with the titles that "render their menus or intros correctly", and the
   02:08 re-sweep was summarised as "every title matches or beats the first run". The sweep verdict never looks
   at the picture (`lan_sweep.py:300-327`), so the S08F frame passed with luma stats (1280, 960, 2.0, 5.3).
2. **Seven findings from this reading.** None of them is on record yet.
   - **(a) The post-OpenGL frames are G01's signature, exactly.**
     - Shogo's menu text is a DirectDraw blit. CShell sends `OptimizeSurfaces 1` only from its launcher-flag
       table (section 2.2), and our DirectDraw HAL offers no keyed blits, so the HEL draws the text with the CPU
       (inferred).
     - The cloth is Direct3D triangles.
     - A stale Glide command FIFO drops PIO triangles while clears and 2D still work (`reports/onbox.md` item 1).
       That gives "text on black" and nothing else.
     - **So the 09-29 and 10-01 frames belong to G01/G03.** Shogo is their cleanest game-level witness.
   - **(b) GDI frames of Shogo's menu are unreliable on every driver, not only ours.** The 08-31 frames from
     seven boxes on other vendors' drivers (among them a GeForce4 Ti, a Radeon, and `.124` itself when it held the
     GeForce2 GTS) show the same menu
     in three states: complete on 2 boxes, backdrop only on 3, the title line only on 1. The compat note
     "client menu renders intermittently" is this. A GDI read of a double-buffered DirectDraw app returns
     whichever buffer sits at the GDI primary. After an odd number of flips that is the buffer being drawn
     (`reports/visual.md` section 2).
   - **(c) The S08F frame fits that capture artefact, and fits a real defect just as well.**
     - On our HAL the 3D part of a frame is slow: PIO, about 4 µs a triangle (`reports/onbox.md` section 5).
       A back-buffer read is therefore likely to land mid-cloth (inferred).
     - No text means the 2D pass had not run yet.
     - Nothing on file separates the two readings.
   - **(d) Shogo has its own frame capture, and it needs no vcrctl.**
     - Releasing **F8** makes `Client.exe` write `Screenshot<N>.bmp` into its working directory.
     - The render DLL writes it from the back buffer, locked with `DDLOCK_WAIT`, between frames: the previous
       complete frame, 2D text included (section 2.3).
     - It travels as a window message, so the agent's `UIKEY` reaches it.
   - **(e) There are two direct in-game routes.**
     - `+runworld worlds\01_Ambush` uses CShell's own `runworld` variable and its first mission name.
     - The readiness recipe's `+Connect 192.168.1.132` reached MCA_12FLOZ on seven boxes on 11-11. Their frames
       are reference images.
   - **(f) LithTech 1 tears its renderer down when it loses the focus** and rebuilds it when it gets the focus
     back. A focus round trip is one of the hypotheses to rule out.
   - **(g) Every polygon is its own `DrawPrimitive(D3DPT_TRIANGLEFAN)`.** LithTech clips each polygon itself, so
     a "black interior, cloth at the edge" frame is not a primitive-type split between the interior and the
     edges.
3. **G70 is therefore an investigation with tooling deliverables.** It designs no driver behaviour change. The
   fixes its outcomes point to belong to G01/G03, G55, G63, G21/G37 and G42/G43, and section 6 maps each outcome
   to its owner.
   - **What G70 owns:** the capture method, the protocol, a frame classifier with its regression test, a
     d3dprobe scene shaped like Shogo's menu, and the first in-game Shogo frames on `.124`.
   - **Optional observation-only counters** (section 7.4). They should merge into G76 if G76 lands first.

## 2. What the game does

### 2.1 Launch and configuration

- **The launcher.** `LIB/Shogo/Play Shogo - direct engine.bat` (md5 `7a93e4f7`) runs FLEETRES, which writes
  `screenwidth`/`screenheight` into `autoexec.cfg` (1280x960 on `.124`). It then runs, from `C:\Games\Shogo`:

  ```
  Client.exe -rez shogo.rez -rez sound.rez -rez ShogoP.rez -rez ShogoP2.rez -rez ShogoP3.rez -rez ShogoP4.rez
  ```

- **`autoexec.cfg`** (md5 `b6e0c7d8`):
  - `screendepth 16` (`:21`);
  - `RenderDll d3d.ren` (`:23`);
  - `MasterPaletteMode 1` (`:25`). It prefers 8-bit palettised textures and falls back to 16-bit ones because
    we offer no P8 (G38, G34);
  - `windowed 0` (`:152`);
  - `EnvPanSpeed` (`:20`), `WarbleScale` (`:34`), `WarbleSpeed` (`:38`): the animated cloth.
- **The command line overrides the config.** `+name value` arguments are applied after `autoexec.cfg`
  (`RDY/recipes.json` Shogo, point 1).

### 2.2 How a menu frame is built

- **Direct3D.**
  - `d3d.ren` imports only `DirectDrawCreate`/`DirectDrawEnumerateA` and drives an `IDirect3DDevice3`.
  - In the `d3d.ren` disassembly, 24 call sites use device slot +0x70 (`DrawPrimitive`). At least 14 of them push
    `D3DPT_TRIANGLEFAN` (6) with:
    - FVF `0x1c4` (XYZRHW|DIFFUSE|SPECULAR|TEX1), 12 sites (e.g. `0x10012ee9`, `0x1001aaa4`);
    - or FVF `0x244` (two texture sets: 1-pass lightmapping), 2 sites (`0x1002dfb9`, `0x10030060`);
    - and flags `0x1c` (DONOTCLIP|DONOTUPDATEEXTENTS|DONOTLIGHT).
  - Two sites use +0x74 (`DrawIndexedPrimitive`).
  - LithTech clips every polygon itself and sends each one as its own fan. Edge and interior cells of the cloth
    therefore reach the HAL the same way (DP2 `TRIANGLEFAN`/`TRIANGLEFAN_IMM`, `vcrdd_d3d.c:1634-1642`,
    `:1678-1689`).
- **The cloth.** CShell names `PolyGrids` and `Cloth`. Together with the warble/env-pan variables, the backdrop is
  an animated, textured grid of polygons (inferred).
- **2D surfaces** come in two kinds.
  - "Optimized" ones are uploaded as textures. `d3d.ren` carries `OptimizeSurfaces`, `FilterOptimized` and
    "Error: tried to render 3D while in optimized 2D mode.".
  - The others are DirectDraw blits ("Warning: drawing a nonoptimized surface while in 3D mode.").
  - CShell v3 has two parallel tables:
    - launcher flags: `EnableMultiTex`, `EnablePixDub`, `DisableLines`, `DisableDx6Cmds`, `EnableTripBuf`,
      `EnableOptSurf`, `DisableMovies`, ... (0x749a8-0x74a64);
    - console commands: `Force1Pass 1`, `PixelDouble 1`, `DrawLineSystems 0`, `UseDX6Commands 0`,
      `TripleBuffer 1`, `OptimizeSurfaces 1`, ... (0x748ac-0x74930).
  - Their order pairs each flag with a command (inferred). So `OptimizeSurfaces 1` is sent only when the launcher
    flag `EnableOptSurf` is set, and the direct-engine launcher sets no flags.
  - **The menu text is therefore drawn with DirectDraw blits.** Our DirectDraw HAL reports
    `DDCAPS_BLT | DDCAPS_BLTCOLORFILL | DDCAPS_READSCANLINE` (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:101`) with no colour-key caps, so a
    keyed blit is done by the HEL with the CPU through `Dd_Lock` (inferred; G61).
- **The cost of a frame on our HAL.** Hundreds of small fans go out as PIO register writes. At the measured
  ~4 µs a triangle (`reports/onbox.md` section 5), a full-screen cloth costs several milliseconds of an 11.8 ms
  refresh at 85 Hz (inferred).

### 2.3 The game's own capture: F8

From `Client.exe`:

- **The window procedure** is `0x4036a0`, registered at `0x4032d5`.
- **`WM_KEYUP`** (`0x4039ee`) goes to `0x403b2e`. When `wParam == VK_F8` (0x77), the code:
  1. formats `"%s%d.bmp"` with `"Screenshot"` (`0x4803dc`, `0x482784`);
  2. takes the first i < 3000 whose file does not exist (an `fopen(..,"rb")` probe);
  3. calls the render DLL's screenshot through `*0x48c1a8`.

From `d3d.ren`, the screenshot is `0x10016760`:

- It calls `GetSurfaceDesc` (+0x58) and then `Lock` (+0x64) **with `DDLOCK_WAIT` (1)** on the surface at
  `[0x10045abc]`.
- It writes a 24-bit BMP ('BM', 0x36-byte header).
- `[0x10045abc]` is set by an `EnumAttachedSurfaces` (+0x24) callback (`0x10013f30`) on the primary flip chain
  (`0x10014a87-0x10014abd`). It is **the back buffer**, the rendering surface.

What this means:

- **It is a complete frame.** The handler runs from the message pump, between frames. After a flip DirectDraw has
  swapped the surfaces, so the back buffer holds the previous complete frame, with its 2D text (inferred: the
  pump runs outside the render).
- **It is unaffected by a pending flip.** Our `Dd_Lock` answers `DDERR_WASSTILLDRAWING` while the buffer is the
  outgoing side of a pending flip (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:653`). `DDLOCK_WAIT` retries until the flip completes.
- **The keys are free.** F8 is `DIK_F8` = `##66`, which `autoexec.cfg` does not bind. The F11/PrintScreen binds
  (`:134`, `:156`) go to CShell's ScreenShot action, through DirectInput.
- **The agent can press it.** F8 travels as a window message, so `UIKEY F8` reaches it while the LithTech
  window has the focus. **Not yet proven on any box** (S1).
- **The file size is known.** At 1280x960 a correct BMP is 54 + 1280·960·3 = 3,686,454 bytes. A failed lock
  leaves a file with the header only (the `0x10016a18` path).

### 2.4 Focus

`WM_ACTIVATEAPP` (0x1c) is handled at `0x4036fb`:

- On deactivation it prints "Losing focus.. shutting down renderer." (`0x4037c1`) and calls
  `0x442910(renderer, 1)`.
- On activation it prints "Regained focus.. initializing renderer.".
- Both are skipped when either of two flags (`[0x48b21c]`, `[0x489d8c]`) is set.
- `alwaysfocused` is looked up at `0x41031e`. When it is 1.0, the renderer is initialised with a different
  argument (`0x410342-0x410353`): probably "do not deactivate" (inferred).
- A round trip rebuilds the DirectDraw/D3D objects and the textures. In the flight recorder it shows as a second
  `ContextCreate` (513/1) for the same pid.
- **Focus was not lost at the samples.** The sweeps recorded the LithTech window as foreground at both samples
  (`S08F/sweep.json`, `foreground.game: true`). A loss and a regain between the samples are not excluded.

### 2.5 Direct in-game routes and diagnostic levers

- **Single player.**
  - CShell v3 reads `runworld` in `OnEngineInitialized` (string at CShell 0x74724). The readiness disassembly
    shows the network path "jumps past runworld, the intro movies and the menu" (`0x100303ca`).
  - The first mission is `Worlds\01_Ambush` (CShell 0x751e4).
  - The route is `+runworld worlds\01_Ambush`. Its spelling and behaviour are unverified; S1 confirms them.
- **Multiplayer.** The readiness launcher (`RDY/recipes.json` Shogo) passes:

  ```
  +windowed 0 +NetHost 0 +NetPlayerName Fleet124 +UpdateRateInitted 1 +UpdateRate 20
  +HaveSettingInfo 1 +DontDisplaySettingsDialog 1 +NetJoinRetry 10 +Connect 192.168.1.132
  ```

  - It joins `shogo-server` (UDP 27888) and drops straight into MCA_12FLOZ.
  - It was proven on 7 boxes on 11-11. `RDY/rounds/Shogo_*.jpg` and `Shogo_montage.jpg` are reference frames of
    that map.
  - End the run with `PROCKILL`. A clean exit would save `Fleet124` over the box's own `NetPlayerName`.
- **Levers.**
  - `Client.exe` console variables:
    - `ErrorLog`, `AlwaysFlushLog`, `errorlogfile` (`error.log`, 0x81d68). Whether they record the console
      output or only errors is unknown (S1);
    - `ShowFrameRate` ("FPS: %.2f"), `MaxFPS`, `HighPriority`, `ForceClear`, `AutoDeactivate`, `DebugLevel`.
  - CShell: `DisableMovies`.
  - `d3d.ren` console commands: `LISTDEVICECAPS`, `LISTDEVICES` (G29). Its device report includes "ZBuffer Depth:
    %d, Device Depth: %d".
  - The launcher flags of section 2.2: `UseDX6Commands`, `TripleBuffer`, `OptimizeSurfaces`.

### 2.6 The Z pick changed under Shogo after the 11-08 frames

- Shogo is DX6, like Rainbow Six. Rainbow Six enumerated Z formats at the 32 bpp desktop, where `D3D32` lists
  D24X8/D24S8, took the deepest one and then rendered at 16 bpp.
- That pair was refused until `be58b7d`. Since `vcrdd 95678704` it is drawn 16/16 (`PLAN:163-174`).
- **S08 and S08F both predate `be58b7d`.** Shogo therefore rendered with a 16-bit Z (or none) in those frames.
- If Shogo picks a 32-bit Z, the deployed build now gives it a device it never had before (inferred).
- Each run records ContextCreate's Z line (513/1 `z %x`) and the game's own "ZBuffer Depth" line.

## 4. Instruments

1. **F8 engine frames: the primary evidence.**
   - Complete composed frames, no display lock, no vcrctl, no escape.
   - Taken only when `WINLIST` says the foreground window is the LithTech window (lan_sweep's `key_to` rule:
     keys go to the focused window).
   - The new `Screenshot*.bmp` files are found by a `DIRLIST` before/after diff (`lan_check.dir_stamps`).
     Each is downloaded, its size checked (3,686,454 bytes at 1280x960), then **deleted, and the delete verified
     by DIRLIST**. GAMESYNC never deletes, so a left-over file stays on the box for good.
2. **A GDI burst.** The agent's `SCREENSHOT 0` every 5 s, offset 2.5 s from the F8 presses, each frame
   classified. This **quantifies** H1: the share of `partial`/`backdrop` frames against the F8 frames.
   - Each GDI read holds win32k's display lock while it reads 2.4 MB of video memory, so the game stalls for a
     moment (inferred).
   - Keep the burst to the menu phase.
3. **A person at the CRT.**
   - Today this is the only view of the scanned-out picture that needs no vcrctl tool, and the only cheap
     detector of H5 flicker.
   - The operator watches each phase for 60 s and reports: steady or flicker, black flashes, black cloth, text
     present.
   - A phone video of the CRT is acceptable evidence.
4. **The flight recorder.**
   - `Diag\LogEntries` = 16384, the maximum (`vcrmp_log.c:281-282`). Entries are 128 bytes (`vcr_log.h:48-57`),
     so the ring takes 2 MB of non-paged pool. It is read at driver load, so it needs one reboot.
   - `LogLevel` stays at its default, 3 (DEBUG). At ~85 `513/8` target changes a second, 16384 entries hold
     about the last 3 minutes of a menu (inferred).
   - The ring is fetched with `KMD/tools/vcrlog.py fetch 192.168.1.124 --save ...` **only after Client.exe has

## 7. Deliverables and gating

### 7.1 `scripts/benchmarks/frame_verdict.py` - a per-title frame classifier (Shogo's menu first)

- **Interface.** `classify_shogo_menu(png_bytes_or_path)` returns one of `menu`, `backdrop`, `text_only`,
  `partial`, `black` or `unclassified`, plus the feature values.
- **Regions** are given in 1280x960 units and scaled for any 4:3 size:
  - the text box: x 520-760, y 340-620;
  - the central region: x 200-1080, y 150-820, minus the text box, sampled every 2 px;
  - the border: the outer 24 px columns.
- **Features:**
  - **orange text px:** R ≥ 200, 60 ≤ G ≤ 150, B ≤ 60;
  - **white text px:** R, G and B ≥ 220;
  - **central black share:** pixels whose largest channel ≤ 4;
  - **central and border mean blue.**
- **Rules** (s = width / 1280):

  | class | condition |
  |---|---|
  | `black` | the frame's largest channel ≤ 8 |
  | text present | orange px ≥ 850·s² (one tenth of the measured 8517) |
  | `menu` | text, central black < 0.5, central blue ≥ 15 |
  | `backdrop` | no text, central black < 0.5, central blue ≥ 15 |
  | `text_only` | text, central black ≥ 0.9, border blue < 5 |
  | `partial` | no text, central black ≥ 0.5, border blue ≥ 15 |
  | `unclassified` | anything else, or not 4:3 |

  The measured features in section 1 separate every archived frame by a wide margin.
- **lan_sweep.** It gains a per-sample `frame` field. For titles with a registered classifier, a sample that is
  not `menu`/`backdrop` adds the CHECK reason "frame not evidenced: <class>".
- **Gating.** Tooling only: it lands default-on, with the PASS/CHECK logic for other titles unchanged. G79
  generalises it.

### 7.2 `lan_check.py`: a `Shogo` route

  - It adds only the phase's `+` arguments (section 5).
- **Keys.** `host_keys` returns the F8 schedule. The runner sends F8 only after `WINLIST` foreground = the LithTech

### 7.3 d3dprobe: `menugrid`, `menugrid2` and a full-target point readback

- **The generator.** A Win32-free header, `KMD/tools/d3dprobe_grid.h`, builds the grid, texel table, clipping
  and centroids, so the native test compiles the same code (section 9).
- **The readback.** `readback_points()` locks the whole back buffer (`D3DLOCK_READONLY`) and reads arbitrary
  points. Today's `readback()` stops at the top-left 256x256 (`d3dprobe.c:292-323`). G74 should absorb the
  helper.
- **Gating.** Explicit tests, not in the default list (`d3dprobe.c:1247`). They join the default list after one
  silicon pass; that moves the default counts and needs the goldens, the test count and the README updated.

### 7.4 Optional HAL instrumentation (observation only; merge into G76 if it lands first)

- **I1.** `texblt()`: a WARN once per context when the destination handle is non-zero and either handle does not

- **I2.** `tri()`: count triangles skipped for an index out of range (`:1369`), and DP2 calls skipped because
  `!drawable` (`:1859`), into the context. Add both to the existing 513/2 "context gone" line.
- **I3.** Per-context counts of `TRIANGLEFAN`, `TRIANGLEFAN_IMM`, `LINE*`, `POINTS` and `TEXBLT` opcodes, in one

- **Gating.** These counters only add increments and log lines on paths that already exist. The register write
  sequence of every drawn triangle is byte-for-byte unchanged, and a test pins that (section 9). They may land
  default-on, as `log_combo` (513/21-23) did. Their cost: two compares per DP2 call, an increment on failure
  paths, and one log line per context.

## 8. The d3dprobe test

**Name:** `menugrid`, with `menugrid2`. Explicit. Modes: 640x480x16 (the default lane), 1280x960x16 (Shogo's mode),
1024x768x32 (the `D3D32` lane) and windowed on the 32 bpp desktop.

**What `menugrid` draws.** Each of 8 frames, phase k/8:

- **Clear** to black, Z 1.0.
- **Texture:** 32x32 R5G6B5, `D3DPOOL_MANAGED`, one level.
  - Texel (i,j) is 565(R = i, G = 2j+1, B = (i XOR j) & 31): unique for every cell and never black.
  - Point filter. `MODULATE(TEXTURE, DIFFUSE)`. No alpha, dither off, Z `LESSEQUAL` with writes on, cull none,
    `D3DRS_CLIPPING` FALSE.
- **Grid:** 24x18 cells over the target.
  - Vertex (i,j) sits at x = i·W/24 + 0.2·(W/24)·sin 2π(j/18 + phase), y = j·H/18 + 0.2·(H/18)·sin 2π(i/24 + phase).
  - Boundary vertices are pushed 0.75 cell outward.
  - The probe clips each cell's quad to [0,W]x[0,H] itself (Sutherland-Hodgman), as LithTech does. Interior
    cells stay 4-vertex fans; edge cells become 3-8-vertex fans.
- **One `DrawPrimitiveUP(D3DPT_TRIANGLEFAN)` per cell** with Shogo's FVF `0x1c4`:
  - rhw 1, z 0.5;
  - diffuse `0xffffffff`, specular 0;
  - every vertex of cell (i,j) carries texel (i,j)'s centre as (u,v). The UV is constant over the cell, so the
    expected colour does not depend on the warp or on interpolation.
- **Readback:** on the last frame, before Present. One point per cell: the centroid of its clipped polygon,
  rounded to a pixel centre, over the **whole** target.

**`menugrid2`** draws the same grid twice a frame, the way multipass engines draw:
- pass 1: untextured, diffuse black, Z write on;
- pass 2: textured, Z `EQUAL`, Z write off, `SRCBLEND ONE` / `DESTBLEND ONE`.

It checks repeatable Z across passes and ONE/ONE over a full-screen mesh. Expected: black + texel = texel.

**Pass criterion.**
- **All 432 centroids** equal their texel's colour (expanded as `d3dprobe.c` `expand()` does), within the `tex`
  test's tolerance.
- Failures are listed per cell and split into "the clear colour (black)", which is S08F's signature, and "other".
- **Reference values** are the texel table from `d3dprobe_grid.h`, the same code the native test checks.

**What it catches.**
- a full-target warped textured mesh made of hundreds of small fans with self-clipped edges, read back past the
  256x256 window. That is the shape of Shogo's menu, and no current test reads it (G74);
- multipass Z-EQUAL and ONE/ONE on that mesh;
- interior cells reading the clear colour.

**On the current build.** It is expected to pass. If it fails at 1280x960 but not at 640x480, that points at
target size or placement, not at Shogo.

## 12. Risk to proven paths

- **The default 16 bpp path, D3D32, BigTex, the flip path and SLI.** G70's own deliverables change no driver
  code; I1-I3 change only logging and counters. The sessions run Shogo exactly as it is staged: the same
  16 bpp target and texture formats, D3D on chip 0 only, and no SLI. The flip path is observed, not changed.
- **Glide/OpenGL coexistence.**
  - S2 keeps the boot free of OpenGL/Glide on purpose.
  - S4 creates the stale-FIFO state on purpose, attended and inside G01's own protocol. Recovery is
    `vcrctl reset-engine`, then `vcrctl modeseq`, or a reboot.
- **Stability.** Shogo on vcr-kmd has crashed nothing in four sweeps. The rules stay:
  - attended;
  - the agent PING watchdog;
  - kill by PID only;
  - no vcrctl under the game;
  - no new title is run on `.124` before S1 has proven its route on `.133`.
- **Box state.**
  - The F8 BMPs (3.7 MB each) and `error.log` land in `C:\Games\Shogo`. The route deletes them and verifies
    the deletes, because GAMESYNC never removes files.
  - A clean exit saves `autoexec.cfg`. The menu and SP runs save only the values FLEETRES already wrote
    (inferred). The MP run ends with `PROCKILL` so `Fleet124` is never saved.
  - `LogEntries` 16384 costs 2 MB of non-paged pool on a 256 MB box until it is deleted.
- **Mode switches.** Shogo's own switches (desktop → 1280x960x16@85 → desktop) cannot be paced. They are the same
  switches it made in every sweep. d3dprobe runs use `--pace 5000`.
- **The GDI burst** stalls the game while each read holds the display lock (inferred). It only affects frames,
  never state, and is kept to the menu phase.

# G52: logging on the drawing path (design)

Lane: **clean-room** (`vcr-kmd`: the display DLL `vcrdd.dll` and the miniport `vcrmp.sys`).
Source: `reports/perf.md` perf-07 (§1.8 lines 198-208, §4 row 5, §4.2 lines 456-465, §5 step P1 lines 582-594).
Related designs: `designs/G46.md` §1.2/§5 (the same exposure bound; it wants a "VcrDd calls that reached the IOCTL"
counter), `designs/G42.md` §2.1 (destroy-notice cost, "G52 makes the per-surface 513/3 cheap"), `designs/G04.md` §3.5
(LogLevel and timing during the Max Payne boots).
This is a design only. Nothing was edited, built or deployed, and no box was contacted.

The gap text's "Fix (P1)" names the perf plan's **step** P1 (`reports/perf.md:582`), not a priority.

Aliases: `KMD` = `voodoo-cleanroom/vcr-kmd`, `VH5` = `retro-3dfx/3dfx Driver Code/H5/W2K/Src/Video/Displays/H5`,
`ONBOX` = `KMD/evidence/d3dplan_1112/onbox`, `WT` = the `v56k-bench` worktree root.

## 0. Verdict

- **The defect is real.** Every line the gap cites was checked (§1). The sweep also found five more per-draw sites:
  the TRACE in `DrvSetPalette`, and four WARNs that repeat once per occurrence (unknown SETRENDERTARGET handle,
  target refused at SETRENDERTARGET, texture refused, unparsed or short DP2 stream).
- **Today's frame-time cost is small. It is now bounded by measurement, not assumed** (§2):
  - **What was measured.** UT2003's flyby advanced the ring sequence by 110,186 for the whole session (load, play and
    exit) over 1,998 frames. That is at most 55 events a frame, of every kind.
  - **The per-event cost is still inferred**, at 5-15 µs. The rings bound it at 26 µs or less, a figure that also
    includes the runtime's own surface work.
  - **So logging costs 0.3-0.8 ms of UT2003's 123 ms frame, under 1 %.** `designs/G46.md` §1.2 reached the same
    bound independently.
  - **G52 does not move G46's 9.41 fps.** It becomes 3-7 % of the frame only once the HAL reaches the 85 Hz budget
    (11.8 ms).
- **The flight recorder pays the larger cost.**
  - `.124` runs at LogLevel 3: DEBUG events are stored, and no TRACE entry appears in any 11-12 ring.
  - At that level a UE2 session writes about 100k events. The 1024-entry ring therefore holds about 2 s of play at
    9 fps, and would hold about 0.2 s at 85 fps.
  - So the lead-up to a freeze (G04) cannot survive in the ring today.
- **Design:**
  - **(A)** The DLL applies **the miniport's own storage rule before formatting**, so a dropped event costs one
    compare.
  - **(B)** The miniport reports its level in an appended `vcr_info.log_level`. `Diag\LogGate = 0` reports
    "ungated", which restores today's traffic exactly.
  - **(C)** The per-draw sites are **counted per context**. Each logs its first N occurrences and then each power of
    two, and the totals are logged when the context goes.
- **What does not change:**
  - no register write;
  - no caps bit;
  - no extra pass;
  - no change to what the ring keeps for any event the DLL still sends.
- **Recommendation:** default **ON**, priority **P2**, effort **S**. It can ride along with the first campaign build
  that touches `vcrdd.dll`.

## 2. What was measured (11-12 on-box evidence, `ONBOX/README.md` runs 14-21)

**Level.** Across every 11-12 ring capture (7,791 entries, the captures overlap) the levels are 0:1, 1:4, 2:798, 3:6988, 4:0. LogLevel is 3, so
every TRACE was formatted, sent and then dropped.

**Volume, from the ring sequence numbers** (`next_seq` of the captures before and after each session):

| session | sequence advanced | frames | DP2 calls (`context gone`) | per frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UT2004 botmatch run 1, 1280x960x16 | 30,121 → 97,382 = **67,261** | not counted (1069 clears) | 26,145 | (≈63 per clear) |
| **UT2003 flyby, 640x480x16** | 97,382 → 207,568 = **110,186** | **1,998** (`reports/onbox.md` §5) | 59,589 | **≤ 55** |
| Halo timedemo, 1280x960 | 207,568 → 306,116 = **98,548** | not counted (764 clears) | 32,670 | - |

- Each figure covers the whole session: load, play and exit.
- The creation path accounts for some thousands of events. UT2003's handles reach about 1857
  (`designs/G42.md` §2.1), so about 2 notices per surface comes to about 4k.
- That leaves about **50 texture binds a frame** for UT2003 (inferred split; measured total).

**Cost of one event: still inferred, but bounded.**

- The densest display-DLL bursts in the rings are destroy notices at game exit:
  - **38 events in one 1 ms tick** at the UT2004 statfps exit (`ONBOX/games/UT2004_statfps/ring.txt`; the clock tick
    there is 1 ms, 64 of 73 tick steps);
  - 32 in one tick at the Halo exit.
- So one VcrDd round trip, plus `name_surface`, plus the runtime's own per-surface teardown, takes **26 µs or less**.
- That fits perf-07's inferred 5-15 µs, but does not pin it. The `texbind` A/B in §6 measures it.

**Exposure.**

- 55 events × 5-15 µs = **0.3-0.8 ms a frame**: 0.2-0.6 % of UT2003's 123 ms frame.
- At the 85 Hz budget the same count would be **2.3-7 %** of the frame.

**The ring.**

- Every game ring captured after its session holds 1008-1013 destroy notices out of 1024 entries.
- The play itself was overwritten:
  - by the per-bind events during play;
  - by the exit's notices at the end.
- The `context gone` line survives only because it is logged after the notices.

## 3. The vendor reference (guidance only)

- **3dfx compiled its D3D tracing out of the free build.** In `VH5/D3GLOBAL.H:1988-1991`, `D3DPRINT` is
  `DebugPrint` only under `WINNT && DBG`, and otherwise `1 ? (void)0 : (void)`: the arguments are not even
  evaluated.
- **In the checked build, `DebugPrint` tests the level before it formats** (`VH5/DEBUG.C:100-115`,
  `if (DebugPrintLevel <= DebugLevel)` at `:110`).
- **We keep the recorder in the free build on purpose.** It is the crash-dump instrument (`tools/vcrdump.py`), so we
  need a **runtime** gate, in the vendor's order: the level test first, formatting second.
- **Not to copy:** `V5DLog` (`VH5/DEBUG.C:25-46`) formats unconditionally (`vsprintf` at `:44`). It is a later
  retro3dfx addition to the vintage tree, not 3dfx's code, and it has the same shape as this gap.
- **There is no register, combine-unit or caps reference to take.** This item touches none of them.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 The gate: a pure-logic header plus `vcrdd_log.c`

**New `KMD/include/vcr_loggate.h`.** It needs no OS header (`vcr_types.h` and `vcr_log.h` only), so the native test
compiles the code the DLL runs:

```c
#define VCR_LOGGATE_UNGATED 0xffffffffu   /* vcr_info.log_level: send every event, as before G52 */
typedef struct vcr_loggate { vcr_u32 level, sent, dropped; } vcr_loggate;
#define VCR_LOGGATE_INIT { VCR_LOGGATE_UNGATED, 0, 0 }      /* until the miniport says */

/* the field is trusted only when the miniport's answer covers it (vcr_info.size) */
static __inline void vcr_loggate_set(vcr_loggate *g, int reported, vcr_u32 level)
{ g->level = reported ? level : VCR_LOGGATE_UNGATED; }

/* EXACTLY the miniport's rule (vcrmp_log.c:326 stores unless level > g_log_level) */
static __inline int vcr_loggate_wants(const vcr_loggate *g, vcr_u32 level)
{ return g->level == VCR_LOGGATE_UNGATED || level <= g->level; }

/* per-draw sampling: count every occurrence; log the first n, then each power of two
 * (n, 2n, 4n ...). Ungated, or a level of TRACE (the firehose was asked for): every one. */
static __inline int vcr_loggate_first(const vcr_loggate *g, vcr_u32 *count, vcr_u32 n)
{
    vcr_u32 c = *count;
    if (c != 0xffffffffu)
        *count = c + 1;                     /* saturates; never wraps back to logging */
    if (g->level == VCR_LOGGATE_UNGATED || g->level >= VCR_LV_TRACE)
        return 1;
    return c < n || (c & (c - 1)) == 0;
}
```

**`KMD/display/vcrdd_log.c`.**

- One static `vcr_loggate g_gate = VCR_LOGGATE_INIT`.
- `VcrDd()` becomes:
  1. `if (!g_hDriver) return;`
  2. `if (!vcr_loggate_wants(&g_gate, level)) { g_gate.dropped++; return; }`
  3. `g_gate.sent++;`
  4. the existing body, unchanged.
- Accessors, declared in `vcrdd.h`:
  - `VcrDdLogGateSet(const vcr_info *, ULONG got)`;
  - `int VcrDdLogFirst(ULONG *count, ULONG n)`;
  - `void VcrDdLogTotals(ULONG *sent, ULONG *dropped)`. This is the counter G46 asks for.
- The header comment changes from "Never on a drawing path" to what is now true: "on a drawing path only through the
  gate and `VcrDdLogFirst`".
- The counters are plain increments. Two threads can only race in the rare case where they log at the same moment,
  and a lost increment of a diagnostic count is harmless.

Before `VcrDd2dInit` first runs, `g_gate` is ungated. So `DrvEnablePDEV`'s few lines on the very first PDEV go out
exactly as today.

### 4.2 Telling the DLL the level, and the kill switch `Diag\LogGate`

- **`KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h`.**
  - Append `vcr_u32 log_level;` to `vcr_info`, after its last field at landing. Today that is `mon_src` (`:107`);
    G15's `d3dfeat` and G02's `d3dsafe` may land first, and order does not matter.
  - Comment it: "the level the miniport stores, `VCR_LOGGATE_UNGATED` when `Diag\LogGate = 0`".
  - The convention is the established one: "appended - older tools read a shorter struct". `vcrctl.c:213` already
    reads `mon_src` that way.
- **`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_log.c`.** Add `ULONG VcrLogLevel(void) { return g_log_level; }`, declared in `vcrmp.h`.
- **`KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c` `fill_info`.**
  - Add `v->log_level = VcrDiagGet(L"LogGate", 1) ? VcrLogLevel() : VCR_LOGGATE_UNGATED;`.
  - It is read at every `IOCTL_VCR_INFO`, like `Accel2DText` and `DdHeapFloor` (`:453-466`). The display driver asks
    at each `DrvEnableSurface`, and XP builds a new PDEV at every mode change (`designs/G41.md` §2). So a change
    takes effect at the next mode set, with no reboot.
  - Corner case: `LogLevel = 0xffffffff` in the registry also reads as "ungated". That is consistent, because the
    miniport then stores everything anyway.
- **`KMD/display/vcrdd_2d.c` `VcrDd2dInit`** (`:432-502`):
  1. `memset(&info, 0, sizeof info)` before the IOCTL (`:443`). The struct is not zeroed today, which `designs/G09.md`
     also flags.
  2. Pass `&got` instead of NULL.
  3. On success:
     `VcrDdLogGateSet(&info, got)`, which uses the field only when
     `info.size >= FIELD_OFFSET(vcr_info, log_level) + sizeof info.log_level` **and** `got` covers it.
  4. Log the new event `VCR_EV_DD_LOG` what 1 (§4.4).
  5. On a failed IOCTL, leave the gate as it was. At DLL load that is ungated.

**The resulting matrix:**

| LogLevel | LogGate | per-bind events | Flip/Lock/Blt TRACE round trips | ring contents |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3 (deployed)** | **absent = on** | the first 16 per context, then each power of two; all counted | **none**: dropped before formatting | sampled per-draw events, everything else as today |
| 3 | 0 | every bind | every one, then dropped by the miniport | **identical per-frame traffic to build 95678704** |
| 2 | on | none sent; counted | none | INFO and up. LogLevel 2 now actually saves the IOCTLs, which G04's boots assume it does not (§9) |
| 4 | on | every bind (TRACE level = no sampling) | every one, stored | everything, as today at 4 |

### 4.3 The per-draw sites

All in `KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c`. Every site keeps its state change. Only the log call gains a guard.

| site | change |
|---|---|

| SETRENDERTARGET unknown handle (`:1731`) | under `VcrDdLogFirst(&c->rt_misses, 8)` |
| `target_refused` at SETRENDERTARGET (`:1739`) | under `VcrDdLogFirst(&c->rt_refusals, 8)`. ContextCreate's call (`:1192`) is unchanged: it refuses the context and is logged once |
| texture refused (`:932-943`) | keep the `tex_refused` dedupe, and add `VcrDdLogFirst(&c->tex_refusals, 8)` around the WARN |

tex_refusals, bad_streams, log_dropped0`) go in `vcr_d3dctx` (`:50-75`):

- a context slot is zero at creation (`ctx_free` `memset`s it, `:1210-1222`);
- `log_dropped0` takes `g_gate.dropped` in `D3d_ContextCreate`.

G76's planned counter framework can absorb them, with the same names.

### 4.4 Events

**`VCR_EV_DD_LOG`** is appended to `KMD/include/vcr_events.h` at the next free 5xx code at landing.

- 514 is free in the tree today. The designs for G02, G04 (514) and G43 (515) also propose codes, so the plan owner
  assigns the number.
- A code of its own keeps G52 off the contended 513 sub-codes 24-29.

Its sub-codes:

| what | level | when | fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | INFO | each `VcrDd2dInit` (one per PDEV) | b = the level the DLL filters at (0xffffffff = ungated); c = events sent since load; d = events not sent. Text: `log gate: the miniport stores level %u and below` or `log gate off (%s): every event sent` (`an older miniport` / `Diag\LogGate = 0`) |
| 2 | INFO | `ctx_free`, after the existing 513/2 line | b = texture binds, c = binds of the handle already bound, d = events the gate dropped during the context. Text adds TEXBLTs, Kpx and target moves |
| 3 | **WARN** | `ctx_free`, only if a sampled WARN site counted more than it logged | b = texture refusals, c = target refusals plus unknown handles, d = bad streams. Text: `... only the first 8 and each power of two were logged` |

- Two lines are needed because a ring message holds 95 characters (`log_combo` makes the same split, `:904`).
- The 513 description gains a=4, 7, 8, 13 and 14, with "d = running count".

### 4.5 What does not change

- **Register traffic:** byte for byte, in every mode. No `vcrdd_3d.c` line and no `VcrDd3d*` call is touched. Each
  bind still sets `dirty`, so `compute_regs` and the re-send run exactly as before.
- **Caps:** nothing changes. Single pass and multipass are not affected.
- **The ring:** for every event the DLL still sends, the miniport stores the same entry. The storage predicate is
  the same comparison; it is merely applied earlier.
- **Tool marks** (`VCR_ESC_LOG_MARK`, `vcrdd_escape.c:305-309`) call the IOCTL directly and never pass through
  `VcrDd`.
- **The DLL's imports:** none added (`tools/check_imports.py`).

### 4.6 The old path

There are two ways to get today's per-frame traffic exactly:

- an older miniport, whose answer is short: `size` does not cover the field;
- `Diag\LogGate = 0`.

Either way every event is formatted and sent, and every bind is logged. The only additions are one event per PDEV and
one or two per context. With a new miniport and an old DLL, the DLL ignores the appended field.

## 5. Gating

- **Default ON. No default-OFF switch is needed for the hardware rule.**
  - The register path is byte-for-byte unchanged in every mode, which is the project rule's own exception ("or the
    proven path byte-for-byte unchanged").
  - The ring keeps every event the DLL still sends, unchanged.
  - What changes is the number of IOCTLs, and per-draw DEBUG/WARN lines past their first N.
- **`Diag\LogGate = 0` is a kill switch,** in the same sense as `CoreClock`'s 0. Absent means on.
  - It restores build 95678704's per-frame IOCTL traffic exactly.
  - It makes the A/B in §6/§8 possible in one build, with no DLL swap.
  - It lets G04 reproduce the 10-04 traffic.
- **It is log-only.** It writes no register and changes no caps, and it is read per PDEV, like `Accel2DText`.
  Toggling it inside one boot is therefore outside the "one Diag change per clean boot" rule, which exists to attribute
  a wedge to a hardware change.
  - If the plan owner wants the rule applied literally, run the A/B as two boots: the deploy boot with the gate on,
    and the next boot with `LogGate = 0`.
- **The first deploy is still one supervised clean boot** (§8), the standard procedure for any driver pair:
  1. `bash tests/run_all.sh` green and `tools/check_imports.py`;
  2. the activation check;
  3. `tools/deploy_box.py`, because both binaries change;
  4. `safe-reboot.py`.

## 6. d3dprobe additions

**`texswitch`: the render check (correctness guard for the bind path).**

- **Set-up.** Three solid 8x8 R5G6B5 textures (`checker(8, c, c)`) whose colours differ in every channel by at least
  0x80: `c08040`, `40c080`, `8040c0`. COLOROP `SELECTARG1(TEXTURE)`, POINT filtering, Z off.
- **Draw.** Two frames. Each draws an 8x8 grid of 24x24 quads over `[Q0,Q1)`.
  - Each quad is its own `DrawPrimitiveUP`, preceded by `SetTexture` with the texture chosen by `lcg()`, seeded 52.
  - About 86 real binds in one context, so the run crosses the 16-bind threshold and the power-of-two markers.
  - d3dprobe counts the binds it issued: the `SetTexture` calls that changed the texture.
- **Pass.** For each frame, all 64 cell centres show their own texture's colour (565-expanded, tolerance 8). That is
  one check entry per frame: `"cells_bad":0`.
- **Failure.** A stale or skipped bind reads as one of the other two colours, at least 0x80 away.
- **Reference.** 64/64 on both builds. The render must not change; G52 only changes the log.
- **Default list.** `texswitch` is opt-in (`--tests texswitch`) for this boot. Adding it to the default list would turn
  the matrix that other designs cite as "40/40" into "42/42" (open question 4).

**`perf --mode texbind --binds K`** (default K = 256, `--frames 200`, `--novsync`, fullscreen 640x480x16).

This is one slice of the `perf --mode state` family proposed by the P0 step (`reports/perf.md:575`). If G78 lands that
family first, `texbind` becomes one value of its `--mode`.

- **Pass "same":** per frame, one `SetTexture(A)`, then K 8x8-pixel quads, each its own `DrawPrimitiveUP`.
- **Pass "alt":** the same K quads, alternating A and B: K binds a frame.
- The geometry and fill are identical, so `us_per_bind = (1/fps_alt - 1/fps_same) / K × 1e6`.
- **RESULT:** `"texbind":{"binds_per_frame":K,"fps_same":..,"fps_alt":..,"us_per_bind":..},"pid":..`
- The difference between `LogGate = 0` and `LogGate` on, on the same build and boot, **is the measured cost of one
  log event**. It replaces perf-07's inferred 5-15 µs in the plan.
- **Pass:** over 3 runs each, the median `us_per_bind` with the gate is lower than without it, by more than the larger
  of the two spreads. Record the value.
- **If it is within the noise,** the perf claim is refuted. G52 still lands for the ring and G46's counter, and the
  plan strikes the perf line.

## 7. Regression tests

**`WT/tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_loggate.c` (new).** It compiles `include/vcr_loggate.h` and cites
`display/vcrdd_log.c:VcrDd` and `miniport/vcrmp_log.c:VcrLogFromUser`.

- `the_dll_sends_exactly_what_the_miniport_stores`: for miniport levels {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 0x7fffffff} and event
  levels 0-4, `vcr_loggate_wants()` equals `!(ev > mp)`, the miniport's own rule.
- `trace_at_the_deployed_level_never_reaches_the_ioctl`:
  - fixed: with the level reported as DEBUG, `wants(TRACE) == 0`;
  - old-buggy: the ungated state, `VCR_LOGGATE_INIT` (today's `VcrDd`), gives `wants(TRACE) == 1`.
- `an_older_miniport_or_loggate_0_keeps_todays_traffic`: not reported, or `UNGATED`:
  - every level is wanted;
  - `first()` returns 1 for 1,000 of 1,000 calls (every bind logged, as today);
  - the count reaches 1,000.
- `first_n_then_powers_of_two_every_one_counted`, gated at DEBUG, 100,000 calls with n = 16:
  - fixed: **29 logged** (indices 0-15, then 16, 32, ..., 65536) and a count of 100,000;
  - old-buggy: 100,000 logged;
  - index 15 and 16 are logged, 17 and 31 are not, 32 is.
- `trace_level_means_every_one`: a gate level of TRACE returns 1 for every call.
- `the_count_saturates`:
  - from 0xfffffffe, one call gives 0xffffffff and is not logged;
  - the next call stays at 0xffffffff and is not logged (no wrap back to logging).

**`WT/tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_abi.c`.** Pin `log_level` as appended: its offset is the previous last field's offset

## 9. Risks to proven paths

| path | risk |
|---|---|
| default 16 bpp D3D, the BigTex/D3D32 paths | **none in registers**. Every state assignment is unchanged; pinned by `test_a_bind_still_binds`, the d3dseq native test and the render matrix |
| **flip** | The TRACE IOCTL between the `vidDesktopStartAddr` write and the retrace sample (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:335-353`) disappears at LogLevel 3 or lower, so the sample comes a few µs sooner. `vcr_flip.h`'s rule is never-early regardless of that delay ("a write racing into a retrace can then only be reported one frame LATE"). The only possible effect is slightly fewer one-frame-late flips. Watch the `flips N: retrace R, deadline D` line in step 5 against 11-12's `300: 284/15`. Not a candidate for G59: G59 depends on bpp, and logging does not |
| Glide/OpenGL coexistence, SLI | none. The HWCEXT escape lines keep their levels and the same storage rule |
| diagnosis | Per-bind lines past the first 16 and the markers are gone at LogLevel 3. Recovery: LogLevel 4 or `LogGate = 0`. `log_combo` (per combination, with texture shape, address and tLOD) is untouched, and it is what G05/G06/G09 rely on. A refusal can no longer hide: past its sampled lines, the what-3 **WARN** reports the count |
| ABI | `vcr_info` grows by 4 bytes, appended; older readers (`vcrctl`, 3dfxctl) read a prefix; `NEED_OUT(FIELD_OFFSET(vcr_info, mon_src))` (`vcrmp.c:794`) still accepts them. Either half may be older than the other (§4.6) |
| G04 (Max Payne) | **timing changes.** `designs/G04.md` §3.5 relies on "LogLevel does not change timing". After G52 it does: level 2 no longer costs IOCTLs, and level 3 sends far fewer. G04's boot 1 deliberately runs 95678704; any later G04 boot must record LogGate, and `LogGate = 0` reproduces the old traffic |
| numbering | the event code (514/515 contended), the append order of `vcr_info` and the integration-test dict are plan-owner allocations. No flag bit is used, deliberately: 0x8000-0x20000 are already contended by seven designs |

## 10. Dependencies and coordination

- **Hard:** none.
- **Verification ordering:** G01's stale command FIFO. The deploy boot is clean, so no Glide or OpenGL session may run
  before the checks.
- **Feeds:**
  - **G46/G78:** `VcrDdLogTotals()` is G46's "logging" counter. `perf --mode texbind` is a `--mode state` slice.
  - **G53:** `binds_same` measures redundant binds.
  - **G76:** the sampler and the per-context counters are a first slice of its framework. Ring capacity and the
    default LogLevel stay G76's.
  - **G42:** under G52, its 513/3 notices cost no IOCTL at LogLevel 2 or lower. At 3 they still do (open question 3).
  - **G04:** its boots must record the `LogGate` setting.

## 11. Games

- **No staged title is blocked by this gap,** so none is unblocked.
- **Who gains CPU time:** every D3D8/9 title that binds through texture-stage state, at the measured ≤ 55 events a
  frame (UE2):
  - UT2003, UT2004, Halo, Max Payne, Red Faction, Hidden & Dangerous Deluxe and BF1942 LAN;

  Lock, Blt and palette change, and per wait retry.
- **Who gains a readable ring:** every title. It helps G04 most.

## 12. Priority and effort

- **P2:** performance with a measurable gain (§6 measures it), plus hygiene for the recorder.
  - It is not P1: it fixes no visible defect, and G04 works around the ring today with `LogLevel 2` +
    `LogEntries 8192`.
  - It is not P3: it is the cheapest item in the perf list, and it removes a bias from every later per-call timing
    (G46).
- **Effort: S.**
  - About 40 lines in the DLL, 10 in the miniport and a 40-line header;
  - the tests;
  - two d3dprobe modes and a host verdict;
  - one supervised boot.

## 13. Open questions

1. **What does one VcrDd round trip cost on `.124`?** Step 6 answers it. Under 1 µs would strike perf-07's cost line;
   15 µs or more would make the creation-path bursts worth sampling too.
2. **Allocation of the event code, the append slot and the integration-dict category.** These are plan-owner calls,
   because sibling designs contend for 513/24-29, 514-515 and `vcr_info`'s tail.
3. **Should the destroy notices (513/3 with `fpVidMem` 0) be sampled per DirectDraw local?**
   - They are 1008-1013 of the 1024 entries of every 11-12 game ring.
   - They overwrite the session's record at every exit.
   - They carry only a handle.
   - They are not the drawing path, so this is left to G76, together with the default LogLevel (deploy_box writes 3)
     and `LogEntries`.
4. **Should `texswitch` join d3dprobe's default list?** It would make the matrix 42/42 fullscreen and 44/44 windowed,
   which other designs' verification steps cite as 40 and 42.
5. **Does the 10-04 Max Payne freeze depend on timing?** If G04 reproduces it, one attended run with `LogGate = 0`
   against one with the gate on would say whether the IOCTL traffic matters.
6. **How many textures do the Dark-engine titles bind per frame?** perf-07's "hundreds" is inferred. The what-2 line
   measures it on the first in-game run (G73).
7. **Should `log_combo`'s per-`compute_regs` hash and linear search be skipped when INFO is not stored (LogLevel 1 or
   lower)?** It is tiny and optional, and it changes nothing at the deployed level.

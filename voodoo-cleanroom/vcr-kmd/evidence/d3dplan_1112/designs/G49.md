# G49: strips, fans and indexed primitives reach the chip as independent triangles

**Gap.** `walk()` turns TRIANGLESTRIP, TRIANGLEFAN, INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST(2), INDEXEDTRIANGLESTRIP/FAN and
TRIANGLEFAN_IMM into `tri()`/`imm_tri()` calls, and every call sends three complete vertices. The setup unit's
own strip/fan continuation (one vertex plus `sDrawTriCMD` per added triangle) is never used.

2026-11-12 and is read-only. Nothing was built, deployed or run, and no fleet box was contacted.

## 1. Verdict

- **Real.** Every strip, fan and indexed primitive goes through `tri()` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:1367-1374`) or
  `imm_tri()` (`:1376-1381`). Each call ends in `VcrDd3dTriangle`, which sends three full vertices with
  `sBeginTriCMD` after the first and `sDrawTriCMD` after the other two (`KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c:225-239`).
- **Fixable, natively.** The setup unit continues a strip or fan by itself (SPEC §8.59-8.60, L3718-3746). 3dfx's
  HAL streamed continuations through packet 3 (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:1238-1281`). Our Glide does the same for every
  `grDrawVertexArray` strip and fan (`GL5/glide3/src/gstrip.c:166-269`). MesaFX sends GL strips, fans and polygons
  that way on this very card (`MESA/fxtris.c:1033`, `:1053`, `:1123`).
- **The gain is guaranteed for explicit strips and fans, and conditional for lists:**
  - an explicit strip or fan of n triangles drops from 3n vertices to n + 2. A d3dprobe quad (a 2-triangle
    strip) drops from 6 to 4;
  - an indexed **list** gains only where consecutive triangles share an edge with the last vertex sent. Part B
    (autostrip) finds those edges, and a per-title census measures how often that happens.
- **Two parts, each behind its own default-OFF bit of `Diag\D3DFastSetup`:**
  - part A (bit 2) covers explicit strips and fans: TRIANGLESTRIP, TRIANGLEFAN, INDEXEDTRIANGLESTRIP,
    INDEXEDTRIANGLEFAN and TRIANGLEFAN_IMM;
  - part B (bit 3) autostrips INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST and INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2.
  - With both bits clear, the register stream stays byte-for-byte what it is today.
- **The census counters are always on.** They log per-opcode triangle counts at "context gone" and touch no
  register.
- **Not a correctness fix.** The images must not change at all, so the item is proven by A/B pixel tests, not
  by a new feature check. **Priority P2, effort M.** The vendor-HAL and P6 (packet 3) reuse is designed in §4.7.

## 2. What the HAL does today (verified)

| opcode | walker | sent per triangle |
|---|---|---|
| TRIANGLESTRIP (19) | `:1621-1633`; odd triangles as `(s+i+1, s+i, s+i+2)` to keep the D3D winding | 3 vertices |
| TRIANGLEFAN (21) | `:1634-1642`, `(s, s+i+1, s+i+2)` | 3 vertices |
| INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST (3) | `:1643-1648`, absolute indices; `wFlags` ignored | 3 vertices |
| INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2 (26) | `:1649-1658`, start + relative indices | 3 vertices |
| INDEXEDTRIANGLESTRIP (20) / FAN (22) | `:1659-1677`, start + n+2 relative indices; strip odd swap `tri(w, b, a, cc)` | 3 vertices |
| TRIANGLEFAN_IMM (23) | `:1678-1689`, inline vertices through `imm_tri` | 3 vertices |
| TRIANGLELIST (18) | `:1612-1620` | 3 vertices (no sharing is possible) |

- **`tri()` calls `prepare()` for every triangle** (`:1369`). `prepare()` re-sends the state only when it is dirty
  or a texture was written (`:1322-1365`). `VcrDd3dState` writes `sSetupMode` as part of the state
  (`vcrdd_3d.c:85`).
- **The value it writes:**
  - `compute_regs` builds `setupMode` from the parameter bits plus culling: CCW gives `SM_CULL | SM_CULL_NEGATIVE`,
    CW gives `SM_CULL` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1021-1026`);
  - nothing ever sets `SM_FAN` or `SM_NO_PINGPONG` (`KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h:193-196`);
  - the context default is `CULLMODE = D3DCULL_CCW` (`:1135`), so games cull in hardware today. d3dprobe forces
    CULLNONE (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c:1493`), so **culling has never been checked on silicon** (G74;
    `reports/history.md:549-550`).
- **The cost of one vertex** (`vertex()`, `vcrdd_3d.c:183-223`):
  - one `VcrDdRoom` status read;
  - 8 writes (x, y, four colour floats, z, Wfbi);
  - plus 3 per textured stage;
  - plus the command write. That makes **9 / 12 / 15 writes per vertex** for 0 / 1 / 2 textures.
- **Today's triangle costs 27 / 36 / 45 writes and at least 3 status reads** (`reports/perf.md` §1.2). A
  continuation would cost 9 / 12 / 15 writes and 1 read.
- **d3dprobe already draws every quad as a 2-triangle TRIANGLESTRIP** through `DrawPrimitiveUP` (`d3dprobe.c:224-266`).
  The whole default render matrix is therefore a ready regression gate for part A, assuming the runtime passes the
  UP strip through as DP2 TRIANGLESTRIP (**inferred**; the census in §4.6 settles it).
- **What UT2003's flyby costs today** (`ONBOX/session_log.md:19`; `ONBOX/games/UT2003_flyby/`):
  - the ring's "context 1 gone" line: 59,589 DP2 and 37,033,284 triangles;
  - the stats CSV has 1,998 frames, so ~18.5k triangles reach the HAL per frame;
  - UE2 reports 116.7 ms of a 123 ms mean frame inside "Hardware DrawPrimitive", about **6.3 µs per triangle**.
    That fits `reports/perf.md` §4.1's inferred 4.6-8.4 µs per textured triangle;
  - UE2's own counters, CSV medians: 25,935 primitives and 30,474 software-processed vertices per frame, i.e.
    indexed content with heavy vertex sharing;
  - only ~63 % of UE2's primitives reach the HAL as triangles. The SWVP runtime probably culls or clips the rest
    before the DDI (**inferred**; the census and `NumPrimitives` against `tris` settle it).

## 3. What the hardware, the vendor and Glide say

### 3.1 The setup unit (SPEC)

- **sSetupMode** (§8.59, L3718-3734):
  - bits [7:0] are the parameters to set up;
  - bit 16 is strip mode (0 = strip, 1 = fan);
  - bit 17 enables culling and bit 18 is the culling sign;
  - bit 19 *"disables the ping pong sign inversion that happens during triangle strips"*.
  - The prose says *"write bit 0"* to choose fan or strip, but the table says bit 16. Bit 16 is right: it is
    Glide's `kSetupFan` shifted into the packet SMode field, and the vendor's `SST_SETUP_FAN BIT(16)`
    (`VH5/H3DEFS.H:995`).
- **Continuation** (§8.60, L3736-3737):
  - write vertex R, then `sBeginTriCMD`; then D1 and `sDrawTriCMD`; then D2 and `sDrawTriCMD`, which draws
    triangle 1;
  - *"As triangle 1 is being rendered, data for vertex D3 will be sent down followed by another write to
    sDrawTriCMD, thus launching another triangle"*;
  - in fan mode, *"only the last 2 get modified"*.
  - This is the register-level mechanism. **Nothing we hold ever drove it with plain register writes on this
    silicon**: the vendor and Glide use packet 3 (below).
- **Packet 3** (§13.1.6, L4832-4889) is the same mechanism wrapped up: *"The sSetupMode register is written with
  the data in bits 27:10"*.
  - Code 001 is `MBDDD...`: start a strip.
  - Code 010 is `MDDD...`: *"continuance of an existing triangle strip"*.
  - Code 000 is `MBDDBDD...`: independent triangles.
  - Header bits 22-25 are the fan, cull, cull-sign and no-ping-pong bits.
  - 86Box decodes it into exactly those register writes (`86B/voodoo_fifo.c:598-655`).
- **§21.3 Triangle Subpixel Correction** (L6220-6222) is a caveat this item must test. With `fbzColorPath[26]`
  (PARMADJUST) set, *"all subsequent triangles must resend starting color, depth, and texture coordinate
  parameters, otherwise the last triangle's subpixel corrected starting parameters are subpixel corrected (again!)"*.
  - The HAL always sets PARMADJUST (`vcrdd_d3d.c:977`; the silicon needs it, `test_the_colour_path_always_carries_parmadjust`).
  - The caveat reads as one about the legacy start-register (`triangleCMD`) interface. Glide also always sets
    PARMADJUST and sends hardware strips that render correctly on this card. So the setup unit's retained
    vertices are probably not double-corrected (**inferred**).
  - E1 (§8) checks it with sub-pixel vertices and steep gradients.

### 3.2 3dfx's NT HAL: autostrip, and when it gave up

- **One code path for strips and lists.**
  - `D6STRIP.C` builds the D3D strip triangles itself: `(v0,v1,v2)`, `(v2,v1,v3)`, `(v2,v3,v4)`... (`VH5/D6STRIP.C:136-158`,
    indexed `:246-268`).
  - It runs them through the same autostrip as lists (`VH5/D6STRIPI.C`, `VH5/D6TRI2I.C`).
  - **Explicit fans are sent as independent triangles.** `D6FANI.C` has no `CMD_CONT` at all (`:949`, `:1719`
    build `CMD_TRI` packets).
- **Detection is by vertex pointer** (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:1076-1112`):
  - "strip" when `PrevC == pA && PrevB == pB`;
  - "fan" when `PrevA == pA && PrevC == pB`;
  - anything else forces a fresh 3-vertex start.
- **The mode bit is set by a parity rule.** The continuation packet carries `BIT22` (fan) when the detected type
  equals the last one, and strip mode when it differs. The Win9x T&L renderer states the same rule:
  `if (currTriType == lastTriType) CmdCont |= STRIP_MODE` (`V9X/TLREND.C:638-665`).
- **When the vendor gave up on continuation (`FORCE_SINGLE`):**
  - flat shading: *"HW cannot support FLAT_SHADE and Auto Strip/Fan"* (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:766-770`;
    `V9X/TLREND.C:250-251`);
  - `D3DWRAP_U/V` cylindrical wrap on either stage (`:941-945`, `:1017-1021`);
  - a two-pass specular triangle (`:1300-1302`);
  - **the "continuation WRAP bug"**: any S/W or T/W of the triangle above 257.0, for *"the remainder of the
    primitive"* (`:986-995`, `:1062-1071`; the same in `D6STRIPI.C:1024-1031`, `:1100-1107` and the Win9x
    `D6TRI2K/D6STRIPK`).
  - The newer Win9x T&L path does not carry the 257 check (`V9X/TLREND.C`, no match case-insensitively). The
    change log ties autostrip fixes to *"shadow maps on 3D Winbench 2k Test 6"* (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:62-64`).
    **Neither the mechanism nor the scope of this "bug" is documented anywhere we hold.**
- **It culls in software first, and still enables hardware culling.**
  - A culled triangle resets the autostrip (`:729-735`, `:1626-1633`).
  - The packets that reach the chip still carry the hardware cull bits (`CMDFIFO_BUILD_PK3(..., setupFlag, ...)`
    with `setupFlag = sSetupMode`; `VH5/FIFOMGR.H:91-93`; cull mapping `VH5/D3RSTATE.C:3440-3478`, the same as
    ours).
  - So the vendor relied on the chip's ping-pong to get the cull sign of mixed strip/fan continuations right.
    Had it been wrong, visible triangles would have vanished.

### 3.3 Our Glide and MesaFX on this card

- **How Glide builds strip headers** (`_grUpdateTriPacketHdr`, `GL5/glide3/src/gglide.c:4772-4808`): with culling,
  ping-pong normal plus the cull enable and sign; without culling, `kSetupPingPongDisable`.
  - Our build has `GLIDE_TRI_CULLING=1` (`GL5/glide3/src/Makefile.mingw:114`).
  - Independent triangles are culled in software. **Strips and fans keep hardware culling with ping-pong normal.**
- **MesaFX's use:**
  - it draws GL strips, fans, quads and polygons with `grDrawVertexArrayContiguous(GR_TRIANGLE_STRIP / FAN / POLYGON)`
    (`MESA/fxtris.c:1033`, `:1053`, `:1104`, `:1123`);
  - it sets the hardware cull mode for triangle rasterisation (`MESA/fxsetup.c:2168-2199`).
  - Quake II on our ICD, our Glide and our kernel driver is the proven whole-stack run. Its world faces are
    `GL_POLYGON` fans with back-face culling, and its models are strips and fans. So hardware continuation, in
    both modes, with culling on, is exercised on this silicon through packet 3 (**inferred** from the code path;
    no test isolates it).

### 3.4 The parity model, and three sources that agree on it

The cull decision of a continued triangle is what can silently go wrong, so the model is written down.

- **Age-ordered model (the planner's).** The chip holds A (oldest; the fan pivot), B and C (newest).
  - A strip update is `(A,B,C) ← (B,C,n)` and toggles a parity bit.
  - A fan update is `(A,B,C) ← (A,C,n)` and keeps the parity.
  - `sBeginTriCMD` resets the parity.
  - The chip takes the area sign in `(A,B,C)` order and inverts it when the parity is odd.
- **86Box's model** (`86B/voodoo_reg.c:811-875`, `voodoo_setup.c:117-140`):
  - three physical slots with ages;
  - strip mode overwrites the oldest slot, and fan mode the second-oldest;
  - the sign is taken in **physical** slot order, and `cull_pingpong` toggles after **every** triangle and is reset
    by `sBeginTriCMD`.
  - Its comment says the scheme *"works with a number of games that switch between strip and fan mode in the middle
    of a run (eg Black & White, Viper Racing)"*.
- **The two are equivalent.**
  - A strip overwrite is a 3-cycle of the age ranks (even), and a fan overwrite is a transposition (odd).
  - So 86Box's factor sgn(slots) × (−1)^(all draws) equals (−1)^(strip updates), the age model's parity.
  - Both put the same triangles on screen and cull the same ones, for any mix of modes.
- **The vendor's XOR rule falls out of the same model.**
  - With `σ` = whether the slots hold the previous triangle with its first two vertices swapped (σ = swap exactly
    when the last detection was "strip"):
    - strip-detect from σ = id needs a strip update;
    - strip-detect from σ = swap needs a fan update;
    - fan-detect from σ = id needs a fan update;
    - fan-detect from σ = swap needs a strip update.
  - That is "fan bit = (detected == last)", and in every case the parity equals the winding relation, so the
    chip's cull decision is D3D's.
- **Consistent winding means a continuation always culls right.** For adjacent triangles that traverse their
  shared edge in opposite directions, the needed relation always equals the predicted parity, whichever update
  is used (proved in the native test, §7.1).
  - A mismatch arises only for an inconsistently wound pair, and then the planner restarts.
- **Proof status.** The model agrees with the vendor's shipped autostrip, 86Box's game-validated emulation, and
  Glide's strips and fans. **It is unproven in register mode on our silicon**; E2 and E3 (§8) prove it.

### 3.5 What Direct3D requires

- **Strips.** Triangle i is `(v_i, v_{i+1}, v_{i+2})` for even i, and has the winding of `(v_{i+1}, v_i, v_{i+2})`
  for odd i. This is the walker's own swap (`:1627-1630`).
  - The hardware's strip mode with ping-pong normal yields exactly that cull decision. Pure strips never need
    a mode change.
- **Fans.** Triangle i is `(v_0, v_{i+1}, v_{i+2})` with consistent winding. That is fan mode, with no inversion.
- **Flat shading** uses the colour of each triangle's first vertex. A continuation re-uses earlier vertices with
  their own colours, so a flat triangle cannot be continued (the vendor's rule). G24 is not implemented today, so
  flat is drawn Gouraud on either path.
- **Cull convention.** With y down, `(x1−x0)(y2−y0) − (x2−x0)(y1−y0) > 0` is clockwise on screen.
  - `D3DCULL_CCW` (the default) culls the negative ones, and `D3DCULL_CW` the positive ones.
  - 86Box's sign test agrees with our `SM_CULL_NEGATIVE` mapping (`voodoo_setup.c:117-138`): with
    `SM_CULL | SM_CULL_NEGATIVE` the `area < 0` triangles are dropped.
  - On silicon, nothing has checked this yet.

## 4. The design

### 4.1 New pure-logic header `KMD/include/vcr_3dstrip.h` (integer only, Win32-free)

The planner decides, per triangle, whether the chip gets a 3-vertex **START** (`B D D`, today's sequence) or a
1-vertex **CONT** (`D`), and whether `sSetupMode` must be written first. It knows nothing about floats or
registers, so `tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_strip.c` compiles it as it is.

```c
#define VCR_ST_NONE   0xffffffffu   /* empty slot: no strip open */
#define VCR_ST_START  1u            /* send v[0], v[1], v[2]: sBeginTriCMD, sDrawTriCMD, sDrawTriCMD */
#define VCR_ST_CONT   2u            /* send v[0]: sDrawTriCMD - the chip forms the next triangle */

typedef struct vcr_strip {
    unsigned a, b, c;    /* the setup unit's vertices by age: a oldest (a fan's pivot), c newest */
    unsigned odd;        /* the model's ping-pong parity for the next continued triangle (section 3.4) */
    unsigned fan;        /* the SM_FAN value the chip holds */
    unsigned single;     /* no more continuation in this primitive (wrap guard) */
} vcr_strip;

typedef struct vcr_strip_step {
    unsigned kind;       /* VCR_ST_START or VCR_ST_CONT */
    unsigned set_mode;   /* 1: write sSetupMode = base | (fan ? SM_FAN : 0) before the first vertex */
    unsigned fan;
    unsigned v[3];       /* vertex ids in send order (v[0] only for a CONT) */
} vcr_strip_step;

void     vcr_strip_open(vcr_strip *s);    /* a primitive begins; the chip holds fan 0 (4.3 invariant) */
void     vcr_strip_seq(vcr_strip *s, unsigned fan, unsigned k,    /* explicit strip (fan 0) / fan (1): */
                       unsigned v0, unsigned v1, unsigned v2,     /* triangle k, its D3D vertices      */
                       vcr_strip_step *o);
void     vcr_strip_tri(vcr_strip *s, unsigned a, unsigned b, unsigned c,  /* autostrip: next list */
                       unsigned cull, vcr_strip_step *o);                 /* triangle, D3D order  */
void     vcr_strip_break(vcr_strip *s);   /* the next triangle is a START (skipped / guard) */
unsigned vcr_strip_close(vcr_strip *s);   /* 1: restore sSetupMode (fan back to 0) */
```

All of these are `static __inline` in the header.

**Explicit sequences (`vcr_strip_seq`, part A)** need no detection, because the D3D primitive is the hardware
primitive:

- **k = 0:** START `(v0, v1, v2)` in D3D order. For a fan, `set_mode = 1, fan = 1` goes on this first step:
  a START draws the same triangle in either mode, and the fan bit only matters from the first continuation on.
- **k ≥ 1:** CONT with the new vertex, `v_{k+2}`.
- **after a guard trip (`single`):** START with the triangle's D3D vertices, i.e. today's `tri()` order, odd swap
  included.
- **Degenerate (stitched) triangles** in a strip are ordinary continuations, as in D3D and in the hardware. Each
  still counts towards the parity.

**Lists (`vcr_strip_tri`, part B)**, in this order:

1. **START** if `single`, if no strip is open (`c == NONE`), or if the triangle repeats an index. A repeated index
   also closes the strip (`c = NONE`), so a degenerate triangle never seeds a continuation.
2. **START** unless exactly one of `a, b, c` is new, **and** the slot `C` is among the other two. The chip can
   keep `(B,C)` or `(A,C)`, never `(A,B)`.
3. **Rotate** the triangle so the new vertex is last: `(x, y, n)`. A rotation keeps the winding.
4. **Pick the update:**
   - if the other shared vertex is `B`: strip update, new slots `(B, C, n)`, `odd ^= 1`, relation
     `r = (x == B) ? 0 : 1`;
   - if it is `A`: fan update, new slots `(A, C, n)`, `odd` kept, relation `r = (x == A) ? 0 : 1`.
5. **With culling on (`SM_CULL` in the state) and `r != odd`:** START. This is an inconsistently wound pair; the
   chip would cull the wrong one.
6. **Otherwise CONT** with `n`. `set_mode = (fan needed != s->fan)`.

After any START, the slots become the triangle `(a, b, c)` as sent, and `odd = 0`.

**The rule the walker keeps:** outside a strip or fan primitive, the chip's `sSetupMode` equals
`c->regs.setupMode`, i.e. `SM_FAN` clear. `vcr_strip_close` reports when a fan bit must be put back, and the
walker writes it.

### 4.2 The emitter in `KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c` (FPU side)

```c
#define VCR3D_ST_FAIL     0u   /* the FIFO never drained: the engine was given up */
#define VCR3D_ST_OK       1u
#define VCR3D_ST_BIG      2u   /* drawn; a vertex exceeded the continuation guard: no continuation from here */
#define VCR3D_ST_RESTART  3u   /* nothing written: the new vertex exceeds the guard - send a START instead */

ULONG VcrDd3dStripStart(VCR_PDEV *pd, const vcr3d_draw *d, ULONG smode,   /* smode ~0: no mode write */
                        const UCHAR *a, const UCHAR *b, const UCHAR *c);
ULONG VcrDd3dStripNext(VCR_PDEV *pd, const vcr3d_draw *d, ULONG smode, const UCHAR *v);
BOOL  VcrDd3dSetupMode(VCR_PDEV *pd, ULONG smode);                       /* the restore */
```

- **`vertex()` becomes `vertex_pre(pd, d, v, smode)`:**
  - it reserves `9 + 3·stages` FIFO slots, plus 1 when `smode != ~0`;
  - then it writes `sSetupMode` (when asked), then the payload exactly as today.
  - `vertex(pd, d, v)` stays a wrapper with `smode = ~0`, so `VcrDd3dTriangle` and every old-path write and room
    count are unchanged. One payload writer serves both paths, so G58 (packed colour) and G48 (credits) change
    both at once.
- **`VcrDd3dStripStart`** writes the same sequence as `VcrDd3dTriangle`: `vertex + B`, `vertex + D`, `vertex + D`,
  with the optional mode write first. It sets `pd->g2d_busy`.
- **`VcrDd3dStripNext`** writes `vertex + D`.
- **The continuation guard** (`d->st_guard`, on unless test bit 7 is set) mirrors the vendor's `FORCE_SINGLE` for
  the "continuation WRAP bug", conservatively:
  - a vertex is *big* when `|u·s_scale·oow|` or `|v·t_scale·oow|` exceeds 256.0 for an active stage. These are
    the values `vertex()` writes to `sSow0/sTow0` (and to `sSow1/sTow1` for the second stage). The vendor tested
    `> 257.0`, positive only, after its texel offset;
  - `StripStart` returns `BIG` if any of its three vertices is big, and the walker sets `single`;
  - `StripNext` returns `RESTART` **before writing anything** when the new vertex is big. The walker then sends that
    triangle as a START and sets `single` for the rest of the primitive, as the vendor did.
  - Cost: two to four float multiplies per vertex, on the new path only.

### 4.3 The walker in `KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c`

- **`dp2walk`** gains:
  - `st_arm`: bit 2 means part A and bit 3 part B, from `pd`;
  - `st_mode`: the `regs.setupMode` that `prepare()` last sent.
  - `w.d.st_guard` is set at the walk's start.
- **`strip_ok(w, bit)`:** armed, `SHADEMODE != D3DSHADE_FLAT`, and `FILLMODE` neither POINT nor WIREFRAME
  (0 = unset counts as solid and Gouraud; the context defaults are 0, `:1128`).
  - This is the one place a future per-triangle or multipass feature must refuse continuation: G24 flat and
    wireframe, the G25 two-pass specular, cylindrical WRAPn (G86).
  - Today it changes no pixel. Flat is drawn Gouraud on either path; only the vertex count differs.
- **Part A cases** (TRIANGLESTRIP, TRIANGLEFAN, INDEXEDTRIANGLESTRIP/FAN, TRIANGLEFAN_IMM):
  - The fast path needs `strip_ok`, `n ≥ 1`, and every vertex in range: `s + n + 2 ≤ nverts`, or each of the
    n + 2 indices below `nverts` (checked first), or `stride != 0` for IMM. Then it calls `prepare()` **once** and
    emits the sequence through `vcr_strip_seq` + `VcrDd3dStripStart/Next`.
  - Otherwise the case runs **today's loop, unchanged**. A malformed primitive therefore draws exactly what it draws
    now: `tri()` skips each out-of-range triangle.
  - **On `RESTART`**, the remaining triangles go through today's `tri()` / `imm_tri()` in D3D order.
  - **On `FAIL`**, the primitive stops. `VcrDdRoom` already returns at once after a give-up, `vcrdd_2d.c:92-99`.
  - After a fan, `vcr_strip_close` → `VcrDd3dSetupMode(pd, w->st_mode)`.
- **Part B cases** (INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST and INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST2), when `strip_ok(w, 8)`:
  - `prepare()` is called once, and each triangle goes through `vcr_strip_tri` with
    `cull = (c->regs.setupMode & SM_CULL) != 0`.
  - An out-of-range triangle is skipped, as today, and `vcr_strip_break` follows.
  - `close` restores the fan bit.
  - TRIANGLELIST (18) is never autostripped. Its triangles share no index, and it is the A/B reference path of §6.
- **`prepare()` per primitive.** Inside one primitive nothing can make the state dirty:
  - RENDERSTATE, TSS, TEXBLT, CLEAR and SETRENDERTARGET are separate commands;
  - the D3D and DirectDraw DDI calls are serialised (**inferred**: dxg's device lock);
  - so one call per primitive equals today's per-triangle calls.
- **`c->tris`** counts every triangle drawn, continued or not, so the existing 513/2 line stays comparable.

### 4.4 What the old path does (both bits clear, the default)

- `walk()` runs today's loops, `tri()`, `imm_tri()` and `VcrDd3dTriangle`.
- `vertex()` keeps its room count and writes.
- No `sSetupMode` write is added.
- The only additions are the census counters (§4.6): integer adds in `walk()` and three INFO lines in `ctx_free`.

### 4.5 The switch and its plumbing

- **`Diag\D3DFastSetup`** (DWORD, absent = 0) is read **at boot** in `VcrFindAdapter`, beside `D3D32` and
  `D3DBigTex` (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:233-251`): `x->d3dfast = VcrDiagGet(L"D3DFastSetup", 0)`.
- **The bits follow `reports/perf.md` P3's numbering:**

  | bit | value | what | owner |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | 0x01 | packed sARGB | G58 |
  | 1 | 0x02 | credit-based FIFO room | G48 |
  | 2 | 0x04 | **strip/fan continuation for explicit primitives** | **G49 part A** |
  | 3 | 0x08 | **autostrip of indexed lists** | **G49 part B** |
  | 4 | 0x10 | W1 sharing | G48/G58 |
  | 7 | 0x80 | **test only: no continuation guard (E4); never left armed** | **G49** |

- **Carried as one appended word**, `vcr_u32 d3dfast;`, after `mon_src` in `vcr_info` (`KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h:86-107`).
  - This is the convention G02 §3.0 and G15 recommend instead of one `VCR_INFO_F_*` bit per feature. Five
    sibling designs already collide on 0x8000/0x10000.
  - `fill_info` sets it (`vcrmp.c:436-466`). `VcrDd2dInit` reads it only when `info.size` covers the field, so an
    older miniport arms nothing (`vcrdd_2d.c:430-467`).
  - It then sets `pd->st_explicit`, `pd->st_auto` and `pd->st_noguard`, and logs one "armed (Diag\D3DFastSetup)"
    INFO line.
  - All three hold on any chip. The setup unit's strip mode exists on Banshee and Voodoo3 too, which makes the
    86Box Voodoo3 bed usable (§8).
- **Why at boot, not at every mode change:** the project's "one driver setting per clean boot" rule
  (`docs/v56k-benchmark-plan.md`). No caps change, so reading it at `DrvEnableSurface` would also be safe. That is
  left as an open question.

### 4.6 Census counters (log only, default-on, no register change)

- **Per context:**
  - triangles and commands per opcode family: L, S, F, IL, IL2, IS, IF, IMM;
  - for the new path: `st_tris`, `st_verts`, `st_start`, `st_cont`, `auto_cont`, `auto_start`, `mode_writes`,
    `guard_trips`.
- **Logged** as three INFO lines in `ctx_free` next to 513/2 (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1210-1215`), only when the context drew
  something. Provisional "what" codes are 26-28; the numbers are allocated at integration, against G02 §3.7's
  collision table.
- **Vertices actually sent** = 3·(tris − st_tris) + st_verts.
- **The census decides two things:**
  - which titles part A or part B serves;
  - whether d3dprobe's UP quads really arrive as DP2 TRIANGLESTRIP and its reference lists as TRIANGLELIST, which
    §6 relies on.
- If G76 lands first, these lines fold into its framework.

### 4.7 Packet 3 in P6 (G47): the same plan, coalesced

- **The plan's step stream is independent of transport.** P6 adds a packet builder (GBK's `gbk_pkt3_hdr`,
  `RA/scripts/3dfx/driver/nt/gbk/gbk_packet.c:36-49`):
  - a START followed by CONTs in the same mode is one code-001 packet, `MBDDD…`, up to 15 vertices;
  - further CONTs go in code-010 packets, `MDDD…`;
  - a mode change starts a new packet, whose header carries the SMode;
  - runs of STARTs become code-000 packets, `MBDDBDD`, 5 triangles each;
  - this matches Glide (`GL5/glide3/src/gstrip.c:180-231`, `gdraw.c:1097-1124`) and the vendor's
    `CMD_START|BIT22` / `CMD_CONT|ContinueTest` (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:1118-1124`, `:1238-1242`).
- **The planner and its native test carry over unchanged.** The P6 test decodes packets into `M/B/D` sequences
  (SPEC L4882-4889) and feeds the same setup-unit model.

### 4.8 Contracts with other gaps

| gap | contract |
|---|---|
| G24 flat / wireframe | `strip_ok` refuses them, so landing G24 cannot silently continue a flat triangle |
| G25 specular (two-pass for textures, if chosen) | a per-triangle second pass must refuse in `strip_ok`; a host-side add of the specular to the diffuse colour (untextured) is per vertex and needs nothing |
| G16 lines and points | a line drawn as a thin quad is a 4-vertex strip (`B D D D`) through `VcrDd3dStripStart/Next`, as Glide draws its lines (`reports/hw.md` Q9) |
| G57 guard band | fewer runtime-clipped TRIANGLEFAN_IMMs, and strips kept whole: more for part A to use |
| G48 credits, G58 packed colour | independent bits of the same word; one payload writer (`vertex_pre`) |
| G47 CMDFIFO | consumes this planner (§4.7) |
| G50 2PPC, G51 SLI | independent: the TMU state and per-chip broadcast are untouched |
| G86 cylindrical WRAPn | if ever emulated per triangle, it must refuse in `strip_ok` (the vendor's `FORCE_SINGLE` on `D3DWRAP_U/V`) |

### 4.9 Considered and rejected

- **The vendor's way: explicit strips through the list autostrip.** It needs a detector that copes with degenerate
  stitching triangles, and gains nothing. A D3D strip *is* a hardware strip.
- **Explicit fans as independent triangles** (the vendor's `D6FANI.C`). Glide fans work on this card, and
  runtime-clipped polygons arrive as fans.
- **Matching TRIANGLELIST vertices by bytes** (a memcmp per triangle). Only if the census shows a slow title that
  draws mostly non-indexed lists.
- **Autostrip across DP2 commands.** Each command restarts it. The gain is small, and the risk is mixing two
  commands' vertex buffers.
- **CPU-side culling** (the vendor culls before sending). That is a separate item: it does not change primitive
  expansion. The UT2003 numbers in §2 suggest the runtime may already cull.

## 5. Gating

- **Default OFF; bits 2 and 3 independent.** It is not a pure correctness fix: it changes the register stream of
  every quad in every Direct3D title, the UI, the HUD and the fonts. With the bits clear, the proven path is
  byte-for-byte unchanged, and `tests/python/test_vcr_kmd_d3d.py` pins that (§7.2).
- **One Diag change per clean boot:** 4, then 12, then (as an experiment) 12 | 0x80 (§8).
- **Flipping the defaults:**
  - bit 2 becomes default-on (absent = on) in a separate commit, after its supervised boot passes every gate in
    §8;
  - bit 3 follows after its own boot and a census-selected game sweep;
  - each commit's test asserts the new default and the old one;
  - bit 7 never becomes a default.
- **Kill switch:** clearing the bit, then a reboot.
- **The census counters are log-only and default-on** (hal.md Phase 0).

## 6. d3dprobe tests (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`, explicit `--tests`, never in the default list)

**The shared method (A/B in one frame).**

- Every mesh is drawn twice in the same frame:
  - copy A through the primitive under test;
  - copy B, 128 px to the right, as the D3D-expanded **non-indexed TRIANGLELIST** (the proven path; never
    autostripped). Odd strip triangles use the `(i+1, i, i+2)` order.
- Both copies fit the 256x256 readback window (`d3dprobe.c:149`, G74).
- **All x/y are multiples of 1/16**, so a 128 px shift is exact in float, and setup is translation-invariant.
  Within a triangle, vertex y values are jittered, so no two share a y. This avoids the setup unit's y-sort tie
  order (**inferred** to matter; `86B/voodoo_setup.c:75-114` sorts by y).
- **`ab_compare` counts:**
  - coverage mismatches (a pixel not the clear colour in one copy only);
  - pixels off by more than 1 native LSB on any channel (R5G6B5: 8/4/8 in 8-bit units; 32 bpp: 1);
  - 1-LSB differences.
- **Pass** = 0 coverage mismatches, 0 pixels over 1 LSB, and 1-LSB pixels ≤ 1 % of the covered ones (setup-order
  rounding). The JSON entry records all three counts.
- **Absolute checks are added where D3D semantics fix the answer.** At an untextured triangle's centroid the
  expected colour is the mean of its three vertex colours, with tolerance 24 (the `gouraud` tests' tolerance).

**The tests.** The group alias `--tests prims` runs all of them except `strip_bigst`.

| test | draws | pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| `strip_ab` | a 24-triangle band strip (26 vertices, 8.5 px columns, rows at y≈16 and y≈112), steep per-vertex colours (`R=37j`, `G=255−23j`, `B=61j` mod 256), Z 0.1-0.9 behind an occluder quad at Z 0.5 over the right third (ZENABLE, LESSEQUAL), via `DrawPrimitiveUP(TRIANGLESTRIP)` | A/B identical as defined; centroid colours of triangles 0, 1, 11, 23 (not occluded) |

| `istrip_ab`, `ifan_ab` | the same meshes via `DrawIndexedPrimitiveUP` with the vertex array shuffled (non-sequential WORD indices) | A/B identical |
| `ilist_ab` | a 6x4 quad grid as an indexed list, one row per pattern: strip order (alternating rotation); every triangle rotated by `i mod 3`; fans around a row vertex; random order with jumps; plus one degenerate triangle and one non-adjacent jump | A/B identical (part B: mode switches mid-strip) |

**`perf --prim list|strip|fan|ilist|ilist_strip --tex 0|1|2`** (the setup-bound mode of `reports/perf.md` §6.1):

**What each outcome means:**

- **E0 fails** (A/B differs with the bits off): the comparator or the 1/16 grid is wrong. Fix the probe; nothing is
  armed until A/B reads identical on the old path.
- **E1 fails on the default matrix** (2-triangle quads): register-mode continuation is not what SPEC §8.60 and
  packet 3 imply. Bit 2 stays off. A minimal probe (one 2-triangle strip, logged register stream) separates "never
  continues" from "wrong vertex retained". Revisit through packet 3 in P6, where Glide proves the mechanism.
- **E1: coverage right but colours drift on continued triangles only:** this is the §21.3 double correction.
  Continuation is unusable in register mode while PARMADJUST is on, and PARMADJUST is mandatory on this silicon.
  Bits stay off; record it in FINDINGS.
- **E2 fails for strips or fans only:** the ping-pong model is wrong for that mode in register mode. The fallback is
  to arm that family only with CULLMODE NONE (`strip_ok` refuses when `SM_CULL` is set). Under most games' culling
  the gain then mostly disappears.
- **E2's absolute check fails on the old path too:** the HAL's CW/CCW mapping is wrong everywhere. That is a new P0
  defect, independent of G49; file it.
- **E3 fails only after a mode switch:** the parity does not survive a mid-strip `sSetupMode` write in register mode.
  The fallback has part B continue only in the current mode (no switch), or not continue with culling on.
- **E4:**
  - identical with the guard off: the bug does not exist here (or not for these values). Drop the guard after one
    more game check, so bit 7's behaviour becomes the code. This matters for UE2 terrain, whose tiling S/W often
    exceeds 256 (**inferred**);
  - different: keep the guard; it is the default anyway.
- **The perf gate:**
  - `perf --prim strip` µs per triangle drops by about 50-70 % (§9);
  - `perf --prim list` is unchanged (no regression);
  - UT2003 `avgfps` rises in boot C. Its size depends on the census hit rate.

## 10. Risk to proven paths

- **The default 16 bpp path:** none while the bits are clear. The register stream is identical, pinned by §7.2.
  The census adds integer adds and three log lines at context destroy.
- **Armed, every quad in every title changes stream.** A defect would show everywhere: missing or garbled odd
  triangles of quads, broken fonts. That is why the d3dprobe default matrix and the A/B set gate it before any
  game.
- **Stability.**
  - No new wait: the room check stays per vertex, bounded by `SPIN_CAP`, and a give-up still returns at once.
  - No new state beyond `sSetupMode`'s fan bit, restored after every fan primitive and re-sent by every
    `prepare()` (every DP2 call starts with `prepared = 0`, `vcrdd_d3d.c:1835`).
  - The setup unit's strip mode is a documented and vendor-shipped feature.
- **Glide and OpenGL coexistence:** none.
  - Glide writes `sSetupMode` in every packet-3 header (the `M` of SPEC L4882-4889), so a fan bit left by D3D cannot
    reach it; and D3D restores it anyway.
  - D3D never draws while Glide holds the chip (`exclusive_pid`, `:1859-1860`).
  - G01 (a stale Glide FIFO) is untouched by this item and still voids any measurement taken after a Glide session.
- **SLI and the flip path:** untouched.
- **The silent kind of defect:** a wrong cull parity drops visible triangles of back-face-culled strips. E2 and E3's
  absolute checks exist for exactly this; the vendor's and 86Box's evidence (§3.4) makes it unlikely.

## 11. Effort, dependencies, titles, priority

- **Effort: M.**
  - The planner header plus native test with the model: about a day.
  - The emitter, walker, switch and census: about a day.
  - The d3dprobe A/B set and `perf --prim`: about a day.
  - Three supervised boots, plus an experiment boot.
- **Dependencies.** None blocks landing the code.
  - **G01:** a stale Glide FIFO voids D3D runs; the cold-boot protocol covers it.
  - **G75:** a DX5/DX7 probe is the only lab route to opcode 3 (INDEXEDTRIANGLELIST with `wFlags`), which D3D8
    cannot send. Until it exists, JK and MotS in game cover it.
  - **G76:** if it lands first, the census folds into it.
  - **G78:** vsync-on headroom measurement.
  - **Downstream:** G47 consumes the planner; G16 can reuse the emitter; G24 and G25 must honour `strip_ok`.
- **Titles.** None is *blocked* by this gap. It speeds up:
  - every D3D title's quads: menus, HUD, fonts;
  - UT2003 and UT2004 (indexed content: part B, size per the census);
  - Halo (strip-heavy content is likely, **inferred**: part A);
  - Jedi Knight DF2 and MotS (execute-buffer strips and fans: part B on opcode 3);
  - Max Payne, Red Faction, Hidden & Dangerous Deluxe, Battlefield 1942 LAN, Thief II and the DX6 titles, per the
    census.
- **Priority: P2.** A performance change with a measurable gain. It renders nothing differently and unblocks no
  title.

## 12. Open questions

1. **Ping-pong in register mode.** Does the VSA-100's parity toggle only on strip continuations and hold on fan ones,
   or the equivalent physical-slot form (§3.4)? The vendor, 86Box and Glide imply it; silicon is unproven (E2, E3).
2. **Does a mid-strip `sSetupMode` write keep the retained vertices and the parity** in register mode, as packet 3's
   per-packet `M` implies (E3)?
3. **Does the vendor's "continuation WRAP bug"** (|S/W| or |T/W| > 256 in a continuation) exist on this silicon, and
   for which cases? Positive and negative, one or two stages (E4). Its answer decides how much of UE2's terrain part
   B can continue.
4. **Does SPEC §21.3's "corrected again" caveat apply to retained setup-unit vertices under PARMADJUST** (E1)? Glide's
   working strips say no (**inferred**).
5. **Which DP2 opcodes does the D3D8 runtime send each title** under software vertex processing (census)? Does it cull
   back faces before the DDI? UE2's 25.9k primitives per frame against the HAL's ~18.5k triangles suggests it does,
   which would cut autostrip's hit rate.
6. **Does `DrawPrimitiveUP(TRIANGLESTRIP)` reach a DX7 driver as DP2 TRIANGLESTRIP,** and `DrawPrimitiveUP(TRIANGLELIST)`
   as TRIANGLELIST? The A/B tests' reference rests on it (census, boot A).
7. **Read `D3DFastSetup` at boot** (as proposed, the one-change-per-boot rule) **or at every mode change** (A/B without a
   reboot, like `Accel2DText`)?
8. **Who allocates the `vcr_info` word and the 513 "what" codes?** `d3dfast` after `mon_src`, provisional codes 26-28;
   G02 §3.7 lists five designs colliding on flags and codes.
9. **If the census shows a slow title drawing mostly non-indexed TRIANGLELIST,** is a byte-match autostrip (§4.9) worth
   its per-triangle memcmp?
10. **Is the CW/CCW mapping right on silicon?** It has never been checked by a probe. `cull_prims` is the first check. A
    failure there is a separate defect that affects every title, armed or not.

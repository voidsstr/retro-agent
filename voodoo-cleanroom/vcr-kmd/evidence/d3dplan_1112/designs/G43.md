# G43: D3D contexts and the PDEV lifetime - what is real, the guards, and the alt-tab investigation

Gap: *"D3D contexts cache a PDEV pointer that a mode change frees (possible use-after-free)"*
(`reports/hal.md` R4, source hal-04). Lane: **clean-room** (`vcr-kmd`, `vcrdd.dll`).
Kind: needs-investigation. This design was written from the code, Microsoft's public W2K DDK
documentation and sample driver, the vendor H5 tree, and rings **already in the repo**. No box was
contacted.

Path aliases: `KMD` = `voodoo-cleanroom/vcr-kmd`, `VH5` = `retro-3dfx/3dfx Driver Code/H5/W2K/Src/Video/Displays/H5`,
`DDK` = `~/retro3dfx-toolchain/devtools/w2kddk` (its `help/d_grafx.chm` and `src/video/displays/3dlabs/driver`),
`RING09` = `KMD/evidence/silicon/d3d32_step16/d3d32_end_vcrlog.txt`, `ONBOX` = `KMD/evidence/d3dplan_1112/onbox`.

---

## 0. Verdict

**The headline claim does not hold.** On XP, a mode change does not free the PDEV that a live D3D
context names. The platform contract says so, and so do seven teardown sequences already recorded on
`.124`:

- **The contract.** W2K DDK, *2.3.3 Supporting Multiple PDEVs*: a driver instance that owns a
  Direct3D object is only disabled by `DrvAssertMode(FALSE)`. It is *retained* until the system
  returns to its mode (it is then *resurrected* with `DrvAssertMode(TRUE)`) or until its last
  referencing object is destroyed. Only then come `DrvDisableDirectDraw`, `DrvDisableSurface` and
  `DrvDisablePDEV`.
- **The measurements.** In all seven sequences, `ContextDestroy` precedes `DrvDisablePDEV` of the
  context's own PDEV. In one of them the PDEV was kept, inactive, for **27.7 s** under a live context.

So `c->pd` is valid whenever dxg calls the context. The gap's proposed fix "re-derive `pd` from
`c->ddlcl->lpGbl->dhpdev` on every call" would be **harmful**:

- in 4 of the 7 sequences `ContextDestroy` arrives *after* `DestroyDDLocal` of the context's local,
  so the re-derivation would dereference a local the driver has already been told is going away;
- if XP re-points a local's global at the new PDEV, re-deriving would aim a context whose surfaces
  are lost at the *active* instance's memory.

**What IS real is narrower and cheap to close:**

- **(R1)** In 3 of the 7 sequences, `DestroyDDLocal` arrives *after* `DrvDisablePDEV`. These are the
  UT2003 and Halo exits on 2026-11-12, and RING09 sequence B. `Dd_DestroyDDLocal` then reads and may
  write `pd->flip` of whatever `pDDLcl->lpGbl->dhpdev` names. Whether that is the freed PDEV is the
  one open stability question.
- **(R2)** A context can live on a *retained, inactive* PDEV. Nothing in DP2, Clear2, TEXBLT or
  `ctx_free` asks whether its PDEV owns the screen. Only the runtime's promise keeps them off the
  chip, plus the fact that `g2d_busy` happens to be 0.
- **(R3)** Several borrowed pointers are never scrubbed: `tex_surf0/1`, `pvmList`, and `ddlcl`
  after `DestroyDDLocal`.
- **(R4)** It is unmeasured whether a mip chain's `VidMemFree` always runs before its heap's
  teardown.
- **(R5)** Alt-tab has never been run. A DX5/6/7 context that survives a loss has its `rt`/`zb`
  cleared at the loss, and nothing re-binds them.

**The design** has three parts:

- **Default-on (proven path byte-for-byte unchanged):** a PDEV liveness registry (pointer compares,
  no dereference), lifetime guards, and instrumentation (event 515, a counters escape).
- **Default-off (`Diag\D3DSafe` bit 4):** a gate that refuses to draw on an inactive PDEV, and
  re-binding `rt`/`zb` by handle.
- **The investigation:** a new `d3dprobe lost` mode, plus an alt-tab matrix on the 86Box bed (with
  Driver Verifier where XP accepts it) and one supervised session on `.124`.

## 2. What the platform promises (public Microsoft DDK, read locally)

All quotes are from the W2K DDK graphics documentation, `DDK/help/d_grafx.chm`:

1. **The PDEV is retained while D3D objects reference it.** *2.3.3 Supporting Multiple PDEVs*
   (`hh/ntddk/d_grafx/dpyddi_6rhj.htm`):
   - "Destruction of the owning driver instance will be deferred": the old instance is disabled by
     `DrvAssertMode(FALSE)` "and is retained until either the system does a mode change back to the
     original mode, or until all the objects that reference the instance are destroyed".
   - On a return it is "resurrected ... re-enabled by a call to DrvAssertMode(TRUE)".
   - It continues: "Direct3D or OpenGL drivers can be called to destroy an inactive driver instance
     at any time ... even if another instance of the driver is currently active, or ... another
     driver owns the hardware entirely".
   - And: "drivers should not manipulate their video hardware in their DrvDisableXxx routines unless
     they know that their instance is currently active (by remembering the state from the last
     DrvAssertMode call)".
2. **A mode change destroys every surface at the driver level.**
   - *3.3.1.2 Creating and Destroying DirectDraw Surfaces*: "Where there is a mode change, all the
     surfaces under the driver's control are destroyed, as far as the driver is concerned."
   - *3.3.1.3 Lost Surfaces*: the runtime object is "lost" and later restored through another
     `DdCreateSurface`.
   - The DDK's Permedia2 sample handles such a destroy by testing `DDRAWISURF_INVALID`
     (`DDK/src/video/displays/3dlabs/driver/ddsurf.c:596-662`). The flag is `0x10000000`, "surface
     has been invalidated by mode set", in `KMD/out/ddi/ddrawint.h:761`.
3. **Contexts survive a loss; rendering resumes on them.** *4.5.8.6 Surface Handles, Lost Surfaces,
   and Contexts* (`d3d_7vaf.htm`):
   - "The context itself may survive ... The run-time guarantees that no rendering commands will be
     passed to the context while it is in this state";
   - lost surfaces "will always be recreated (restored) with the same handle values before rendering
     can resume on this context", possibly at a different `fpVidMem`.
   - The page recommends that a context keep **handles**, not pointers. The Permedia2 sample does:
     `RenderSurfaceHandle` and `ZBufferHandle`, `3dlabs/driver/d3d.c:205-209`,
     `d3ddx6.c:2251-2252`.
4. **Everyone caches the PDEV in the context.**
   - The DDK sample stores `pContext->ppdev` and uses it at destroy (`d3d.c:144,1005-1012`).
   - 3dfx's own HAL does too: `newContext->ppdev` (`VH5/D3CONTXT.C:342-343,404`), and every DP2
     takes it back through `SETUP_PPDEV` (`VH5/D3GLOBAL.H:2387-2388`, `VH5/D6DP2.C:1201-1203`).
   - The vendor kept the context list **global** "due to calling sequence on W2K" (`g_pContexts`,
     `VH5/D3CONTXT.H:151-155`), because a context can outlive the PDEV that was current when it
     was made.
   - Its DX7 note "copy the context info between pdev's in DrvResetPDEV" (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:843-845`) was
     never implemented (`VH5/ENABLE.C:2807-2836`).
   - Its chain free uses the heap saved at allocation, exactly like ours (`VH5/DDMEMMGR.C:688-705`).

   The vendor reference is guidance only. It does not solve this problem; it relies on the same
   contract.

## 3. What `.124` has already measured (XP SP3, our driver, rings in the repo)

`a<b` means event `a` was logged before `b`. "Ctx" = `context N gone` (`D3d_ContextDestroy`),
"DisPDEV" = `DrvDisablePDEV` of the context's own PDEV, "DDLcl" = `DestroyDDLocal`.

| # | run | where | mode revert (`DrvAssertMode` 0 on the game PDEV) | order at teardown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | d3dprobe 640x480x32, 09-27 | RING09 lines 530-599 | 574.548 s | DDLcl 9033 < Ctx 9034 < DisDD/DisSurf/DisPDEV 9035-9039 (574.631 s) |
| 2 | d3dprobe 1280x960x16, 09-27 | RING09 lines 906-980 | **681.764 s** | Ctx 9414 < DisPDEV 9419 < **DDLcl 9420**. The PDEV was **retained inactive 27.7 s** under the live context (709.495 s) |
| 3 | d3dprobe perf 640x480x16 (pid 1164), 11-12 | `ONBOX/ring_after_probes.txt` 194-255 | 83764.440 s | DDLcl 29345 < Ctx 29346 < DisPDEV 29351 (94 ms after the revert) |
| 4 | d3dprobe perf 1024x768x32 (pid 536), 11-12 | same file, 964-1025 | 83777.424 s | DDLcl 30115 < Ctx 30116 < DisPDEV 30121 |
| 5 | **UT2004 botmatch, killed by pid** | `ONBOX/games/UT2004_botmatch/ring.txt` 1019-1025 | (wrapped) | DDLcl 97376 < Ctx 97377 < DisPDEV 97382 |
| 6 | UT2003 flyby, console exit | `ONBOX/games/UT2003_flyby/ring.txt` 1015-1021 | (wrapped) | Ctx 207558 < DisPDEV 207563 < **DDLcl 207564 (16 ms later)** |
| 7 | Halo timedemo, WM_CLOSE | `ONBOX/games/Halo_timedemo/ring.txt` 1018-1024 | (wrapped) | Ctx 306109 < DisPDEV 306114 < **DDLcl 306115** |

Facts that follow:

- **Ctx < DisPDEV in 7/7**, including a forced kill (#5) and a 27.7 s retention (#2). The
  "use-after-free through `c->pd`" never had the chance to happen.
- In every sequence the game PDEV was already **inactive** when `ContextDestroy` came: its
  `DrvAssertMode(FALSE)` preceded the destroy.
  - In #1-#4 this is seen in the ring.
  - In #5-#7 the revert has wrapped out of the window, but it is implied: the game PDEV is torn
    down with no PDEV enabled after it, so the desktop PDEV was already up.

  `ctx_free`'s `VcrDd2dSync` therefore ran against an inactive instance. It wrote nothing only
  because `DrvAssertMode(FALSE)` had cleared `g2d_busy` (`vcrdd.c:613`, `vcrdd_2d.c:121-122`).
- **DDLcl < Ctx in 4/7**: a context outlives its DirectDraw local by microseconds. Anything that
  dereferences `c->ddlcl` in `ContextDestroy` is unsafe.
- **DDLcl > DisPDEV in 3/7** (#2, #6, #7), where `Dd_DestroyDDLocal` dereferences
  `p->pDDLcl->lpGbl->dhpdev`. The log line reads `flips of 0` (`c = pd->flip.owner`) in all three.
  That cannot tell the two cases apart:
  - a freed PDEV whose `owner` had been reset at "mode off"; or
  - the live desktop PDEV (dxg re-pointed the local's global).

  **This is the question the investigation must settle (R1).**

## 4. Real versus not real

| item | status | why |
|---|---|---|
| `c->pd` freed by a mode change while dxg still calls the context | **not real** under the contract and 7/7 measurements | the PDEV is retained until its D3D objects are gone (section 2.1, section 3) |
| "re-derive `pd` per call" (the gap's fix) | **rejected** | it dereferences `c->ddlcl` past `DestroyDDLocal` (4/7); under a global swap it would re-target a lost context into the active instance's memory. Replaced by a *liveness check* (pointer compare against a registry, no dereference) |
| R1: `Dd_DestroyDDLocal` after `DrvDisablePDEV` | **open, live order** on two staged titles | 3/7 sequences. If `dhpdev` names the freed PDEV: a read of freed pool, and possibly a write (`vcr_flip_stats_reset`) into a reused block |
| R2: a context on a retained inactive PDEV | **real gap in defence** | DP2, Clear2, TEXBLT and `ctx_free` have no active check. TEXBLT copies into video memory even with no drawable target. The Glide owner check uses that PDEV's own `exclusive_pid`, which is 0 on the inactive instance, so a Glide session on the active PDEV is invisible to it (G01 hazard). Today only the runtime's promise and `g2d_busy == 0` stand between it and the chip |
| R3: `tex_surf0/1`, `pvmList`, `ddlcl` not scrubbed | **real latent stale pointers** | trivial to clear |
| R4: chain `VidMemFree` versus heap teardown | **unmeasured** | `Dd_DestroySurface` logs nothing today. The DDK says the loss-destroy comes at the mode change, while that instance's DirectDraw is still enabled |
| R5: alt-tab of a DX5/6/7 title (context survives the loss) | **unmeasured, likely functional bug** | `VcrDdD3dSurfaceGone` clears `c->rt`/`c->zb` at the loss. If the runtime resumes rendering without a SETRENDERTARGET, nothing is drawn (black after alt-tab). Not a crash |

## 5. Design

### 5.1 A Win32-free lifetime header: `KMD/include/vcr_d3dlife.h` (new, host-tested)

This follows the project pattern (`vcr_flip.h`, `vcr_rtfmt.h`): the decisions are in an integer-only
header; the glue in `display/` stays thin. Keys are `const void *` and are **only compared, never
dereferenced**.

```c
#define VCR_LIFE_MAX_PDEV 16
#define VCR_LIFE_MAX_HEAP 16
enum { VCR_LIFE_DEAD, VCR_LIFE_INACTIVE, VCR_LIFE_ACTIVE, VCR_LIFE_UNKNOWN };
typedef struct vcr_life_stats {            /* VCR_ESC_D3D_LIFE, size-versioned like vcr_2d_stats */
    vcr_u32 size, pdev_on, pdev_off, pdev_live, pdev_live_max, retained_with_ctx,
            resurrected, ctx_orphaned, ctx_after_ddlocal, call_on_inactive,
            ddlocal_dead_pdev, ddlocal_swapped, chain_loss_frees, chain_dead_heap_skips,
            epoch;
} vcr_life_stats;
typedef struct vcr_life {
    const void *pdev[VCR_LIFE_MAX_PDEV]; vcr_u32 gen[VCR_LIFE_MAX_PDEV], active[VCR_LIFE_MAX_PDEV];
    vcr_u32 next_gen, overflow;                     /* overflow: liveness UNKNOWN -> fail open */
    const void *dead_heap[VCR_LIFE_MAX_HEAP]; vcr_u32 ndead;
    vcr_life_stats st;
} vcr_life;

vcr_u32 vcr_life_pdev_on(vcr_life *, const void *pd);            /* DrvEnablePDEV -> gen (0: overflow) */
void    vcr_life_pdev_active(vcr_life *, const void *pd, int on); /* EnableSurface/AssertMode/DisableSurface */
vcr_u32 vcr_life_pdev_off(vcr_life *, const void *pd);           /* DrvDisablePDEV -> its gen */
int     vcr_life_state(const vcr_life *, const void *pd);         /* DEAD/INACTIVE/ACTIVE/UNKNOWN */
int     vcr_life_may_read(int state);   /* memory of the PDEV: != DEAD (UNKNOWN: yes, today's behaviour) */
int     vcr_life_may_hw(int state);     /* the chip through it: ACTIVE or UNKNOWN */
void    vcr_life_heap_live(vcr_life *, const void *heap);   /* a chain was placed in it (re-arms a reused address) */
void    vcr_life_heap_dead(vcr_life *, const void *heap);   /* DrvDisableDirectDraw of the PDEV that used it */
int     vcr_life_heap_may_free(const vcr_life *, const void *heap);  /* 0: that heap is gone */
enum { VCR_CTX_DRAW, VCR_CTX_DRAW_LOGGED, VCR_CTX_LOST, VCR_CTX_BAD };
int     vcr_life_ctx_verdict(int state, int orphaned, int gate /* D3DSafe bit 4 */);
```

`vcr_life_ctx_verdict` returns:

| case | verdict |
|---|---|
| orphaned, or DEAD | `BAD` |
| ACTIVE or UNKNOWN | `DRAW` |
| INACTIVE, gate off | `DRAW_LOGGED`: today's behaviour, but counted and logged |
| INACTIVE, gate on | `LOST` |

A full registry (17 live PDEVs, which is implausible) answers `UNKNOWN`. Every guard then behaves
exactly as today, so the registry can never make a working box refuse to draw.

### 5.2 Glue, default-on

On every path where the contract holds, these changes add pointer bookkeeping and logs and change
**no register write and no answer**.

| function | change |
|---|---|
| `DrvEnablePDEV` (`vcrdd.c:397-427`) | after the allocation: `pd->life_gen = vcr_life_pdev_on(&g_life, pd)`; 515/1 INFO |
| `DrvEnableSurface` (`vcrdd.c:539-540`) | `vcr_life_pdev_active(&g_life, pd, 1)` after `VcrDd2dInit` |
| `DrvAssertMode` (`vcrdd.c:570-618`) | FALSE: `active = 0` after the sync and reset. TRUE: `active = ok`. 515/3 INFO carries gen, the new state and how many contexts name this PDEV. **A TRUE with contexts naming the PDEV is the "resurrection" the DDK describes: count it** |
| `DrvDisableSurface` (`vcrdd.c:547-568`) | **first** `VcrDdD3dPdevGone(pd, 1)` (below), then `active = 0`, then today's teardown. That is the point where `pvRamBase`/`pjRegs` stop being valid |
| `DrvDisablePDEV` (`vcrdd.c:436-445`) | `VcrDdD3dPdevGone(pd, 2)`, then `vcr_life_pdev_off`, **then** `EngFreeMem(pd)`. 515/2 INFO with gen and "contexts still naming it" (expected 0) |
| `DrvDisableDirectDraw` (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:737-743`) | `VcrDdD3dDirectDrawOff(pd)`: `vcr_life_heap_dead(&g_life, pd->heap_seen)`, `pd->pvmList = NULL`, `pd->heap_seen = NULL`; 515/5 INFO |
| new `VcrDdD3dPdevGone(pd, where)` (`vcrdd_d3d.c`) | for every in-use context with `c->pd == pd`, which the contract says never happens: **orphan** it (`c->pd = NULL`, `rt/zb/tex_surf0/tex_surf1 = NULL`, `c->orphaned = where`), keep the slot reserved until the runtime's `ContextDestroy`, log 515/7 **ERROR** once (slot, gen, pid, dp2s) and count `ctx_orphaned` |
| `ctx_of` (`:1119-1124`) | returns NULL for an orphan, so DP2, Clear2 and ValidateTSS answer `D3DNTHAL_CONTEXT_BAD`, the code they already give an unknown handle. A new `ctx_slot_of` (in use, orphan or not) serves `D3d_ContextDestroy` only, so an orphan's slot and `fpu` are freed by the runtime's own destroy and **a reused slot can never alias a stale handle** |
| `ctx_free` (`:1210-1221`) | `VcrDd2dSync(c->pd)` only when `c->pd && vcr_life_may_hw(vcr_life_state(&g_life, c->pd))`. Otherwise 515/8 DEBUG "sync skipped: PDEV inactive". This is the DDK rule. On sequences #1-#7 it changes nothing: `g2d_busy` was 0 |
| `D3d_ContextCreate` (`:1163-1208`) | refuse (`DDERR_GENERIC`, 515/7 ERROR) if `pd` is not live, which cannot happen. Store `c->life_epoch`. Log 515/4 INFO: slot, PDEV gen, `ddlcl`, `ddlcl->lpGbl`, `dhpdev`, and **the runtime's interface hint `p->dwhContext` on entry** (0 DX3 .. 4 DX8, as `VH5/D3CONTXT.C:356-357` reads it). This tells whether the context will be kept across a loss (DX5-7) or re-created at Reset (DX8) |
| `D3d_DrawPrimitives2` / `D3d_Clear2` (`:1821-1903`) | at entry, once per **lifecycle epoch** (a global counter bumped by every PDEV on/off/active change): `state = vcr_life_state(&g_life, c->pd)`. While `!c->ddlcl_gone`, also compare `c->ddlcl->lpGbl->dhpdev` with `c->pd` and log 515/6 INFO once if they differ: the **global-swap detector**. Then apply `vcr_life_ctx_verdict` (5.3). With the gate off, an INACTIVE PDEV logs 515/8 WARN once per context per episode, counts `call_on_inactive`, and continues exactly as today |
| `Dd_DestroyDDLocal` (`:1971-1978`) | `pd = p->pDDLcl->lpGbl->dhpdev`. Call `VcrDdFlipLocalGone(pd)` **only if `vcr_life_may_read(state)`**. Otherwise 515/9 WARN "DestroyDDLocal names PDEV %p - not live (last freed: gen %u)" and count `ddlocal_dead_pdev`. Always log 515/12 INFO: local, global, `dhpdev`, live/active, and whether `dhpdev` differs from what that local's contexts were created with (`ddlocal_swapped`). Mark every context with `c->ddlcl == p->pDDLcl` `ddlcl_gone = 1` (count `ctx_after_ddlocal`). Nothing dereferences their `ddlcl` afterwards. Then `handle_forget` as today |
| `VcrDdD3dSurfaceGone` (`:148-160`) | also clear `tex_surf0` and `tex_surf1` when they equal `s`, and set `c->dirty = 1` (R3) |
| `mipchain_ext` / `VcrDdD3dCreateMipChain` (`:418-456`, `:545-574`) | after a successful `HeapVidMemAllocAligned`: `pd->heap_seen = vm->lpHeap; vcr_life_heap_live(&g_life, vm->lpHeap)` |
| `Dd_DestroySurface` / `VcrDdD3dFreeMipChain` (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:663-674`, `:586-601`) | for a `MIP_TOP`: if `!vcr_life_heap_may_free(heap)`, **skip `VidMemFree`** (the heap and its memory are gone) and log 515/10 ERROR. Else free as today, and if `lpDDSurface->dwFlags & DDRAWISURF_INVALID` count `chain_loss_frees` (515/11 DEBUG). This settles R4 |

Instrumentation (default-on, rare events only, nothing per triangle, which matters for G52):

- **Event 515 `VCR_EV_DD_LIFE`** in `KMD/include/vcr_events.h`. `tools/vcrlog.py` parses that table,
  so it is named automatically.
  - What codes: 1 PDEV on, 2 PDEV off, 3 active change, 4 context bound, 5 DirectDraw off and heap
    dead, 6 global swap seen, 7 orphan/refusal (ERROR), 8 call or sync on an inactive PDEV,
    9 DestroyDDLocal on a non-live PDEV, 10 dead-heap free skipped (ERROR), 11 chain freed on a loss,
    12 every DestroyDDLocal (local, global, `dhpdev`, live/active, swapped).
  - 514 is already contested between G02 (`VCR_EV_DD_ENGINE`) and G04 (`VCR_EV_DD_BEAT`); renumber
    at integration if needed.
- **`VCR_ESC_D3D_LIFE` = `VCR_ESC_BASE + 14`** (read-only; +12 is G01, +13 is G02). It returns
  `vcr_life_stats`, which survives the 1024-entry ring that a game wraps in seconds (G76).
  `vcrctl d3dlife` prints it.
  - **Read only after the D3D program has exited.** Never run ExtEscape tools under a running D3D
    game.

### 5.3 The behavioural gate: `Diag\D3DSafe` bit 4 (value 16), default OFF

Bits 0-3 and 7 are taken by G01, G03, G02, G10 and G02's test bit. Bit 4 is read at boot by the
miniport beside `D3D32`/`Reset3D`, exactly as G01/G02 plan (`x->d3dsafe`). The display DLL learns it
through `vcr_info.d3dsafe` (G02's recommendation), or else through flag `0x8000 << 4` (G03's scheme).

With bit 4 set:

1. **An inactive PDEV never draws.**
   - **DP2:** parses the whole stream, so render and stage states are kept for the resume. It writes
     no register, skips TEXBLT, and answers `DDERR_SURFACELOST`.
   - **Clear2:** answers `DDERR_SURFACELOST` and draws nothing.

   This uses the same "answer lost" helper G02 designs for the engine give-up, so the two cannot
   diverge.
2. **Render target and Z by handle** (R5; the DDK's recommended model).
   - Store `c->rt_h`/`c->zb_h` (`lpSurfMore->dwSurfaceHandle`) at ContextCreate and SETRENDERTARGET.
   - At every DP2/Clear2, re-resolve them with `handle_get(c->ddlcl, h)` when `c->rt == NULL` (that
     is, after a loss) and `!c->ddlcl_gone`.
   - A handle that does not resolve leaves the target empty, as today.

   This is armed only if the bed shows Outcome 5 (section 6). On the proven path `c->rt` is not
   NULL while DP2 runs, so the lookup never runs. A render target that was really destroyed, not
   lost, has had its handle forgotten, so the lookup returns NULL: the same empty target as today.

Off: the switch changes nothing; 515/8 is the detector that says whether bit 4 is ever needed.

### 5.4 Old path and caps

- **No caps change.** No register field or value changes.
- **No single-pass or multipass change.** The old path is today's code wherever every PDEV and heap
  is live, which is all seven recorded sequences.
- The only new answers on a working box:
  - `D3DNTHAL_CONTEXT_BAD` for an orphan, which is a NULL dereference without it;
  - a refused `ContextCreate` on a non-live PDEV, which cannot occur.

## 7. d3dprobe test: `lost`

`d3dprobe lost [--full] [--res WxH] [--bpp 16|32] [--pace MS] [--tests clear,tex[,mip]] [--release-while-lost] [--hold-lost S] [--external]`

1. **Create and test.**
   - Create the device (fullscreen: switch 1, through `vcr_pace_before_switch`/`_after_switch`).
   - Create the managed textures. `tex` is the 64x64 checker; `mip` is the existing chain test,
     and its vidmem copy is our mip-chain block.
   - Run the tests and read back. These are the same analytic references `render` uses: the exact
     clear colour; the checker texels; level colours by texel-to-pixel ratio for `mip`, silicon only,
     because 86Box samples level 0 at every size.
2. **Lose the device** (switch 2).
   - `vcr_pace_before_switch()`, then `ShowWindow(hwnd, SW_MINIMIZE)`. The runtime's window hook
     gives the desktop back at once. Pump for 500 ms, then `vcr_pace_after_switch()`.
   - The wndproc's focus-lost "end the run" is suppressed for this deliberate deactivation only.
   - Record `TestCooperativeLevel`; expect `D3DERR_DEVICELOST`. Call `Present` (expect
     `D3DERR_DEVICELOST`) and one `DrawPrimitiveUP`, which must return.
3. **`--release-while-lost`:** release the device now and end. The RESULT records the
   release return value.
4. **Restore** (switch 3).
   - Wait until the pace allows. `vcr_pace_before_switch()`, then `ShowWindow(SW_RESTORE)` and
     `SetForegroundWindow`.
   - Pump until `TestCooperativeLevel == D3DERR_DEVICENOTRESET` (at most 10 s), then
     `vcr_pace_after_switch()`.
   - `Reset(&pp)`, paced as a switch.
   - Redraw and read back the same tests.
5. **Release** (switch 4, paced).

`RESULT {"mode":"lost","before":{"pass":..,"fail":..},"after":{...},"lost_hr":"88760868","notreset_hr":"88760869","reset_hr":"00000000","switches":4}`.

**Pass criterion:**

- every check passes before **and** after, pixel-exact against the references;
- `lost_hr` = `D3DERR_DEVICELOST`, `notreset_hr` = `D3DERR_DEVICENOTRESET`, and `reset_hr` = `D3D_OK`;
- host side, read after exit:
  - `vcr_life_stats` reads `ctx_orphaned == 0`, `ddlocal_dead_pdev == 0`,
    `chain_dead_heap_skips == 0` and `call_on_inactive == 0`;
  - `chain_loss_frees >= 1` when `mip`/`tex` are present;
  - the ring shows the old context destroyed before any `DrvDisablePDEV` of its PDEV, and a new
    `ContextCreate` after the Reset.

There are four paced switches, at least 5 s apart with `--pace 5000`, which follows the CRT rule.
`d3dprobe_run.py` gains the `lost` mode and its post-exit `vcrctl d3dlife` read.

## 8. Regression tests

**`tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_d3dlife.c`** is a TRUE-SOURCE test of `include/vcr_d3dlife.h`. It
replays the **seven measured sequences** of section 3 as event arrays, plus the bad orders the
contract forbids. Each assertion checks the fixed verdict **and** the old-buggy one. An `old_*`
model encodes today's code: "always live", "sync if `pd != NULL`", "free always".

1. **Registry.**
   - `on(P1)` returns gen 1; `state(P1)` is INACTIVE, then ACTIVE after `active(P1,1)`.
   - After `off(P1)`, `state(P1)` is DEAD, while `old_state(P1)` is "live".
2. **Sequences #2, #6 and #7** under both hypotheses for `dhpdev` at DestroyDDLocal.
   - Freed P: `may_read` is 0, while `old_may_read` is 1 (the UAF).
   - Live desktop D: `may_read` is 1, the same as old. The proven path is unchanged.
3. **ContextDestroy after `DrvAssertMode(P,0)`** (all seven sequences).
   - `may_hw(INACTIVE)` is 0, while `old_sync` (`pd != NULL`) is 1.
   - `may_hw(ACTIVE)` is 1 under both models.
4. **The forbidden order** `ContextCreate(P) -> DisablePDEV(P) -> DP2`.
   - The context is orphaned and the verdict is `BAD`.
   - The old model dereferences the freed pointer: `old_dp2_reads_dead == 1`.
5. **Slot reuse.** The orphan's slot stays reserved, so a new context gets another slot. A stale
   handle never resolves to the new context; with plain slot+1 handles and immediate free, the old
   model aliases.
6. **Heaps.**
   - `heap_live(H)`, then `heap_dead(H)`: `may_free(H)` is 0, while old is 1.
   - A reused address (`heap_live(H)` again) gives `may_free` 1.
7. **Gate.**

   | PDEV state | gate | verdict |
   |---|---|---|
   | INACTIVE | off | `DRAW_LOGGED` (today's behaviour, counted) |
   | INACTIVE | on | `LOST` |
   | ACTIVE | either | `DRAW` |
8. **Overflow.** A 17th PDEV gives UNKNOWN, and `may_read`/`may_hw` stay 1 (fail open).

**`tests/python/test_vcr_kmd_d3dlife.py`** holds glue source assertions. Each asserts the new guard
**and the absence of the old unguarded text**:

- `DrvEnablePDEV` calls `vcr_life_pdev_on`.
- `DrvDisablePDEV` calls `VcrDdD3dPdevGone` and `vcr_life_pdev_off` **before** `EngFreeMem(pd)`.
- `DrvDisableSurface` calls `VcrDdD3dPdevGone` before `VcrDd2dTerm`.
- `DrvAssertMode` sets `active` on both branches.
- `Dd_DestroyDDLocal`'s `VcrDdFlipLocalGone` sits under `vcr_life_may_read(`. The old unconditional
  `VcrDdFlipLocalGone((VCR_PDEV *)p->pDDLcl->lpGbl->dhpdev);` is gone.
- `ctx_free`'s `VcrDd2dSync` sits under `vcr_life_may_hw(`. The old `if (c->pd)\n        VcrDd2dSync(c->pd);` is gone.
- `DrvDisableDirectDraw` clears `pvmList` and calls `vcr_life_heap_dead`.
- `VcrDdD3dFreeMipChain` calls `vcr_life_heap_may_free` before `VidMemFree`.
- `VcrDdD3dSurfaceGone` clears `tex_surf0`/`tex_surf1`.
- `ctx_of` excludes orphans; `D3d_ContextDestroy` uses `ctx_slot_of`.
- No `->lpGbl->dhpdev` dereference in `ctx_free`, and in DP2/Clear2 only under `!c->ddlcl_gone`. This
  guards against the gap's "re-derive" fix creeping back.
- Event 515 is in `vcr_events.h`; the README's Diag table has the `D3DSafe` bit 4 row.

`tests/README.md` gains the fix-to-test row. `bash tests/run_all.sh` must stay green.

## 10. Risk to proven paths

| path | effect |
|---|---|
| **Default 16 bpp D3D** | no register write changes. Each DP2/Clear2 adds one global epoch compare (the registry scan, at most 16 pointer compares, runs only when the epoch moved). Logs only on lifecycle events, never per triangle |
| **Glide/OpenGL coexistence** | escapes are untouched (HWCEXT, exclusive owner). With bit 4, an inactive-PDEV context can no longer write 3D registers under a Glide owner registered on the *active* PDEV, which today it could |
| **SLI state** | none |
| **Flip path** | `Dd_Flip`/`GetFlipStatus` untouched. Only `DestroyDDLocal`'s flip-stats log is skipped, and only when its PDEV is not live, where today it would read freed memory |
| **DirectDraw** | `DrvDisableDirectDraw` now clears `pvmList`. A chain allocation after that, which should not occur, falls back to DirectDraw's placement with today's WARN (513/10) instead of using a freed array |
| **Failure modes of the change itself** | a registry bug that marked a live PDEV DEAD would refuse that box's contexts. Three things prevent it: the registry fails open (UNKNOWN), the host tests replay the real sequences, and d3dprobe `render`/`lost` run on the bed before silicon |
| **Concurrency** | `g_ctx`, `g_h` and the registry are global and unlocked, as `g_ctx` and `g_h` already are. The scrub touches only contexts naming the PDEV being destroyed. Whether dxg serializes `DrvDisablePDEV` against another PDEV's DP2 is open question 6 |

## 12. Open questions

1. At a `DestroyDDLocal` that follows `DrvDisablePDEV` (sequences #2, #6, #7), does
   `pDDLcl->lpGbl->dhpdev` name the freed game PDEV, or the live desktop PDEV because dxg re-pointed
   the local's global? This is R1, and T1/T3 and run 4 on `.124` answer it.
2. Does dxg ever deliver DP2/Clear2 or TEXBLT to a context whose PDEV is retained and inactive? The
   case to watch: a DX5-7 title that restores into a mode recreated as a *new* PDEV rather than a
   resurrected one.
3. After a DX5/6/7 alt-tab, does the runtime re-send `CreateSurfaceEx` for the restored video-memory
   surfaces, and a SETRENDERTARGET before rendering resumes? (R5)
4. Is the loss-destroy of our chains (`DDRAWISURF_INVALID`) always before `DrvDisableDirectDraw` of
   the owning PDEV? (R4)
5. Does XP SP3's Driver Verifier accept `vcrdd.dll` (special pool through `EngAllocMem`) on the
   bed? Confirm with `verifier /query` before relying on it.
6. Are `DrvDisablePDEV`/`DrvDisableSurface` serialized by GDI/dxg against DP2 of another PDEV's
   contexts? If not, the scrub needs an `EngAcquireSemaphore` around `g_ctx`. That would also protect
   today's unlocked `ContextCreate` scan.
7. The final numbering at integration: event 515 (514 is contested by G02/G04), escape `+14`, and
   `D3DSafe` bit 4.
8. Should `gamerun.py` read `vcrctl d3dlife` automatically after every game exit? It is cheap, and
   it makes every future sweep a lifetime check.

## 0. Verdict

1. **The gap is real, but its value is not where the gap puts it.**
   - As a *fallback* it is worth little. Every title on the list has a measured, user-accepted Glide or OpenGL path on `.124` (`EV/gametune_1001/README.md:14-26`).
   - The dormant D3D paths are also **not one setting away**. The switch that routes them, `HKLM\Software\RetroAgent\GlideRender` → `FR_UE1DEV` (`WT/provisioning/fleetres/fleetres.c:798-812`, `:1051-1052`), is read by **15 staged launchers of 7 titles**: Descent 3 (2), Deus Ex, Hexen II (3), Serious Sam TFE (3), Serious Sam TSE (3), Unreal Gold and UT 436 (2), by `grep -l FR_UE1DEV LIB/*/*.bat`.
   - Setting it to 0 would also drop Hexen II's uncap and Serious Sam's `ogl_bExclusive=1` line (`WT/scripts/fleet/stage-fleetres.py:695-706`, `:1008`). Without that line TFE stops running on `.124` (`EV/gametune_1001/README.md:55-58`).
   - **This design therefore never touches `GlideRender`.** Each experiment is a per-title, box-local override with a byte-for-byte restore.
2. **Where its value is: the UE1 `D3DDrv` renderers are the best D3D test bed `.124` has.**
   - **UT 436's UTbench on D3DDrv is the only D3D workload with a same-card vendor reference, including single-chip rows.** AmigaMerlin 3.1-R11 on this V5 6000 measured it at 1, 2 and 4 chips (§2.2):
     - one chip (cfg 0): 70.26 / 70.04 / 68.59 fps at 640x480, 800x600 and 1024x768 x16;
     - 69.33 / 67.74 at 640x480 and 800x600 x32.

     Our D3D renders on one chip, so cfg 0 is the right row to compare against. The result answers G46's open question 5 and its "transport versus fill" question in a second title (§7).
   - **UE1 D3DDrv is a DX7-interface (`d3dim700`) client** (`Direct3D7.cpp`, `IDirect3DDevice7`, `DirectDrawCreateEx` loaded at run time). It is one of only two DX7 paths among the titles on `.124`; the other is Tribes 2's wrapper. Every active D3D title there is DX5/DX6 (`d3dim.dll`), D3D8 or D3D9 (`reports/games.md` §3a).
   - A `d3dim700` stream has met our HAL once, on 86Box, before most of today's HAL existed.
   - **The game's own log prints the caps it sees and the video memory it is told about** (§3.4). That turns G64's open question 1 and part of the caps review into a free measurement.
3. **Four corrections to the record** (new here):
   - a. **Two dormant renderers did run on vcr-kmd's D3D HAL**, in the 10-01 baseline sweep, which ran with `D3D32`/`D3DBigTex` already armed (§2.1):
     - **Descent 3** (staged Direct3D until 10-02) drew its intro movies at 640x480. Those are textured 2D quads (inferred). It ran after `d1x-rebirth` closed by WM_CLOSE.
     - **Deus Ex** (D3DDrv until 10-02) at 1280x960x32 gave **two pure-black frames**, (0,0,0) only, at 30 s and at 60 s. It ran right after `d2x-rebirth` (OpenGL → our h5 Glide) was **force-closed**. That is G01's signature, but nothing proves it.

     No 3D scene of any dormant renderer exists on silicon.
   - b. `EV/86box_v3/unreal_gold_d3d_vcrkmd_flyby.png` is **the XP desktop after `taskkill /f /im Unreal.exe`**, not a game frame. The only D3DDrv frame is `..._flyby2.png`, from the emulated Voodoo3.
   - c. **"UT99 D3DDrv dies at 1280x960+" (PLAN:91, FIND:2063) is wrong.** The CSV rows read `process-exited, game process gone before any fps line`, but the kept logs say otherwise:
     - at 1024x768x32, 1280x960x16 and 1600x1200x16 the log reads `AddAttachedSurface failed for Z (DDERR_CANNOTATTACHSURFACE)` → `3d hardware initialization failed` → `Bound to SoftDrv.dll`;
     - at 1280x960x32 and 1600x1200x32 D3D initialised with a texture budget of 9693K and 1599K, and the demo did not finish inside the harness's wait.

     Nothing died. The harness labels a renderer fallback as a crash and records `D3DDrv.dll` as the renderer when the game drew with `SoftDrv` (§2.2).
   - d. **Small facts that change test plans:**
     - Serious Sam TFE's staged `Bin/` has **no Direct3D at all**; only TSE's `Engine.dll` has D3D8. A case-insensitive search for `d3d8.dll`/`Direct3DCreate8` finds it in no TFE file.
     - UT 436 stages `GameRenderDevice=OpenGLDrv`, not D3DDrv (`LIB/UnrealTournament436/System/UnrealTournament.ini:21`). Its `[D3DDrv.D3DRenderDevice]` section carries a GeForce2 GTS's device description (`:289-297`), a captured machine's fingerprint.
     - Turok 2's Glide module imports `glide3x.dll`, not Glide 2. The game loads every `Video_*.dll*` in its folder, so a `.off` rename does not hide a module (§1).
     - Carmageddon 2's Direct3D renderer (`Driver=3`) crashed on `.246` (`LIB/Carmageddon2/install.reg`). It is not a working fallback on any box.
4. **The static census of UE1 D3DDrv answers several gaps without box time** (§3). It covers all three staged builds (Unreal Gold `f924e209`, UT 436 `dd6e3692`, Deus Ex `6179c83c`).
   - **What it does not use:**
     - **G05 and G12:** every texture-stage op is in our `TextureOpCaps` 0x184f. The stage-0 colour op is initialised to **MODULATE, never MODULATE2X**.
     - **G09:** masked textures use the alpha test (`ALPHAFUNC GREATER`, `ALPHAREF 127`), not `COLORKEYENABLE`.
     - **G28 and G29:** its blend factors are all inside our caps.
     - **G40:** state blocks (G40's count).
     - **G15:** `ValidateDevice`.
     - **G10:** its Z depth follows the colour depth (16/16, 32/32).
   - **What it does use:** `LINESTRIP` at two call sites (G16), `ZBIAS 15` (G22), `SHADEMODE FLAT` (G24), `SPECULARENABLE` (G25), `MIPMAPLODBIAS` (G27), `DITHERENABLE` at any depth (G30) and `FOGTABLEMODE LINEAR` with computed start and end (G31). It also uses palettes when offered (`UsePalettes=True`, G38), procedural textures (G37, G56) and the free-memory report (G64). `ZENABLE=USEW` is used only with the WBUFFER cap (G23's reading).
5. **What this design delivers.**
   - **Host tooling** (§5):
     - the benchmark harness stops leaving the player's UE1 configs changed;
     - it stops calling a renderer fallback a crash;
     - it stops cutting a slow-but-working demo short;
     - a UE1 log parser;
     - a `--renderer` switch for Descent 3's timetest;
     - "dormant" scenes for G73's book, with a route and a verified restore for each.
   - **One d3dprobe test, `chain`** (§8.1). It builds the triple-buffered chains UE1 asks for, at the sizes the vendor HAL refused. **No triple-buffered chain has ever run on this HAL** that the record shows (§3.1).
   - **One supervised session in a Glide-free boot** (§6), with a decision table (§7).
   - **No driver change and no Diag switch.** A route change for a staged title is not part of G82. It is the user's call after the numbers (§7, Q7).
6. **Priority P2, effort M.** Part A could share G46's S2 boot, since it is the same UTbench measurement. Part B is P3.

## 3. Static census: what UE1's D3DDrv asks of the HAL (new; method in Appendix A)

### 3.1 Interface, chain, selection

- **Interface.** All three builds are DX7: `IDirect3DDevice7` error strings, `Direct3D7.cpp` source paths, and
  `DirectDrawCreateEx` named as a string. None imports `DDRAW`; each loads it at run time.
- **Device selection.** UE1 enumerates DirectDraw devices and logs `DirectDraw drivers detected: display
  (Primary Display Driver)` (the vendor logs). `.124` has one display device.
- **Swap chain.** UT 436 and Deus Ex stage `UseTripleBuffering=True` (`UnrealTournament.ini:286`,
  `DEUSEX.INI:201`); Unreal Gold stages `False`.
  - **No record shows a triple-buffered chain on our HAL.** d3dprobe always asks for one back buffer
    (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c:1422`), UT2003 and UT2004 stage `UseTripleBuffering=False` (`UT2003.ini:252`,
    `UT2004.ini:322`), and the 86Box run was double-buffered.
  - The other active D3D8/D3D9 titles' back-buffer counts are not recorded. G76's `ContextCreate` record or the
    flip counters would show them.

### 3.2 Texture-stage operations (`IDirect3DDevice7::SetTextureStageState`, vtable `+0x94`)

| build | call sites | constant COLOROP values | the computed stage-0 COLOROP |
|---|---|---|---|
| UT 436 | 75 | DISABLE, MODULATE (stages 0, 1), BLENDDIFFUSEALPHA | member `this+0xa04`, set to **4 = MODULATE** (`movl $0x4,0xa04(%ebx)` at `0x1000a92d`; `0x1000ab22`); alpha-op member `+0xa08` = 2 SELECTARG1 |
| Deus Ex | 70 | DISABLE, BLENDDIFFUSEALPHA | member `+0xa00` from `mov $0x4,%ebx` (`0x10009ead` → `0x10009f7c`) = **MODULATE** |
| Unreal Gold | 75 | as UT 436 | member `+0x9f0` = **4** |

- **Every op is in our `TextureOpCaps` 0x184f** (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:2165-2168`).
- **UE1's D3D path never asks for MODULATE2X.** G05's half-bright world cannot happen here; UE2 is a different
  renderer.
- No constant `D3DTA_*` modifier was decoded. The computed arguments were not traced, so G13 is
  `UNMEASURED` here, not cleared.

### 3.3 Render states (`SetRenderState`, `+0x50`) and primitives (`DrawPrimitiveVB`, `+0x7c`)

Constant `(state, value)` pairs, UT 436. Deus Ex has the same set with a few computed values, plus
`DESTBLEND SRCCOLOR`.

| state(s) | values | our HAL | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ZENABLE` | 1; 2 (`USEW`) | `USEW` turns depth off (`vcrdd_d3d.c:986`); UE1 picks it only when `WBUFFER` is reported (G23 §3 reads the test at `0x1000c744`), which we do not report | G23 (not taken) |

## 8. Tests

### 8.1 d3dprobe `chain` (explicit, never in the default list, `--full` only)

- **New option `--backbuffers N`** (1 or 2). The default 1 leaves every existing cell byte-identical
  (`pp.BackBufferCount = 1`, `KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c:1422`). The device keeps `D3DPRESENTFLAG_LOCKABLE_BACKBUFFER`
  (`:1452`).
- **What it draws**, for each back buffer b = 0..N:
  1. `GetBackBuffer(b)`, then `SetRenderTarget(bb_b, autoZ)`;
  2. `Clear(TARGET|ZBUFFER, C_b, 1.0)`;
  3. quad A: a point-sampled 2x2 texture of colour T_b, z = 0.25, over the centre 64x64;
  4. quad B: solid K, z = 0.75, `ZFUNC LESSEQUAL`, over the centre 128x128 (hidden where A is);
  5. `LockRect`.

  Then `Present` N+1 times, vsync off, and each buffer is locked again.
- **Pass criterion** (computed reference, exact):
  - (8,8) holds C_b;
  - the centre holds T_b;
  - (W/2−48, H/2) holds K;
  - all compared as **raw pixel words** (565: 0xF800, 0x07E0, 0x001F, 0xFFFF; 8888: 0x00FF0000, ...);
  - every buffer still holds its own pattern after the rotation;
  - the run's pid shows no 513/12, 15 or 17 refusal and no 513/10 heap failure in the ring.
- **Cells:**
  - 1280x960x16 with D16;
  - 1280x960x32 with D24X8 (`skipped`, not failed, without `D3D32`);
  - 1024x768x32 with D24X8.

  These are UE1's triple chains at the sizes AmigaMerlin's HAL refused.
- **Reported, not judged:** `GetAvailableTextureMem()` after `CreateDevice` and after 8 MB of textures (G64);
  the chain's offsets as the ring logs them.
- **Expected today: pass** (inferred). The heap is about 26-28 MB of chip 0, and a 1280x960x32 chain of two back
  buffers plus D24X8 is 14.7 MB (the front buffer is the primary). A failure is a finding: the surface set UE1

## 10. Risk

| risk | to what | mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| A wedge or freeze in an untested D3D path | the box | Attended only. The vendor's failing cells come last. AmigaMerlin's board wedged once on this game path (09-23); Max Payne froze in 10-04 (G04). Ring after every title; G04's beat if built |
| The player's configs left changed | `.124`'s own play | Every route has a byte-for-byte restore, read back; the session stops on a mismatch. `GlideRender` is never written. `.v56kbak` is not used as the restore |
| An escape while a D3D game runs | win32k's display lock (Max Payne precedent) | No `vcrctl` escape during a game. At most one `fbshot --probe` (`vcrprobe.sys`, no escape) per scene |
| CRT mode switching | the monitor | `--pace 5000`; at least 10 s between scenes; few modes (`vcr_pace.h` rules) |
| Glide/OpenGL coexistence | validity of every D3D result | No Glide in the boot until the final reference step; the preflight refuses on `cmdFifo0` bit 8 |
| SLI state | Glide titles after the session | D3D writes no SLI register. The Glide reference step comes last and leaves the board as Glide does |
| The flip path | `vcrdd` | A triple-buffered chain is new to this HAL (§3.1). `chain` runs it in the lab before any game does; UE1 with `UseVSync=False` uses the proven `FLIPNOVSYNC` |
| Proven paths | default 16 bpp HAL, OpenGL, Glide | none: nothing in `vcrdd`/`vcrmp` changes |

## Appendix A: re-deriving the UE1 static census

```
for t in UnrealTournament436 DeusEx UnrealGold; do
  i686-w64-mingw32-objdump -d --no-show-raw-insn "LIB/$t/System/D3DDrv.dll" > $t.asm   # DeusEx: D3DDRV.DLL
done
```

- **Call sites:** `IDirect3DDevice7` vtable slots:
  - `call *0x50(%reg)` = SetRenderState (slot 20);
  - `*0x94` = SetTextureStageState (37);
  - `*0x7c` = DrawPrimitiveVB (31);
  - `*0x98` = ValidateDevice (38);
  - `*0x9c` ... `*0xa8` = the state-block calls (G40).
- **Arguments:** for each site, read the `push` instructions back to the previous `call` or `ret`, nearest
  first: `this`, then the arguments left to right. For SetRenderState that is (state, value); for
  SetTextureStageState (stage, type, value); for DrawPrimitiveVB (type, ...). A pushed register is "computed".
- **Computed stage ops:** find the member the register was loaded from (`mov 0xa04(%esi),%ecx`, UT 436), then
  the constants written to that member (`movl $0x4,0xa04(%ebx)`).
- **Caveat:** the sites were not each traced to the device object. A surface's slot 20 (GetPalette) or slot 37
  could share an offset, but the decoded pairs are all valid `D3DRENDERSTATETYPE` and `D3DTEXTURESTAGESTATETYPE`
  values with valid values, which such a call would not produce.
- **Counts here:**

  | build | SetTextureStageState | SetRenderState | ValidateDevice |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | UT 436 | 75 | 57 | 0 |
  | Deus Ex | 70 | 52 | 0 |
  | Unreal Gold | 75 | 57 | 0 |

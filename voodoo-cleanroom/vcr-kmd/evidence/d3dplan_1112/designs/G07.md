## 1. Verdict

**The gap is real and fixable. The hardware supports it (single-base mode), and no new register field is needed.**

There are three separate ways the allocator gives the TMU texels that are not where it reads them.

1. **Scatter (the big one, in game).** XP sends one `CreateSurface` per mip level (`dwSCnt` = 1). Each call allocates its own block, and the TMU then reads level *k* at top + offset(*k*).
   - First-fit puts small levels into any earlier hole that fits them, while the top goes further up.
   - Every level below the first misplaced one then samples foreign memory: another texture, a render target, or stale data.
   - This needs a fragmented heap, which a game produces and a fresh d3dprobe device does not.
2. **Small-level padding (even on a fresh heap).** Each per-level block starts 16-byte aligned, but the TMU packs levels byte-exact.
   - The packing rule: "the others are packed contiguously after", and a texel's address is `{texbaseaddr} + lod_offset + s + t*lod_width` (SPEC §20, L6083-6089, L6112-6114).
   - An R5G6B5 2x2 level is 8 bytes. The 1x1 level that follows it is therefore placed 8 bytes past where the TMU reads it.
   - 3dfx met the same rule. Their rev 22 (8/13/99) said "enforce 16 byte alignment of mip levels"; rev 7 (9/30/99) "Took out 16 byte alignment. Fixes PRS 8811 and some DCT250 mip filter tests" (`VH5/D3TXTR.C` header, lines ~100-160).
3. **A 16 bpp texture whose top is 1x1 (adjacent finding, same arithmetic).**
   - Its absent levels sum to 0x2AAA8, so `texBaseAddr = (off - 0x2AAA8) & ~15` is 8 bytes low when `off` is 16-aligned. The TMU then reads the texel 8 bytes before the texture.
   - 3dfx places such a texture 8 bytes into its block (`VH5/D3TXTR.C` initMipMapChain, "1x1 textures need to be aligned 8 bytes from vaddr0", PC99 TextureSizes).
   - Our allocator places it 16-aligned (`mipchain_ext`, `KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:437-450`).

**Is (1) the cause of the in-game block noise (G06)? Not yet proven.**
- The pattern fits: noise only on minified surfaces (distant terrain and rock), near surfaces clean (`ONBOX/session_log.md:17,19,21`; `ONBOX/games/UT2004_botmatch/UT2004_botmatch_090s_zoom_terrain.png`).
- Competing candidates exist: the DXT small-level layout and texture-cache flushes (runtime-12 §7.1).
- The design therefore measures first (§4.0, a counter that names every split chain the TMU is pointed at).
- The guard (§4.2) removes the symptom whatever the answer turns out to be.

## 2. What the code does today (verified)

- **Allocation.** `Dd_CreateSurface` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c:633-645`) calls `VcrDdD3dCreateMipChain`.
  - With D3DBigTex it calls `mipchain_ext` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:367-466`); the default path is `:522-584`.
  - Both compute `levels` from the surfaces of **that call only** (`:423-436`, `:550-559`), allocate one block with `HeapVidMemAllocAligned(…, 16)` (`:437-441`, `:563-566`), and mark the call's own top `MIP_TOP` (`:457`, `:575`).
  - Both loops already handle a multi-surface list: the level index comes from the size, and mipchain_ext checks that the FOURCC is consistent.
- **One call per level on XP.**
  - DDK: dwSCnt "is usually 1 on Windows 2000. However, if you support … GUID_NTPrivateDriverCaps … the member will contain the actual number of surfaces" (`W2KDDK` d_grafx.chm, DdCreateSurface page; `DDK8/ddrawint.h:1022-1035`).
  - Measured on the 86Box bed: "seven calls for a 64x64 D3D8 texture, DDSD_MIPMAPCOUNT 7 on each" (`RA/tests/python/test_vcr_kmd_d3d.py:106-114`). The same descriptor arrives on every call.
  - XP asks `GUID_NTPrivateDriverCaps` (fad16a23, expected size 8) at every DirectDraw enable, and we decline it (`ONBOX/ring_after_probe_ii_decoded.txt` seq 24832: `GetDriverInfo fad16a23 00000008 88760028`; `vcrdd_ddraw.c:683-706`).
- **Sampling.**
  - `tex_view` (`:754-799`, LODMAX block `:779-787`) and `tex_view_ext` (`:806-837`, `:814-824`) walk the attach list (`next_mip`, `:617-626`) from a `MIP_TOP` surface.
  - They set `tLOD` LODMAX (bits 11:6, `TL_LODMAX(l) = l << 8`, `KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h:179`) over every attached level. **Nobody checks that a level is where the TMU will look.**
- **Coverage.** The measured passes are on a fresh heap only:
  - `mip` (64x64 R5G6B5, levels 0-2) and `mip2048` (levels 0-4), both 83/0 at 1024x768x32 (`ONBOX/d3dprobe_textures_a_1024x768x32.txt:11-22`).
  - No test reads a level below 16x16 of a 64 chain or a 1x1 level, and no test builds a DXT or A8R8G8B8 chain: every one uses `Levels = 1` (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c:488, 536, 718`; chains only at `:823, :993`).
- **Heap placement.** The heap is first-fit and ascending, with no per-block overhead: a 64x64 R5G6B5 texture at 0x900000, then a 16x16 at 0x902000 (`ONBOX/ring_after_probe_ii_decoded.txt` seq 24918/24921).
- **The FOURCC fallback is dead code under per-level calls.** A chain "whose levels' FOURCC differs" is left to DirectDraw (`:432-433`), but a single-surface call cannot trigger it. hal-23's reading is confirmed.

## 3. Options considered

| option | what | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **A. `DDHAL_PRIVATECAP_ATOMICSURFACECREATION`** | answer `GUID_NTPrivateDriverCaps`; dxg then passes the whole complex surface in one `CreateSurface` (`dwSCnt` = levels), and the existing loops pack it as one block | **CHOSEN (bit 1).** It is the documented NT mechanism for exactly this need, and 3dfx's shipping NT5 HAL for this chip answers it (`VH5/DDFXNT.C:1224-1248`, active because `USE_NT5_DDMEMMGR` = 1 for `_WIN32_WINNT >= 0x0500`, `VH5/DRIVER.H:40-45`). Our allocators already handle lists. There is no state between calls and no assumption about ordering. Its cost is global: dxg batches *every* complex surface (flip chains), so it gets the widest on-box verification |
| B. a reservation matched by descriptor | at a `dwSCnt`=1 call whose surface is the descriptor's top (`DDSD_MIPMAPCOUNT` N > 1), reserve N levels and keep a pending record (descriptor pointer, w0/h0, format, base, placed mask); place later level calls into it | **FALLBACK**, built only if §9 shows that A does not take for D3D8 textures or disturbs a flip/2D path. It is contained to textures, but relies on unproven facts: top-first and consecutive calls, the same descriptor pointer, and `dwWidth/dwHeight` = the top's |
| C. repack at `CreateSurfaceEx` | the attach lists exist there, so move the levels into one block | **REJECTED.** It changes `fpVidMem` after dxg has returned the surfaces to user mode (inferred hazard), and the DDK gives no support for it |
| D. `tLOD` bit 24 `tmultibaseaddr` | `texBaseAddr1/2/38` for LOD 1, 2 and 3-8 (SPEC §8.79 L3963-3981, §20 L6085) | **REJECTED.** LODs 3-8 must still be contiguous. It costs three more TMU writes per bind per TMU (0x310/0x314/0x318), and its TBIG semantics are undocumented (Glide's own `grTexMultibaseAddress` has a FIXME for Napalm ranges) |
| E. a contiguity guard only | at bind, sample only the levels that sit where the TMU reads them | **KEPT as bit 0.** It is the safety net for any per-level placement (restore, fallbacks) and is the measurement itself |

## 4. The design

### 4.0 Instrumentation (log only, no register change, default ON)

This follows the Phase-0 convention of hal §10: pinned by a Python source test, it lands default-on.

- **Creation.** Events 513/11 and 513/20 keep their numbers. Their message gains:
  - `dwSCnt`, the descriptor's `dwWidth x dwHeight` and `dwMipMapCount`;
  - the surface's own `lpSurfMore->dwMipMapCount`;
  - whether `lpAttachListFrom` is already set.
  - The `c` argument packs `(dwSCnt << 24) | (desc mipcount << 16) | (kind << 8) | levels`. No new per-call event, so no extra IOCTL (G52).
  - Event 7 (`CreateSurface`, `vcrdd_ddraw.c:636`) gets the descriptor's mipcount as `c`.
  - The FOURCC fallback (`:432-433`) logs a WARN the first time it fires.
- **Binding.** `tex_view`/`tex_view_ext` always compute the contiguous prefix (§4.2's function) and return it in `tmu_view`.
  - `compute_regs` (`:921`) counts each bind of a split chain: `c->mip_split`, split into "shallow" (first misplaced level ≤ 3) and "deep" (≥ 4), and `c->mip_guarded`.
  - The first 8 per context are logged at WARN: `texture %u: %u of %u levels in place (level %u at %x, TMU reads %x)`.
- **Summary.** A new INFO event, logged next to "context N gone" (`ctx_free`, `:1210-1221`): `context %u mip: %u split binds (%u shallow), %u guarded`.
  - `Dd_DestroyDDLocal` (`:1971`) logs the per-PDEV creation counters: chains created whole, per-level calls, chain allocation failures (`DDERR_OUTOFVIDEOMEMORY` answered).
  - Use the next free 513 sub-numbers when implementing (24-27 are free today, but other G-items add events too).

### 4.1 The switch: `Diag\D3DMipChain` (DWORD, absent = 0)

- **Where it is read.** The miniport reads it at every `IOCTL_VCR_INFO`, like `DdHeapFloor` (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:442-466`), so the next PDEV picks it up.
- **The flags.** It sets two positive flags, so an older miniport never arms them: `VCR_INFO_F_MIPGUARD 0x8000` (bit 0) and `VCR_INFO_F_MIPWHOLE 0x10000` (bit 1), in `KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h` after `0x4000`.
- **The display side.** `vcrdd_2d.c:442-466` copies them to the new `VCR_PDEV` fields `mip_guard` and `mip_whole` (`vcrdd.h`). It logs an "armed (Diag\D3DMipChain): guard %u, whole chains %u" INFO event, as 513/16 and 513/18 do.
- **Any card.** Both bits apply on a Banshee/Voodoo3 too, because the default 16 bpp texture path is the same code. That is what lets the 86Box Voodoo3 bed check the creation pattern first.
- **Deployed value:** 3.

### 4.2 Bit 0: the contiguity guard (`tex_view`, `tex_view_ext`)

A new pure function in `KMD/include/vcr_texlod.h` / `KMD/common/vcr_texlod.c`:

```c
/* levels of a chain, from its top, that sit where the TMU reads them: level j
 * at at[0] + vcr_tex_chain_offset(kind, w0, h0, j); an uncompressed level must
 * also have the row pitch the TMU assumes (w * bytes); a compressed level's
 * "pitch" is its linear size and is not looked at. 1..have (0 for have 0). */
vcr_u32 vcr_tex_chain_contig(vcr_u32 kind, vcr_u32 w0, vcr_u32 h0, const vcr_u32 *at,
                             const vcr_u32 *pitch, vcr_u32 have);
```

- **The walk.** In both views, the existing `while ((m = next_mip(m)) …)` loop (`:780-786` and `:815-824`) also records each level's `fpVidMem` and `lPitch`, into arrays of 9 or 12 entries.
  - `contig = vcr_tex_chain_contig(...)`. The default path passes `VCR_TEXK_RGB16`; its `mip_offset` (`:235`) is the same arithmetic as `vcr_tex_chain_offset(RGB16)`, and a native test pins that.
- **The clamp.** Only when `pd->mip_guard` is set does it apply `n = min(n, contig)` before LODMAX (`tex_view_ext` then still applies `vcr_tex_chain_usable`). `tex_view` gains a `VCR_PDEV *` parameter, passed by `view_of` (`:840`).
- **Registers.** Only the LODMAX field (tLOD bits 11:6) can change, and only downward. The values stay `LODMIN = top`, aspect, S_IS_WIDER and TBIG; `texBaseAddr` is still computed from the top.
  - **For a chain whose levels are all in place, the written tLOD is identical to today's.**
  - A split chain samples its in-place prefix. If level 1 is misplaced, it samples the top only, which is the proven `MIPFILTER NONE` register shape. The result is aliasing at distance instead of foreign colours.

### 4.3 Bit 1: whole chains (`DdGetDriverInfo`, the two allocators, `VcrDdD3dFreeMipChain`)

1. **Answer the private caps.** In `DdGetDriverInfo` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c:683-706`), only when `pd->mip_whole` is set **and** the D3D half answers (the same `pjRegs && g2d_ok && !d3d_disabled` test as `VcrDdD3dDriverInfo`, `vcrdd_d3d.c:2136`), answer GUID `{0xfad16a23,0x7b66,0x11d2,{0x83,0xd7,0x00,0xc0,0x4f,0x7c,0xe5,0x8c}}`:
   - with `DD_NTPRIVATEDRIVERCAPS { dwSize = 8, dwPrivateCaps = DDHAL_PRIVATECAP_ATOMICSURFACECREATION }`, `dwActualSize = 8` and `ddRVal = DD_OK`;
   - **never** `NOTIFYPRIMARYCREATION`; the primary stays dxg's.
   - The ring then shows `GetDriverInfo fad16a23 00000008 00000000`.
2. **The allocators take the list as it comes.** A new pure helper is used by both allocators:
   - `vcr_tex_call_levels(w0, h0, w[], h[], n)`. It counts from the **largest** surface in the list, not from `lplpSList[0]`, and returns 0 if a surface is not a halving or two surfaces are the same level.
   - Both allocators pick `top` = the largest surface. With `dwSCnt` = 1 this is exactly today's `lplpSList[0]` and `levels` = 1.
   - Everything else in the loops is unchanged: format, block size `vcr_tex_chain_offset(kind, w0, h0, levels)` (or `mip_offset`), per-level `fpVidMem = base + offset(k)`, pitch, and `MIP_TOP`/`MIP_LEVEL`.
3. **The 1x1 placement rule.** Under bit 1, `vcr_tex_chain_pad(kind, w0, h0)` = 8 for `VCR_TEXK_RGB16` with a 1x1 top, else 0.
   - The block is allocated `total + pad`. The top goes at `base + pad`, so the TMU base `(off − 0x2AAA8)` is 16-aligned.
   - `SF_PAD8` (0x8 in the surface-local `dwReserved1` flags, next to `SF_DIRTY` 0x4) records it, and `VcrDdD3dFreeMipChain` (`:586`) frees `fpVidMem − pad`.
   - This changes nothing for any other shape: the pad is 0 for every other kind and size (proven in §7).
4. **Free.** Unchanged: the top frees the block, and levels clear their flag (`:586-601`). With atomic creation, dxg still calls DestroySurface per surface (inferred). The levels return HANDLED, so dxg never frees memory it did not allocate.
5. **What stays per level.** With bit 1 on:
   - A call that still arrives per level (a lost surface's `Restore`, or any path dxg does not batch) takes today's single-level path.
   - Bit 0 then keeps the TMU inside what is in place, and the bind counter shows it.

### 4.4 The old path

With `D3DMipChain` absent or 0:
- the GUID stays unanswered (`DDERR_CURRENTLYNOTAVAIL`, as at seq 24832), so dxg keeps sending one level per call;
- the allocators compute `levels` = 1 per call as today;
- the views write today's LODMAX;
- no pad is applied.

The register traffic, allocations and DirectDraw call pattern are byte-for-byte today's. Only the §4.0 counters and log text are added.

### 4.5 Caps

No caps change. `DDSCAPS_MIPMAP` is already advertised (`vcrdd_d3d.c:2110-2111`), as are `TextureCaps MIPMAP` and `TextureFilterCaps MIPFPOINT`. Trilinear (G26) and LOD bias (G27) need correct chains and come after this item.

### 4.6 Fallback B (only if §9 says A did not take)

- **Data.** Add `vcr_mip_pending { PDD_SURFACE_LOCAL top; PDD_SURFACEDESC desc; ULONG w0, h0, kind, fcc, levels, placed; FLATPTR base; PVOID heap; }` in `VCR_PDEV`.
- **Opening a record.** At a `dwSCnt`=1 texture call that satisfies all of:
  - `DDSCAPS_MIPMAP` is set, `DDSD_MIPMAPCOUNT` N > 1 is set, and the surface is the descriptor's top (its size equals the descriptor's `dwWidth x dwHeight`, cross-checked with `lpSurfMore->dwMipMapCount` = N),
  
  then reserve N levels, place the top and open the record.
- **Placing a level.** A later call with the same descriptor pointer, the same format, and level *k* = `vcr_tex_level_index(w0, h0, w, h)` not yet placed goes at `base + offset(k)` as `MIP_LEVEL`.
- **Closing.** Anything else closes the record, and the remaining levels fall back to per-level placement, guarded by bit 0. DestroySurface of the record's top also closes it.
- **Testing.** The bookkeeping is a pure function, host-tested the same way.

## 5. Gating

- **The instrumentation** (§4.0) is log-only and default-on, pinned by a Python source test.
- **Bit 0 (guard)** lands **default-off**.
  - Its proven-path cost is nil: the tLOD it writes for any in-place chain equals today's, pinned in §7, so every passing d3dprobe check today writes the same registers.
  - It only ever lowers LODMAX, to levels the driver owns.
  - **It is a safe default-on candidate.** After one supervised clean boot passes §8 boot B (mipfrag* report 0 foreign, the default render list 40/40 unchanged), flip its default to ON (absent = on, 0 = off) in a follow-up commit.
- **Bit 1 (whole chains)** lands **default-off** and stays off until boot C's full matrix passes: flips, DirectDraw 2D, windowed D3D, and the three games. It changes dxg's call pattern for every complex surface, so it is **not** default-on material on a d3dprobe pass alone.
  - Make it default-on together with G20's D3D32/BigTex decision, because DXT and A8R8G8B8 chains exist only with D3DBigTex.
- **Process.** One Diag change per clean boot. The switch is read at every mode change, but supervised runs still use a clean boot (G01).

## 6. d3dprobe tests (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`, explicit, never in the default list)

Every test follows the same rules:
- **Level colours.** Each level gets one solid colour from a 12-entry table: distinct, 565-exact or within 12 per channel after DXT/565 decode, none black, white, grey or the poison. Examples: ff0000, 00ff00, 0000ff, ffff00, ff00ff, 00ffff, ff8000, 8000ff, 00ff80, 80ff00, ff0080, 0080ff. DXT levels are solid 4x4 blocks (`dxt_block`, `:457`).
- **Level selection.** Each level *L* is drawn with MIN/MAG/MIP filter POINT at a texel:pixel ratio *r* = 2^(*L*+0.26), so floor and round agree as in `mip2048`.
  - The quad is centred on (128,128) and `W = min(192, 16·S/r)` px wide, S being the top's size, with UV extent `W·r/S` ≤ 16 wraps. This stays inside the 14.18 iterator range (G89).
  - The centre pixel is read.
- **Verdicts.** Each probe is **ok** (within 12 of level *L*'s colour), **clamped** (within 12 of the top's colour, *L* > 0), or **foreign** (anything else). The JSON adds `levels_ok`, `levels_clamped` and `levels_foreign`.
- **Skips.** A test whose format or size the HAL does not offer is SKIPPED, as `test_cells` does.

**Layout model: fresh heap, MANAGED, filled by LockRect (as `mip` does).** These are hal-23's three missing tests plus one more.

| test | chain | levels probed | pass | today (expected) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mipdeep` | 64x64 R5G6B5, 7 levels | 0-6 | all ok | 0-5 ok; **6 foreign** if the heap starts the 1x1 block on the next 16 bytes (padding, §1.2). That is the first silicon measurement of that claim |
| `mipdxt1` | 256x256 DXT1, 9 levels | 0-5 (the levels ≥ 8 wide, `vcr_tex_chain_usable`) | all ok | all ok: offsets 0, 0x8000, 0xA000, 0xA800, 0xAA00, 0xAA80 (TMU 8x4 units). A failure here means the DXT layout model is wrong (a G06 candidate), not G07 |
| `mipdxt5` | 256x256 DXT5, 9 levels | 0-8 | all ok | all ok (every level ≥ 16 bytes in 4x4 units) |
| `mip8888` | 256x256 A8R8G8B8, 9 levels | 0-8 | all ok | all ok (the 2x2 level is 16 bytes, so per-level blocks happen to be packed) |

**Allocation: a fragmented heap, DEFAULT pool, filled by `UpdateTexture` from a SYSTEMMEM chain (DP2 TEXBLT walks every level, `vcrdd_d3d.c:1499-1549`).**
- **Isolation.** Run these first, in their own d3dprobe invocation, so no older holes exist.
- **Construction**, all DEFAULT-pool textures of the test's format:
  1. `S0`: a poison spacer.
  2. `H`: a hole-maker of at least the sub-levels' sum and smaller than the top.
  3. `S1`: a poison spacer.
  4. `T`: a placeholder exactly the top's size.
  5. `P`: a poison block of at least the whole chain.
  6. Release `H` and `T`, then create the chain.
- **Poison** is 0x804020.
- **The sizes:**

| test | chain | H | T | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mipfrag` | 64x64 R5G6B5 (8192 + 2730) | 64x32 | 64x64 | 128x64 |
| `mipfrag_dxt1` | 256x256 DXT1 (32768 + 10960) | 256x128 | 256x256 | 512x256 |
| `mipfrag_dxt5` | 256x256 DXT5 (65536 + 21872) | 256x128 | 256x256 | 512x256 |
| `mipfrag_8888` | 256x256 A8R8G8B8 (262144 + 87380) | 256x128 | 256x256 | 512x256 |

- **What each path should show:**
  - **Per-level path:** the top lands in `T`'s hole, its levels in `H`'s, and the TMU reads them inside `P`, giving **foreign** (the bug reproduced).
  - **Bit 0:** **clamped** (no foreign).
  - **Bit 1:** the whole chain fits neither hole, so it is packed above `P`: **all ok**.
- **Probes:** levels 0-4 (DXT1: 0-4, all ≥ 16 wide).
- **Pass** = all ok. **Guard verdict** = 0 foreign.
- **Ground truth.** The ring's 513/20 lines (level addresses, now carrying `dwSCnt`) are the record of where each level landed. Copy them into the run's evidence.

Optional, for open question 3: `ddlab mipchain`. A DirectDraw 7 64x64 R5G6B5 `DDSCAPS_TEXTURE|MIPMAP|COMPLEX` in video memory is lost by one paced `SetDisplayMode` and then `Restore()`d; the ring shows whether a restore reaches `CreateSurface` per level.

## 7. Host regression tests

**`RA/tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_mipchain.c` (new; it `#include`s `common/vcr_texlod.c`, as `test_vcr_kmd_texlod.c` does).** It asserts both the fixed and the old value.
- **`vcr_tex_chain_contig`, 64x64 RGB16 at A = 0x900000:**
  - packed (0, 0x2000, 0x2800, 0x2A00, 0x2A80, 0x2AA0, 0x2AA8) → **7**, the same count the old code sampled, so registers are unchanged.
  - today's per-level fresh-heap layout (1x1 at +0x2AB0) → **6** (old: 7, so the TMU read +0x2AA8).
  - first-fit scatter (level 1 below the top) → **1** (old: 7).
  - a level with a wrong pitch → stops there.
- **`vcr_tex_chain_offset` agrees with `mip_offset`** for every RGB16 shape to 256. The DXT1 256 offsets are pinned (…0xAAA0, 0xAAB0, 0xAAC0, total 0xAAD0), and `vcr_tex_chain_usable(DXT1, 256, 256, 9)` = 6, unchanged.
- **`vcr_tex_call_levels`:** a 7-surface list → **7** (atomic); one surface → **1** (the old per-level block, documented as the bug); a duplicate level or a non-halving → 0 (declined).
- **`vcr_tex_chain_pad`:** RGB16 1x1 → **8** (old 0). The test also checks the result: with the pad, `vcr_texlod_compute_ext(1,1,RGB16,2,0x900008,…)` gives `lin_base + 0x2AAA8 == 0x900008`; without it, `0x900000 − 8`. Every other kind and size from 1x1 to 2048x2048 → 0.

| observation | meaning | next |
|---|---|---|
| boot A: UT2004 split binds > 0, mostly shallow; boot B: noise gone | G07 is a cause of G06's noise | ship bit 0 default-on; bit 1 restores the mips |

## 10. Risk to proven paths

- **Switch off:** none to registers, allocation or the dxg call pattern. Only log text and counters change, and the per-bind contiguity check is up to 12 pointer compares inside the existing walk.
- **Bit 0:** registers change only for split chains (LODMAX lowered). The cost is aliasing, and possibly some TMU cache thrash, on those textures, so measure UT2003 fps. It adds no new register and no allocation.
- **Bit 1:**
  - dxg batches every complex surface, including D3D and DirectDraw flip chains. We still return NOTHANDLED for them. This is the one global change, which is why ddlab flip/blt/sdlddraw, d3dprobe perf and a 2D title are in boot C.
  - Whole-chain blocks are bigger single allocations, so OUTOFVIDEOMEMORY can come sooner under fragmentation. A MANAGED texture is evicted and retried by the runtime; a DEFAULT-pool `CreateTexture` fails sooner.
  - G63's offset-0 block loss is unchanged.
- **The default 16 bpp path:** only behind the switch. The 16 bpp chain allocator gets whole chains under bit 1, which is the fix for a fresh install as well.
- **Glide/OpenGL coexistence, SLI state, command FIFO:** untouched. No register writes are added, and Glide does not use DirectDraw surfaces.
- **The flip path:** bit 1 only changes how back buffers reach `CreateSurface`; `Dd_Flip` is untouched.

## 12. Open questions

1. Under `ATOMICSURFACECREATION` on XP SP3, does a D3D8 texture arrive as one call (`dwSCnt` = levels) with the top first? Are the attach lists already linked at that call? 3dfx's `initMipMapChain` walks them at creation (inferred to require it); ours does not need them.
2. With per-level calls, is the descriptor the same pointer on every call, and does it carry the top's `dwWidth`/`dwHeight`? This is needed only by fallback B.
3. Does a DX5-7 title's `Restore()` of a lost mipmap reach `CreateSurface` per level even with the cap? 3dfx met per-level restore calls on Win9x (Redline Racer, `VH5/D3TXTR.C:1750-1785`). If so, a restored chain is guarded but not mipmapped. A follow-up could place a restored level into its top's block through `lpAttachListFrom`.
4. Does `HeapVidMemAllocAligned` start the next block on the next 16 bytes, or round sizes further? mipdeep level 6 answers it.
5. Is G07 the cause of G06's noise (§9)? Is UT2004's heap (about 61 MB at 1280x960x16, less its back buffer and Z; `ONBOX/ring_after_probe_ii_decoded.txt` seq 24821 shows 62460 KB at 1024x768x32) churned enough to scatter chains?
6. Does whole-chain allocation raise OUTOFVIDEOMEMORY or eviction enough to cost fps in UT2003/UT2004?
7. Should a bound `MIP_LEVEL` surface (a DX7 application binding a sub-level directly) sample its in-place sub-chain under bit 0? Today, and under bit 1, it samples only that level.
8. When the guard is default-on, should the WARN cap of 8 per context fall to 0 at LogLevel 2, given the per-VcrDd IOCTL cost (G52)?

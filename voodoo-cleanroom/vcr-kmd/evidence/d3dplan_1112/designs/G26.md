## 1. Verdict

**The gap is real and fixable. For one textured stage the hardware does trilinear in a single pass. For two textured stages it cannot, and LOD dither stands in.** No investigation is needed before building it. The open points are silicon details, and the probe below settles each of them.

- **One textured stage: NATIVE, single pass, both TMUs, one copy of the chain.**
  - TMU1 samples the odd LODs and passes its texel down. TMU0 samples the even LODs and blends the two by the LOD fraction.
  - The register words are 3dfx's own for this exact chip: `VH5/D6MT.C:5453-5526`, the `D3DTP_LINEAR` row, `HOME_BOTH` column. They match what Glide does in UMA mode (`GL5/glide3/src/gtex.c:1410-1416`).
  - The pixel rate does not drop: our D3D runs at 1 pixel per clock today (`combineMode` = 0). Trilinear only rules out the 2 pixels per clock that G50 may add later.
- **Two textured stages: IMPOSSIBLE in one pass.**
  - 3dfx refused the combination in validation unless its `LOD_DITHER` key was set (`VH5/D6MT.C:3265-3305`).
  - Its draw path dithered the LOD on each linear-mip stage either way (`SST_TLODDITHER`, `:5931-5933`, `:6097-6099`).
  - This design does the same, behind its own bit.
- **It is a caps change as well as a register change.**
  - UT2003 on `.124` logs `Supports BLENDDIFFUSEALPHA` and no `Supports trilinear` (`ONBOX/games/UT2003_flyby/UT2003.log:41-42`). Unreal Engine 2 therefore reads the caps and runs its staged `UseTrilinear=True` without trilinear.
  - Once `MIPFLINEAR` is reported, the titles change what they send. That is why this lands default-off (§5).
- **Nothing in our stacks has ever set these bits on this silicon.**
  - MesaFX falls back to nearest-mip on Napalm: `MESA/fxddtex.c:328-339, 351-362`, and `MESA/fxsetup.h:630` reads "XXX todo - GR_CMBX_LOD_FRAC?".
  - That fallback is a limit of **MesaFX's combine-extension abstraction**, not of the chip. 3dfx shipped the legacy-combine words below on Napalm, and our HAL uses the legacy combine (`combineMode` = 0).
- **What is proven on `.124`, and what this design builds on:**
  - **TMU1 → TMU0 texel flow:** `tex2mod`/`tex2add` pass 40/40 (`ONBOX/d3dprobe_render_1024x768x32_clean.txt`).
  - **Chain LOD selection:** `mip` 3/3 and `mip2048` 5/5 (`ONBOX/d3dprobe_textures_a_1024x768x32.txt`).
  - **Placement:** chains are placed 16-byte aligned by the driver.

## 3. What the hardware and the references say

### 3.1 The mechanism

- **textureMode[30] `trilinear`** ("Enable trilinear texture mapping"), per TMU (SPEC §8.76, hw.md L3852).
- **`tc_mselect` / `tca_mselect` = 5 `LOD_frac`** (SPEC textureMode table, L3839). With `tc_reverse_blend` this picks f or 1−f.
- **tLOD[18] `lod_odd`** ("0=even, 1=odd") and **tLOD[19] `lod_tsplit`**. The spec text for `lod_tsplit`: "0 = texture contains all LOD levels, 1 = odd or even levels only, as controlled by lod_odd" (L3918-3919).
  - With `tsplit` = 0, both TMUs walk the **same** full chain from one `texBaseAddr`. `lod_odd` then only says which parity that TMU takes.
  - That is the "UMA" form. `texBaseAddr` is a full 26-bit address in shared memory (SPEC §8.79, L3966), so one copy serves both TMUs.
  - Glide's version: "Hack to use single pass trilinear in UMA state … tmu == GR_TMU1 → `tLod |= SST_LOD_ODD`" (`GL5/glide3/src/gtex.c:1410-1416`, also `:1913`, `:2285`). 3dfx's D3D version: `HOME_BOTH`, `tLOD[1] = tLOD[0]`, `baseAddr[1] = baseAddr[0]` (`VH5/D3TXTR.C:2189-2193`).
- **Both TMUs get the same coordinates without any extra per-vertex writes.**
  - SPEC: "The sWtmu0 register is all the TMUs local 1/W" and "The S/W0 register is the S coordinate of the current vertex divided by W, for all TMUs" (SPEC §8.68-8.70, hw.md L3785-3797).
  - `sSetupMode` keeps `SM_W0|SM_ST0` only. TMU1's own S1/T1/W1 are used only when `SM_W1|SM_ST1` are set.
  - 86Box models it the same way: `voodoo_setup.c:195-216` copies TMU0's start and gradients into TMU1 unless W1/ST1 are set.
- **LOD dither, textureMode[4] `tloddither`:** "This adds an average of 3/8 (.375) to the LOD value and needs to compensated in the amount of lodbias" (L3861).
- **The bias and the clamp:** "lodbias is added to the calculated LOD value, then it is clamped to the range [lodmin, min(8.0, lodmax)]", or 11.0 with `tbig` (SPEC tLOD section). The bias is 4.2 signed in tLOD[17:12].
- **SPEC §3.3's "trilinear texture filtering as a two-pass operation" (L554) is stale.** §3.3 opens with "Please ignore this section" (L539). §3.5 L631 and §1 L439 say 2 TMUs give trilinear at a pixel per clock (hw.md Q1).

### 3.3 Parity, magnification and the end of the chain (86Box model, plus reasoning)

These are the hazards a reviewer would raise. The probe in §6 checks each one on silicon.

- **Which level each TMU fetches.** With `trilinear` set:
  - the even TMU maps a computed LOD *l* to *l* rounded up to even;
  - the odd TMU maps it to *l* rounded up to odd;
  - so the pair is always {⌊l⌋, ⌊l⌋+1} (`86B/voodoo_texture.c:190-235`).
  - Parity is by **absolute** LOD (0 = 256 wide, or 2048 with `tbig`). A 128x128 chain therefore starts on an odd LOD and needs nothing special: the same words serve it.
- **Blend direction.** When the integer LOD is odd, the TMU flips its reverse-blend sense (`86B/voodoo_render.c:445-451, 544-550`).
  - Even *l*: out = even·(1−f) + odd·f.
  - Odd *l*: out = odd·(1−f) + even·f.
  - The finer level always gets 1−f. That is why `REVERSE_BLEND` in TMU0 is correct when the odd levels are on TMU1, matching Glide's case 3 (`GL5/glide3/src/gtex.c:944-954`).
- **Magnification and the far end.** The LOD is clamped to [LODMIN, LODMAX] *before* the fraction is taken (`86B/voodoo_render.c:414-418`; SPEC tLOD text). At either clamp f = 0:
  - at an even clamp the result is the even TMU's level, the right one;
  - at an odd clamp the factor is 1 − 0, so the result is the odd TMU's level, again the right one.
  - The other TMU then reads the level one beyond LODMAX at weight 0. That is a read past the chain's end, and nothing more. 3dfx's `HOME_BOTH` tLOD spans `lodminAll..lodmaxAll` with no parity adjustment (`VH5/D3TXTR.C:1388-1391`), so it did the same.
  - Whether weight 0 is exactly 0 on the chip, or ±1 LSB, is open question 3. The `trilinear_end` probe answers it.
- **A single-level texture** (D3DBigTex places it as a chain of one) would be clamped to one LOD while TMU1 fetches a level that does not exist. **This design never arms trilinear for fewer than 2 sampled levels.**

### 3.4 Two textured stages: LOD dither

- Each TMU computes its own LOD and fetches one level. Blending two levels needs a second TMU, which the second texture already occupies. No register combines two trilinear stages in one pass (hw.md Q1).
- 3dfx's two-stage draw path sets `SST_TLODDITHER` on every stage with `mipFilter == D3DTFP_LINEAR` (`VH5/D6MT.C:5931-5933`, `:6097-6099`), whatever the `LOD_DITHER` key says. That key (default 0, `VH5/D3INIT.C:1014-1024`) only changes validation: `D3DERR_TOOMANYOPERATIONS` "because V5 doesn't support it" (`:3265-3305`).
- **Bias.** 3dfx used +0.25 (`LB_DITH`, `VH5/D6MT.C:5512`) for a dithered single TMU, and +0.5 for any two-stage draw the application gave no bias (`:5927`, `:6093`).
  - D3D's linear mip filter is "coarser level with probability f". A uniform dither over [0,1) with bias 0 would do exactly that.
  - The chip's dither averages 0.375 (spec), so the ideal bias is about +0.125. That is not representable: the field steps in quarters.
  - This design starts at **+0.25** (3dfx's `LB_DITH`). The probe records the measured coarse-level fraction, so 0 against +0.25 is settled by measurement (open question 4).
- **SLI.** 3dfx also gave each chip its own LOD bias (0/0.25/0.5/0.75 on 4 chips) for multitexture with trilinear (`VH5/D6MT.C:4300-4348`). That needs D3D SLI, so it is noted for G51 and not designed here.

## 4. The design

### 4.1 New header `KMD/include/vcr_texfilt.h` (integer only, Win32-free)

It follows the pattern of `vcr_3dseq.h`, `vcr_texlod.h` and `vcr_rtfmt.h`: the driver and `tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_texfilt.c` compile the **same** decision and the same word composition. New bit names go into `KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h` beside the existing `TM_*` and `TL_*` names:

```c
/* vcr_3dregs.h additions (h5 h3defs.h names in comments; pinned by the python GPL-header test) */
#define TM_TLODDITHER        (1u << 4)                               /* SST_TLODDITHER */
#define TM_TC_REVERSE        (1u << 17)                              /* SST_TC_REVERSE_BLEND */
#define TM_TCA_REVERSE       (1u << 26)                              /* SST_TCA_REVERSE_BLEND */
#define TM_TC_BLEND_LODFRAC  ((1u << 13) | (5u << 14) | (1u << 18))  /* SST_TC_BLEND_LODFRAC */
#define TM_TCA_BLEND_LODFRAC ((1u << 22) | (5u << 23) | (1u << 27))  /* SST_TCA_BLEND_LODFRAC */
#define TM_TRILINEAR         (1u << 30)                              /* SST_TRILINEAR (textureMode) */
#define TL_LODBIAS_MASK      (0x3fu << 12)                           /* SST_LODBIAS: 4.2 signed */
#define TL_LOD_ODD           (1u << 18)                              /* SST_LOD_ODD */
#define TL_LOD_TSPLIT        (1u << 19)                              /* SST_LOD_TSPLIT: never set (one copy) */
```

```c
/* vcr_texfilt.h */
#define VCR_TFP_LINEAR      3u      /* DX7 D3DTFP_LINEAR, as the runtime hands it to a DX7 DDI */
#define VCR_TF_ARM_TRI      0x1u    /* Diag\D3DFeat TRILIN    (pd->tf_arm) */
#define VCR_TF_ARM_DITHER   0x2u    /* Diag\D3DFeat LODDITHER (pd->tf_arm) */
enum { VCR_TF_NONE, VCR_TF_TRI, VCR_TF_DITHER, VCR_TF_NEAREST };
#define VCR_TF_TMU0_TRI  (TM_TRILINEAR | TM_TC_BLEND_LODFRAC | TM_TC_REVERSE | \
                          TM_TCA_BLEND_LODFRAC | TM_TCA_REVERSE)              /* 0x4ec76000 */
#define VCR_TF_TMU1_TRI  (TM_TRILINEAR | TM_TC_REPLACE | TM_TCA_REPLACE)      /* 0x48241000 */
#define VCR_TF_DITHER_BIAS_Q 1      /* +0.25 LOD: 3dfx LB_DITH (VH5/D6MT.C:5512); open question 4 */

typedef struct vcr_tf_in {
    unsigned mip;        /* the stage's D3DTSS_MIPFILTER */
    unsigned levels;     /* levels the TMU may sample (tmu_view.levels): 1 = no chain */
    unsigned ntex;       /* textured stages in this draw, 1 or 2 */
    unsigned two_tmus;   /* Voodoo3 or VSA-100 */
    unsigned tmu0_free;  /* TMU0 only forwards its texel (REPLACE); 1 for every set-up today */
    unsigned arm;        /* VCR_TF_ARM_* */
} vcr_tf_in;

static __inline unsigned vcr_tf_decide(const vcr_tf_in *in)
{
    if (in->mip != VCR_TFP_LINEAR || in->levels < 2)
        return VCR_TF_NONE;                         /* nothing to blend: today's words */
    if ((in->arm & VCR_TF_ARM_TRI) && in->two_tmus && in->ntex == 1 && in->tmu0_free)
        return VCR_TF_TRI;
    if (in->arm & VCR_TF_ARM_DITHER)
        return VCR_TF_DITHER;
    return VCR_TF_NEAREST;                          /* asked, drawn as today (counted) */
}

typedef struct vcr_tf_words { unsigned tm0, tl0, tm1, tl1, base1, tmu1; } vcr_tf_words;

/* one textured stage: today's TMU0 word for NONE/NEAREST, the HOME_BOTH pair for TRI */
static __inline void vcr_tf_single(unsigned how, unsigned view_tm, unsigned view_tl,
                                   unsigned base, vcr_tf_words *w);
/* add a 4.2 quarter-LOD bias to a tLOD word: sign-extend the field, add, clamp -32..31
 * (3dfx's clamp, VH5/D6MT.C:5424-5427), re-encode; the rest of the word is kept */
static __inline unsigned vcr_tf_lodbias(unsigned tlod, int add_q);
/* a dithered TMU: textureMode |= TM_TLODDITHER, tLOD += VCR_TF_DITHER_BIAS_Q */
static __inline void vcr_tf_dither(unsigned *tm, unsigned *tl);
```

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NONE / POINT / anything else | any | any | any | any | any | any | **NONE**: today's words, byte for byte |
| LINEAR | 1 | any | any | any | any | any | **NONE** |
| LINEAR | ≥ 2 | 1 | Voodoo3 / VSA-100 | yes | 1 | any | **TRI** (§3.2) |
| LINEAR | ≥ 2 | 1 | | no (a G12 TMU-tier placement) | 1 | 1 | **DITHER** |
| LINEAR | ≥ 2 | 2 | | | any | 1 | **DITHER**, per stage, on that stage's TMU |
| LINEAR | ≥ 2 | any | | | 0 | 0 | **NEAREST**: today's words, counted |
| LINEAR | ≥ 2 | 1 | | | 0 | 1 | **DITHER**, for titles that set LINEAR without the cap |

A Banshee (one TMU) never arms either bit (§4.6).

### 4.3 `KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c`

1. **`tmu_view` gains `ULONG levels`.**
   - `tex_view` (`:779-787`) and `tex_view_ext` (`:814-824`) already count the chain (`n`, and `vcr_tex_chain_usable` on the ext path). They store `levels = lodmax - lod + 1`, or 1 when the surface is not a placed chain or MIPFILTER is NONE.
   - CPU only. When G07's contiguity guard lands, it lowers `levels` too.
2. **`compute_regs` (`:921-1029`):**
   - after `two` is known, `ntex = two ? 2 : tex`, and build `vcr_tf_in` from the stage that owns each texture;
   - **one texture:** `vcr_tf_single(how0, v0.textureMode, v0.tLOD, v0.texBaseAddr, &w)`, then `r->textureMode = w.tm0`, `r->tLOD = w.tl0`, `r->tmu1_tri = w.tmu1`, and with `w.tmu1` `r->textureMode1 = w.tm1`, `r->tLOD1 = w.tl1`, `r->texBaseAddr1 = w.base1`;
   - **two textures:** today's words, then `vcr_tf_dither()` on TMU1's pair if stage 0 decided DITHER, and on TMU0's pair if stage 1 did;
   - `r->textured1 = two` is **unchanged**: trilinear programs TMU1's state but sends no TMU1 coordinates;
   - **ordering rule for later integrators (G27 bias/MAXMIPLEVEL, G33 MIRROR):** `tLOD1` and `textureMode1` are derived from TMU0's **final** words. Every other contributor goes first, and `vcr_tf_single` runs last.
   - Increment `c->tf_n[how]` (CPU only; §4.7).
3. **`prepare` (`:1322-1370`):** the untextured fallback (`:1353`) also clears `c->regs.tmu1_tri`. `setupMode` already drops `SM_W1|SM_ST1` there and never sets them for trilinear.
4. **`log_combo` (`:882-919`):** hash and print `tm1` when `tmu1_tri` as well as when `two`, so a trilinear set-up is logged as its own combination.
5. **`prim_caps(D3DPRIMCAPS *c, ULONG tri)` (`:2013-2046`):**
   - with `tri`: `dwTextureFilterCaps |= D3DPTFILTERCAPS_MIPLINEAR | D3DPTFILTERCAPS_LINEARMIPLINEAR | D3DPTFILTERCAPS_MIPFLINEAR`, giving `0x0303033F`, exactly 3dfx's (`VH5/D3INIT.C:2840-2847, 3046-3061`);
   - the call sites `:2071-2072` pass `pd->tf_arm & VCR_TF_ARM_TRI` for the tri **and** line caps, as 3dfx did;
   - LODDITHER alone does not change the caps.
6. **`ctx_free` (`:1210-1215`):** one more INFO event, only when a counter is non-zero (§4.7).
7. **`D3d_ValidateTextureStageState` (`:1905-1910`):** unchanged. G15 calls `vcr_tf_decide` for its honest answer (§4.9).

### 4.4 `KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.h` and `vcrdd_3d.c`

- `vcr3d_regs` gains `ULONG tmu1_tri;`: write TMU1's state without TMU1 coordinates.
- `VcrDd3dState` (`:119`): `if (r->textured1 || r->tmu1_tri)` around the **existing** four TMU1 writes (room 4).
  - With `tmu1_tri` = 0 the sequence is today's, write for write.
  - The compressed-texture `nopCMD` (`:107-112`) already fires on TMU0's word, which carries `TM_COMPRESSED` for the same texture, and it is addressed to TMU0|TMU1.
- `vertex()` and `VcrDd3dTriangle` do not change: no extra per-vertex writes.
- The texture flush (`VcrDd3dTexFlush`, `:253-270`) flips `texBaseAddr` with chip field 0, which reaches every TMU. TMU1's cache is therefore flushed for the shared texture with no change, and `prepare` re-sends the full state after a flush (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1327-1333`).

### 4.5 What the old path does (both bits clear, the default)

- `vcr_tf_decide` returns NONE or NEAREST, and `vcr_tf_single` composes `view | TM_TC_REPLACE | TM_TCA_REPLACE` with `tmu1 = 0`. Those are today's words.
- `tmu1_tri` stays 0, so `VcrDd3dState` writes what it writes today.
- `prim_caps` reports `0x03010317`.
- The only difference is CPU-side counting. The native test pins all of this (§7).

### 4.6 The switch: `Diag\D3DFeat`, two positive bits, read at boot

- **TRILIN** (proposed `0x400`, bit 10): the caps of §4.3.5 and the TRI row of §4.2.
- **LODDITHER** (proposed `0x800`, bit 11): the DITHER rows.
- **Why these numbers:** hal.md §10 reserves bits 0-5 for Phase 2 and 6+ for Phase 3. G15 has bit 0, G08 bit 1, G16 bit 4 and G12 bits 6-8 (G05 and G13 propose 6 and 7). The final numbers belong to whoever lands the `D3DFeat` register header first (G05's proposed `vcr_d3dfeat.h`).
- **Plumbing (shared):**
  - The miniport reads `VcrDiagGet(L"D3DFeat", 0)` at boot, beside `D3DBigTex` (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:238-244`).
  - It reports the word in the appended `vcr_info.d3dfeat` (G15's convention, `KMD/include/vcr_ioctl.h:63-108`). `VcrDd2dInit` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_2d.c:432-475`) zeroes `info` first, so an older miniport reads as 0.
  - G26 creates this plumbing only if no other D3DFeat item has landed it.
- **Arming:** `pd->tf_arm = two_tmus ? (d3dfeat >> 10) & 3 : 0`, with `two_tmus = pd->napalm || info.device == 0x0005 || info.device == 0x0004` (Glide gives `num_tmu = 2` to H4/H4_OEM and Napalm, `GL5/glide3/src/gpci.c:1166-1183`).
  - The Voodoo3 is included only so that the 86Box Voodoo3 bed can smoke-test the armed path (§8.0).
  - A Banshee never arms.
  - At boot, like D3D32/D3DBigTex: the caps a title reads at its desktop must be its fullscreen PDEV's.
- **Event:** 513, next free `what` (24-26 are proposed by G05/G16/G08): `armed (Diag\D3DFeat %x): trilinear (2 TMUs, one textured stage) %s, LOD dither %s`.

### 4.7 Counters (log only, default-on, no register change)

- **Fields:** `vcr_d3dctx` gains `ULONG tf_n[4]`, counted per state computation: NONE, TRI, DITHER, NEAREST.
- **Event:** `ctx_free` logs `context %u linear-mip: %u trilinear, %u dithered, %u nearest` when TRI + DITHER + NEAREST > 0.
- **Value with both bits clear:** it shows which titles send `MIPFILTER LINEAR` without the cap, and how often (open question 7).
- **Value when armed:** it shows that a game really reached the new path.
- **Why the ring keeps it:** one line per context at LogLevel ≥ 2. The ring wraps within seconds (G76), but a context's "gone" lines are the last the ring writes for it.

### 4.8 Contracts with other gaps

| gap | contract |
|---|---|
| **G03** (D3D-owned 3D baseline) | A TMU's other/local selects come from `combineMode` **always** (SPEC §8.21 L2836, via G03 and G12). A stale MesaFX `combineMode` can make TMU0's "other" something other than TMU1, and a stale 2ppc bit broadcasts TMU0's words into TMU1 (L2838). Either breaks trilinear, exactly as both already break the two-stage path. TRILIN can be proven on a clean boot, but it does not graduate to default-on before G03's baseline (TMU `combineMode` = 0, 12 NOPs, 2ppc off) |
| **G07** (chain packing / contiguity guard) | Trilinear reads two levels per pixel, so a scattered level shows on more of the screen. In-game judgement and graduation wait for G07. The probe (fresh heap, levels ≥ 8x8) does not |
| **G12** (combine engine) | G12 places every single-stage op in the FBI and leaves TMU0 a REPLACE forwarder (its placement "F"), which is `tmu0_free = 1`. A placement that needs TMU0's own combine reports `tmu0_free = 0`, and G26 dithers instead. With G12 armed, TMU `combineMode` stays a legacy mirror (selects 0), which this needs |
| **G15** (ValidateTextureStageState) | One stage with LINEAR answers OK when TRILIN is armed. Two stages with LINEAR are TOOMANYOPERATIONS in 3dfx's default; the proposal is OK (dithered when LODDITHER is armed, nearest otherwise, both drawn today). G15 decides (open question 5) |
| **G27** (LOD bias, MAXMIPLEVEL, +0.5 nearest rounding) | One field, one helper: `vcr_tf_lodbias` adds the application bias and the +0.5 rounding for POINT mips. G26 leaves POINT exactly as today, so each change is proven alone. 3dfx used stage 0's MAXMIPLEVEL for both TMUs; for trilinear (one texture) that is right by construction, because `tLOD1` derives from TMU0's final word |
| **G50** (2 pixels per clock) | `tmu1_tri` means both TMUs are busy. G50 must keep 1 ppc for those draws, as 3dfx did (`VH5/D6MT.C:5679`), including the 12 TMU NOPs on leaving 2ppc. "Dither + 2ppc instead of trilinear" is a later knob ("Optimization: do LODdither and 2ppc instead?", same line) |
| **G51** (SLI) | Nothing to do for TRI. The per-chip LOD-bias trick for two-stage linear mips is G51's option |
| **G08** (legacy DX5 states) | DX5 titles reach trilinear only through G08's mapping of `TEXTUREMIN = D3DFILTER_LINEARMIPLINEAR/MIPLINEAR` onto `MIPFILTER LINEAR`. Advertising the legacy bits before G08 changes nothing for them: they are point-sampled today anyway |
| **G33** (MIRROR) | tLOD bits 28/29 reach TMU1 through `tLOD1 = tLOD0 | LOD_ODD` |

## 5. Gating

- **Default OFF.** The two positive `Diag\D3DFeat` bits are read at boot. With both clear, the caps and every register write are today's (§4.5, pinned in §7).
- **Not safe to land default-on now:**
  - It changes the caps, so titles change their own filter choice (UE2, RF, NewDark).
  - It sets bits (`TRILINEAR`, `LOD_FRAC` blend, `LOD_ODD`, `TLODDITHER`) that no stack of ours has ever set on this silicon.
- **One Diag change per clean boot:**
  1. TRILIN alone;
  2. then LODDITHER.

  Each bit is proven alone with the probe (§6) in one supervised boot.
- **Graduation to default-on** (a later, one-line change plus test update) needs:
  1. TRILIN passes `trilinear*` at 640x480x16 and 1024x768x32;
  2. G03's baseline has landed, or the after-OpenGL run (§8, step B5) passed;
  3. G07 has landed;
  4. one in-game A/B (§8, boot B) shows no new artefact.

  LODDITHER graduates separately, on its own probe and A/B.

## 6. d3dprobe tests (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`, explicit `--tests`, never in the default list)

**Common set-up.** Each level of a chain is one solid colour, cycling **red, green, blue** (`ff0000`, `00ff00`, `0000ff`). Adjacent levels then differ in exactly two channels, which run 255→0 and 0→255.

**The measured value.** The fraction f of the coarser level = (coarser's channel) / 255, cross-checked with 1 − (finer's channel) / 255. Each sample averages 5 pixels: the centre and ±40 px in x and y.

**Expectation follows the caps.**
- **`MIPFLINEAR` reported:** the D3D reference (blend).
- **`MIPFLINEAR` not reported:** the **old value**, nearest by truncation. The test still runs, sets `D3DTEXF_LINEAR` and pins that every φ draws C_k.

So the same build gives the before and after evidence. The RESULT records `"mipflinear":0|1` and the measured f per sample. Following G15's proposal, it also records `"d3dfeat"` read from `HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\vcrmp\Diag`.

**The S-iterator limit.** S/T iterate in 14.18 at our 256 scaling, about ±32 repeats (G89), so every quad below keeps |S| < 8192.

| test | what it draws | pass (MIPFLINEAR reported) | old value (not reported) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `trilinear` | 64x64 R5G6B5 full chain (top = absolute LOD 2, even). States: SELECTARG1(TEXTURE), MIN/MAG LINEAR, MIP LINEAR. Constant-LOD quads [32,224)², `rhw` 1, uv span = 3·2^(k+φ), for k ∈ {0,1,2} (absolute LODs 2, 3, 4: both parities) and φ ∈ {0, .25, .5, .75}: 12 frames | φ = 0: C_k, tol 16 (catches a parity/reverse error, which gives C_k+1). φ > 0: \|f − φ\| ≤ 0.15 **and** f(.25) < f(.5) < f(.75) for each k. ## 7. Host regression tests

### 7.1 New: `RA/tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_texfilt.c`

It compiles `KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h` and `vcr_texfilt.h`, the same code the driver runs. Every case asserts **both the fixed and the old-buggy value**:

1. **`the_switch_off_draws_todays_words`.** With `arm` 0, LINEAR on a 7-level chain gives NEAREST, and the words are `tm0 = 0x08241A0F`, `tl0 = 0x808`, `tmu1 = 0`: today's REPLACE word for a bilinear R5G6B5 view. POINT and NONE give NONE with the same words.
2. **`one_stage_linear_on_a_chain_is_single_pass_trilinear`.** TRI gives `tm0 = 0x4EC76A0F`, `tl0 = 0x808`, `tm1 = 0x48241A0F`, `tl1 = 0x40808`, `base1 = base`, `tmu1 = 1`. It also asserts `tm0 != 0x08241A0F`, the nearest-mip word drawn before.
3. **`the_words_are_3dfxs_home_both_linear_row`.** Each SST_ macro is rebuilt from its `h3defs.h` bit definitions, written out in the test with their line numbers. The rows checked are `textureModeCombineT0/T1[LINEAR][BOTH]` (`VH5/D6MT.C:5453-5508`), `tLodT0[LINEAR][BOTH]` = 0 and `tLodT1` = `SST_LOD_ODD` (`:5514-5526`). The test equates them with `VCR_TF_TMU0_TRI`, `VCR_TF_TMU1_TRI` and `TL_LOD_ODD`.
4. **`tmu1_inherits_tbig_aspect_and_mirror`.** A view `tl` with TBIG, LODMAX 11, aspect and the mirror bits gives `tl1 = tl0 | 0x40000`, with no `TSPLIT`.
5. **`nothing_to_blend_is_not_trilinear`.** `levels` 1 gives NONE, and `levels` 2 gives TRI.
6. **`two_textured_stages_dither_only_with_its_bit`.**
   - `ntex` 2 with TRILIN gives NEAREST.
   - Adding LODDITHER gives DITHER: `tm |= 0x10`, `tl` LODBIAS `0x1000` (+0.25), no TRILINEAR, no LOD_ODD.
7. **`a_one_tmu_chip_never_gets_trilinear`** and **`tmu0_busy_falls_back`.**
8. **`lodbias_field`.**
   - +1 on `0x808` gives `0x1808`.
   - +1 on an existing −2 (`0x3E000`) gives −1 (`0x3F000`).
   - The field clamps at +31 and −32, and the bits outside 17:12 are untouched.
9. **`the_decision_is_total`.** An exhaustive sweep over `mip` 0..5, `levels` 0..12, `ntex` 0..2 and every combination of the other inputs. The result is always one of the four values, and TRI only when every TRI condition holds.

## 9. Risk to proven paths

| path | risk | why / mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| default 16 bpp HAL, switch off | **none** | the words, the write sequence and the caps are pinned equal to today's (§7.1 case 1, §7.2) |
| armed, a draw that is not LINEAR-mip on a chain | none | the decision is NONE and the words are today's |
| armed, LINEAR-mip draws | **medium**: never-run bits | 3dfx shipped these exact words on this chip. The probe checks parity, both clamps and TBIG before any game |
| Glide/OpenGL coexistence | medium, shared with today's two-stage path | trilinear adds no new kind of exposure. A stale TMU `combineMode` or 2ppc from Glide corrupts it, as it does every TMU combine today (G03). Our TMU1 writes are ordinary per-draw state, which Glide re-programs at its next `grTexSource` (inferred) |
| SLI | none | D3D renders on chip 0, and nothing here touches `sliCtrl` or `chipMask` |
| flip path, DirectDraw | none | not touched |
| box stability | low | the far-end TMU reads one level past a chain at weight 0, which is a VRAM read and nothing more (3dfx did the same, `VH5/D3TXTR.C:1388-1391`). No new FIFO or idle behaviour |
| performance | low now | +4 TMU1 writes per state change on LINEAR-mip single-stage draws. Today the frame is CPU/PIO-bound (116.7 of 123 ms in DrawPrimitive, `G46`). Later, trilinear rules out 2ppc for those draws (G50), which is why `perf --fill` measures it |

## 11. Open questions (each answered by a step above)

1. **The mechanism on this chip.** Does the VSA-100 do UMA single-pass trilinear (`tsplit` 0, one base, `LOD_ODD` on TMU1 only) exactly as Glide's UMA hack and 3dfx's `HOME_BOTH` row assume? Boot B, `trilinear`/`trilinear_odd`. **If it does not**, fall back to 3dfx's split homes (`VH5/D3RSTATE.C:1830-1960`, `TSPLIT`/`ZEROFRAC` columns). That needs a second copy of the odd levels, which is a much larger allocator change.
2. **LOD accuracy.** How accurate is the hardware's LOD fraction (its log2)? The probe records f(φ). A curve far from φ would also matter for G27's bias choices.
3. **The far end.** Is the weight of the level past LODMAX exactly 0 at an odd LODMAX? (`trilinear_end`.)
4. **The dither.** What is the real dither distribution, and which bias gives the closest linear-mip average, 0 or +0.25? (`trilinear2` fractions.)
5. **Validation for two stages.** Should two stages with LINEAR be D3D_OK (proposed) or 3dfx's `TOOMANYOPERATIONS`? It depends on what UE2, NewDark and Halo do on a validation failure (G15).
6. **Trilinear against 2 pixels per clock.** Once G50 exists, should the default be trilinear at 1 ppc, or 3dfx's "LODdither and 2ppc"? A later knob, decided by `perf --fill`.
7. **Who sends LINEAR without the cap.** Which titles send `MIPFILTER LINEAR` without `MIPFLINEAR`, and do Red Faction and NewDark switch on with the cap? Boot A/B counters, per title.
8. **The `D3DFeat` bit numbers** (proposed 0x400/0x800), and the shared `vcr_info.d3dfeat` plumbing. Settled by the first D3DFeat item to land.
9. **SLI.** Under G51, is 3dfx's per-chip LOD bias (0/.25/.5/.75) a better two-stage stand-in than `TLODDITHER`?
10. **Compressed and 32-bit chains.** Do DXT and A8R8G8B8 chains trilinear-filter cleanly (any TMU erratum with the compressed formats in both TMUs)? `trilinear_dxt5`/`trilinear_8888`, after G07.

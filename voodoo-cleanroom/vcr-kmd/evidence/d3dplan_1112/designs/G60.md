# G60 design: small CPU-side trims, flip polls without the IOCTL, and Glide's alpha-test skip

- **Lane:** clean-room `vcr-kmd` (`vcrmp.sys` + `vcrdd.dll`), the Voodoo 5 6000 in `.124`. This covers Direct3D plus
  the DirectDraw flip path that every D3D title presents through.
- **Kind:** fixable (performance). **Priority P3.** **Effort M:** four S-sized pieces, two small measurements, and
  two or three supervised boots.
- **Depends on:**
  - G46: the `D3DStats` counters, its fine timing, and the chip's `fbiAfuncFail`/`fbiPixelsOut` per frame;
  - G74 and G78: the d3dprobe `perf` fill mode and the vsync-on method;
  - G01: a clean engine before any on-box D3D run.
- **Soft dependencies:**
  - G52, so the per-frame DEBUG log IOCTL does not swamp the per-call measurement;
  - G28, whose target-has-alpha flag and alpha-channel factors widen B4;
  - G35 (stencil) and G91 (blend op): new inputs to B4's rule;
  - G50, which makes B4 worth more;
  - G54, which supersedes B3;
  - G59, which shares the flip counters and must not be confounded.
- **Sources:**
  - perf-13 (`reports/perf.md` §4.2) and hw-23 (`reports/hw.md` §4.1, "A cheap performance trick");
  - the code at the deployed tree (`display/vcrdd_d3d.c` md5 `51e98e53…`, last changed in `481acfd`);
  - Glide `GL5/glide3/src/distate.c`; 3dfx's NT HAL (`VH5`);
  - the VSA-100 specification `SPEC` = `…/H5/DOCS/avenger_spec.doc` (md5 `8f49e07f…`). `Lnnnn` is a line of its
    `soffice --headless --convert-to txt:Text` output, as in `reports/hw.md` §0.
- **Evidence:** today's on-box rings (`evidence/d3dplan_1112/onbox/`). This is a design only: nothing was built,
  deployed or run, and no box was contacted.

## 0. Verdict

The gap holds three different things, and each gets its own answer.

1. **The per-call setup of `DrawPrimitives2`/`Clear2` is real, but too small to matter.** It covers the FPU save
   and restore, a `memset`, `target_of` and `fvf_layout`.
   - All of it together is about 0.5-1.5 µs a call (inferred).
   - UT2003 makes 29.9 DP2 calls and 2 Clear2 calls a frame (G46 §1.2). That is at most ~50 µs a frame: 0.04 % of
     its 123 ms frame, and under 0.5 % of an 85 Hz frame.
   - **The FPU bracket is a correctness requirement, not a trim target.** It stays.
   - This part is measured once, as a by-product of G46's fine timing. **It is expected to close with no code
     change.**
2. **The vblank IOCTL is real, and the ring already shows its cost.**
   - Today every retrace read for a flip is an `IOCTL_VCR_VBLANK` round trip into the miniport.
   - A d3dprobe vsync run made **876 of them per flip**.
   - Single reads took **1.08-2.6 ms**. The vsync pulse they are looking for is about 33-44 µs long at the staged
     85 Hz modes (inferred from VESA timings).
   - **B2** reads chip 0's `status[6]` through the register window the DLL already maps.
   - **B3** is optional and costs nothing extra. While a flip is pending, the status reads `VcrDdRoom` already makes
     before every vertex also watch for the retrace.
     - That catches the pulse in the "D3D pattern": the app renders through the pulse and polls only afterwards.
     - In that pattern the flip deadline fires today. That is the likely cause of the 15 of 300 deadline-completed
       flips in the 1024x768x32 vsync run.
3. **Glide's alpha-test skip is real silicon behaviour, with a gain that is still inferred.**
   - **What SPEC guarantees.** A pixel that fails the alpha test is invalidated "and no drawing occurs to the alpha,
     color, depth, or stencil buffers" (SPEC §8.23.1 L2913), and `fbiAfuncFail` counts it (L3667-3668).
   - **When forcing `GREATER 0` changes nothing.** With the blend factors where a source alpha of 0 means
     "destination unchanged", Z writes off and no stencil, forcing the test changes no byte that Direct3D defines.
     A host test proves this exhaustively against a blender model. Forcing it then skips the frame-buffer
     read-modify-write of every fully transparent pixel.
   - **The gain can be measured on today's driver.** The application's own alpha test (`GREATER`, ref 0) programs
     exactly the register value B4 would program. So the measurement needs no driver change.
   - **UE2 already does this itself on most of its blended draws.** UT2003 logged `am 45119`/`44119` for 8 of 12
     blended menu combines, and `45110`, which B4 would cover, for 3.

**The plan:**
1. Measure (§3).
2. Then four pieces behind one new default-OFF switch, `Diag\D3DTrim`, one bit per clean boot:
   - **B1:** per-call trims. Expected to be empty.
   - **B2:** direct vblank reads.
   - **B3:** retrace snoop.
   - **B4:** alpha-test skip.
3. No caps change. Everything is single pass. With the switch absent, register traffic and IOCTL traffic are as
   today.

## 1. What the code does today

### 1.1 Per DDI call

- `D3d_DrawPrimitives2` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:1821-1871`) does these steps in order:
  1. `ctx_of`;
  2. `memset(&w)` over `dp2walk`, about 112 bytes (`:1835`, struct at `:1244-1252`);
  3. `fvf_layout` (`:1837`);
  4. `target_of` (`:1852`), plus a DEBUG `VcrDd` when the target moved (`:1851-1855`). That happens on the first
     DP2 after every flip, and is a `vsnprintf` plus an IOCTL (G52);
  5. the drawable test;
  6. **`EngSaveFloatingPointState`** (`:1861`);
  7. `VcrDd3dDrawInit` (`:1862`);
  8. `walk`;
  9. **`EngRestoreFloatingPointState`** (`:1868`).
- `D3d_Clear2` (`:1873-1900`) does the same minus `fvf_layout`.
- **Why the bracket exists.** `vcrdd_3d.c` and `common/vcr_fog.c` are the only objects built with the x87
  (`KMD/Makefile:63`, `:65`). `vcrdd_d3d.c` is built `-mgeneral-regs-only`, so the compiler refuses a float there.
  This is pinned by `test_floats_live_in_one_object_behind_the_fpu_bracket` (`WT/tests/python/test_vcr_kmd_d3d.py:85-91`).
- **The vendor does not save the FPU state.** 3dfx's NT HAL never calls `EngSaveFloatingPointState`: there are 0
  hits for `EngSaveFloat|KeSaveFloat|fxsave|fnsave`, searched case-insensitively over the whole `H5/W2K` tree. Yet
  it does float and SSE work in its DDI (`VH5/I386/AXTRIKNI.ASM`, the `STBKNI` paths in `D6MT.C`). Its miniport
  does save the state (`FSAVE`, `W2K/Src/Video/Miniport/H5/h3modeset.c:924`).
  - Perhaps XP's DirectX kernel brackets D3D DDI calls itself (inferred, unverified). §4 explains why that is not
    worth settling.

### 1.2 Every retrace read is an IOCTL

- `vblank()` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c:147-151`) is `EngDeviceIoControl(IOCTL_VCR_VBLANK)`. That goes to
  videoprt and then to `VcrDdIoctl` (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_dd.c:81-103`), which:
  - reads chip 0's `status` and `vidCurrentLine` (0x94);
  - returns them with `dd_scan`;
  - also returns `dd_refresh_mhz`, the achieved rate, which is non-zero only with `Diag\FlipDeadline` = 1
    (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp_hw.c:714`).
- **Callers:**
  - `flip_done` (`:246-255`), through `flip_sample` (`:232-240`). That serves:
    - `Dd_Flip` while the previous flip is pending (`:330`);
    - `Dd_GetFlipStatus` (`:363-368`);
    - a Blt into `flip_from` (`:501`);
    - a Lock of `flip_from` (`:647-659`).
  - `Dd_Flip`'s one post-write sample (`:352`). It must stay after the start-address write
    (`test_the_retrace_state_is_read_after_the_start_address_write`).
  - `Dd_WaitForVerticalBlank`, with two loops of up to 200,000 IOCTLs each (`:153-182`).
  - `Dd_GetScanLine` (`:196-212`).
- **The DLL already maps the same window.** `VcrDd2dInit` maps chip 0's register window
  (`KMD/display/vcrdd_2d.c:480`; the miniport side is `KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:720-745`, "io, CMDFIFO, 2D, 3D").
  - The DLL reads `status` at offset 0 in `VcrDdRoom` before every vertex (`vcrdd_2d.c:92-108`;
    `vcrdd_3d.c:188`), and in every `VcrDd2dSync` (`:118-134`).
  - The retrace bit is in every one of those reads, and nobody looks at it.

### 1.3 `alphaMode`

- **`compute_regs` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:991-998`):**
  - It sets the alpha test only when the application enables it: `AM_ATEST | AM_AFUNC(cmp(ALPHAFUNC)) |
    AM_AREF(ALPHAREF)`.
  - Blending takes the RGB factors from `blend_src`/`blend_dst` (`:630-663`). The alpha-channel factors are always
    `AM_ASRC(BF_ONE) | AM_ADST(BF_ZERO)`.
  - `fogMode` is 0 or `FM_ENABLE | FM_DITHER` (`:1002-1006`). So the blend order and operation bits [11:8] are
    always 0, i.e. ADD (SPEC L2853-2856).
  - `VcrDd3dState` rewrites the whole of both registers at every state flush (`vcrdd_3d.c:75-84`).
- **Glide** (`GL5/glide3/src/distate.c:2032-2066`) forces the test when all of these hold:
  - RGB `SRC_ALPHA`/`ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`, operation ADD;
  - depth mask off;
  - stencil write mask 0, or stencil off;
  - the application's own test off.

  It then sets `SST_ENALPHAFUNC | SST_ALPHAFUNC_GT` with the reference cleared, i.e. `GREATER 0`. Glide does
  not look at the alpha-channel factors.
- **3dfx's D3D HAL does not do it.** `alphaTestEnable` only mirrors the application's state
  (`VH5/D3RSTATE.C:3312-3341`).
- **Register values**, from `KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h:120-136`:
  - `GREATER` is `AM_AFUNC(4)` (`cmp(D3DCMP_GREATER)`, `vcrdd_d3d.c:665-668`), which is Glide's
    `SST_ALPHAFUNC_GT` = BIT(3).
  - So SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA without a test is **`0x00045110`**, and with the forced test it is **`0x00045119`**.

## 2. What the evidence on file says

| run (`ONBOX/README.md`) | flip counters (511/12, 511/13) | where |
|---|---|---|
| #13 `d3dprobe perf --full 640x480x16`, vsync, 85.1 fps | flips 300: retrace 297, **deadline 2**; **262,965 reads** (876 a flip), longest **1,228 µs**, longest wait 6,728 µs | `ONBOX/ring_after_probes_decoded.txt:192-193` |
| #14 `d3dprobe perf --full 1024x768x32`, vsync, 84.4 fps | retrace 284, **deadline 15**; 30,325 reads (101 a flip), longest **1,078 µs**, longest wait 2,025 µs | `:962-963` |
| #5 `d3dprobe render --full 1024x768x32` | flips 14: retrace 4, **deadline 9**; 4,384 reads, longest **2,615 µs**, longest wait 10,107 µs | `ONBOX/ring_after_probe_ii_decoded.txt:185-186` |

**Interpretation:**

- **The ordinary IOCTL is cheap.** 262,965 reads in 300 frames (3.53 s) bound one complete poll iteration at
  ≤ 13.4 µs. That includes the runtime's user-mode retry loop and the syscall.
  - This matches the model the flip test already uses: 3 + 3 µs of IOCTL plus a 9 µs loop
    (`WT/tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_flip.c`, `IN_NS`/`OUT_NS`/`GAP_NS`).
  - So the ordinary IOCTL is **not** a frame-rate cost. Nobody's frame is spent there, because the app is waiting
    anyway.
- **The long reads are what matter.**
  - A single read of 1-2.6 ms is 25-75 times the vsync pulse. That is 3 lines at the staged modes (inferred, VESA):
    about 33 µs at 1280x1024@85, 35 µs at 1280x960@85, 44 µs at 1024x768@85.
  - A read that long can step over the whole pulse. The flip then completes by **deadline**, at 1.125 frames
    (`include/vcr_flip.h:22-30`).
  - Why a read takes a millisecond is **not known**. Candidates are the miniport's request serialisation in
    videoprt (inferred), a DPC, or simple preemption. B2 removes only the first. Its A/B says which.
- **The 15 deadline completions of run #14 are probably the D3D pattern, not slow reads** (inferred).
  - At 1024x768x32 a frame took most of a refresh.
  - The pulse passed while d3dprobe was still rendering, and nobody was polling.
  - `KMD/README.md` and the flip test document this pattern's 0.889 cap (`the_d3d_pattern_old_rule_0_889_new_rule_0_95`).
  - B2 cannot change that; B3 can. M1 below separates the two causes.
- **DP2 rate.** UT2003 makes 29.9 DP2 calls a frame and the runtime batches 9.6 game draws per call (G46 §1.2).
  Per-call costs are multiplied by 30, not by the triangle count.
- **Alpha test in the combines on file.**
  - **UT2003's 11-08 menu combines:** `am 0` ×14 (blend off), `45119` ×7 and `44119` ×1 (the game's own `GREATER
    0`), `45110` ×3 (SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA without a test, which B4 would cover) and `46410` ×1
    (ONE/INVSRCCOLOR, not eligible). Source: `KMD/evidence/d3dtune_1014/sweep_1108/UT2003_combos_unaligned.txt`,
    tallied for this design.
  - No in-game combine log survives for Halo or UT2004 (the ring wraps; G76).
- **Silicon:** there is no measurement of the skip's speed on this chip.

## 3. Measure first

None of these three measurements changes what is drawn.

### M1. Flip pacing counters (log-only, `include/vcr_flip.h`, default ON like the existing flip counters)

- **`sum_poll`** (`vcr_ticks`) in `vcr_flip_note_poll`. The mean read is `sum_poll / polls`. Today only the longest
  is kept.
- **Split `by_deadline`** into two counters. `by_deadline` itself stays, so the 511/12 text is byte for byte today's.
  - **`deadline_polling`:** the wait that ended at the deadline began within `deadline / 9` of the flip, i.e. within
    the first eighth of the frame under the nominal rule. A caller spun through the latch and still missed the
    pulse, which means **poll gaps**. This is B2's case.
  - **`deadline_idle`:** everything else. **The D3D pattern.** This is B3's case.
- **`by_snoop`:** flips that B3 completed. Always 0 while B3 is off.
- **Invariant:** `flips == by_retrace + by_snoop + by_deadline + superseded + pending`.
- **Logged:** one new 511 line, the next free `what` (15 at 95678704), at the four points `flip_stats_log`
  already uses (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:277-292`): `flip pacing: mean read %u us, deadline polling %u idle %u, snoop %u`.
  It contains no register access and no hardware IOCTL (the rule of `test_flip_counters_*`).

### M2. Per-call overhead (G46 `D3DStats` fine mode, bit 1)

Three more brackets, active only under fine mode:
1. **The prologue:** from entry to just before `walk()`.
2. **The epilogue:** from after `walk()` to return.
3. **The `EngSave` + `EngRestore` pair on its own.**

These apply to both DP2 and Clear2. They are summed per session and printed as µs a call and µs a frame, with the
empty-bracket cost subtracted (G46 calibrates `cpuid`/`rdtsc`).
- Take the measurement after G52.
- Or subtract G46's bracketed `VcrDd` time. The target-moved DEBUG line at `:1851-1855` fires once a frame and costs
  more than everything else in the prologue (perf-07).

### M3. What the skip is worth on this silicon, with today's driver

This uses G74/G78's `d3dprobe perf --mode fill` (G46 §4), with three options added by this item.

**The three options:**
- `--blend sa|add`: SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA, or SRCALPHA/ONE.
- `--alpha0 PCT`: a 256x256 A4R4G4B4 texture, tiled. Its left PCT % of columns have alpha 0, the rest 8/15. It is
  point-sampled, so the share of alpha-0 pixels is exact.
- `--atest off|gt0|always`:
  - `gt0` is `ALPHATESTENABLE` on, `GREATER`, ref 0. That makes `compute_regs` program `AM_ATEST | AM_AFUNC(4) |
    AM_AREF(0)`, **exactly B4's value** (`AlphaCmpCaps` is `0xff` on `.124`, `evidence/d3dplan_1112/caps_124.txt`).
  - `always` keeps the same colour arithmetic with no skip.

**The A/B:**
- Compare `gt0` against `always`, Z off and Z test on/Z write off.
- Run at `PCT` 50 and 75, at 1024x768x16, 1280x960x16 and 1024x768x32, with `--novsync`. Vsync off is for
  measurement only; the campaign itself runs with vsync on.
- **Report:** Mpix/s from the analytic pixel count.
- **Check:** with `D3DStats` on, Σ `fbiAfuncFail` per frame == W x H x layers x PCT %. That proves the skip happened
  and settles the counter's width. Read the counter at 3D offset 0x158 (SPEC L2326).

### Decision table

| measured | means | do |
|---|---|---|
| M2: prologue + epilogue ≥ 1 % of the frame time of a title running at 60-85 fps | the per-call setup matters (not expected: about 4 µs a call would be needed at 30 calls) | B1 trims in the order of §4 |
| M2 below that | sub-microsecond, as estimated | **B1 closed with the number**; nothing changes |
| M1: `deadline_polling` > 0, or a read ≥ 100 µs, in a vsync session | the poll loop has gaps longer than the pulse | B2, then repeat M1 with B2 on: if `deadline_polling` falls, the IOCTL was the gap; if not, it is preemption, and G54 is the fix |
| M1: `deadline_idle` ≥ 2 % of flips (d3dprobe perf vsync at a fill-heavy mode, or any staged title) | the D3D pattern loses refreshes | B3 |
| M3: `gt0` ≥ 5 % faster than `always` at PCT 50 in any mode | the skip saves frame-buffer bandwidth on this chip | B4 |
| M3: < 2 % everywhere | the chip is not bandwidth-bound for blended pixels at 1 px/clk | park B4; re-measure after G50 (2PPC); close it if still < 2 % |
| M3: `gt0` slower than `always` | the alpha test itself costs time | B4 is dropped for good |

## 4. B1: per-call trims (expected: none)

**The FPU bracket stays, whatever M2 says.**
- The HAL's floats run in the calling game thread's FPU context. An unsaved state corrupts the game's math, silently
  and intermittently. That is the worst kind of defect to chase here.
- In the common case the pair is cheap (inferred: XP's `KeSaveFloatingPointState` at PASSIVE_LEVEL saves only the
  control word and MXCSR, then `fninit`).
- The vendor's omission (§1.1) is not evidence that dxg saves the state for us.
- Making the save lazy (only when the walk first reaches `vcrdd_3d.c`) saves the pair only on DP2 calls that carry no
  draw. To do it, every call into an x87 object would need its own guard, and a missed guard is the corruption
  above. It is not proposed.

**If M2 crosses its threshold** (not expected), the candidate trims are, in order:
1. **`target_of` fast path.**
   - Compare `rt`, `zb`, each one's `fpVidMem`, `lPitch` and size with the last call's, and keep the last target
     when all are equal.
   - The surface is still read on every call. `test_the_render_target_is_read_at_every_call` keeps its meaning.
     Extend it to require the comparison.
2. **Initialise `dp2walk` field by field instead of `memset`.**
   - This saves about 30 ns. Do it only with a Python test listing every field that `walk()` reads before it writes
     it.
3. **Cache the last FVF's layout.** This saves about 20 ns.

## 5. B2: flip polls read `status[6]` in the DLL (`D3DTrim` bit 0, `VBDIRECT`)

**One polarity, shared.** A new pure header, `KMD/include/vcr_vblank.h`, includes `vcr_regs.h`:

```c
/* status[6] is CLEAR during vertical retrace (Glide grSstVRetraceOn) */
static __inline int vcr_vb_in_retrace(vcr_u32 status)
{
    return (status & VCR_STATUS_VRETRACE) ? 0 : 1;
}
```

Both the miniport's IOCTL handler (`vcrmp_dd.c:93`) and the DLL call this function. The inverted reading once
"waited for the END of the blank and tore every flip" (`KMD/README.md`). With one shared helper it cannot come back
in a second copy.

**`vcrdd_ddraw.c`:**

- `vblank()` is unchanged. It is still the IOCTL, and the miniport's full answer.
- New `vblank_now(pd, v, line)`:
  - When `pd->vb_direct && pd->pjRegs`, it zeroes `*v` and sets `in_vblank =
    vcr_vb_in_retrace(*(volatile ULONG *)(pd->pjRegs + VCR_R_STATUS))`.
  - With `line`, it also sets `scanline = *(volatile ULONG *)(pd->pjRegs + VCR_R_VIDCURRENTLINE) & 0x7ff`. That is
    the miniport's own two reads, done here.
  - Otherwise it calls `vblank()`.
- `flip_sample(pd, v, now, poll)`:
  - `poll` = 1 from `flip_done` uses `vblank_now`.
  - `poll` = 0 from `Dd_Flip`'s post-write sample keeps `vblank()`. That one read per flip carries `refresh_mhz`
    for `Diag\FlipDeadline`, so the achieved-rate rule keeps working.
  - Both are still timed with the clock read after the read (`vcr_flip_note_poll`).
- `Dd_WaitForVerticalBlank` and `Dd_GetScanLine` call `vblank_now`, with `line` = 1 for GetScanLine. Their answers
  are byte for byte what the IOCTL returned for the same register values.
- **The loop bound of `WaitForVerticalBlank` stays at 200,000 iterations.**
  - At about 1 µs a read that is about 0.2-0.4 s instead of about 2 s. That is still more than ten frames at any
    refresh of 50 Hz or more.
  - A box whose retrace bit never moves now returns sooner, which is the purpose of the bound.
- **No `pjRegs`** (the BOCHS/86Box VM backend, or a failed register map): the IOCTL path, as today.

**What it changes:**
- **No register writes**, and no read that the DLL does not already make thousands of times a frame.
- **About 2 µs instead of about 6 µs plus the IRP per poll** (inferred).
- **Polls no longer pass through the miniport's request path.** That removes one candidate for the 1-2.6 ms reads.
- **Half the register reads per poll:** `vidCurrentLine` is read only for GetScanLine.

**Never-early is unchanged.** The rule (`vcr_flip.h`) still gets "a sample after the write" and "the clock after the
read". Only where the read is made moves.

## 6. B3: retrace snoop (`D3DTrim` bit 1, `VBSNOOP`; optional, decided by M1)

**In `vcr_flip.h`:**

```c
/* a status read the display driver made anyway (VcrDdRoom, VcrDd2dSync) while a
 * vsync'd flip is pending: the same evidence as a poll - active seen after the
 * write, then the retrace - with no clock (the deadline stays vcr_flip_poll's) */
static __inline void vcr_flip_snoop(vcr_flip_state *s, int in_vblank)
{
    if (!s->pending)
        return;
    if (!in_vblank)
        s->seen_active = 1;
    else if (s->seen_active) {
        s->pending = 0;
        s->by_snoop++;
    }
}
```

**In `VcrDdRoom` and `VcrDd2dSync`:** right after their existing `s = rd(pd, 0)`, add
`if (pd->vb_snoop && pd->flip.pending) vcr_flip_snoop(&pd->flip, vcr_vb_in_retrace(s));`.
- No register read is added.
- With the bit off, it costs one branch on a PDEV field per status read, which is about 1 µs each.

**Why it is safe:**
- Every one of these calls runs under the PDEV's display lock, as `Dd_Flip` does.
- `pending` is set only in `vcr_flip_begin`, after the start-address write and its sample.
- So a snoop sample always comes after the write. That is the same never-early argument as a poll, resting on the
  same one silicon assumption: `status[6]` does not rise before the latch (`vcr_flip.h:22-25`).
- More samples make an early completion more likely **if** that assumption is false. That is why ddlab's fast-frame
  count is part of B3's proof (§9).

**What it buys:**
- A title rendering between 1.0 and 1.125 refreshes a frame today flips at 0.889 of the refresh (the nominal rule).
  With the snoop it flips at its own rate, 1/r, because the pulse is seen during the next frame's `VcrDdRoom`
  spins. At r = 1.05 that is 0.952.
- With heavy rendering, `VcrDdRoom` reads status every few µs, so a 33-44 µs pulse is not missed.
- With light rendering or none, the snoop simply has no samples, and today's polls and deadline apply.

**What supersedes it:** G48's FIFO credit thins the snoop's samples (a read only when credit runs out), but they stay
dense. G47 (CMDFIFO) removes them, and **G54** (hardware swap queue, `status[30:28]`) replaces the whole sampling
scheme. B3 is an interim fix for the PIO path.

## 7. B4: alpha-test skip (`D3DTrim` bit 2, `ASKIP`)

**The exactness argument.**
- Skipping a pixel leaves every buffer as it was (SPEC L2913). Blending it would have written:
  - RGB = S·f_s + D·f_d (operation ADD; SPEC §8.23.2 L2924-2938, the equation at L2928);
  - at 32 bpp, alpha = A_s·a_s + A_old·a_d (L2940-2953, the equation at L2943).
- With A_s = 0, the write is a no-op exactly when all of these hold:
  - **RGB:** f_s = 0 and f_d = 1. Among the factor codes (L2955-2972; `vcr_3dregs.h:128-136`) that means:
    - source **ZERO, SRCALPHA or SATURATE**. SATURATE is min(A_s, 1-A_d) = 0;
    - destination **ONE or INVSRCALPHA**.

    Every other code is non-zero or non-one at A_s = 0. That includes the colour factors `BF_COLOR`/`BF_INVCOLOR`:
    UT2003's `46410` is ONE/INVSRCCOLOR.
  - **Alpha channel, only where the target's alpha is defined:**
    - the source term is A_s·a_s = 0 for **any** a_s;
    - so only **a_d ∈ {ONE, INVSRCALPHA}** is needed.
  - **Depth:** no write, i.e. `FZ_ZAWRITE` clear. That also covers a 16 bpp aux alpha buffer
    (`FZ_ALPHABUFFER`), which shares that mask.
  - **Stencil:** no write. None exists today; G35 supplies the input.
  - **Blend operation:** plain ADD (`fogMode[11:8]` = 0). Reverse-subtract would also be exact; this rule does not
    need it. G91 supplies the input.
  - **The application's alpha test is off.** Its own function wins otherwise, as in Glide.
- **The test uses the same alpha as the blender.** SPEC's AlphaSrc is "the alpha value of the incoming source
  pixel", which is the blender's A_s (L2913; "AS: the new source alpha being generated", L2950). So As > 0 pixels draw exactly as before.

**Where Direct3D defines the alpha byte.**
- The flip chain's 32 bpp back buffers are X8R8G8B8: `fill_pixfmt` has no alpha mask (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:30-43`;
  G28 §1). The X byte is undefined to the application. The DAC ignores it and no D3D path may read it.
- So the alpha-channel condition applies only to a target whose pixel format has `DDPF_ALPHAPIXELS`: an A8R8G8B8
  render-target texture or offscreen target. That is G28's `VCR_BL_RT_ALPHA` flag.
- **Until G28 lands,** such a target keeps `ADST(ZERO)` and is not eligible: it gets today's register value.
- **After G28's part B,** SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA has `ADST(INVSRCALPHA)` there and becomes eligible. B4 needs no
  change for that.

**The only differences from today, both toward the Direct3D reference:**
1. **The X byte of an X8R8G8B8 target.** An As = 0 pixel used to store 0 there (`ASRC(ONE)`/`ADST(ZERO)` writes
   As). Now it keeps the old byte. No defined path reads it.
2. **Dither drift at 16 bpp with dithering on.**
   - The blender takes the 565 destination to 888 and dithers the result back. The "undither" of SPEC L3268 and
     L3318 is off in our HAL.
   - An unchanged destination can therefore move by one LSB at some matrix positions on every blend pass (inferred
     from MAME's Voodoo model: `DITHER_RB(132, 15) >> 3` = 17 for a stored 16).
   - Skipped pixels cannot drift. Direct3D says "unchanged".
   - The askip probe (§9) **measures** this instead of assuming it.

**The rule** goes in a new pure header, `KMD/include/vcr_askip.h`. It is Win32-free and integer-only:

```c
#define VCR_ASKIP_GT 4u     /* alphaMode[3:1] = 4: greater than (SPEC 8.23.1) */

static __inline int vcr_askip_rgb_ok(unsigned sf, unsigned df)
{
    return (sf == BF_ZERO || sf == BF_SRCALPHA || sf == BF_SATURATE) &&
           (df == BF_ONE || df == BF_INVSRCALPHA);
}

/* alphaMode with the test forced to GREATER 0 where a source alpha of 0 leaves
 * every defined byte as it was; otherwise alphaMode, unchanged */
static __inline unsigned vcr_askip(unsigned alphaMode, unsigned fbzMode, unsigned fogMode,
                                   int rt_alpha, int stencil_writes)
{
    unsigned sf = (alphaMode >> 8) & 0xf, df = (alphaMode >> 12) & 0xf, adf = (alphaMode >> 20) & 0xf;
    if (!(alphaMode & AM_BLEND) || (alphaMode & AM_ATEST) || (fbzMode & FZ_ZAWRITE) ||
        stencil_writes || (fogMode & 0xf00u) || !vcr_askip_rgb_ok(sf, df) ||
        (rt_alpha && adf != BF_ONE && adf != BF_INVSRCALPHA))
        return alphaMode;
    return (alphaMode & ~(AM_AFUNC(7) | AM_AREF(0xff))) | AM_ATEST | AM_AFUNC(VCR_ASKIP_GT);
}
```

**The call site** is in `compute_regs`, after `fbzMode`, `alphaMode` and `fogMode` are final and before
`log_combo(...)` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1027`), so the logged `am` shows the forced test:

```c
    if (c->pd->askip) {
        ULONG am = vcr_askip(r->alphaMode, r->fbzMode, r->fogMode, c->target.rt_alpha, 0 /* G35 */);
        c->regs_sets++;
        if (am != r->alphaMode) {
            c->askip_sets++;
            r->alphaMode = am;
        }
    }
```

**Details:**
- `rt_alpha` is a new `vcr3d_target` field set in `target_of` (`:1047-1101`): a 32 bpp target with a pixel format
  whose `dwFlags` has `DDPF_ALPHAPIXELS`. Use G28's flag instead if it has landed.
- `prepare()` may later clear the vertex-fog `fogMode` (`:1359-1360`). That never sets bits [11:8], so the
  decision holds.
- **Visibility:**
  - `ctx_free` logs one more INFO line, the next free 513 `what`, only while the bit is armed:
    `context %u: alpha-test skip on %u of %u state sets`.
  - `VcrDd2dInit` logs the armed bits once, like 513/16 and 513/18 (`vcrdd_2d.c:458-471`).
- **No caps change.** It is a single pass.
- **It is not a fix for G17** (Halo's glow squares). It cannot change what an As > 0 pixel looks like.

## 8. Gating

**The switch:** one new registry value, `HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\vcrmp\Diag\D3DTrim` (DWORD).

| bit | name | what |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (1) | `VBDIRECT` | §5 |
| 1 (2) | `VBSNOOP` | §6 |
| 2 (4) | `ASKIP` | §7 |

- **Default:** absent or 0 means all off.
- **When it is read:** at every `IOCTL_VCR_INFO`, in `fill_info`'s per-INFO block (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:447-466`),
  like `DdHeapFloor` and G46's `D3DStats`. A mode change picks it up, and `= 0` is a kill switch that needs no reboot.
- **How it reaches the DLL:** the raw DWORD is appended to `vcr_info` as `d3d_trim`. Bit 0x8000 of the flag word is
  already claimed by five sibling designs, so a separate field avoids the collision.
  - `VcrDd2dInit` zeroes `info` before the IOCTL. It reads the field only when `info.size` covers it, so an older
    miniport means off.
  - It sets `pd->vb_direct`/`pd->vb_snoop` only when `pjRegs` mapped, i.e. on the Voodoo backend. `pd->askip` is
    set on every chip: `alphaMode` is the same on Banshee, Voodoo3 and VSA-100, so the 86Box Voodoo3 bed can run it
    first.
  - If the integrator has already added one appended D3D word (G46 `d3d_perf`, G05 `d3dfeat`), these become bits
    16-18 of it instead. Pick one convention for all of them.
- **What the switch leaves alone:**
  - With `D3DTrim` absent, `alphaMode`, the IOCTL sequence, every register access and every existing log line are
    exactly today's.
  - The only addition is M1's new 511 line, which is log-only.
- **Proof rule:** one bit per supervised clean boot (§10).
- **Graduation:** a bit becomes default-on (the miniport ORs a default mask, and `D3DTrim` = 0 is then the kill
  switch, as `Accel2DPattern` did) only after:
  1. its proof boot passes;
  2. one more ordinary session runs with no regression;
  3. for ASKIP only: M3 showed a gain.
- **Not default-on at landing.** It changes register values and flip timing on paths that are proven today.

### d3dprobe test `askip` (explicit, not in the default list)

`--tests askip`. Run it at 16 bpp (R5G6B5 + D16) and 32 bpp (X8R8G8B8 + D24X8). It runs, and it passes, on today's
driver too.

**Content:**
- **Texture:** 64x64 A4R4G4B4 (offered without BigTex). Columns 0-31 have alpha 0 with a bright RGB (0xF0F); columns
  32-63 have alpha 1-15.
- **Mapping:** drawn over [32, 224) at 3 px a texel, point-sampled. The quad constants are `Q0`/`Q1`
  (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c:150-151`), inside the 256x256 readback.
- **Background:** an opaque gouraud quad (0x204060 to 0xc0a080), so that dither drift is visible.

**Four renderings per case** (BG, A, B, C), each in a fresh frame and read back:

| rendering | content |
|---|---|
| BG | the background alone |
| A | the case's state: eligible, with no alpha test |
| B | A plus `ALPHATESTENABLE`, `ALPHAFUNC ALWAYS`. B4 does not apply, so this is today's result |
| C | A plus `GREATER`, ref 0. This is B4's register value |

**Cases:**
1. SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA with dither off;
2. the same with dither on;
3. SRCALPHA/ONE, dither on;
4. ZERO/INVSRCALPHA, dither on;
5. SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA bilinear, magnified over a 0|1 alpha-texel edge, so that interpolated alphas 1-16 occur.
   This is the `GREATER 0` boundary;
6. ONE/INVSRCALPHA, which is not eligible;
7. SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA with Z test and Z write on at z = 0.3, then an opaque green quad at z = 0.6 over it.

**Pass criteria.** Tolerance **0**: the expanded readback compared exactly.

- **P1, cases 1-4:** A == B on every pixel with texel alpha > 0. The skip changes nothing that draws.
- **P2, cases 1-4:** C == BG on every alpha-0 pixel. The chip's alpha test writes nothing (SPEC L2913), on silicon.
- **P3, cases 1-5:** A == B everywhere, or A == C everywhere. A is one of the two legal renderings.
  - In case 5, also B == C wherever C ≠ BG.
- **Case 6:** A == B everywhere. Not eligible, so untouched.
- **Case 7:** the alpha-0 region == BG, i.e. Z was written and the green quad is hidden. The skip must not apply
  with Z writes on.
- **Reported:**
  - `drift_old`: B ≠ BG on alpha-0 pixels;
  - `drift_new`: A ≠ BG there;
  - `engaged`: 1 when A == C ≠ B, 0 when A == B ≠ C, otherwise "unknown (no drift)".

  On an armed box with dithering at 16 bpp, `drift_old` > 0 would prove engagement from pixels alone. The
  per-context 513 line, and G46's `fbiAfuncFail`, prove it in every case.

A Python source test on `d3dprobe.c` pins the seven cases, the three states, and the tolerance of 0, in the manner of
`test_d3dprobe_noz_and_the_depth_stencil_caps_report`.

## 11. Risk to proven paths

**The default 16 bpp path and every armed path, with `D3DTrim` absent:** none. Register traffic and IOCTLs are as
today, pinned by the Python tests. M1 adds one log-only line at session end.

**ASKIP:**
- **What could go wrong:** an admitted combination that is not neutral on silicon would make transparent-looking
  pixels vanish or change.
- **What guards against it:**
  - the rule is a proof against SPEC's semantics and a blender model;
  - premultiplied and colour-factor modes are excluded;
  - the askip probe checks it at tolerance 0;
  - the bit is default-off with a per-PDEV kill switch.
- **Glide and OpenGL are unaffected.** It writes only the D3D `alphaMode`; Glide programs its own.
- **SLI:** none. D3D is single-chip.
- **Interactions to recheck when they land:**
  - G12/G13 (`combineMode`): A_s is still the combine's final alpha, so the rule is unchanged.
  - G35 must pass `stencil_writes`.
  - G91 must keep `fogMode[11:8]` an input.
  - G28 widens the rule to alpha targets.

**VBDIRECT:**
- **It only reads.** It reads the register the DLL already polls, with the same polarity helper.
- **When nobody can call it:** DirectDraw is disabled across mode sets, and `VcrDd2dTerm` unmaps `pjRegs` only after
  `DrvDisableDirectDraw`.
- **Under Glide exclusive mode:** a read of `status` has no side effect (Glide polls it constantly).
- **The flip rule and the flip IOCTL are unchanged.**

**VBSNOOP:**
- **It completes flips sooner, never before the latch, under today's single assumption.**
- **What a broken assumption would look like:** tearing or a skipped frame. Boot C's fast-frame count and screenshots
  are there for that.
- **Superseded** by G54.

**B1:** no change is expected. If one is ever made, the `target_of` fast path is the only one with a real hazard: a
stale target after a flip. Its test is the existing re-read invariant.

## 13. Open questions

1. **Does XP's DirectX kernel bracket D3D DDI calls with an FPU save?** 3dfx's NT HAL never saves the state. Not
   worth settling: our pair costs about a microsecond a call, and removing it is unsafe either way.
2. **Is `status[6]` the vsync pulse, or the whole vertical blank, on the VSA-100?**
   - 86Box gives the pulse, about 33-44 µs at 85 Hz. A whole blank would be about 0.4 ms.
   - The answer decides how often polls can miss it, and therefore B2's and B3's value.
   - **How to answer it:** a host-launched tool loop of direct `status` reads (not during a game) that reports the
     width of the bit-6-clear window.
3. **Why do single vblank reads take 1-2.6 ms?** The candidates are videoprt's request serialisation, DPCs and
   preemption. The B2 A/B separates the first from the rest.
4. **Is the drift real?** Does the VSA-100 re-dither an unchanged 16 bpp destination (MAME's model says yes at one
   matrix position in 16)? The askip probe's `drift_old` answers it.
5. **Does an alpha-failed pixel save time at all on this chip?** It might still be read for Z, or the pipeline
   might not be memory-bound at 1 px/clk. M3 answers it, and it should be re-measured after G50.
6. **The X byte.** Is the review content with the principled rule? The X8R8G8B8 X byte is undefined, so X8R8G8B8
   targets are eligible today. The strict alternative, "no byte may differ except dither", makes every 32 bpp
   target wait for G28.
7. **Where should the bits live?** Choices: `vcr_info.d3d_trim`, G46's `d3d_perf`, or G05's `d3dfeat`. One appended
   word for all D3D switches would be cleaner; the integrator decides.
8. **B3's ownership.** Should B3 stay here, or move to G54/G59? It touches the same flip rule G59 is measuring. It
   must never be armed in a G59 measurement boot.
9. **M1's heuristic.** Is `deadline / 9` the right threshold between "polling" and "idle" deadline completions for
   the achieved-rate rule? There a frame is 32/33 of the deadline, so the cut sits at 0.115 of a frame instead of
   0.125.

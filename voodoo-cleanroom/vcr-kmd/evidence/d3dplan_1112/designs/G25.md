## 0. Summary

- **The gap is real, and it is exactly as described.**
  - `D3DRENDERSTATE_SPECULARENABLE` (29) is stored by `set_rs` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:1393-1406`) and read
    nowhere. `compute_regs` (`:921-1029`) is the only consumer of `c->rs[]`, and a case-insensitive grep for
    "specular" in `KMD/display` and `KMD/include` finds only the vertex-fog use.
  - The specular DWORD is read for its **alpha** only, as the vertex-fog factor (`KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c:202-209`;
    `vcrdd_d3d.c:1000-1018, 1358-1360`). Its RGB is never read.
  - ShadeCaps are built as `0x500a` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:2032-2033`). The D3D8 runtime reports them as `0x00004008`
    (`KMD/evidence/d3dplan_1112/caps_124.txt`). There is no `SPECULARGOURAUDRGB` (0x200).
- **The fix uses two mechanisms, picked per state set by one Win32-free planner, `KMD/include/vcr_spec.h` (new).**
  - **FOLD.** When the FBI's RGB result is the iterated colour (DISABLE, SELECTARG(DIFFUSE), the texture-less
    fallback), or another input *plus* the iterated colour at unit gain (ADD), the specular RGB is added to the
    vertex colour on the host, per channel, `min(d + s, 255)`. Alpha stays the diffuse alpha. This writes no new
    register: it changes only the four colour floats `vertex()` already sends.
  - **PASS2.** In every other case (MODULATE and any other textured op) the triangle is drawn a second time, right
    after itself. That pass draws the iterated specular RGB additively (ONE/ONE, or the app's source-only factor with
    destination ONE). It runs `FOGADD` fog and `ZFUNC EQUAL` with Z writes off whenever pass 1 wrote Z. The alpha path
    and the texture are kept only where the source alpha matters (an alpha-reading blend factor or the alpha test).
    The destination alpha is left untouched. Then pass 1's registers are restored.
  - **SKIP.** For blend modes where the specular term cannot be separated (a source factor that reads the
    destination or the source colour, a destination factor that reads the source colour), and for a colour key or
    stencil with no Z-write guard, the triangle is drawn as today and counted.
  - A triangle whose three specular RGBs are all 0 (the common "fog factor only" vertex) gets no second pass. The
    fold of 0 is the identity, so such triangles send today's register stream **byte for byte**.
- **Caps.** Armed, `dwShadeCaps |= D3DPSHADECAPS_SPECULARGOURAUDRGB`: DX7 `0x520a`, D3D8 `0x00004208`.
  `SPECULARFLATRGB` is added only together with G24's flat shading.
- **Gating.**
  - The drawing and the cap sit behind **`Diag\D3DFeat` bit 3 (0x8), default OFF**. The bit is read at boot (it
    changes caps), one Diag change per clean boot. It is honoured on every chip, because it uses only registers
    Banshee, Voodoo3 and VSA-100 share, so the 86Box Voodoo3 bed can prove it first.
  - A **shadow planner with counters** (log-only, no register write) is **default-on**. It tells, before anything
    is armed, which staged titles send lit specular and what each would get.
  - **Not safe to land default-on:** pass 2 is new per-triangle register traffic, and the cap moves titles onto new
    render paths.
- **Tests.**
  - Thirteen d3dprobe checks (`spec_*`), each against an analytic reference.
  - `tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_spec.c` asserts the fixed and the old values.
  - Python source pins.
- **Priority P2.** No staged title is *shown* to lose highlights yet. The candidates, with their evidence:
  - **Hidden & Dangerous Deluxe:** its caps dump names `D3DPSHADECAPS_SPECULARGOURAUDRGB`, and `i3d2.dll` carries
    `specular_begin`/`r_specular` lighting fragments.
  - **Max Payne:** the MAX-FX layer combiner has `SPECULAR_ADD succeeded` and "newPass: specular is the last
    operation", and validates through our always-OK `ValidateTextureStageState`.
  - **Halo:** `SPECULARENABLE`, `MATERIALSPECULAR` and a "Specular" video option. Its `config.txt` gives
    `0x121a:0x0009` only `DisableRenderTargets`, not `DisableSpecular`.

  **Escalate to P1** if the default-on counter shows a staged title sending lit specular in game.
- **Effort M.** **Dependencies:** G01 (a trustworthy silicon run), plus the shared `D3DFeat` plumbing that whichever
  of G05/G12/G16/G09/G14 lands first creates. Interactions with ten other gaps are listed in 3.10.

## 2. What Direct3D asks for, what the chip has, and what 3dfx did

### 2.1 D3D's rule (fixed-function pixel pipeline)

- The specular RGB is added **after** the texture-stage cascade and **before** fog. Per pixel:
  `C = sat(C_stages + S.rgb)`, `A = A_stages`.
- Then fog: `C' = f*C + (1-f)*C_fog`. Then the alpha test on `A`. Then the blend: `D = C'*Fs + D*Fd`.
- The specular **alpha** is the vertex-fog factor and is never added.
- Under gouraud shading both colours are interpolated, and the sum is clamped **per pixel**.
- This is inferred from the DirectX documentation. It matches 3dfx's own comments: "highlights are added into
  textures" and `FOG(T1 + T2)` (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:1286-1291, 1305-1309`).

### 2.2 The chip: one colour per triangle

- **Colour transport.** SPEC's register table lists `sARGB` (0x26c) as "FBI+TREX*", and `sRed`/`sGreen`/`sBlue`
  (0x270-0x278) with no TREX mark (FBI only). The setup unit takes one ARGB per vertex.
  - The FBI combine therefore sees one iterated colour.
  - D3DXLS row 309 reads "Specular into texture stage(s): Napalm No ... Just a second iterated color into texture
    stages" (`REP/hw.md` Q16).
  - Specular is therefore either host arithmetic or a second pass.
- **Fog unit.** SPEC's fogMode equation table, bits(2:1): fogadd = 1 gives `Cout = (1-Afog)*Cin`; fogmult = 1 gives
  `Cout = Afog*Cfog`. These are `GL5/incsrc/h3defs.h:881-883`: `SST_FOGADD` "generates (1-Afog)*Color".
  `KMD/include/vcr_3dregs.h` defines only `FM_ENABLE` and `FM_DITHER` today.
- **Depth.** fbzMode bits 7:5 are the Z function (EQ = bit 6) and bit 10 is the aux write
  (`vcr_3dregs.h:109-113`).
- **Blend.** alphaMode `[11:8]`/`[15:12]` are the RGB factors and `[19:16]`/`[23:20]` the alpha-channel factors;
  `ZERO` = 0 and `ONE` = 4 (`vcr_3dregs.h:119-136`).
- **Chroma key.** "The chroma-key color compare is performed immediately after texture mapping lookup, but before
  the color combine unit and fog" (SPEC, fbzMode bit 1). It tests the colour that the RGB select delivers. A pass
  that selects the iterated colour would therefore key the *specular* colour (see 3.10, G09).

### 2.3 3dfx's shipped NT HAL, and what this design takes from it

What 3dfx does (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C`, `IGX_SPECULAR_FIX` set in `VH5/D6GLOBAL.H:102-110`):

- **Untextured:** `CLAMP888` per vertex (`:772-800`).
- **Textured:** a second pass whenever any vertex's specular RGB is non-zero (`:1283-1293`, `CI_MASKALPHA`). In it:
  - fogMode becomes `FOGADD` (`:1309-1313`);
  - alphaMode becomes ONE/ONE, or the app's alphaMode with destination ONE (`:1319-1326`);
  - fbzMode gets `ZFUNC EQ` when Z writes are on (`:1328-1338`);
  - the FBI `combineMode` has its CC fields cleared plus `OTHERSELECT_IRGB`, and `fbzColorPath` has its CC bits
    cleared (`:1340-1366`);
  - the setup keeps S/T/W only under alpha blending (`:1370-1384`);
  - the colour is the specular RGB with the **diffuse alpha** (`:1390`);
  - then it restores (`:1601-1623`), with auto-strip off (`:1302`).
- **Win9x revision 8, "SPECULARFIX":** "non-textured specular tri's are now handled the same as textured specular
  tri's (both are 2 pass) ... when a vert has a diffuse + spec > 255, we clamp to 255 and the hardware then
  interpolates along that edge" (`W9X/D6LINE.C:30-36`, `W9X/D3TRI.C:20-26, 291-315`, `W9X/D6TRI2I.C:1288`).
- **Caps:** all four SPECULAR shade caps on triangles and lines (`VH5/D3INIT.C:2790-2812, 2994-3016`).
  `D3DTA_SPECULAR` as an argument is refused (`VH5/D6MT.C:3184, 3538-3546`).

| vendor behaviour | this design | why |
|---|---|---|
| `CLAMP888` "loses one bit of accuracy" (`VH5/D3TRI.H:74-95`): each channel masked with 0xfe, so `CLAMP888(0xff010101, 0) = 0xfe000000` | exact per-channel `min(d+s,255)`, alpha copied | no LSB loss; pinned by a test |
| fold when `pRc->texture == 0` | fold when the **FBI RGB equation** is iterated, or X + iterated, at unit gain | `SELECTARG1(TFACTOR)` and `MODULATE(DIFFUSE, TFACTOR)` are untextured but do not output the iterated colour |
| pass 2 keeps the Z write under EQUAL | Z write **off** under EQUAL | identical result, less aux traffic |
| blend off: S/T/W dropped from the setup but the texture left enabled, so an alpha test samples the TMU's leftover iterators | texture **dropped** when the source alpha cannot matter; **kept and S/T/W re-sent** when it can (blend factor or alpha test) | exact, and it relies only on documented behaviour |
| the app's alpha-channel factors kept | `ASRC ZERO`, `ADST ONE` in pass 2 | a 32 bpp target's alpha byte stays pass 1's |
| pass 2 under every blend mode | skipped (and counted) when the blend is not separable (3.4) | never worse than today |
| non-CMDFIFO build: `sSetupMode` is never restored after pass 2 | restored explicitly | we write registers; the next triangle needs pass 1's |
| `D3DTA_SPECULAR` as an argument refused | out of scope here; stays G15's job | no hardware path (2.2) |

## 3. The design

### 3.1 One planner, Win32-free: `KMD/include/vcr_spec.h` (new)

It includes `vcr_3dregs.h`, which is pure `#define`s, and nothing else. The host test compiles exactly this file.

```c
/* Direct3D specular (D3DRENDERSTATE_SPECULARENABLE) on a chip with ONE iterated
 * colour per triangle (Banshee, Voodoo3, VSA-100): fold it into the vertex colour
 * where the FBI outputs that colour at unit gain, else a second, additive pass. */
#define VCR_SPEC_NONE   0   /* nothing to add */
#define VCR_SPEC_FOLD   1   /* vertex colour = sat(diffuse + specular), alpha = diffuse's */
#define VCR_SPEC_PASS2  2   /* redraw: iterated specular, additive, after each lit triangle */
#define VCR_SPEC_SKIP   3   /* not separable here: drawn as before, counted */

#define VCR_SPEC_WHY_OFF        0   /* switch off / SPECULARENABLE off / no specular in the FVF */
#define VCR_SPEC_WHY_NOTHING    1   /* exact without a pass: Fs ZERO, ZFUNC NEVER, colour writes off */
#define VCR_SPEC_WHY_BLEND_SRC  2   /* Fs reads the destination or the source colour */
#define VCR_SPEC_WHY_BLEND_DST  3   /* Fd reads the source colour (SRCCOLOR, INVSRCCOLOR) */
#define VCR_SPEC_WHY_KEY        4   /* a colour key with no Z-write EQUAL guard (G09) */
#define VCR_SPEC_WHY_STENCIL    5   /* stencil on with no Z-write EQUAL guard (G35) */

/* d3dtypes.h D3DBLEND_*, named here so the header stays Win32-free (a Python
 * test pins the values against the SDK header) */
#define VCR_D3DBLEND_ZERO 1 /* ... ONE 2, SRCCOLOR 3, INVSRCCOLOR 4, SRCALPHA 5, INVSRCALPHA 6,
                               DESTALPHA 7, INVDESTALPHA 8, DESTCOLOR 9, INVDESTCOLOR 10,
                               SRCALPHASAT 11, BOTHSRCALPHA 12, BOTHINVSRCALPHA 13 */

typedef struct vcr_spec_in {
    unsigned armed;              /* Diag\D3DFeat bit 3 (1 for the shadow plan) */
    unsigned enable, has_spec;   /* SPECULARENABLE != 0; the FVF has D3DFVF_SPECULAR */
    unsigned cp, fz, am, fm, sm; /* pass 1 as SENT: fbzColorPath, fbzMode, alphaMode, fogMode, sSetupMode */
    unsigned cm_fbi;             /* the FBI combineMode pass 1 runs with (0: the legacy combine) */
    unsigned blend, src, dst;    /* ALPHABLENDENABLE, SRCBLEND, DESTBLEND (D3D values) */
    unsigned atest, stencil;     /* ALPHATESTENABLE; stencil test or ops live (G35; 0 today) */
} vcr_spec_in;

typedef struct vcr_spec_out {
    unsigned mode, why, keep_tex;
    unsigned cp, fz, am, fm, sm, cm_fbi, cm_live;   /* pass 2's words */
} vcr_spec_out;

#define VCR_SPEC_SHADECAPS  0x00000200u     /* D3DPSHADECAPS_SPECULARGOURAUDRGB */

static __inline unsigned vcr_spec_fold(unsigned diffuse, unsigned specular);       /* per channel min(d+s,255) */
static __inline unsigned vcr_spec_pass2_color(unsigned diffuse, unsigned specular) /* s.rgb | d.alpha */;
static __inline int      vcr_spec_lit(unsigned sa, unsigned sb, unsigned sc);      /* ((sa|sb|sc) & 0xffffff) != 0 */
static __inline int      vcr_spec_foldable(unsigned cp, unsigned cm_fbi);
static __inline void     vcr_spec_plan(const vcr_spec_in *in, vcr_spec_out *o);
```

New constants in `vcr_3dregs.h`:

- `FM_FOGADD (1u << 1)` and `FM_FOGMULT (1u << 2)`.
- The masks `CP_RGB_FIELDS` = `0x3 | 0x10 | 0x80 | 0x1ff00` (RGB select, local select, the ATEX override, CC bits
  8-16) and `CP_ALPHA_FIELDS` = `0xc | 0x60 | 0x3fe0000` (alpha select, alpha-local select, CCA bits 17-25).
- `CM_USE (1u << 31)`, `CM_2PPC (1u << 29)` and `CM_CC_FIELDS 0xffffu`. These come from G05 if it lands first.

### 3.2 The decision (`vcr_spec_plan`), in order

| # | condition | mode / why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | not `armed`, not `enable`, or no specular in the FVF | NONE / OFF (**today's behaviour**) |
| 2 | `!(fz & FZ_RGBWRITE)`, or depth on with ZFUNC = NEVER (bits 7:5 = 0) | NONE / NOTHING (pass 1 drew nothing to add to) |
| 3 | `vcr_spec_foldable(cp, cm_fbi)` (below) | FOLD |
| 4 | blend on, Fs = ZERO (after BOTH* expansion) | NONE / NOTHING (D3D adds `(T+S)*0`) |
| 5 | blend on, Fs not in {ONE, SRCALPHA, INVSRCALPHA, BOTHSRCALPHA, BOTHINVSRCALPHA} | SKIP / BLEND_SRC |
| 6 | blend on, Fd in {SRCCOLOR, INVSRCCOLOR} (and Fs not BOTH*) | SKIP / BLEND_DST |
| 7 | `fz & FZ_CHROMAKEY` without the guard (depth on **and** Z write on) | SKIP / KEY |
| 8 | `stencil` without the guard | SKIP / STENCIL |
| 9 | otherwise | PASS2 with the words of 3.3 |

**`vcr_spec_foldable(cp, cm)`** is true only with the legacy FBI combine (`!(cm & CM_USE)`) and the colour local
iterated (`!(cp & CP_LOCAL_C0)`), and then either:

- **(a)** `RGBSEL_ITER` and CC bits 8-16 all 0. The output is the iterated colour.
- **(b)** `RGBSEL` = TMU or C1 and the CC bits exactly `CP_CC_ADD_CLOCAL`. The output is other + iterated.

D3D's ADD stage clamps first, `sat(sat(T+D)+S)`. That equals the hardware's `sat(T + (D+S))` whenever no vertex
saturates `D+S` (inferred arithmetic). An ADD with *other* = iterated (`DIFFUSE + DIFFUSE`) is excluded, because
it would double the specular. With G12's extended combine live this returns false (PASS2, always correct) until
G12's compiler exports a "unit-gain iterated" flag.

`color_path` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:676-745`) gives these words:

| D3D stage 0 | `fbzColorPath` RGB part | mode |
|---|---|---|
| DISABLE, SELECTARG(DIFFUSE/CURRENT), any op on TEXTURE with no texture bound | `RGBSEL_ITER`, CC 0 | FOLD |
| ADD(TEXTURE or TFACTOR, DIFFUSE) | `RGBSEL_TMU`/`C1` + `CC_ADD_CLOCAL` | FOLD |
| SELECTARG(TEXTURE), SELECTARG(TFACTOR) | `RGBSEL_TMU`/`C1`, CC 0 | PASS2 |
| MODULATE (incl. 2X/4X today), BLENDTEXTUREALPHA, BLENDDIFFUSEALPHA, all the rest | multiply or lerp | PASS2 |
| any two-stage set-up (the FBI applies stage 0's op with diffuse, `:977`) | as above | per the FBI word |

### 3.3 The pass-2 words

`guarded` means depth on and Z write on (`FZ_DEPTH && FZ_ZAWRITE`).

`alpha_matters` = alpha test on, **or** blend on with Fs in {SRCALPHA, INVSRCALPHA, BOTH*}.

`keep_tex` = `alpha_matters && (cp & CP_TEXTURE)`, **or** 2 ppc live (`cm_fbi & CM_2PPC`, G50) with
`cp & CP_TEXTURE`. 3dfx kept texturing enabled in every pass 2 (`VH5/D6TRI2I.C:1350`). Under 2 ppc this design does
the same and re-sends S/T/W, because "2 pixel-per-clock rendering is only allowed to be enabled when a single
texture is being applied per triangle" (SPEC combineMode).

| register | pass 1 | pass 2 | restored |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fogMode` | `FM_ENABLE|FM_DITHER` or 0 | `(fm & ~FM_FOGMULT) | FM_FOGADD` when `FM_ENABLE`; else unchanged and not written | yes, if written |
| `alphaMode` | the app's | blend off: `AM_BLEND | RGBSRC(ONE) | RGBDST(ONE) | ASRC(ZERO) | ADST(ONE)`, plus the ATEST/AFUNC/AREF bits when the alpha test is on. Blend on: `am` with RGBDST := ONE, ASRC := ZERO, ADST := ONE; RGBSRC and the alpha test kept | yes |
| `fbzMode` | the app's | `guarded`: `(fz & ~(FZ_ZFUNC(7) | FZ_ZAWRITE | FZ_CHROMAKEY)) | FZ_ZFUNC(2 = EQ)`. Otherwise unchanged (depth off, or Z write off: the stored Z did not move, so the same pixels pass) | yes |
| FBI `combineMode` (FBI-only chip field, G05's offset) | only when G05/G12 have it live | `cm_fbi & ~CM_CC_FIELDS`: other = IRGB, unit factor, 1x shift; 2 ppc, chroma-substitution and CCA fields kept. This is 3dfx's `NEW_CCU` word, `VH5/D6TRI2I.C:1342-1348` | yes |
| `fbzColorPath` | `color_path | PARMADJUST` | `cp & ~CP_RGB_FIELDS` (RGB = iterated, passed). If `!alpha_matters` also `& ~CP_ALPHA_FIELDS` (alpha = iterated diffuse alpha, passed); if `!keep_tex`, `& ~CP_TEXTURE`. `PARMADJUST` always kept | yes |
| `sSetupMode` | `SM_RGB|SM_A|SM_Z|SM_WFBI` (+ ST/W per TMU, cull bits) | `keep_tex` ? unchanged : `& ~(SM_W0|SM_ST0|SM_W1|SM_ST1)`; cull bits kept | yes |
| vertex colour | diffuse (folded or not) | `vcr_spec_pass2_color(diffuse, specular)`: specular RGB, **diffuse alpha** (pass 1's source alpha) | n/a |
| x, y, z, Wfbi | | identical floats (same code path): the iterated Z equals pass 1's, so EQUAL holds; the fog W (table or vertex ramp) is the same | n/a |

**Worked example: UT2003's logged stage 0.**

- The input is `MODULATE(TEXTURE, DIFFUSE)`, `cp 0x0c482405` (`KMD/evidence/d3dtune_1014/sweep_1108/UT2003_combos_unaligned.txt`).
- Z is LESSEQUAL with writes (`fz 0x671`), there is no blend or fog, and the setup with cull CCW is `sm 0x6003f`.
- Pass 2: `cp 0x04000000`, `fz 0x251`, `am 0x00404410`, `fm 0`, `sm 0x6000f`, `keep_tex 0`.
- Under SRCALPHA/INVSRCALPHA (`am 0x45110`): `am 0x00404110`, `cp 0x0c480004` (texture alpha kept),
  `sm 0x6003f`, `keep_tex 1`.

### 3.4 Why pass 2 is exact where it runs, and why the SKIP cases are skipped

- **Opaque.** Pass 1 writes `f*T + (1-f)*Cf`; pass 2 adds `(1-f)*S`. The sum is `f*Cf + (1-f)*(T+S)`, which is D3D's
  `fog(T+S)`. It differs only where `T+S > 1`: D3D clamps before fog and the blender clamps after it (inferred,
  small).
- **Blend, Fs source-only.** Pass 1 gives `fog(T)*Fs + D*Fd`; pass 2 adds `(1-f)*S*Fs` with the same Fs, because the
  alpha path and texture are kept, so the alpha is pass 1's. The total is `fog(T+S)*Fs + D*Fd`, exact.
- **Fs reads the destination** (DESTCOLOR, INVDESTCOLOR, DESTALPHA, INVDESTALPHA, SRCALPHASAT). Pass 2 would read
  pass 1's result instead of D. Not exact: SKIP.
- **Fs = SRCCOLOR** (a ONE stand-in today, `blend_src :643`; `SAMECOLOR` under G29). `(T+S)^2 != T^2 + S^2`: SKIP.
- **Fd = SRCCOLOR / INVSRCCOLOR.** D3D's Fd uses `T+S`, while pass 1 used `T`. No additive term repairs that: SKIP.
- **Coverage.**
  - Z write on: EQUAL passes only where pass 1 wrote Z. Pixels that pass 1 lost to the depth test, the alpha test or
    a key do not get specular, except a coincidentally equal older depth (a coplanar surface, inferred rare).
  - Z write off: the stored depth did not change, so the same function passes the same pixels.
  - Alpha test with `keep_tex`: the same alpha fails the same pixels.
  - The setup unit culls pass 2 exactly as it culled pass 1 (same vertices, same cull bits).
- **Z determinism.** EQUAL needs pass 2's iterated Z to be bit-identical to pass 1's, while `sSetupMode` differs in
  the ST/W bits. 3dfx relied on this on this silicon (inferred from `:1328-1338`). `spec_tex_z` (section 5) proves it
  on ours.

### 3.5 The vertex writer (`KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c`, the one FPU file)

- **One writer, `vertex_at(pd, d, v, x, y, argb, tmus)`.** It is today's `vertex()` body with the colour and "send
  this pass's S/T/W" as arguments.
  - This extends G16's `vertex_at(pd, d, v, x, y)`; whichever item lands first creates it.
  - It is declared before `vertex()` and **defined between `vertex()` and `VcrDd3dTriangle`**, so the existing pins
    stay valid. `test_vertex_colour_goes_to_the_setup_unit_as_floats_not_packed_sargb` slices
    `static BOOL vertex(`..`BOOL VcrDd3dTriangle(`, and `test_vertices_get_the_half_pixel_centre_offset` reads
    `p[0] + d->xy_bias` in `vertex()`.
  - The write order is exactly today's: x, y, R, G, B, A, z, Wfbi or the fog ramp, then S/T/W per TMU when
    `tmus && d->textured(1)`.
- **`vertex()`:** `argb = diffuse or white; if (d->spec_fold) argb = vcr_spec_fold(argb, SPEC(v)); return
  vertex_at(pd, d, v, p[0] + d->xy_bias, p[1] + d->xy_bias, argb, 1);`. The fold is integer arithmetic in the FPU
  file, with no new register write.
- **`BOOL VcrDd3dSpecBegin(pd, s)`:** one `VcrDdRoom(6)`, then `fogMode` (only if it changes), `alphaMode`,
  `fbzMode`, FBI `combineMode` (only if `cm_live`), `fbzColorPath` and `sSetupMode`.
- **`BOOL VcrDd3dSpecEnd(pd, s, r)`:** one `VcrDdRoom(6)`, then the same registers back from `r` (the `vcr3d_regs`
  pass 1 was sent with, after `prepare()`'s edits). `g2d_busy = 1`.
- **`BOOL VcrDd3dSpecTriangle(pd, d, s, r, a, b, c)`:** Begin; then for each vertex
  `vertex_at(..., vcr_spec_pass2_color(DIFF(v), SPEC(v)), s->keep_tex)`, followed by `sBeginTriCMD` after the first
  and `sDrawTriCMD` after the others; then End. G16's quads use Begin, their own `vertex_at` loop and End.
- **Hooks for later items:**
  - **G24 (flat):** the caller passes the provoking vertex's diffuse and specular as `argb`.
  - **G58 (packed `sARGB`):** `vertex_at` is the one place the colour transport changes. Under packed colour the
    TMUs see `spec.rgb | diff.alpha` in pass 2; the FBI ignores the TMU RGB there, and any TMU alpha that uses
    iterated alpha sees pass 1's alpha.
- **Write cost** (perf.md's 5-8 us per textured triangle, inferred):
  - Begin + End = 10-12 writes, plus 3 x 8 vertex writes (3 x 11 or 14 with `keep_tex`), plus 3 commands. That is
    37-57 writes, about one more textured triangle per lit triangle.

### 3.6 The walker (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c`, integer-only)

- **`prepare()`.** At its end, after the texture-coordinate and vertex-fog edits (`:1336-1360`) and before
  `VcrDd3dTarget`/`VcrDd3dState` (`:1361`):
  - fill a `vcr_spec_in` from `c->regs` as it will be **sent**, `c->rs[]` and `w->d.spec_off`;
  - run `vcr_spec_plan` with `armed = 1` into `w->spec_shadow`. The effective plan is `w->spec = pd->d3d_spec ?
    w->spec_shadow : NONE`;
  - set `w->d.spec_fold = (w->spec.mode == FOLD)` and `w->d.spec_pass2 = (w->spec.mode == PASS2)`.

  The plan is recomputed exactly when the registers are (each DP2's first draw, and every dirty state), so it never
  goes stale.
- **`tri()` and `imm_tri()`** go through one helper `draw3(w, a, b, c)`:
  - `VcrDd3dTriangle` as today;
  - then, if the triangle is lit (`spec_off && rs[29] && vcr_spec_lit(SPEC(a), SPEC(b), SPEC(c))`): count it under
    `w->spec_shadow.mode`/`why`;
  - if `w->d.spec_pass2`, `VcrDd3dSpecTriangle`. If that returns FALSE, set `w->prepared = 0`, so a half-restored
    state is re-sent before the next triangle.
- **Per-context counters** in `vcr_d3dctx`: `spec_lit`, `spec_fold`, `spec_pass2`, `spec_skip[6]` and
  `spec_seen_fvf`. There is no logging per triangle (G52).
  - **First time in a context** that SPECULARENABLE is on with a specular FVF: one INFO event (513, next free
    sub-code), "ctx %u specular on, FVF %x: %s (D3DFeat bit 3 %s)". `%s` is fold, second pass or skip:why.
  - **At `ctx_free`, when `spec_lit != 0`:** one INFO, "ctx %u specular: %u lit, %u fold, %u pass2, %u skip (bl %u/%u
    key %u st %u)". This fits the 95-character ring message (`VCR_LOG_MSG_LEN`), and being logged last it survives a
    wrapped ring (G76).
- **Counting runs with the switch off** (the shadow plan). That makes it the measurement Phase 0 asks for
  (`REP/hal.md` §10), at a cost of three loads and an OR per triangle, and only while SPECULARENABLE is on with a
  specular FVF.

### 3.7 Caps

- `prim_caps()` (`:2013-2046`) gains a `pd` (or flag) argument: `dwShadeCaps |= pd->d3d_spec ? VCR_SPEC_SHADECAPS :
  0`. That is `0x500a -> 0x520a` on both the triangle and line caps.
- D3D8 then reports `0x00004208`, because the runtime keeps `COLORGOURAUDRGB`, `SPECULARGOURAUDRGB` and
  `ALPHAGOURAUDBLEND` of these.
- `SPECULARFLATRGB` (0x80) is added only when G24's flat shading is armed too: flat specular uses the provoking
  vertex's specular, which only G24 provides.
- The line caps claim specular for lines. Lines are not drawn at all until G16 is armed, an over-promise that exists
  today; with G16 they get the fold and pass 2 through the same functions.

### 3.9 The old path, with the bit off

- `pd->d3d_spec = 0`, so `w->spec` is NONE: `spec_fold = spec_pass2 = 0`.
- `vertex()` passes `argb` unchanged to `vertex_at` with `tmus = 1`, so the writes are the same values in the same
  order.
- `draw3()` does today's draw. The specular branch only reads vertex memory and bumps counters.
- `prim_caps` produces `0x500a`. No register, no cap and no DDI answer changes.
- **Armed, but SPECULARENABLE off or no specular in the FVF:** byte-identical apart from the cap.
- **Armed with lit triangles whose specular RGBs are all 0** (fog-only TLVERTEX data, d3dprobe's `fogvertex`):
  byte-identical as well, because the fold of 0 is the identity and no pass 2 runs.

### 3.10 Interactions with other gaps (rules this item writes down for them)

| gap | rule |
|---|---|
| G05/G12 (`combineMode`) | pass 2 rewrites only the FBI's CC fields and restores the live word. G05's "back to 0 at the end of every DP2" still holds. FOLD is disabled while the FBI extended combine is live, unless G12 exports "unit-gain iterated" |
| G09 (texture colour key, FBI chroma) | the chroma test sees the RGB select (2.2), so pass 2 clears `FZ_CHROMAKEY` and relies on the Z-write EQUAL guard; without the guard it skips (`WHY_KEY`) |
| G35 (stencil) | pass 2 must not run stencil ops twice (INCR would double) and its test would see pass 1's update. With stencil live: stencil off in pass 2 under the EQUAL guard, else skip (`WHY_STENCIL`). G35 adds its words to the planner |
| G24 (flat) | the provoking vertex's diffuse **and** specular feed `vertex_at`. `SPECULARFLATRGB` only with both bits |
| G16 (lines/points) | the fold is automatic. Pass 2 is `VcrDd3dSpecBegin` + the quad + `VcrDd3dSpecEnd` |
| G49 (strips/fans) | a strip/fan emitter must break the strip at a lit triangle, or send that primitive as independent triangles (3dfx: `FORCE_SINGLE`, `:1302`) |
| G50 (2 ppc) | `keep_tex` while 2 ppc is live (3.3); no 12-NOP exit, because the 2 ppc bit is never cleared |
| G58 (packed colour) | one transport in `vertex_at` (3.5) |
| G15 (validation) | `D3DTA_SPECULAR` as a stage argument stays unsupported; G15 should refuse it, as 3dfx did, so MAX-FX's `SPECULAR_COPY` falls back honestly |
| G32 (vertex fog source) | pass 2 sends the same Wfbi/Z, so any future fog source (W, Z or iterated alpha) is the same in both passes. If G32 ever moves the fog factor into the **iterated alpha**, the pass-2 colour alpha must carry the fog factor, not the diffuse alpha: a planner input |
| G47 (CMDFIFO) | Begin/End become packet-1 writes and the vertices a packet 3, as in 3dfx's code. No logic change |
| G22 (Z bias) | `FZ_ZBIAS` and `zaColor` are untouched in pass 2, so the bias is identical and EQUAL holds |

### 3.11 Alternatives considered, not taken now

1. **Untextured two-pass, as Win9x SPECULARFIX did.** It is exact where a vertex saturates, at twice the cost.
   - Kept as a one-line planner change (route FOLD to PASS2) if a title shows clamped highlights.
   - `spec_untex_sat` documents the difference in the meantime.
2. **Single pass through a TMU colour iterator** (`REP/hw.md` Q16, silicon check S3): diffuse in TMU0 via a
   chip-field `sARGB` and specular in the FBI. This needs G12 bits 7/8, G58 and an unproven silicon behaviour.
   It is worth an experiment after them, because it would halve the cost for single-texture lit geometry.
3. **Batching pass 2 per primitive** (all pass 1s, one state switch, all pass 2s). It is valid only for opaque,
   Z-write geometry: coplanar overlaps inside one batch would gain a neighbour's specular. A performance follow-up
   after G47/G49, measured.
4. **3dfx's leftover-iterator trick** (texture enabled, S/T/W not re-sent, the TMU reusing pass 1's gradients). It
   saves 3-6 writes per vertex when alpha matters, but rests on undocumented behaviour. Not adopted; open question.
5. **A host-side back-face test before pass 2.** It saves the writes for culled triangles, but a sign convention that
   disagreed with `SM_CULL_NEGATIVE` would silently drop highlights. Follow-up only, with a probe.

## 4. Gating

- **Drawing and caps:** `Diag\D3DFeat` bit 3 (0x8), default **OFF**, read by the miniport **at boot**. The caps a
  title reads at its desktop must be its fullscreen PDEV's, as for `D3DBigTex`. Positive semantics: absent means
  off.
- **Chips:** every chip. This allows a proof on the 86Box Voodoo3 bed before silicon.
- **Counters and shadow plan:** default-on, log-only. Pinned by a Python test that no register write depends on
  them.
- **One Diag change per clean boot.** Arm with `REGWRITE HKLM SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\vcrmp\Diag D3DFeat
  REG_DWORD 8` (OR'd with any other `D3DFeat` bits already proven), `REGREAD` it back, activation check, then
  `scripts/fleet/safe-reboot.py`.
- **Not default-on at landing.** Pass 2 is new per-triangle traffic (state flips between triangles, Z EQUAL), and the
  cap moves Halo, H&D and Max Payne onto paths never run on this HAL.
- **Promotion to default-on** follows the convention the `D3DFeat` owner picks (a default mask OR'd in by the
  miniport, or a `D3DFeatOff` kill mask; G05 open question). It requires:
  - the d3dprobe `spec_*` matrix passing on silicon at 16 and 32 bpp;
  - the default matrix unchanged;
  - one supervised session of every title whose counter showed lit specular, with A/B frames clean.

## 5. d3dprobe tests (`KMD/tools/d3dprobe.c`)

**Common.** A new vertex type with diffuse, specular and one or two texture sets (`FVF_ST`, `FVF_ST2`), and
`SetRenderState(D3DRS_SPECULARENABLE, TRUE)` inside each test, reset after it.

**Skips.**

- Every test except the controls is **SKIPPED** when `!(caps.ShadeCaps & D3DPSHADECAPS_SPECULARGOURAUDRGB)`, as the
  texture tests skip unoffered formats.
- A new `--force` runs them anyway, to record the gap on the old build.
- `spec_tex_z` also skips under `--noz`.

**Pass rule.** Each `expect()` is checked per channel against the analytic value below.

- Tolerance is 12-20 at 16 bpp: two passes means two 565 roundings. Fog half-tones use 40, like `fogtable`.
- Each reference sits more than twice the tolerance away from the old-HAL value and from the likely wrong
  implementations: a double add, a lost alpha, the fog colour added twice, a lost alpha test, a broken EQUAL.

## 6. Host regression tests

### 6.1 `WT/tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_spec.c` (new, true-source, munit)

It compiles `KMD/include/vcr_spec.h`. `tests/run_all.sh` globs `test_*.c`, so no registration is needed. Each case
asserts both the fixed and the old value.

1. **`fold_adds_rgb_keeps_alpha`:** `vcr_spec_fold(0xff402010, 0x00204080) == 0xff606090`. The old HAL's colour was
   the diffuse, `0xff402010`, and the test asserts the two differ.
2. **`fold_saturates_per_channel_alpha_from_diffuse`:** `(0x80c0c0c0, 0x7f808080) -> 0x80ffffff`. The specular alpha
   `0x7f` (a fog factor) never reaches the colour.
3. **`fold_keeps_the_lsb`:** `(0xff010101, 0) -> 0xff010101`. The test reimplements 3dfx's `CLAMP888` as the
   reference *not* followed and asserts it gives `0xfe000000`.
4. **`zero_specular_is_the_identity`:** for 64 diffuse values, `vcr_spec_fold(d, a << 24) == d`. Also
   `vcr_spec_lit(0xff000000, 0x80000000, 0) == 0` and `vcr_spec_lit(0, 1, 0) == 1`. This is the byte-identical
   property behind 3.9.
5. **`switch_off_is_today`:** a grid of `cp`/`fz`/`am`/`fm`/blend/atest inputs with `armed = 0` gives mode NONE every
   time.
6. **`state_off_or_no_specular`:** `armed = 1` with `enable = 0`, or with `has_spec = 0`, gives NONE.

   - FOLD: `0x04000000` (diffuse, PARMADJUST); ADD(TMU, iter) `0x0c004001`; ADD(C1, iter) `0x04004002`.
   - PASS2: MODULATE `0x0c482405`; SELECTARG1(TFACTOR) `0x04000002`; ADD(iter, iter) `0x04004000`; `CP_LOCAL_C0`;
     `cm_fbi = CM_USE`.
8. **`pass2_words_opaque_zwrite`:** the UT2003 worked example of 3.3, word for word (`cp 0x04000000`, `fz 0x251`,
   `am 0x00404410`, `sm 0x6000f`, `keep_tex 0`). The old HAL drew no second pass (mode NONE).
9. **`pass2_blend_keeps_source_factor_and_texture_alpha`:** `am 0x45110 -> 0x00404110`, `cp 0x0c480004`,
   `sm 0x6003f`, `keep_tex 1`.
10. **`pass2_alpha_test_keeps_the_test`:** ATEST GREATER `0x80` with no blend gives the test bits plus
    `0x00404410`, and `keep_tex 1`.
11. **`pass2_fog_is_fogadd`:** `fm 0x41 -> 0x43`, and an input with `FOGMULT` set has it cleared.

14. **`chroma_with_guard`:** chroma, depth and Z write give PASS2 with `FZ_CHROMAKEY` cleared and EQUAL.
15. **`combine_mode_live`:** `cm 0x80004000` (USE + `CC_OUTSHIFT_2X`, G05's MODULATE2X) gives `cm 0x80000000`,

16. **`shadecaps`:** `0x500a | VCR_SPEC_SHADECAPS == 0x520a`, and the D3D8 view `0x4008 -> 0x4208`. The old value is

## 8. Risk to proven paths

- **The default 16 bpp path, bit off: none.** The register stream, the caps and the DDI answers are byte for byte
  today's (3.9). The only new code on the drawing path is integer counting.
- **Bit on, unlit triangles: none,** for the same reason (zero-RGB identity, no pass 2).
- **Bit on, lit triangles:**
  - **State flips between triangles.** fogMode, alphaMode, fbzMode, fbzColorPath, sSetupMode (and the FBI
    combineMode under G05/G12) change before and after each pass 2. The chip consumes register writes in order; 3dfx
    did exactly this per triangle on this silicon (3.4).
  - **EQUAL depends on identical iterated Z.** `spec_tex_z` proves it before any game runs.
  - **Throughput.** About 2x the PIO writes per lit triangle (3.5). A specular-heavy scene can lose up to half its
    triangle rate until G47/G49. The counters and `perf --spec` make that visible.
- **The cap change** puts Halo, Max Payne and H&D onto specular paths never run on this HAL. That is the intent, and
  the reason for default-off and the A/B frames. Max Payne stays attended-only (G04).
- **Glide/OpenGL coexistence: none new.** Pass 2 writes only registers `VcrDd3dState` re-sends at every DP2's first
  draw (`w.prepared = 0`). A failed restore forces a re-send. No state outlives a DP2 call, and `exclusive_pid`
  gating is unchanged. The existing G01/G03 hazards (a stale command FIFO, Glide-left `combineMode`) hit pass 2 as
  they hit pass 1, no more.
- **SLI: none.** D3D stays chip 0, and every write is broadcast as today.
- **Flip path: none.**
- **86Box:** the same legacy registers, so the bed proves the logic before silicon.

## 10. Open questions

1. **Does any staged title send SPECULARENABLE with non-zero specular RGB in game,** and how many triangles a frame?
   Boot A's shadow counters answer it, and set both the priority and the performance stakes.
2. **The DX5-DX7 runtimes' SPECULARENABLE default.** What does `d3dim`/`d3dim700` send a DX7-DDI driver at context
   creation, and what do Jedi Knight/MotS put in `D3DTLVERTEX.specular`? A title that relies on a TRUE default with
   garbage RGB would change look when armed. The JK/MotS A/B guards it; a DX7 probe (G75) can settle it.
3. **Is the iterated Z bit-identical** between passes whose `sSetupMode` differs only in ST/W? `spec_tex_z` answers
   it. If not, Z-write geometry needs `keep_tex` everywhere, or a Z-bias-free LESSEQUAL with Z write off in pass 2.
4. **Fold or two-pass for untextured specular** (3.11.1)? The fold is the vendor's NT choice; Win9x moved to two
   passes for WHQL exactness. It is decided by evidence of clamped highlights in a title.
5. **3dfx's leftover-iterator trick** (3.11.4): does the TMU really reuse pass 1's S/T/W gradients when the setup
   omits them? It would cut 3-6 writes per vertex for alpha-tested and blended lit geometry. A bed-then-silicon probe
   variant of `spec_tex_blend_ta`.
6. **Single-pass specular through a TMU iterator** (hw S3) after G12/G58: is a chip-field `sARGB` to TMU0 kept apart
   from the FBI's colour?
7. **The `D3DFeat` plumbing convention and bit numbers.** An appended `vcr_info.d3dfeat` or flags; bit 3 here per
   `REP/hal.md` §10 Phase 2 item 4, which also names flat shading. G24 needs its own bit, or shares this one with a
   documented split. The default-on promotion convention.
8. **513 sub-code allocation** (24/25 are proposed by G05, G10, G12 and G16).
9. **Do the SKIP blend cases occur in staged titles?** (`skip bl %u/%u` in the totals.) If one does in a common scene,
   the next step is the source-colour cases via G29's `SAMECOLOR` (`(T+S)` as a factor), which need a design of their
   own.

## 1. Verdict

**The gap is real. The white has one dominant cause, now identified from the game data, and the driver can fix it.**

DM-Antalus is the map behind both staged UE2 test scenes: the UT2003 flyby and the UT2004 botmatch/statfps runs. It draws its sky in a six-face BSP sky box, brush `Polys29`. The brush's face list is identical in the staged UT2003 and UT2004 maps; the UT2003 level model differs only on the floor (below):

| face (brush space 1024x1024x512) | material | size, format | our HAL today |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 side walls (-X, +Y, -Y) | `AntalusTextures.sky.antalusB0000` | **8x512 DXT5**, 10 levels, U and V clamped. A vertical gradient from (198,128,93) at the top to (231,219,206) at the bottom | **refused: 64:1 aspect** |
| 1 side wall (+X) | `antalusB0001` | 1024x512 DXT5, 11 levels: a planet over the same gradient (its edge columns equal B0000) | sampled |
| ceiling | `antalusB0321` | 8x8 DXT5, solid (198,128,93) | sampled |
| floor | brush `Polys29`: `cp_wasteland.sky.cp_white1`. The UT2003 level model (`Polys26`) retextured this face to `SkyRenders.ElecFields.Cloudcombine`, a Combiner (`CombineOperation` 5, CO_AlphaBlend_With_Mask by enum order, inferred) of two panning cloud layers. The UT2004 map no longer imports `Cloudcombine`, so its floor is `cp_white1` (inferred from the imports) | `cp_white1`: 8x8 RGBA8, **(255,255,255) with alpha 0: white by design**. `Cloudcombine`: two 1024x1024 DXT5 | sampled |
| cloud dome over all of it | `sky2dome` (Shader `sky2.cloudsky2`: SelfIllumination = `cloud03panner`, Opacity = `cloud03maskpanner`, `OutputBlending` = 2, OB_Modulate by UE2's enum order, inferred) | 512x512 DXT5 pair | sampled |

**The failure chain.**
1. The VSA-100 TMU samples at most 8:1 (tLOD aspect field 0..3).
2. `mipchain_ext` leaves the 8x512 texture to DirectDraw (`KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:416-417`).
3. At draw time, `ext_layout` → `vcr_texlod_compute_ext` refuses it (`KMD/common/vcr_texlod.c:105`: log2(512/8) = 6 > 3).
4. `compute_regs` logs 513/7 once and the draw goes **untextured** (`vcrdd_d3d.c:932-944`; `color_path` `:690`).
5. An untextured vertex with no diffuse in its FVF is 0xFFFFFFFF (`KMD/display/vcrdd_3d.c:187`).

So three of the four walls are drawn solid (255,255,255). The cloud dome's modulate over white stays saturated.

**What ties the frames to this mechanism:**
- **The white is pure (255,255,255).** That is saturated, not a fog or texture colour. Measured: UT2003 flyby 30 s 64.2 % of the frame, 120 s 3.2 %; UT2004 statfps 100 s 36.8 %, 80 s 0.9 %; UT2004 botmatch 65 s 10.4 % (§2.1).
- **UT2004 statfps 100 s.** The orange strip at the top, (255,190,123), is the **ceiling**: B0321 under the cloud dome. Its straight, sloped lower edge is the ceiling/wall edge of the sky box. Everything below it, down to the terrain silhouette, is wall, and it is white. The 8x8 ceiling is drawn; the 8x512 walls are not.
- **UT2004 botmatch 65 s.** The flat area is (198,125/130,90/99). That is the 565 dither of exactly (198,128,93): B0321's colour, which is also B0000's top row. So the sky box *is* drawn with these textures, and the white sits where the B0000 faces are.
- **Fog cannot produce it.**
  - DM-Antalus's main zones fog toward (118,105,65) from 2000 to 9000 units. That is inside the W table (1 .. 52428.8, `KMD/common/vcr_fog.c:36-62`).
  - The sky zone's fog colour is (123,147,157), and its `bDistanceFog` is off.
  - Neither colour is white, and a fully fogged pixel takes the fog colour.
- **3dfx's own W2K HAL met the same textures and handled them:**
  - textures above 8:1 are allocated as 8:1 (`AspectRatioGT8`, `VH5/D3TXTR.C:1788-1885`);
  - they are stretched at TEXBLT (`CREATENEWTEXTURE`, `:2645-2680`, `:4012-4100`, "AABBAABB, not ABABABAB");
  - DXT textures are stretched at Unlock (`stretchDXTn`, `VH5/DDSURF.C:2072-2087`, `VH5/D3TXTR.C:4371-4520`);
  - all while still reporting `dwMaxTextureAspectRatio` 8 (`VH5/D3INIT.C:3213-3215`).
- **Our own OpenGL ICD does the same** (`MESAFX/fxddtex.c:676-735` `fxTexGetInfo`; `:1440-1460` rescale at TexImage).

So **G88's "aspect up to 8:1" is a TMU limit that the driver can hide**, as the vendor did. It is not a reason to draw white.

**Scope across the library.** The staged UT2003 has 11 textures above 8:1 and the staged UT2004 has 25 (Appendix A):
- the Antalus sky;
- `rope` (1024x64; DM-TokaraForest in both titles);
- UT2004 menu art: `2K4Menus` `sectionback` 8x512, `menuBackground2` 4x512, `Bgndtile` 32x1024, `ScrollSmall` 1024x32;
- the logo effect (`ULogo` `Scanning_1` 4x256, `FadePanner` 256x4);
- Onslaught beams (2048x32, 1x256), `GammaBar`, `wm_` gradients and CJK font pages.

All of them draw as their diffuse colour today. One is very likely visible already: the flat light band across the UT2004 menu at `S08F/Unreal_Tournament_2004_60s.png` y 218-256 (grey 165-198). `ScrollSmall` (1024x32) scaled to 1280 wide is 40 px tall, and the band is 38-39 px (inferred match).

**What is not this item:**
- **Stripes, the cyan triangle and the striped floor.** These are garbage read from minified levels: G06/G07 (split mip chains). The 65 s stripe band sits where the planet panel (1024x512 DXT5, 11 levels) and the dome are seen at grazing angles (inferred).
- **visual-11** (`S08F/Unreal_Tournament_2003_60s.png`). This is a thin noisy band on black at y 484-493, with no white in the frame. It is most likely a menu-opening transition (inferred). It goes to G79 for a recapture.
- **TFACTOR (G14) and blend factors (G28/G29).** They are not needed to explain the white, and their instrumentation stays with those items.
- **The sky box floor.** In UT2004 it is `cp_white1`, white by design, and stays white wherever it is legitimately visible. In UT2003 it is the `Cloudcombine` combiner. If the UT2003 30 s white region turns out to be that floor, its white is a combine defect (G12's BLEND*/stage-order mapping, or G14), not this item.

**The plan:**
- **Part A, default-on, log-only.** Every refused or over-aspect texture becomes visible in the ring, at creation and for both stages.
- **Part B, the fix.** Every texture above 8:1 gets a driver-owned 8:1 **shadow**, refreshed from the application's surface by lossless texel/index replication. It sits behind `Diag\D3DFeat` bit ASPECT.
- **Part C, contingency.** A debug-only tint (`Diag\D3DDebugTint`) paints refused textures magenta, for attribution if any white survives Part B.

### 2.3 The code path, read

- **Creation.**
  - `Dd_CreateSurface` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_ddraw.c:636-645`) calls `VcrDdD3dCreateMipChain` → `mipchain_ext`, which returns 0 for the 8x512 top ("the chip cannot sample it: the runtime places it", `vcrdd_d3d.c:417`).
  - `VcrDdD3dCreateTexSurface` sizes the DXT block (`:473-505`) and DirectDraw places it.
- **Draw.**
  - `compute_regs` (`:921`) → `view_of` (`:840-843`) → `tex_view_ext` → `ext_layout` (`:349-363`) → `vcr_texlod_compute_ext` returns -1.
  - Stage 0: `tex = 0`, so 513/7 is logged once per refused handle (`:932-944`) and `color_path` selects the iterated colour (`:690`).
  - **Stage 1 is refused silently:** `two` simply becomes 0 (`:928-929`); no event names it.
- **Default path** (no D3DBigTex, a fresh install): `vcr_texlod_compute` refuses the same way (`common/vcr_texlod.c:24`). There UE2 would hand over a downscaled 4x256 (still 64:1) (inferred), with the same result.
- **Caps:** `dwMaxTextureAspectRatio` = 8 (`vcrdd_d3d.c:2159`). That is honest about the TMU, and it matches 3dfx.

## 4. Design of the fix

### 4.1 Part A: make it visible (default-on, log-only, no register change)

1. **At creation**, in a new `VcrDdD3dAspectCreate(pd, p)` called from `Dd_CreateSurface` after the existing two calls:
   - **When:** for each surface in `lplpSList` that is a video-memory texture (`DDSCAPS_TEXTURE`; not `SYSTEMMEMORY`, not `3DDEVICE`), not a mip sublevel (`lpSurfMore->ddsCapsEx.dwCaps2 & DDSCAPS2_MIPMAPSUBLEVEL` = 0x10000 clear), power of two, with aspect above 8:1.
   - **What:** one WARN, a new 513 what id (the next free one; 1-23 are taken):
     - fields: a = (w<<16)|h, b = FOURCC or bpp, c = the stretch factor;
     - text: `"texture %ux%u is %u:1 - the TMU samples 8:1 at most: %s"`, ending in `expanded to %ux%u` or `drawn UNTEXTURED (Diag\D3DFeat ASPECT off)`;
     - a shadow allocation failure is its own WARN.
   - **Cost:** UE2 creates these once, at precache.
2. **Stage-1 refusals are logged like stage 0's.** Add a `tex_refused1` beside `tex_refused` (`:932`), so a refused second-stage texture is no longer silent.
3. **The context-end summary** (`ctx_free`, `:1210-1215`) gains a second INFO event: refused stage-0 and stage-1 binds, and the first refused shape.

All three are logging only. A Python test pins that the draw path is unchanged.

### 4.2 Part B: the 8:1 shadow (`Diag\D3DFeat` bit ASPECT)

**Idea.** The application's surface stays exactly what it is today. DirectDraw places it, and the runtime Locks it and TEXBLTs into it. The driver keeps a **separate, packed, 16-byte-aligned shadow chain** at 8:1, expands the application's texels into it whenever they change, and points the TMU at the shadow.

**Why a shadow, not 3dfx's in-place 8:1 surface:** 3dfx allocated the surface at 8:1 and had to stretch after Unlock and **shrink before Lock** (`shrinkDXTn`). It also decoded and **re-encoded** DXT blocks (`stretchDXTn`), which is lossy. Our surfaces are placed by DirectDraw and locked by the runtime. A shadow needs no Lock interception, replication is lossless, and the shadow packs its own chain, so it is immune to G07's split chains.

#### 4.2.1 Pure arithmetic: `KMD/include/vcr_texaspect.h` + `KMD/common/vcr_texaspect.c` (new, Win32-free, integer-only)

**The plan.** `int vcr_tex_aspect_plan(w, h, kind, flags, vcr_tex_aspect *a)`:
- **Expanded top:** `W' = max(w, big/8)`, `H' = max(h, big/8)`, so the long side is unchanged and the narrow side is raised to big/8.
- **Its outputs:**
  - `axis`: S if w is narrow, T if h is;
  - `k0` = W'/w or H'/h;
  - `levels_max` = log2(big) + 1;
  - `bytes` = `vcr_tex_chain_offset(kind, W', H', levels_max)`.
- **Returns:**
  - 1 when no shadow is needed (aspect ≤ 8:1);
  - -1 when the expanded texture still cannot be sampled (not power of two; above 2048, or above 256 without `VCR_TEXF_BIG`; DXT1 with W' < 8);
  - 0 otherwise.
- The per-level factor is `k_i = max(W'>>i,1) / max(w>>i,1)` on the narrow axis (8x512: 8,8,8,8,4,2,1,1,1,1).

**Expansion is nearest-neighbour replication along the narrow axis:** output texel (x', y) ← source (x'/k_i, y), or the transpose for axis T. This is 3dfx's "AABB" and MesaFX's wScale/hScale.
- **16/32-bit:** `vcr_tex_aspect_expand_lin(src, src_pitch, w, h, bpp, axis, k, dst)`. The source pitch is the surface's `lPitch`; the destination rows are tight (W' × bpp), which is the TMU's linear layout.
- **DXT1/DXT3/DXT5:** `vcr_tex_aspect_expand_dxt(src, w, h, kind, axis, k, dst)`. It is **lossless and works without decoding**:
  - each output 4x4 block draws all its texels from **one** source block (k is a power of two);
  - so the colour endpoints (and DXT5's alpha endpoints) are copied as they are;
  - only the per-texel index fields are rebuilt: 2-bit colour, DXT3's 4-bit alpha, DXT5's 3-bit alpha.
  - DXT1's 3-colour mode is preserved, because c0 ≤ c1 is untouched.
  - The source is D3D's block layout: rows of ceil(w/4) blocks, the "pitch" being the linear size.
  - Source levels narrower than a block (2x128, 1x64) read only their valid columns, and an expanded block's padding columns repeat the last valid one.

**TMU-walkable levels.** `vcr_tex_chain_usable(kind, W', H', n)` already bounds DXT1 to levels with W' ≥ 8. Shadow DXT1 levels below that are neither written nor sampled.

#### 4.2.2 Creation and lifetime

- **Creation.** `VcrDdD3dAspectCreate` (Part A's hook), with ASPECT armed and `vcr_tex_aspect_plan(…) == 0`:
  - allocate `bytes` with `HeapVidMemAllocAligned` (start and pitch alignment 16, as `mipchain_ext` does);
  - `EngAllocMem` a small descriptor {heap, base, W', H', kind, axis, levels_max};
  - put the descriptor pointer in `s->lpGbl->dwReserved1`, which is free for DirectDraw-placed surfaces (`mipchain_ext` uses it only for chains it places itself);
  - set `s->dwReserved1 = SF_MAGIC | SF_ASPECT` (new bit 0x8; not yet filled);
  - return not-handled as today: DirectDraw still places the application's surface.
- **Allocation failure.** WARN, and no `SF_ASPECT`, so the texture is refused exactly as today.
- **The 16 MB rule on the default path.** On a path without D3DBigTex, a shadow at or above 16 MB would be sampled 16 MB lower (G21's 24-bit `texBaseAddr`). Free it and refuse, logged, until G21 lands.
- **Free.** `Dd_DestroySurface` (`vcrdd_ddraw.c:664-674`): for `SF_ASPECT`, `VidMemFree(desc->heap, desc->base)` and `EngFreeMem(desc)`, then **return not-handled**, so DirectDraw frees its own surface. `VcrDdD3dFreeMipChain` must not claim it.
- **Unlock and TEXBLT** already mark the written surface dirty (`VcrDdD3dTexWritten`, `vcrdd_d3d.c:606-614`; Unlock `vcrdd_ddraw.c:676-681`; TEXBLT `vcrdd_d3d.c:1499-1549`). `SF_DIRTY` sits on whichever level was written, and is ORed in, so `SF_ASPECT` survives.

#### 4.2.3 Refresh and the TMU view

- **`view_of` (`:840-843`).** When `pd->d3d_aspect && SF_IS(s, SF_ASPECT)`, call `aspect_view()`. Otherwise run the existing code, unchanged.
- **`aspect_view()` refreshes when needed.** If the shadow was never filled, or any level of the application's chain has `SF_DIRTY` (walk `next_mip`, at most 12 levels):
  - `VcrDd2dSync(pd)`, because queued triangles may still sample the shadow (the `texblt` rule, `:1528`);
  - expand every present level from `pvRamBase + fpVidMem` into `shadow + vcr_tex_chain_offset(kind, W', H', i)`;
  - clear the dirty bits and mark the shadow filled;
  - `VcrDd3dTexFlush` on the shadow's register base. The texture-port write-back fires only where `vcr_tex_writeback_addr` allows it today.
- **`aspect_view()` then builds the view.**
  - It is the existing view (`tex_view_ext` on the VSA-100 path, `tex_view` on the default path), refactored to take {w, h, pitch, offset, levels} explicitly rather than read them from the surface.
  - It is given the shadow as a placed chain of `n` levels, where n is the application chain's present levels.
  - Aspect is 3 and `s_is_wider` comes from W' vs H'. TBIG, the 26-bit munged base, the filters, clamp and LODMAX are all the existing rules.
  - `v->w`, `v->h` = W', H', so `VcrDd3dTexScale` maps u ∈ [0,1] across the whole expanded width. With replication, that is exact for WRAP and CLAMP at texel granularity.
- **Dirty while not bound.** `flush_written` (`:1287-1320`) defers to the same refresh for `SF_ASPECT` surfaces. The check at bind (above) also covers a texture written while not bound. The global-counter shortcut in `prepare` (`:1327-1334`) would otherwise skip it.
- **No new registers and no new register fields.** `textureMode`, `tLOD` and `texBaseAddr` are the same writes with other values. Single pass.

#### 4.2.4 Caps, and what stays out

- **`dwMaxTextureAspectRatio` stays 8.** That is the TMU's truth, as 3dfx reported, and it keeps well-behaved titles on native textures. Raise it to 2048 while ASPECT is armed only if E1 shows the runtime refusing creation.
- **Out of scope:**
  - render-target textures (`3DDEVICE`): refused as today;
  - system-memory textures: never sampled;
  - non-power-of-two: refused, as the caps say.
- **Filtering on the narrow axis** is coarser than D3D's bilinear: replicated texels blend only at every k-th texel. This is what 3dfx and MesaFX ship. B0000 is constant across its narrow axis, so it shows no difference.

### 4.3 Part C: `Diag\D3DDebugTint` (contingency; debug only, never graduates)

- **What it does.** Bit 0, read at boot: a draw whose stage-0 texture is refused gets:
  - `fbzColorPath` = `CP_LOCAL_C0 | CP_CC_ZERO_OTHER | CP_CC_ADD_CLOCAL | CP_PARMADJUST`, with the alpha bits as computed;
  - `c0` = 0x00FF00FF.

  That is magenta, from registers the HAL already writes. `c0` is otherwise always 0 (`:1019`).
- **Default.** Absent = 0, and the registers are byte-identical.
- **Its use.** Attribution in E3, and a reusable census tool for G06/G44.

## 5. Gating

- **Part A: default-on.** It is pure logging, with no register traffic, and a Python test proves the draw path is untouched. It costs one IOCTL per over-aspect texture creation (a few per title) plus a summary event per context. This follows hal.md §10's "Phase 0, log-only, default-on".
- **Part B: default OFF**, behind `Diag\D3DFeat` bit `VCR_D3DFEAT_ASPECT`, read at boot.
  - **With it off:** no shadow is allocated and `SF_ASPECT` is never set, so `view_of` runs today's code and every register write is identical. Only Part A's logging differs.
  - **Not safe to land default-on yet:**
    - it allocates video memory and writes `texBaseAddr`/`tLOD` values no silicon run has used;
    - 8:1 DXT and 8:1 TBIG have never been probed: `dxt5_512x128` is 4:1, the widest tested.
  - **Graduation:** after boot B3 (§7) passes and a D3D title sweep (G73) with ASPECT armed shows no regression, flip the default (with the plan's shared D3DFeat convention: default mask or kill switch).
- **Part C:** `Diag\D3DDebugTint`, default 0, debug only, never default-on. A Python test pins absent = off.
- **One Diag change per clean boot:** E1 = LogLevel; B1 = the build alone; B3 = ASPECT; B4 = the tint, if needed.

## 8. Risk to proven paths

- **The default 16 bpp path and the 11-08 fixes.**
  - With ASPECT off, the only difference is logging (pinned).
  - With it on, only textures above 8:1 change, and today those draw white.
  - The `tex_view`/`tex_view_ext` refactor is the one shared-code change. It is guarded by the existing `test_vcr_kmd_texlod.c` and a new Python assertion that both views read the surface's own geometry when it is not a shadow.
- **Video memory.**
  - A shadow is small: 43,792 bytes for B0000; about 175 KB with mips for the 1024x32 / 32x1024 / 8x512 menu strips and `rope`; about 700 KB for each 2048x32 DXT5 Onslaught beam (2048x256 at 8:1). All 25 UT2004 shapes together come to about 3 MB, an upper bound never resident at once (inferred: the beams load only in Onslaught maps).
  - It is allocated before DirectDraw places the application's surface. On a nearly full heap, the creation could then fail and the runtime would evict and retry (inferred).
  - G63 (the heap at offset 0) applies as it does to `mipchain_ext`: a block at 0 reads as a failure, so the texture is refused, logged.
- **Coherency.**
  - The refresh is a CPU write into video memory, after `VcrDd2dSync`, then the existing TMU flush, the same rules as TEXBLT.
  - A 2D-engine `Blt` into an over-aspect texture is not flagged dirty, so the shadow goes stale. That is G37's gap, and inherited.
- **Lifetime.** A shadow lives exactly as long as its surface (`DestroySurface`). If dxg ever drops a surface without that callback at a mode change, the descriptor leaks and the heap is rebuilt underneath it. That is the open question G43/R4 already carries for mip chains.
- **Glide/OpenGL coexistence:** none new. The shadow is in the DirectDraw heap of a D3D session, and Glide owns the board only exclusively.
- **SLI:** none. D3D is chip 0 only.
- **The flip path:** none.
- **Unproven silicon behaviour the probes cover first:** 8:1 DXT, 8:1 TBIG, and 8:1 A8R8G8B8.

## Appendix B: how the packages were read (so the census can be repeated)

The reader was a short Python script, kept outside the repo.

**The package format (UE2, versions 120 and 126):**
- **Header:** magic 0x9E2A83C1; then name, export and import counts and offsets.
- **Names:** an FString plus flags.
- **Imports:** class package, class name, package index, object name.
- **Exports:** class, super and object names as compact indices; the package as int32; flags; then serial size and offset.
- **Object properties:** tagged. Each tag is a name; an info byte (bits 0-3 the type, 4-6 the size code, bit 7 the array/bool flag); a struct name for type 10; then the value. `None` ends the list.

**What was read:**
- **Texture sizes:** `USize`, `VSize` and `Format` (TEXF_P8 0, RGBA8 5, DXT1 3, DXT3 7, DXT5 8, G16 10).
- **Texels:** decoded from the `Mips` array after the properties. Each entry is an int32 skip position, the compact data size, the data, then USize/VSize as int32 and UBits/VBits as bytes.
- **Brush faces:** the `Polys` objects after their properties. An int32 count and an int32 max, then per FPoly:
  - a compact vertex count;
  - Base, Normal, TextureU and TextureV;
  - the vertices;
  - PolyFlags;
  - Actor, Material, ItemName, iLink and iBrushPoly as compact indices;
  - LightMapScale.

A checked-in `KMD/tools/ue2tex.py` built from this, which lists every texture the TMU cannot sample as-is, would make this census repeatable for G34/G38/G88 (a suggestion, not part of this item).

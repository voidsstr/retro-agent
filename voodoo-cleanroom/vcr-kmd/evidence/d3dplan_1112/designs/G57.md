## 0. Verdict

1. **The gap is real.** `VcrDdD3dDriverInfo` computes -2048/2048 and throws them away (`(void)guard; (void)guard_pos;`,
   `KMD/display/vcrdd_d3d.c:2152-2162`). All four guard-band extents therefore go to the runtime as 0, so Direct3D
   clips in software every triangle that crosses the viewport. (`d3dprobe caps` does not even print the field yet,
   `evidence/d3dplan_1112/caps_124.txt`: G74.)
2. **It is not a caps-only fix.** Reporting extents is a promise. Per the DDK: *"Coordinates inside this rectangle
   but outside the viewport rectangle are automatically clipped."* Our HAL clips only to the **render target**
   (fbzMode `FZ_RECTCLIP` always, `vcrdd_d3d.c:978`, with clip rect 0 = the target, `vcr_3dseq.h:75-76`). It also
   skips `D3DDP2OP_VIEWPORTINFO` (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1555`). So the fix has three parts, the three 3dfx's own NT HAL had:
   1. **caps:** -200, -200, 2048, 1536, the vendor's Napalm values;
   2. **a viewport scissor:** VIEWPORTINFO is parsed, and clip rect 0 becomes viewport ∩ target, exact. It is the
      whole target until the first VIEWPORTINFO arrives: the vendor's PRS 12728 was a black Hidden & Dangerous
      intro without that initial value;
   3. **the hardware guard band:** `renderMode[21]` plus clip rect 1 = the same rectangle, rounded out to even x.
   The dead -2048/2048 pair was wrong for the VSA-100 anyway. It is the Voodoo3's 12.4 range, while the VSA-100
   rasterises in [-4k, 4k] (SPEC L607, L655).
3. **It is a performance item, and its sign is unmeasured.** Nothing renders wrong today because of G57.
   - **Gain:** the runtime's clip CPU, plus the extra HAL triangles a clipped polygon becomes (a fan of 2-3 triangles
     for one, inferred). G46 bounds the HAL at 6.28 µs a triangle in UT2003.
   - **Cost:** the off-screen raster the chip now walks and discards. In today's PIO transport the CPU waits for the
     chip (perf §1.2), so chip time is frame time.
   - Only measurement settles the net. G46's decision table already says when: **G57 is worth arming if
     TRIANGLEFAN_IMM triangles are ≥ 5 % of HAL triangles** in a staged title.
4. **Gating:** it lands **default-OFF** behind `Diag\D3DGuardBand`, with the off path byte-for-byte unchanged. A
   log-only *observe* bit gathers the evidence on the unchanged path first. It is **not** a candidate for default-on
   at landing: it changes what the runtime sends, and it moves viewport clipping from the runtime into the HAL.

## 2. The hardware (SPEC)

- **Coordinate range.** *"increase of rendering window to -4k to 4k"* (L607), *"Support for [-4k,4k] rasterization
  coordinate space"* (L655). The legacy vertex registers are 12.4 two's complement (L2538). We use the setup unit's
  float `sVx`/`sVy` (`vcrdd_3d.c:190-191`). Every extent proposed here stays inside ±2048, plus the 0.5 centre bias.
- **renderMode[21], guard-band clipping** (L3299, L3314):
  - *"pixels which fall outside of the clipping rectangle defined by clipLeftRight1 and clipTopBottom1 will not be
    rendered"*;
  - *"The triangle iterators are optimized to quickly disgard pixels outside of the guardband clipping region"*;
  - top1 < bottom1 and left1 < right1; left/right inclusive/exclusive like rect 0;
  - *"the left and right edges ... must be aligned on even pixel boundries"* (also L3627, L6249).
- **clip rect 1** (§8.45 L3624-3642): its bit 31 enables the *secondary* rectangle. *"when guardband clipping is
  enabled, typically the second regular clipping rectangle ... will be disabled (clipLeftRight1 bit(31)=0)"* (L3627).
  It is an FBI register, R/W, and sync (L2342-2343).
- **clip rect 0** (§8.44 L3605): *"if clipping is not enabled, rendering must not be specified to occur outside of
  the screen resolution."* `FZ_RECTCLIP` therefore stays on, as it already is.
- **Cost of the extra writes.** clip0, clip1, renderMode and colBufferAddr are all sync registers (L2308-2309, L2333,
  L2336, L2342-2343). The target sequence already writes five of them back to back, so two more add little
  (inferred).
- **The spec disagrees with itself.** The register table still lists renderMode as bits 20:0 (L2333). Bit 21 was
  added in revision 1.08 (*"Added renderMode[21] to control triangle iterators guardband clipping"*, L6567). The
  newer text wins.
- **On `.124` today:**
  - Our h5 Glide sets the bit in every session: `FX_GLIDE_GBC` defaults to 1 (`GL5/glide3/src/gpci.c:1872`,
    `gsst.c:4177-4178`), and clip1 is written from `grClipWindow` (`distate.c:2181-2187`, `gglide.c:3349-3357`).
  - Glide's clip1 *bottom/top* formula ORs miny and maxy into the low field (`gglide.c:3354-3357`; the same in 3dfx's
    `H5/GLIDE3/SRC/GGLIDE.C:3125`). That is benign only because clip rect 0 does the exact clip and clip1 stays a
    superset of it, which is the shape this design copies.
  - MesaFX has no guard-band path (a case-insensitive grep for `guard` in `build/retro3dfx-gl/src/mesa/drivers/glide`
    finds nothing): it clips in software. So **renderMode[21] with genuinely off-screen vertices has never been
    exercised on `.124`.**

| item | vendor | source |
|---|---|---|

| caps | Napalm only: -200, -200, 2048, 1536; otherwise 0. dvExtentsAdjust 0 | `VH5/D3INIT.C:3221-3238` |

| context defaults | `renderMode \|= SST_RM_ENGUARDBAND`; clip1 = `0x0000ffe` / `0x0000fff` (*"largest inner guardband"*); fbzMode always `SST_ENRECTCLIP` | `VH5/D3RSTATE.C:3807-3812`, `:3758` |
| clip 0 at context create | the target's size, *"until the viewport clipping values are invoked. This fixes PRS 12728 ... Hidden & Dangerous, black screen with sound during intro scenes"* | `VH5/D3CONTXT.C:445-449` |
| VIEWPORTINFO | clip1 = viewport: left `& 0xffe`, right rounded up to even, right ≤ 0xffe, bottom ≤ 0xfff. With `NEW_CLIP_FOR_GB` (defined for WINNT) clip0 = the viewport, exact | `VH5/D6DP2.C:1562-1618`; `VH5/DDFXNT95.H:149` |
| flush | clip0 and clip1 re-sent when changed | `VH5/D6DP2.C:5068-5088`, `:5141-5167` |
| clears | clip0 restored to the context's viewport clip, not the target | `VH5/D7DP2.C:588-598`, `:955-966` |

**Not to copy (G95):**
- **First record versus last.** clip1 comes from the **first** VIEWPORTINFO record and clip0 from the **last**, and
  the stream advances by one record whatever `wStateCount` says (`VH5/D6DP2.C:1567`, `:1586-1606`, `:1613-1614`,
  `:1617`). We take the last record for both and keep our walker's `n`-record step.
- **The DEBUG validation inset** (`:5169-5216`): its vertical half *grows* the rectangle (`ulBottom += 20`,
  `ulTop -= 20`).
- **No clip handling at SETRENDERTARGET** (`VH5/D7DP2.C:61-152`). We clamp the kept viewport to the new target
  (§5.4).

## 4. What the runtime does

**Documented:**
- **VIEWPORTINFO (DDK):**
  - *"used to inform guard-band aware drivers of the view clipping rectangle"*;
  - *"One D3DHAL_DP2VIEWPORTINFO structure follows the D3DHAL_DP2COMMAND structure"*;
  - *"The driver can use this information to perform guard band clipping. Subguard-band drivers should ignore and
    skip over these instructions."*
- **Extended caps (DDK):** *"Coordinates inside this rectangle but outside the viewport rectangle are automatically
  clipped."*
- **NVIDIA (2000):**
  - *"The Direct3D pipeline automatically recognizes the guard band capability and will use it if present, and does
    not require the application to enable it."*
  - Software clipping costs *"Extra vertices produced"*, interpolation of every attribute, and the *"Breaking up of
    strips and fans"*.
  - D3DTLVERTEX geometry that relies on D3D clipping is un-projected, clipped in 3D and re-projected, which is
    expensive.
  - Applications *"can detect"* a guard band and then cull less themselves.

**Inferred, and measured by the observe bit (§5.6) before anything depends on them:**
- **(a)** Our DX7-level HAL receives the runtime's clipped polygons as `TRIANGLEFAN_IMM`. `CLIPPEDTRIANGLEFAN` (58)
  is the DX8 DDI's. G46 already counts `fan_imm` as "the G57 measure".
- **(b)** With a guard band, triangles wholly outside the viewport are still rejected by the runtime and are not sent
  to us.
- **(c)** Pre-transformed (XYZRHW) geometry that the runtime clips is tested against the guard band too, not only
  software-VP geometry.
- **(d)** The runtime sends VIEWPORTINFO with `wStateCount` = 1, and again after SetViewport, a device reset and
  SetRenderTarget.

## 5. The design

### 5.1 The switch

`HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\vcrmp\Diag\D3DGuardBand` (REG_DWORD). It is read **at boot**, like D3D32 and
D3DBigTex, because the desktop PDEV and a fullscreen PDEV must report the same caps. VSA-100 only. Absent or 0 is off.

| bit | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | **ON:** report the band, scissor to the viewport, clip1 + renderMode[21] |
| 1 | 2 | experiment, with bit 0: clip1 written, renderMode[21] **left clear**. It measures what the iterators' quick discard buys (`gbfill`) |
| 2 | 4 | experiment, with bit 0: wide band -1024, -1024, 2048, 2048. Measures more margin; also covers 2048-tall render targets (open question 6) |
| 3 | 8 | **observe:** log-only viewport bookkeeping and per-context counters. No caps change, no register change. Works alone |

The useful values are 8 (evidence on the unchanged path), 9 (on + observe: the verification boot), 1 (production),
11 and 13 (the two experiments).

**Transport:**
- The miniport reads `x->d3dgb = VcrDiagGet(L"D3DGuardBand", 0);` beside D3DBigTex (`KMD/miniport/vcrmp.c:238-244`).
- It passes the raw word in a field appended to `vcr_info`, `vcr_u32 d3d_gb;`. This is G46's convention: flag bit
  0x8000 is already claimed by several designs.
- `VcrDd2dInit` (`KMD/display/vcrdd_2d.c:443-473`) reads it only when `info.size` covers the field, so an older
  miniport reads as off, then sets `pd->gb = pd->napalm ? (info.d3d_gb & VCR_GB_KNOWN) : 0;`.
- **Events** (513, the next free "what" codes at implementation: G03 claims 24/25, G05 24, G24 27):
  - when armed: *"armed (Diag\D3DGuardBand %x): guard band -200..2048 x -200..1536, viewport scissor, renderMode[21]
    %s"*;
  - on a Banshee/Voodoo3 with the switch set: *"ignored: not a VSA-100"*.

### 5.2 `KMD/include/vcr_guard.h` (new; integer only, Win32-free)

The HAL is built `-mgeneral-regs-only`, so every float here is an IEEE bit pattern. The host test compiles the same
header.

```c
#define VCR_GB_ON       0x1u
#define VCR_GB_NO_RM21  0x2u
#define VCR_GB_WIDE     0x4u
#define VCR_GB_OBSERVE  0x8u
#define VCR_GB_KNOWN    0xfu
#define RM_GUARDBAND    (1u << 21)      /* VINC/H3DEFS.H:143 SST_RM_ENGUARDBAND */

#define VCR_GB_F_M200   0xc3480000u     /* -200.0f  */
#define VCR_GB_F_M1024  0xc4800000u     /* -1024.0f */
#define VCR_GB_F_1536   0x44c00000u     /* 1536.0f  */
#define VCR_GB_F_2048   0x45000000u     /* 2048.0f  */

/* left, top, right, bottom as D3DVALUE bits; all 0 = no guard band (the old value) */
static __inline void vcr_gb_caps(unsigned gb, unsigned napalm, unsigned out[4]);

typedef struct vcr_gb_clip {
    unsigned lr, bt;        /* clipLeftRight / clipBottomTop (rect 0, FZ_RECTCLIP): exact */
    unsigned lr1, bt1;      /* clipLeftRight1 / clipBottomTop1: the band rect, bit 31 clear */
    unsigned rm;            /* OR-ed into renderMode: RM_GUARDBAND, or 0 */
    unsigned n1;            /* clip1 writes: 2 when on, 0 when off */
    unsigned why;           /* VCR_GB_VP_OK, _UNSET (no VIEWPORTINFO yet), _EMPTY (clamped to nothing) */
} vcr_gb_clip;

static __inline void vcr_gb_clip_of(unsigned gb, unsigned napalm, unsigned vp_set,
                                    unsigned vx, unsigned vy, unsigned vw, unsigned vh,
                                    unsigned tw, unsigned th, vcr_gb_clip *o);

/* observe: IEEE bits -> an unsigned key that orders like the floats (-0 == +0, NaN above +inf) */
static __inline unsigned vcr_f_key(unsigned bits);
static __inline unsigned vcr_i2f(int v);        /* exact for |v| < 2^24 */
```

**`vcr_gb_clip_of`:**
- **Off** (`!napalm || !(gb & VCR_GB_ON)`): `lr = tw & 0xfff`, `bt = th & 0xfff`, `n1 = rm = 0`. That is today's
  value, value for value.
- **On:**
  1. **Clamp:** `x0 = min(vx, tw)`, `y0 = min(vy, th)`; `x1 = vw > tw - x0 ? tw : x0 + vw` (no overflow), and the same
     for y.
  2. **Never a zero clip:** an unset or empty result falls back to the **whole target** (PRS 12728).
  3. `lr = (l << 16) | r`, `bt = (t << 16) | b`.
  4. `lr1 = ((l & ~1) << 16) | min((r + 1) & ~1, 0xffe)`: rounded **out** to even, so clip1 ⊇ clip0 and the exact
     edge stays rect 0's.
  5. `bt1 = bt`; bit 31 clear in both.
  6. `rm = (gb & VCR_GB_NO_RM21) ? 0 : RM_GUARDBAND`, `n1 = 2`.

### 5.3 Caps (`VcrDdD3dDriverInfo`, the GUID_D3DExtendedCaps branch)

The dead `guard`/`guard_pos` pair is deleted and replaced with
`vcr_gb_caps(pd->gb, pd->napalm, gbc); memcpy(&x.dvGuardBandLeft, &gbc[0], 4); ...` for all four.
`dvExtentsAdjust` stays 0. Nothing else in the caps changes.

| D3DCAPS8 field | off (today) | bit 0 | bits 0+2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| GuardBandLeft / Top | 0 / 0 | -200 / -200 | -1024 / -1024 |
| GuardBandRight / Bottom | 0 / 0 | 2048 / 1536 | 2048 / 2048 |

### 5.4 The viewport (`vcrdd_d3d.c`)

- **The context** (`vcr_d3dctx`, `:49-74`) gains `vp_x, vp_y, vp_w, vp_h, vp_set` and the observe counters (§5.6).
  ContextCreate's `memset` (`:1176`) leaves `vp_set = 0`, which means the whole target.
- **VIEWPORTINFO gets its own `case` in `walk()`**, out of `rec_size`:
  - `NEED(n * sizeof(D3DNTHAL_DP2VIEWPORTINFO))`;
  - if `pd->gb & (VCR_GB_ON | VCR_GB_OBSERVE)` and `n`: read the **last** record and store it, `vp_set = 1`, and
    count it (sub-target, `n != 1`);
  - if ON: `gb_clip(c)` and `w->prepared = 0`, so the next triangle re-sends the target with the new clip;
  - then `p += n * 16`, the same step as today.
  - With the switch off nothing is stored or written. The Python test pins that.
- **`target_of()`'s tail** (after `t->width/height`, `:1081`) calls `gb_clip(c)`. That stores
  `vcr_gb_clip_of(pd->gb, pd->napalm, c->vp_set, c->vp_*, t->width, t->height)` into the target. ContextCreate, every
  DP2, every Clear2 and SETRENDERTARGET therefore all get the same clamp.
- **SETRENDERTARGET keeps the viewport and clamps it** to the new target (target_of runs there, `:1737`). This is the
  vendor's behaviour: it resets nothing. The runtime is expected to send a fresh VIEWPORTINFO after SetRenderTarget
  (open question 8; the observe log shows the order).

### 5.5 Registers

- **`vcr3d_target`** (`vcrdd_3d.h:27-34`) gains `clip_lr, clip_bt, clip1_lr, clip1_bt, gb_rm, gb_n1`.
- **`vcr_3dseq.h`, new `vcr_3d_target_gb(rm_or, lr, bt, lr1, bt1, n1, w, n)`.** It post-processes
  `vcr_3d_target_seq`'s array, which stays untouched:
  - it ORs `rm_or` into the `V3D_RENDERMODE` write (OR, so G50's band bits can join);
  - it sets the two clip-0 values;
  - when `n1` is set, it appends `V3D_CLIPLEFTRIGHT1` (0x200) and `V3D_CLIPBOTTOMTOP1` (0x204).
  - With the switch off it returns the identical array and `n`. `VCR_3D_TARGET_GB_MAX` = 11.
- **`vcr_3dregs.h`** gains `V3D_CLIPLEFTRIGHT1 0x200`, `V3D_CLIPBOTTOMTOP1 0x204` (G03 adds the same two; one
  definition) and `RM_GUARDBAND`.
- **`VcrDd3dTarget`** (`vcrdd_3d.c:45-69`) sizes its array to 11 and calls the post-processor. `VcrDdRoom(n > 7 ? n :
  7)` stays: 9 or 11 entries against the learned 31.
- **`VcrDd3dClear`** restores clip 0 to `t->clip_lr` / `t->clip_bt` (`vcrdd_3d.c:175-176`). Those equal
  `width & 0xfff` / `height & 0xfff` when off, so the bytes do not change. When on they are the viewport, as the
  vendor's clears do. Per-rectangle clears keep their target clamp. D3D clears are already inside the viewport
  (inferred; `gbviewport` checks).
- **Order.** renderMode and clip1 are in the same sequence, written before the state and before any triangle. So
  ENGUARDBAND never meets a stale clip1 at a triangle, including clip1 = 0 from G03's baseline, which runs before the
  target (G03 §2.2).

Example values (VSA-100):

| case | renderMode | clipLeftRight | clipBottomTop | clipLeftRight1 | clipBottomTop1 | writes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| off, 640x480x16 (today) | `0x001e0000` | `0x00000280` | `0x000001e0` | - | - | 7 |
| on, 640x480x16, full viewport | `0x003e0000` | `0x00000280` | `0x000001e0` | `0x00000280` | `0x000001e0` | 9 |
| on, 1024x768x32, full viewport | `0x003e0002` | `0x00000400` | `0x00000300` | `0x00000400` | `0x00000300` | 11 |
| on, 256x256, viewport (63, 47, 130x131) | `0x003e0000` | `0x003f00c1` | `0x002f00b2` | `0x003e00c2` | `0x002f00b2` | 9 |
| on bits 0+1 (experiment) | bit 21 clear | as above | as above | written | written | 9 / 11 |

**Single pass.** No multipass, no new waits, no new register reads.

### 5.6 Observe (bit 3, log-only)

Per context, integer-only. The x/y bits of each XYZRHW vertex (offsets 0 and 4) are compared through `vcr_f_key`
against keys of the scissor edges, which are precomputed at `gb_clip`. A 1-pixel margin absorbs the runtime's float
error on its clip edges. The walk sets `w->observe` once per DP2 call, so the off path costs one branch per call.

| counter | what it settles |
|---|---|
| `fanimm_tris`, `fanimm_fans` | how much the runtime clips (inferred (a)): G46's ≥ 5 % rule |
| `out_vp`: triangles with a vertex outside the scissor | **off build:** must be 0. Non-zero means the title draws outside its viewport today (DONOTCLIP or CLIPPING FALSE), and the scissor would cut it: a regression risk to inspect before arming. **On build:** > 0 proves the runtime passes guard-band geometry (inferred (c)) |
| `out_rt`: a vertex outside the target | the same, at target level |
| `gone`: bbox misses the scissor entirely | inferred (b): non-zero on the on build means the runtime sends fully off-screen triangles. That is pure HAL cost (6 µs each); the mitigation would be a HAL bbox cull |
| `far`: a vertex beyond the reported band | must be 0 on the on build. Non-zero is app geometry the runtime never clipped |
| `vp_infos`, `vp_sub`, `vp_n_not_1`, first sub-target and last viewport | which titles use a sub-screen viewport (Jedi Knight's top-aligned view, G97), and inferred (d) |

These are logged **once at `ctx_free`** (`vcrdd_d3d.c:1210`) as one INFO line per context, never per frame (G52/G76).
If G46's `Diag\D3DStats` lands first, its per-op counters replace `fanimm_*` and observe adds only the rest.

### 5.7 The old path

- Bits clear (the default), or a Banshee/Voodoo3:
  - caps: the four extents stay 0;
  - registers: the target sequence's renderMode and clip values are identical, with no clip1 write;
  - the walker steps VIEWPORTINFO the same way and stores nothing;
  - `VcrDd3dClear` writes the same bytes.
- **Byte-for-byte, pinned by the native tests in §7.**

### 5.8 Interactions

- **G03:** its baseline writes clip1 = 0 before `VcrDd3dTarget`; with G57 on, the target then writes the band rect.
  This is the "G57 writes clip1 = viewport" row of G03 §2.8.
- **G50:** renderMode[24:22] and bit 21 share one write, so both are OR-ed (tested).
- **G51:** clip1 and renderMode go to every chip with the same screen-space values, as Glide does (`BROADCAST_ID`,
  `distate.c:2181-2187`).
- **G53/G47:** clip0, clip1 and renderMode join the shadowed set, and VIEWPORTINFO invalidates them. If the target
  stops being re-sent after a clear, the clear's restore (now the viewport) keeps the scissor right.
- **G16:** lines drawn as quads inherit the hardware clip.
- **G24:** fewer runtime-clipped flat triangles (its `flatclip` question).
- **G36:** render targets taller than 1536 (open question 6).
- **G45:** compare UT2004's bottom row on boots A and B; the line may be a runtime-clip edge artefact (inferred).

## 6. Gating

- **Default OFF** behind `Diag\D3DGuardBand`. The proven path is byte-for-byte unchanged when off (§5.7). Observe
  (8) changes neither caps nor registers.
- **Arming and disarming:** `REGWRITE HKLM SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\vcrmp\Diag D3DGuardBand REG_DWORD <v>`,
  `REGREAD` it back, an activation check, then `scripts/fleet/safe-reboot.py`. One Diag change per clean boot. To
  disarm, write 0 and reboot (`REGDELETE` removes keys, not values).
- **Not safe to land default-on.** It changes the runtime's output (unclipped geometry, maybe a different DP2 shape)
  and makes the HAL responsible for viewport clipping.
- **Default-on after a supervised boot only when all of these hold:**
  - the §7.4 gb tests are 0-fail at 16 and 32 bpp, windowed and fullscreen;
  - the default d3dprobe matrix is unchanged;
  - every title on the §8 list looks the same or better in fullscreen screenshots;
  - H&D's intro is not black, and the Jedi Knight/MotS view does not spill;
  - the gain is measured beyond run-to-run noise (3 runs each, ranges not overlapping).
- **If the gain is absent,** it stays off and drops to P3.

### 7.1 Native: `tests/native/test_vcr_kmd_guardband.c` (new; compiles `vcr_guard.h`; floats allowed host-side)

1. **`the_guard_band_off_is_todays_caps_and_clip`.** The **old value**: `gb` = 0 (and `gb` = 1 with `napalm` = 0)
   gives caps 0,0,0,0 and `lr == tw & 0xfff`, `bt == th & 0xfff`, `n1 == 0`, `rm == 0`, for 256², 640x480, 800x600,

2. **`the_caps_are_the_vendors_napalm_values`.** The **fixed value**: bit 0 gives `0xc3480000, 0xc3480000,
   0x45000000, 0x44c00000`, checked against `(float)` -200/2048/1536 and asserted ≠ the old 0. Bit 2 gives
   -1024/-1024/2048/2048. The dead -2048 (`0xc5000000`) never appears.
3. **`an_unset_or_empty_viewport_is_the_whole_target`** (PRS 12728): unset, (700,0,10,10) on 640x480, and width 0
   each give clip 0 = the whole target. Never `l >= r` or `t >= b`.
4. **`an_odd_viewport_is_exact_in_rect0_and_even_outward_in_rect1`.** (63,47,130,131) on 256² gives `0x003f00c1 /
   0x002f00b2`, clip1 `0x003e00c2 / 0x002f00b2`. The old-buggy value for the same viewport, `0x00000100`, is the
   whole target.
5. **`a_viewport_past_the_target_is_clamped_without_overflow`:** (600,400,100,100) → r = 640, b = 480;

6. **`enguardband_never_meets_a_bad_clip1`.** An exhaustive grid of targets (1..2048) and viewports, degenerate ones
   included: whenever `rm` has bit 21, clip1 is non-empty, its left/right are even, right1 ≤ 0xffe, clip0 ⊆ clip1 and
   clip0 ⊆ the target.
7. **`clip1_matches_the_vendor_rounding`:** for every grid viewport, clip1 equals `VH5/D6DP2.C:1595-1606`'s formula
   applied to the same record.
8. **`the_float_keys_order_like_floats`:** `vcr_f_key`/`vcr_i2f` against real float compares over a sweep that
   includes -0.0, ±inf, NaN, ±0.5 and ±4095.

## 9. Risks

- **Viewport semantics move into the HAL.**
  - A wrong VIEWPORTINFO parse, or a viewport never sent (DX5 execute-buffer titles: open question 8), would cut
    geometry (scissor too small) or let it spill (too large).
  - Mitigations: the whole target until the first VIEWPORTINFO; clamp-and-fall-back, never an empty clip; `out_vp`
    measured on the off build first. H&D (PRS 12728) and JK/MotS (sub-screen view, G97) are the named checks.
- **Unproven hardware path.** renderMode[21] has run in every Glide session on this silicon, but never with real
  off-screen geometry (§2). `gbhw` at step 0 tests the FZ_RECTCLIP half with no driver change; boot B tests the rest.
- **Fill cost.**
  - Off-screen pixels inside the band are now walked by the chip, and in today's serial PIO transport that is CPU
    time.
  - The vendor's asymmetric -200 versus 2048/1536 suggests left/top rows and columns cost iteration while right/bottom
    end early (inferred). `gbfill` and boot C measure it.
  - The net can be negative, which is why there is a gate.
- **Runtime behaviour differs from the docs.** If the runtime sends wholly off-screen triangles (`gone`), each costs
  ~6 µs of PIO. A HAL bbox cull of a few integer compares would be the follow-up.
- **Precision.** Parameters iterate from vertices up to 200 px (left/top) off the target before the first visible
  pixel. The colour/texture tolerances in `gbhw`/`gbedge` catch drift (expected negligible: 200 steps of 2^-12 in
  12.12 colour, inferred).
- **Proven paths:**
  - the default 16 bpp path and Voodoo3: unchanged when off (native-pinned);
  - Glide/OpenGL: Glide sets renderMode/clip1 itself at open, and D3D writes both in full at every target, so no
    residue in either direction;
  - SLI: D3D is single-chip; G51 must broadcast both;
  - the flip path: untouched.
- **Application adaptation.** Titles that read the band may cull less themselves (NVIDIA paper). That is covered by
  the same counters and A/B.
- **Stability:** no new waits, reads or allocations. It adds 2 posted sync writes per target setup and a
  bounds-checked 16-byte read per VIEWPORTINFO.

## 11. Open questions

1. Does XP's D3D8 runtime send VIEWPORTINFO to this DX7-DDI HAL, with `wStateCount` 1 (the DDK says one record
   follows; our walker steps `n`)? Observe: `vp_infos`, `vp_n_not_1`.
2. Are the runtime's clipped polygons TRIANGLEFAN_IMM, and what share of HAL triangles are they per title (G46)?
3. With a band, are wholly off-screen triangles still rejected (`gone`), and is XYZRHW geometry tested against the
   band (`gbedge` observe)?
4. Does FASTFILL honour renderMode[21]/clip1? It matters only if a clear rect reaches outside the viewport;
   `gbviewport`'s clear step shows it.
5. How cheap is the iterators' discard on each side (`gbfill`, boots C/D), and is -200 right for a 1280x960 title?
6. Render targets taller than 1536 (2048² with D3DBigTex, G36): does the runtime fall back to viewport clipping when
   the viewport exceeds the band, or does it clip at the band? The wide bit covers 2048.
7. Does any staged title change its own culling when it sees a band (`out_vp`/`fanimm` A/B per title)?
8. After SetRenderTarget, a device Reset or an alt-tab, does the runtime re-send VIEWPORTINFO, and do DX5
   execute-buffer titles (JK, MotS) get one at all? (Observe: the order log.)
9. `far` > 0 under CLIPPING FALSE apps: vertices beyond ±4096 wrap in hardware whatever G57 does. A pre-existing
   hazard for a separate HAL cull, if it is ever seen.

WHAT THIS PROVES ABOUT THE LOG ITSELF
  glidelab writes each step with fflush() + FlushFileBuffers()
  (tools/glidelab.c:78-79). Both logs came back intact after a hard freeze
  (CPU locked, Num Lock dead) and a power cycle. The flushed step log therefore
  survives this kind of wedge. This is measured, not assumed.

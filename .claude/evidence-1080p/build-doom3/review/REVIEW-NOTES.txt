CONCLUSIONS THAT CHANGED THE REPORT:
  - the pk4 is PURE_NEVER: never on a pure list, still used on a pure server. The builder's
    "excluded on a pure server" risk and "--install-server keeps a pure server's list" rationale were
    wrong. The property is load-bearing because every fleet LAN game (Host launcher) is PURE: a
    non-GUI member would put the pk4 on the host's list (mpmain.gui is read at map load) and lock out
    boxes without it. --build now refuses that.

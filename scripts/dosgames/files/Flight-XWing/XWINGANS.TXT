X-WING (1993, floppy v1.0) - THE MANUAL QUESTION, ANSWERED
==========================================================

If the game shows a Copy Protection box with three symbols and
'See Manual Page N', type ONE of the two words for that page and
press Enter, as written here - the game stores them in lower case.
Either word for the page is accepted; three wrong answers fail.

  Page  Type either
  ----  -----------
     3  mantooine or corellian
     4  alderaan or ghorman
     5  calamari or gamorr
     6  ithor or ottega
     7  sullust or ryloth
     8  hoth or dagobah
     9  geedon or delalt
    10  bimmisaari or dantooine
    11  briggia or massassi
    12  chandrila or agamar
    13  lahara or yavin
    14  hadar or condorra
    15  endaba or tullianne
    16  ehapah or altar
    17  antike or dargoan
    18  kashyyyk or atrivis

Where this comes from: FRONTEND.OVL in this folder - its 32x3 symbol
table, the 32 planet names after it (file offset 0x7E0B2) and
page = (question / 2) + 3. Read out of the code, not seen in the game:
in DOSBox and in the Win98 build VM the box never appeared through a
new pilot, the Tech Room, Tour I mission 1 in flight and a Historical
Combat briefing (research R6, 2026-10-01).

Do NOT run XWINGPAT.COM, the scene patch shipped in this folder: it
writes B8 00 00 at 0x7983A, which in this build is a screen-ID
compare, not the protection check.
